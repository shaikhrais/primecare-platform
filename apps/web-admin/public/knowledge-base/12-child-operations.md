# Child Tenant Operations: The Lean Machine

Child Tenants are designed to run as incredibly lean, hyper-efficient local operations. They do not have HR departments, legal teams, or accounting floors. 

Their only mandate is to violently recruit local Service Providers and ensure their clinical credentials are pure.

## The Algorithmic Workflow
Here is how a Child Tenant monetizes the platform without overhead:
1.  **Recruitment:** The Child Agency recruits "Nurse Jane" through a local Facebook group.
2.  **Onboarding:** Nurse Jane downloads the mobile app and uploads her state licenses directly to the Child Agency portal.
3.  **Broadcast:** The Master Franchise secures a hospital contract and broadcasts an "Overflow Shift" down into the Private Marketplace.
4.  **The Match:** The Auto-Pilot sees that Nurse Jane is compliant, nearby, and matches the skill requirement. It sends her the offer.
5.  **The Execution:** Nurse Jane claims it, arrives, checks in via GPS, performs care, and checks out.

## The Silent Ledger
At the moment of check-out, the magic happens. The platform algorithms automatically track the exact provenance of the labor. The FinTech router takes the hospital's $500 payment and mathematically dissects it: sending the Provider Cut to Nurse Jane, the Margin Split to the Child Agency, the Master Sourcing Fee to the Master Franchise, and the transaction micro-fee up to PrimeCare HQ. 

All instantly. All without a single human accountant opening Excel.
