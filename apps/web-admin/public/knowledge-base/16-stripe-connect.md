# Stripe Connect: The FinTech Backbone

Without a robust, automated financial routing system, the Fractal SaaS would collapse under the weight of manual accounting. Stripe Connect underpins the entire financial topology of PrimeCare, allowing complex N-party fund routing legally and compliantly globally.

## The Financial Hierarchy
*   **Platform HQ (The Platform):** You operate the overarching Stripe Connect "Platform" instance. You bear ultimate KYC (Know Your Customer) responsibility and dictate the routing rules, but importantly, you touch almost no actual care funds directly, shielding you from tax burdens on the gross volume.
*   **Master Franchises (Custom/Express Accounts):** They are onboarded as high-level connected accounts to collect their massive Master revenue streams.
*   **Child Agencies (Sub-Accounts):** They are connected beneath the Master, creating a nested financial tree.
*   **Providers (Direct Payouts):** The nurses connect their bank accounts or debit cards securely via Stripe to receive direct, fast transfers.

## Millisecond Routing
All complex revenue splits—calculating the nurse's hourly rate, subtracting the child agency margin, subtracting the master sourcing fee, and extracting the algorithmic HQ SaaS fee—are calculated natively via the Connect API at the exact millisecond the credit card or ACH transfer is captured. The system maintains 100% compliance and zero manual ledger entry throughout the fractal.
