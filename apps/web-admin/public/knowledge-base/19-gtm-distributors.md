# Go-To-Market: Targeting the "Top 5" Distributors

To maximize the Fractal SaaS network effect, your Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy must be completely inverted from typical B2B SaaS playbooks. If you execute a standard bottom-up SaaS strategy, you will fail.

## The Anti-SMB Strategy
Do not waste marketing capital conducting direct sales to single nurses. Do not run Facebook ads targeting standalone small clinics with 5 employees. The Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. Lifetime Value (LTV) ratio is terrible, and the churn at the bottom of the market is intensely high.

## The Whale Hunting Strategy
You must focus 100% of your sales and executive effort on securing the "Whales". 

Identify the **Top 5** largest, most heavily capitalized staffing agencies, medical distributors, or serial healthcare entrepreneurs in your primary target states or provinces. 

You do not sell them a "software tool." You sell them the "Reseller Hub" and the vision of them becoming a software-enabled platform. Once you close a Whale, they act as the catalyst. They will deploy their own localized sales forces and capital to aggressively recruit the thousands of sub-agencies and nurses downline. 

They bear the heavy, localized Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to grow the base, while you reap the systemic, exponential volume growth hitting your servers. Hook 5 Whales, and you capture the ocean.
