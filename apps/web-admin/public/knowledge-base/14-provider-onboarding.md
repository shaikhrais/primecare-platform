# Provider Onboarding & Compliance Engine

The Service Providers (Nurses, Personal Support Workers, Physiotherapists) are the absolute lifeblood of care delivery. But in healthcare, an unverified provider is a massive legal liability.

The compliance engine must be ruthless and mathematically absolute.

## Unified Verification Flow
When a provider applies to work for a Child Agency, the system presents a sleek, consumer-grade onboarding flow. It requires them to upload standard documentation: State License numbers, criminal background checks, immunization records, and vitals training certificates.

The platform uses OCR (Optical Character Recognition) and API integrations with state registries to instantly verify the authenticity of the documents.

## Hard-Coded Expirations: The Ultimate Defense
The true power of the platform is how it weapons compliance data. The Platform HQ enforces strict, algorithmically impenetrable expiration logic. 

If a nurse's required tuberculosis test expires at midnight on a Friday, the platform physically blocks the Auto-Pilot from assigning them shifts on Saturday morning. The nurse is temporarily quarantined from the matchmaking pool. 

This completely eliminates the manual human error inherent in traditional staffing (where a tired scheduler accidentally tasks a nurse with an expired license). By utilizing hard-coded defensive logic, the platform protects the Master Franchise's lucrative hospital contracts from catastrophic government audit failures.
