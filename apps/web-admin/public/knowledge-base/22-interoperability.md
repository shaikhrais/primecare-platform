# Interoperability: The HL7/FHIR Mandate

The Fractal SaaS does not operate in a vacuum. To win massive government contracts or secure integrations with monolithic hospital systems (like Epic or Cerner), the platform must speak the deeply entrenched, global language of healthcare data.

## The Enterprise Integration Standard
PrimeCare is built to be seamlessly interoperable. All patient electronic health records (EHR), dynamic care plans, and vital telemetry captured by Providers using the PrimeCare mobile apps are not locked away in proprietary SQL databases.

The backend infrastructure seamlessly structures and exposes this data using global **HL7/FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources)** JSON models.

## Bidding for the Mega-Contracts
This provides a massive strategic advantage to your Master Franchisees. When a Root Tenant goes to bid for a $50 Million State Government or National Health Service contract, they face intense scrutiny regarding data silos. 

Because of PrimeCare's architecture, the Master Franchise can confidently state that their underlying technology natively integrates directly into national health registries, allowing data to flow freely between the local nurses taking vitals and the central government mainframes. You weaponize interoperability as a sales tool.
