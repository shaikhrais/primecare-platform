# Revenue Streams: The Economics of the Fractal

The PrimeCare Platform is architected to generate revenue through two distinct channels. One creates a stable operational floor, while the other provides an uncapped ceiling for exponential fintech growth.

## 1. Master Subscription Tiers (The SaaS Floor)
Software-as-a-Service subscriptions provide predictable, high-margin monthly recurring revenue (MRR) to cover your engineering overhead.

HQ charges Root Tenants (Master Franchises) a hefty monthly base fee for the privilege of accessing the massive power of the Reseller Hub and White-Labeling engine.
*   **Example Tier:** *The Enterprise Distributor - $999/month.* This grants the Master Franchise the right to spawn up to 10 Child Agencies beneath them, and custom-brand the entire stack.
*   **Example Tier:** *The Sovereign Network - $4,999/month.* Limitless child spawning and dedicated DB clusters.

Because you are selling to highly capitalized distributors, these SaaS fees are easily absorbed by their operational budgets.

## 2. Transaction Micro-Fees (The FinTech Ceiling)
This is the golden goose. This is where PrimeCare transitions from a software company into an economic network.

Because PrimeCare handles the physical algorithmic routing of money via Stripe Connect (to power "Instant Settlements"), HQ hard-codes a microscopic cut of all volume flowing through the system.

*   **The Mechanics:** Every time a $200 hospital shift is settled and paid out to a nurse, HQ takes a transaction bound—for example, 1.5% + $0.30.
*   **The Scale:** A single Master Franchise might spawn 50 Child Agencies. Those 50 agencies might employ 10 nurses each. Those 500 nurses might work 4 shifts a week. That is 2,000 shifts per week, or 104,000 shifts a year. 
*   At an average shift value of $300, that is $31.2 Million in labor volume from *one* Master Franchise network. A 1.5% cut yields $468,000 in pure, highly-automated FinTech profit to HQ.

As you onboard your 5th, 10th, and 50th Master Franchise, these FinTech micro-fees will eclipse the pure SaaS revenue exponentially, scaling without requiring any additional customer support or sales effort.
