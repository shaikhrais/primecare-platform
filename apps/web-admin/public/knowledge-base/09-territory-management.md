# Territory Acquisition: Feeding the Machine

For a Master Franchise, having world-class software is useless without localized demand. Their day-to-day focus must pivot entirely toward Territory Acquisition.

## The B2B Sales Focus
Because PrimeCare HQ handles the algorithms, the compliance, and the payroll routing, the Master Franchise's executives are freed from the back-office. They turn 100% of their attention to securing massive B2B (Business-to-Business) contracts with local Long-Term Care (LTC) facilities, Hospital networks, or Regional Health Authorities within their designated geolocation territory.

## The Demand Generation Cycle
1.  **The Kill:** The Master Franchise wins a lucrative 3-year contract to be the primary vendor for "Regional Hospital Network A".
2.  **The Ingestion:** Regional Hospital Network A suddenly signals an urgent need for 150 nursing shifts over a holiday weekend.
3.  **The Routing:** The Master Franchise dumps that massive demand volume directly into their branded PrimeCare portal.
4.  **The Execution:** The system ingests the demand, and the Auto-Pilot instantly begins algorithmically matching it against the Master's pool of Child Agencies and direct Providers, sending out push notifications in milliseconds.

The Master Franchise focuses solely on enterprise relationships and feeding demand into the top of the funnel; the PrimeCare platform autonomously handles the chaotic execution of fulfilling that demand at the bottom of the funnel.
