# The Software Provider Business Model (HQ)

To successfully operate PrimeCare HQ, you must undergo a profound psychological shift: **You are no longer in the healthcare business; you are in the fintech and infrastructure business.**

As the overarching technology provider, PrimeCare HQ sits entirely in the cloud, far removed from the messy realities of the agency fray.

## The Prime Directive: Remain the Rail
*   You do not coordinate patient care. 
*   You do not interview or hire nurses. 
*   You do not hold patient liability.
*   You do not deal with clinical malpractice claims or union disputes.

If you ever find yourself involved in localized staffing disputes, you have breached the architecture. Your business model is **pure technology licensing and transaction routing**.

## The Three Pillars of HQ Operations
1.  **Licensing the overarching software rails:** You sell access to the "God-Mode" Reseller Hub to massive Master Franchises.
2.  **Monitoring ecosystem health:** You observe the global network from the Risk Surveillance Engine, ensuring that bad actors (sub-agencies falsifying records or failing to pay providers) are algorithmically quarantined before they threaten the integrity of the Stripe Connect merchant accounts.
3.  **Collecting micro-transactions:** You passively collect microscopic fractional fees on the millions of automated settlements flowing through the algorithmic platform.

The HQ is a pure software development and infrastructure company. Your daily operational focus should exclusively be on keeping the "Clinical Auto-Pilot" algorithms blindingly fast, ensuring the backend worker-apis never drop a packet, and keeping the compliance engines mathematically stringent. The scaling of the healthcare labor happens autonomously beneath you.
