PrimeCare public enquiry information

The PrimeCare territory enquiry form accepts two service-interest categories:
- PSW
- Home care

These are enquiry categories, not a guarantee of a bookable service, a provider's eligibility, or available appointments.

This information contains no approved prices, discounts, opening hours, service-area guarantees, insurance coverage, cancellation terms, clinical advice or appointment availability. A team member must confirm details that are not available from a current authorized business result.

An enquiry is not a confirmed appointment. A saved callback request is not a guarantee that a callback has been assigned or completed. A CRM consultation record does not confirm clinical care.

For administrative enquiries, ask the preferred service, service area and timing one question at a time. Do not request clinical histories, payment-card details or government identification.

For questions requiring individual records, use the existing approved account workflow. Do not reveal account information from this public knowledge source.
