## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I report a bug?
If you encounter an issue on any platform (iOS, Android, Windows, Linux, or Web), please use the "Report an Issue" button within the Governance Dashboard or email support directly with your app version and device details.

### How do I request a feature?
Feature requests are managed through the Governance Portal. Submit a ticket under the "Enhancements" category.

### I forgot my password, how can I log in?
Please contact your local Franchise or Corporate administrator to reset your credentials. The PrimeCare platform uses enforced role-based authentication and does not support self-service password resets for security reasons.
