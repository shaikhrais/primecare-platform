# PrimeCare Platform Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the PrimeCare Platform ("PrimeCare", "we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your health and personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard data across all our desktop, web, and mobile applications, including the PrimeCare Governance app.

## 2. Data Collection
We collect the following types of information:
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, and role-based access controls.
- **Clinical & Governance Telemetry:** Metadata required to ensure platform stability and audit logs.
- **Device Information:** Operating system, device IDs, and crash logs to improve app performance.

## 3. Data Usage
Information is used strictly to:
- Provide and maintain the PrimeCare platform services.
- Authenticate user access based on role (e.g., Corporate, Franchise, PSW).
- Ensure regulatory compliance through automated security and audit logs.

## 4. Data Protection
We employ robust security measures including encryption at rest and in transit. The PrimeCare platform operates under a strict role-based access control (RBAC) model ensuring data is only accessible to authorized personnel.

## 5. Third-Party Services
We do not sell your personal or clinical data. We may use third-party services (like Cloudflare) for infrastructure hosting and telemetry, all of which are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

## 6. User Rights
You have the right to access, modify, or request deletion of your personal data. Please contact our support team to exercise these rights.
