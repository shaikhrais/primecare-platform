# Corporate Leadership (C-Suite)

**Importance:** Crucial for institutional governance, investor relations, ensuring legal/tax compliance, and directing the overarching platform roadmap and expansion strategies.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [CEO](../roles/ceo.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 5 (Ultimate)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Enterprise overview, high-level approvals, risk management.

### [COO](../roles/coo.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 5 (Ultimate)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Tracking global staffing, operational efficiency, routing bottlenecks.

### [CFO](../roles/cfo.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 5 (Ultimate)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Overall consolidated ledgers, corporate tax compliance, P&L.

### [CTO](../roles/cto.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 5 (Ultimate)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Platform health, global security flags, API and infrastructure monitoring.

### [Compliance Manager](../roles/compliance_manager.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 4 (High)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Auditing global incidents, policy enforcement, and credential expirations.

### [Training Director](../roles/training_director.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 4 (High)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Defining universal training matrix and global staff curriculum.
