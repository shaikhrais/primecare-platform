# Clinical Operations (Field)

**Importance:** The core product. This is where PrimeCare's digital architecture converts into physical, high-quality, logistical patient care.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [Personal Support Worker (PSW)](../roles/psw.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 1 (Field)
- **Primary Responsibility:** GPS-coordinated care provisioning, treatment tasks, and daily note filing.

### [Registered Nurse](../roles/nurse.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 2 (Active)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Advanced clinical care provisioning.
