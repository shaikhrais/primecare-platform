# Client & Family Portals

**Importance:** Promotes transparent service consumption, automated self-serve billing, and reduces overhead by eliminating manual family updates.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [Client (Patient)](../roles/client.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 0 (External)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Requesting care, paying invoices, looking up care history.

### [Family Member (Proxy)](../roles/family_member.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 0 (External)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Monitoring caregiver ETAs, receiving updates, managing proxy billing.
