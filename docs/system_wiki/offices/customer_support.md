# Customer Support & Quality Assurance

**Importance:** Ensures retention, safety, and reputational integrity. This office mitigates risk and ensures every intake is properly validated.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [Customer Support Agent](../roles/customer_support.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 1 (Field)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Triaging direct patient tickets and tracking escalations.

### [Intake Coordinator](../roles/intake_coordinator.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 2 (Active)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Validating requesting patient eligibility and compiling intake forms.

### [Quality Assurance](../roles/quality_assurance.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Running field audits, scoring caregivers, reviewing subjective complaints.

### [Training Coordinator](../roles/training_coordinator.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 2 (Active)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Scheduling and tracking caregiver certifications and local course progression.
