# Business Development & Expansion

**Importance:** Drives the external growth and scaling of the PrimeCare platform, turning initial geographic layouts into sprawling commercial domains.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [Territory Expansion Manager](roles/territory_expansion_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 4 (High)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Demographic heatmapping and drafting new sector expansions.

### [Franchise Sales Manager](roles/franchise_sales_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Prospecting leads, closing contracts for new franchise branches.

### [Partnership Manager](roles/partnership_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Managing institutional affiliations and external referral active deals.

### [Regional BDM (Ontario)](roles/regional_bdm_ontario/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Sourcing B2B leads, completing strategic cross-business outreach.

### [Regional BDM (USA)](roles/regional_bdm_usa/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Sourcing US-based strategic partnerships and franchise sales.
