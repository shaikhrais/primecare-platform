# Regional Business Development Manager (USA) Overview

## Role Summary
The Regional Business Development Manager for the USA is responsible for managing territory expansion, franchise sales, and enterprise partnerships across the US region.

## Objectives
- Drive territory adoption and franchise sales within the US.
- Build regional partnerships and network with healthcare systems.
- Report pipeline performance to the Head of Business Development.

## Hierarchy
- **Reports To:** Head of Business Development
- **Office:** Business Development Team
