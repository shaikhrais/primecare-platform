# Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Overview

## Role Summary
The Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) provides rehabilitative and therapeutic massage services to PrimeCare patients to improve circulation, mobility, and healing.

## Objectives
- Deliver therapeutic massage treatments based on patient care plans.
- Assess patient pain and mobility.
- Document treatment efficacy and adjust treatment plans.

## Hierarchy
- **Reports To:** RN (Registered Nurse) / Clinical Lead
- **Office:** Clinical Team
