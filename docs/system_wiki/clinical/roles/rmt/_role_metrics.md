# Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Metrics

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Patient Mobility Improvement Score:** Measured over a 4-week treatment block.
- **Treatment Adherence Rate:** Target >90% patient attendance.
- **Documentation Compliance:** Complete charts within 24 hours.

## Analytics Goals
- Monitor long-term patient rehabilitative success.
- Increase volume of successful full-course treatments.
