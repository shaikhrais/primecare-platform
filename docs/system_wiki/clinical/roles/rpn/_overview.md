# Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Overview

## Role Summary
The Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) is an essential part of the clinical team, responsible for providing specialized, practical nursing care to PrimeCare patients under the direction of an RN.

## Objectives
- Perform clinical assessments and execute care plans.
- Document patient progress and administer medications.
- Collaborate with the clinical care team and support PSWs.

## Hierarchy
- **Reports To:** RN (Registered Nurse) / Clinical Lead
- **Office:** Clinical Team
