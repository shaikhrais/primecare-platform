# Review Franchise Proposal (tsk_cor_hbd_01)

## Action Name:
Review and Approve Franchise Proposal

## Steps
1. Navigate to 'Active Proposals' widget.
2. Open pending proposal.
3. Verify territory rights and capital requirements.
4. Click "Approve" or "Reject".

## Linked Components
- `ProposalListWidget`
- `ProposalApprovalDialog`
