# Head of Business Development Overview

## Role Summary
The Head of Business Development is responsible for leading the growth strategy of PrimeCare at the corporate level, expanding franchises, negotiating partnerships, and ensuring the business development team meets overarching revenue targets.

## Objectives
- Drive corporate revenue growth and franchise expansion.
- Secure strategic national and international partnerships.
- Mentor and lead regional BDMs (Ontario and USA).

## Hierarchy
- **Reports To:** Founder / CEO
- **Office:** Corporate / Head Office
