# Marketing & Outreach

**Importance:** Fuels the top-of-funnel conversion pipelines for both new potential clients and new prospective franchise partners.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [Head of Marketing](roles/head_of_marketing/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 4 (High)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Global multi-region media strategy and funnel supervision.

### [Local Marketing Manager](roles/local_marketing_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 2 (Active)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Budgeting localized branch advertisements and content calendars.

### [Community Outreach](roles/community_outreach/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 1 (Field)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Coordinating grass-roots volunteer events and local partnerships.

### [Territory Sales Manager](roles/territory_sales_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 2 (Active)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Monitoring regional conversions and external B2B area performance.
