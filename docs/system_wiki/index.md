# PrimeCare Operating Offices

Welcome to the PrimeCare Institutional Wiki.

## [Corporate Leadership (C-Suite)](./corporate/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: The highest echelon of the PrimeCare ecosystem. Responsible for strategic oversight, enterprise financial health, global compliance, and macro-level decision making.
- **Importance**: Crucial for institutional governance, investor relations, ensuring legal/tax compliance, and directing the overarching platform roadmap and expansion strategies.

## [Franchise Administration](./franchise/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: Localized branch management ensuring daily operational fluidity, tactical shift scheduling, local recruiting, and branch-level billing.
- **Importance**: The engine of PrimeCare. Franchises are the direct operators. Smooth localized administration directly impacts care delivery and franchise profitability.

## [Business Development & Expansion](./business_development/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: Responsible for acquiring new franchise partners, opening up new territories, and managing strategic B2B partnerships/referral channels.
- **Importance**: Drives the external growth and scaling of the PrimeCare platform, turning initial geographic layouts into sprawling commercial domains.

## [Marketing & Outreach](./marketing/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: Dedicated to brand awareness, advertising pipelines, grass-roots community engagements, and regional commercial advertising.
- **Importance**: Fuels the top-of-funnel conversion pipelines for both new potential clients and new prospective franchise partners.

## [Customer Support & Quality Assurance](./customer_support/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: The frontlines of client communication, intake processing, compliance auditing, and managing escalations directly from patients or caregivers.
- **Importance**: Ensures retention, safety, and reputational integrity. This office mitigates risk and ensures every intake is properly validated.

## [Clinical Operations (Field)](./clinical/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: Direct provisioning of healthcare to clients. Handled remotely or within the local clinics by active medical and support professionals.
- **Importance**: The core product. This is where PrimeCare's digital architecture converts into physical, high-quality, logistical patient care.

## [Client & Family Portals](./client_family/_overview.md)
- **Summary**: Asynchronous portals designated for patients receiving care, or their legal/familial proxies.
- **Importance**: Promotes transparent service consumption, automated self-serve billing, and reduces overhead by eliminating manual family updates.
