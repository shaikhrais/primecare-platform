# Franchise Administration

**Importance:** The engine of PrimeCare. Franchises are the direct operators. Smooth localized administration directly impacts care delivery and franchise profitability.

## Assigned Roles & Authority Levels

### [Franchise Owner](roles/franchise_owner/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 4 (High)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Branch P&L, conversion rates, localized analytics.

### [Operations Manager](roles/operations_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Daily shift coordination, resolving on-ground logistical anomalies.

### [Scheduler Coordinator](roles/scheduler_coordinator/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 2 (Active)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Assigning caregivers, managing shift overlaps and conflicts.

### [Billing Admin](roles/billing_admin/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Branch invoices, refunds, caregiver payroll reconciliation.

### [HR Manager](roles/hr_manager/_role_metrics.md)
- **Authority Level:** Level 3 (Medium)
- **Primary Responsibility:** Recruiting pipelines, onboarding, localized credentialing.
