# The Child Tenant: The Local Entrepreneur

A fractal must be strong at its smallest point. The Child Tenant is the fundamental building block of the network effect.

## Who is the Child Tenant Profile?
They are typically small, highly agile local agencies. Most commonly, they are started by a veteran Registered Nurse or healthcare administrator who has profound local relationships and 10 to 20 colleagues ready to work for them immediately. 

However, they face an insurmountable barrier to entry: they have absolutely no enterprise software, they cannot win massive hospital contracts because of their size, and they have no operating capital to float a $50,000 weekly bi-weekly payroll.

## Removing the Barrier to Entry
By joining a Master Franchise network as a "Child Tenant", this local entrepreneur is granted superpowers:
1.  **Instant Enterprise Software:** Provided by the Master Franchise via the Reseller Hub, ready instantly, fully branded to make them look legitimate.
2.  **Instant Enterprise Demand:** They are plugged into the Master's Private Marketplace, granting them the ability to accept overflow shifts from massive hospital contracts they could never secure on their own.
3.  **Instant Payroll Float:** Because PrimeCare HQ handles the Stripe settlements natively, when their nurses finish a shift, the funds route automatically. The local RN entrepreneur never has to secure a bank loan to float payroll. 

By making it frictionless for the Child Tenant to operate, the Master Franchise can spawn hundreds of them, creating an unstoppable local labor force.
