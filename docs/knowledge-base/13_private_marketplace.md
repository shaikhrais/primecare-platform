# The Private Resource Marketplace: The Walled Garden

Master Franchisees need a way to monetize their Child Agencies beyond just taking algorithmic splits on nursing shifts. They need to lock the Child Agencies into their ecosystem.

We built the **Private Marketplace** to serve as the ultimate B2B Walled Garden.

## Building Internal Economies
Each Master Tenant is equipped with an internal digital marketplace that is visible only to their specific spawned Sub-Agencies. 

Instead of just shifting care hours, the Master can utilize this hub to sell high-margin proprietary B2B assets directly to their downline:
*   **Proprietary Training & Certifications:** E.g., The Master sells an "Advanced Dementia Protocol Certification" module for $500. Child agencies buy it to upskill their nurses so they can qualify for higher-paying auto-pilot shifts.
*   **Compliance Services:** E.g., The Master operates a legal wing and sells "Expedited Background Check Verifications" for $50 to Child Agencies trying to onboard nurses over a weekend.
*   **Emergency On-Call Pools:** Allowing smaller Child Agencies to pay a monthly subscription fee to access the Master's elite emergency staff float when their own nurses call out sick.

By creating a thriving internal economy, the Master Franchise extracts maximum lifetime value from the Child Agencies, while making it operationally impossible for the Child Agency to ever leave the network.
