# The Risk Surveillance Engine: Defending the Fractal

Because Platform HQ does not manage patient outcomes or hire nurses directly, there is a temptation to ignore the clinical reality happening on the ground. However, HQ must safeguard the ecosystem from catastrophic bad actors that could threaten the Stripe processing privileges.

## The Silent Guardian
The Risk Surveillance Engine is a massive backend aggregation tool that perpetually scans all spawned Tenants (Roots and Children alike) in the system.

It acts as an algorithmic panopticon, aggregating compliance risk into a live dashboard for Super Admins at HQ. It monitors complex vectors:
*   "Percentage of Shifts Offered with Expired Credentials"
*   "Provider No-Show and Abandonment Ratios"
*   "Dispute Chargeback Frequencies by Agency"
*   "Sudden spikes in manual override commands"

## The Quarantine Protocol
If a Master Franchise becomes complacent and allows their Child Agencies to run rampant with compliance violations or payroll fraud, the Risk Surveillance Engine flashes red. 

HQ receives automated alerts to step in, throttle Auto-Pilot functionality, freeze Stripe Connect payouts, and sandbox the offending agencies. By programmatically detecting risk before a massive hospital audit occurs, HQ legally protects the software infrastructure and ensures the longevity of the platform.
