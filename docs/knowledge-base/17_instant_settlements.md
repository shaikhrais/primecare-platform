# The "Instant Settlements" Lever

In the healthcare staffing industry, a massive, unaddressed friction point exists: Hospital invoices take 30 to 60 days to pay. Yet, frontline essential workers (nurses) need to be paid weekly, creating massive cash-flow issues for small agencies.

PrimeCare solves this structurally with **Instant Settlements**, turning payroll from a liability into the ultimate provider acquisition lever.

## The Dopamine Loop of Same-Day Pay
When a nurse successfully completes an auto-pilot shift, they execute a geo-fenced "Check-out" on their mobile app as they leave the hospital doors. 

Because the backend is wired directly into Stripe Connect's Instant Payouts API, this GPS check-out event triggers an immediate, programmatic dispatch of funds directly to the provider's attached debit card. 

The nurse receives their hard-earned funds within 30 minutes of walking to their car. 

## The Acquisition Advantage
By offering same-day pay powered by the platform's financial rails—while the Master Franchise utilizes B2B float to bridge the gap—the agencies on the PrimeCare network can violently poach the best clinical talent from legacy competitors who force their nurses to wait two weeks for a paper paycheck. Instant liquidity commands ultimate workforce loyalty.
