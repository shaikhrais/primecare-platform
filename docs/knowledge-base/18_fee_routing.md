# Automated Fee Routing: The Anatomy of a Split

To truly understand how PrimeCare generates immense value without manual human intervention, you must examine the life cycle of a single algorithmic transaction through the Fractal SaaS.

The financial engine executes multi-party splits at the exact millisecond of fund capture.

## The Anatomy of a $100 Shift
Imagine a local hospital urgently requests an hour of emergency ICU coverage and pays $100 via the portal:

1.  **Capture ($100):** The Stripe API captures the funds from the local hospital's ACH or corporate card on file.
2.  **The Labor Cost ($70):** The algorithm determines the market rate for the nurse who accepted the Auto-Pilot shift and Instantly Settles it to their debit card.
3.  **The Agency Margin ($20):** The system routes a split to the Child Agency who originally recruited and onboarded the Nurse into the ecosystem.
4.  **The Master Sourcing Fee ($8):** The system routes a split to the Master Franchise as a reward for securing the hospital contract that generated the demand.
5.  **The HQ Tech Toll ($2):** The system seamlessly extracts 2% ($2.00) and routes it all the way up the chain into the Platform HQ treasury for powering the underlying infrastructure.

A complex, 4-party financial settlement is executed flawlessly, instantly, legally, and automatically, millions of times a year.
