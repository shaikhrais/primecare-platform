# The Reseller Hub: The Engine of Exponential Spawning

The Reseller Hub is the crown jewel mechanism of the Fractal SaaS. It is the specific feature that separates PrimeCare from every other monolithic software in existence.

## The Problem: Overflow Demand
Eventually, a highly successful Master Franchisee secures more B2B hospital contracts than their direct hires can possibly handle. The demand outstrips their local supply. In a traditional model, they would have to painfully reject lucrative contracts, leaving money on the table because they can't recruit fast enough.

## The Solution: The Spawner
Instead of rejecting contracts, the Master Franchise opens their **Reseller Hub**. 

From this dashboard, they make the transition from a staffing agency to a software reseller. With one click of "Spawn Child Tenant", the backend instantly provisions an entirely isolated, containerized mini-agency database instance.

## The Mechanics of Spawning
The Master Franchise sells this newly spawned instance to a local entrepreneur—perhaps a veteran Nurse Practitioner who wants to start her own tiny agency but lacks capital. The local entrepreneur begins fiercely recruiting her own friends and colleagues into her sub-system.

When the Master Franchise receives their massive overflow demand from the hospital, they broadcast it down into the Child Agency ecosystem. The local entrepreneur's nurses pick up the shifts. The Master Franchise takes a systemic cut of the revenue for sourcing the contract, without ever having to manage the nurses themselves. The growth becomes exponential.
