# Macro Ecosystem Crisis Matrix: Synchronized Company Defense

PrimeCare is an interconnected organism. When a structural, company-wide crisis occurs, isolated individual actions are insufficient. This **Master Corporate List** defines how all 9 Entities mobilize **simultaneously** to defend the company, the department, and the patients.

---

## Macro Crisis 1: The "Code Black" Regional Staff Shortage
*Trigger: A severe external event (Blizzard, Union Strike, Transit Collapse) causes 60% of Field Workers to cancel their shifts simultaneously.*

**Simultaneous Entity Synchronization:**
1. **General Manager (GM):** Immediately authorizes an uncapped "Code Black" Emergency Budget. Redirects all marketing ad-spend entirely into emergency recruitment and hazard bonuses.
2. **Manager (HR):** Disables standard "Time-Off Request" approvals in the home natively and blasts an SMS to all off-duty staff.
3. **Coordinator (Logistics):** Hits the `Code Black` toggle on the active Jane Matrix. The system automatically scrubs and physically cancels all "Non-Essential / Routine" shifts locally to preserve resources.
4. **Registered Nurse (RN):** Performs a rapid digital triage on the remaining patient list, tagging only "Life-Critical" (e.g., oxygen replacement) shifts as mandatory.
5. **Personal Support Worker (PSW):** Sees a massive Purple `+2.0x HAZARD PAY` override natively on their app, overriding all standard pay limits to financially incentivize them to travel.
6. **Client:** The App locks out new bookings visually and displays a "State of Emergency - High Severity Cases Only" modal.
7. **Scrum Master:** Monitors the Edge Server limits, ensuring the massive wave of push notifications and SMS broadcasts does not trigger Cloudflare Rate Limiting.

## Macro Crisis 2: The Catastrophic API / Server Outage
*Trigger: The Cloudflare Edge network goes down, or the primary PostgreSQL database crashes entirely.*

**Simultaneous Entity Synchronization:**
1. **Scrum Master:** Executes the literal "Break Glass" protocol natively, switching the main DNS routing to the read-only Database Replica to restore read access globally.
2. **Personal Support Worker (PSW):** Their mobile device detects the HTTP 500 crashes and activates the `SQLite Offline Engine`. They continue collecting EVV signatures and SOAP notes locally on the device SSD transparently.
3. **Coordinator (Logistics):** The Jane Grid goes read-only. The Coordinator relies on the automatically cached schedule and resorts to cellular phone calls to execute routing.
4. **General Manager (GM):** Receives the automated PagerDuty alert and reviews the SLA liability caps mathematically.
5. **Client:** Forms are disabled, replaced with an "Under Maintenance - Local Clinics Offline" standard error boundary.

## Macro Crisis 3: Severe Clinical Liability (The Legal Event)
*Trigger: A patient suffers severe trauma, or threatens a lawsuit against the organization related to physical care.*

**Simultaneous Entity Synchronization:**
1. **Registered Nurse (RN):** Instantly triggers the `Legal Freeze` protocol on the specific underlying Patient UUID.
2. **Personal Support Worker (PSW):** Their S.O.A.P notes and GPS check-in data are instantly locked and physically encrypted into a Read-Only state. They cannot delete or amend their logs, preserving the physical legal evidence of the encounter natively.
3. **Manager (HR):** The system automatically suspends the involved PSW's ability to claim new shifts, placing them on "Administrative Leave" pending a full RN audit.
4. **Coordinator:** The Jane Matrix mathematically rips the suspended PSW out of the active grid and reroutes their upcoming shifts to idle workers automatically.
5. **Scrum Master:** Generates an immutable, cryptographic hash-log of the database records from the incident day natively to hand over to external auditors/legal counsel proving data integrity.
6. **General Manager (GM):** Audits the `Cost Reduction` home mapping the potential insurance deductible hits mapped against the region's current EBITDA natively.

---

**Architectural Takeaway:**
By programming these simultaneous triggers directly into the platform, PrimeCare eliminates human hesitation. The company acts with absolute synchronization, instantly shielding its margins, its data, and its patients the second a macro-level anomaly hits.
