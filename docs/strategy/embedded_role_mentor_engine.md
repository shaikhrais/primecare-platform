# The Embedded AI Mentor & Behavioral Correction Engine
*How PrimeCare physically trains, lectures, and corrects its own workforce autonomously.*

A massive problem with scaling a healthcare business is the cost of human management. If a Coordinator has to spend hours "lecturing" Caregivers on how to use the app or why they shouldn't be late, the business cannot scale.

To solve this, the PrimeCare Ecosystem incorporates an **Embedded Behavioral Mentor**.

---

## 1. The "Day-One" Role Lecture (Automated Onboarding)
The very first time *any* role logs into the system (Coordinator, RN, or Field Worker), the app locks them out of the main home until they clear the **Mandatory Job Briefing**.

Instead of a boring PDF, the system gives them a highly engaging, interactive "Lecture" defining their absolute responsibilities:
- **For a new Coordinator:** *"Your sole objective in this software is to keep the Unfilled Shifts number at zero. If the Surge Budget hits the red zone, you will be penalized. Here is how you use the Geo-Map to dispatch."*
- **For a new Field Worker (PSW):** *"You are the physical backbone of this company. Your job is to arrive precisely on time and log Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) data at the patient's door. Failing to do this destroys the company's compliance."*

## 2. The Algorithmic Disciplinary Engine (Auto-Correction)
The Mentor does not stop after Day-One. It constantly watches the user's mathematical footprint. If a user "misbehaves," the system corrects them instantly without needing a human Supervisor.

**The "Time-Out" Protocol for Field Workers:**
If a worker's `TrustScore` drops due to missing 2 EVV GPS check-ins:
1. **The Lockout:** When they open the app, all their upcoming shifts are hidden.
2. **The Lecture:** The app displays a massive screen: *"Behavioral Alert. You have failed to verify your GPS location twice this week. This compromises patient safety."*
3. **The Retraining:** The software forces them to complete a 2-minute mandatory interactive module on "How to use the EVV Scanner properly" before physically unlocking their shifts again.

## 3. The Escalation Matrix (When the Mentor Fails)
If the Embedded Mentor tries to "lecture" and correct a user, but the user continues to fail mathematically (e.g., they drop 4 shifts in a row), the system engages the **Supervisor Escalation Hook**.

- The system automatically strips the worker of their active schedule.
- It generates a High-Priority Ticket in the **General Manager's Home**.
- The Ticket says: *"System Notice: Worker [John Doe] failed algorithmic retraining 3 times. They are now suspended. Recommend human intervention or termination."*

---
**Summary:** You do not manage the bottom 80% of behavioral issues. The Embedded AI Mentor lectures them, trains them, and corrects them proactively, only escalating the true failures to human managers.
