# PrimeCare Platform - Master Strategy & Architecture Wiki

Welcome to the PrimeCare Operations and Strategy documentation. This repository contains the high-level business logic, operational matrices, and growth playbooks for the platform.

### 📚 Core Reading Material

If you are a Founder, Developer, or Manager onboarding onto the ecosystem, please read these documents in order:

#### 1. [The Superuser Growth Playbook](./superuser_growth_playbook.md)
* **Audience:** Executive / General Manager
* **Content:** The step-by-step business strategy on how PrimeCare is initialized from zero, achieves operational stability, and prepares KPIs ($50k MRR, margins) to raise Series A Venture Capital.

#### 2. [Business Operational Flows](./business_operational_flows.md)
* **Audience:** Operations & Dev Team
* **Content:** Visual flowcharts mapped with High-Fidelity UI mockups detailing the actual lifecycle of the platform. Covers the Master Boot Sequence, the infinite Dispatch Matchmaking Loop, and Clinical SOS Incident overrides.

#### 3. [Entities, Screens & Crisis Protocols Matrix](./entity_operations_matrix.md)
* **Audience:** Scrum Masters & Cloud Architects
* **Content:** The rigorous technical mapping of the 9 specific Platform Roles (Client, PSW, RN, Coordinator). Details exactly which Flutter Screens they are authorized to access, and the rigid SOPs (Crisis Protocols) handling Edge-case failures like Database crashes or Patient trauma.

#### 4. [Human Growth & Crisis Guidance Engine](./human_centric_growth_crisis_engine.md)
* **Audience:** Everyone
* **Content:** Defines how the platform acts as a mentor instead of just a tool. Covers Contextual Crisis Wizards handling operator panic, peer-to-peer social network utilization, and gamifying successful crisis resolution into career promotions.

#### 5. [Macro Ecosystem Crisis Matrix](./macro_ecosystem_crisis_matrix.md)
* **Audience:** Executive Leadership & General Managers
* **Content:** Maps exactly how all 9 Entities synchronize *simultaneously* to defend the entire business during corporate-level catastrophes (e.g. Extreme Staff Shortages, API Database crashes, Legal/Liability Lawsuits).

#### 6. [Master UI Role Showcase Gallery](./role_ui_showcase.md)
* **Audience:** Everyone
* **Content:** A visual gallery of stunning AI-generated UI mockups demonstrating exactly what each of the core Roles sees on their screens.

#### 7. [PrimeCare Autopilot: The Minimal Supervision Engine](./autonomous_platform_autopilot.md)
* **Audience:** Superuser / CEO
* **Content:** Defines the CRON-based Cloudflare logic that allows the company to run autonomously on "Autopilot". Covers auto-surging shifts, Haversine geo-dispatch, dynamic margin governors, and automated employee discipline tracking without manual human intervention.
