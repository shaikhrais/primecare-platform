# PrimeCare Autopilot: The Minimal Supervision Engine

To run a massive, multi-city healthcare platform without losing your sanity, you cannot rely entirely on human Coordinators or Managers. Humans get tired, miss data patterns, and operate slowly.

The **PrimeCare Autopilot** is your silent, algorithmic CEO. Once configured, it operates the Dispatch Matrix and protects the company's financial margins autonomously. Your only job is to supervise the Autopilot's daily summary.

---

## 1. The Autonomous CRON Dispatch Heartbeat
Instead of a human Coordinator starving at a screen trying to find a worker, the **Cloudflare Edge CRON Job** wakes up automatically every 5 minutes and evaluates the entire active database.

- **Algorithmic Matchmaking (The Haversine Push):** The Autopilot scans the grid for unfilled shifts. It uses Haversine GPS mathematics to find all idle Field Workers within a 5km radius and automatically pushes tailored SMS and App Notifications to them.
- **The Escalating Surge Engine:** If a critical shift is 4 hours away and empty, the Autopilot automatically attaches a `+1.1x` pay multiplier. If it's 2 hours away, it bumps it to `+1.25x`. It does this relentlessly until a worker physically accepts the shift, governed entirely by a daily budget cap you set (e.g. Max $200 Surge Spend per day).

## 2. The Margin Governor (Financial Autopilot)
Running an agency often leads to bleeding cash if Coordinators approve too much overtime. The Autopilot strictly enforces your profit margins dynamically.

- **The Freeze Protocol:** The Autopilot calculates the Gross Margin of every single shifted executed today. If the Regional Margin drops below `25%`, the Autopilot instantly *disables* the ability to approve Overtime or Surge Pricing for the rest of the week globally. It forces Operations to run hyper-lean until the margin mathematically recovers.

## 3. The Quality Trust Graph (Algorithmic HR)
You do not need to manually monitor bad employees. The system handles disciplinary actions mathematically.

- **Reputation Decay:** If a Caregiver clocks in late three times, or their EVV GPS location routinely fails verification, the Autopilot degrades their `Internal Trust Score`.
- **Ghosting Shields:** Caregivers with a low Trust Score are algorithmically hidden from high-paying, premium shifts. The system forces them to execute standard, lower-tier shifts perfectly to rebuild their trust metric before allowing them back into the premium pool. This naturally cleanses your fleet of toxic actors without you needing to fire them manually.

Because the Autopilot is handling 95% of the operational noise, the **Superuser Home** transforms from a chaotic control center into a beautiful, calming **Morning Briefing**.

When you wake up and open the App, it says:
