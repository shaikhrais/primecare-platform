# Superuser Day-1 Diagnostic Engine & B2B Growth Advisor
*The interactive algorithmic guide that builds the PrimeCare business from $0 to Scale.*

When a software ecosystem is this massive, dropping a user into a home with 100 empty charts is paralyzing. 

To solve this, the PrimeCare engine includes a **Day-One Diagnostic Advisor**. It continuously evaluates the mathematical parameters of your Database and tells you *exactly what your next physical business move is*, acting as your digital Chief Operating Officer.

---

## 1. The Day-One Empty State (The First Move)
When you log in as Super Admin for the very first time, the platform detects that the business is at `$0` revenue with `0` staff. It locks out the advanced operations and launches a sequential Business-Builder Wizard:

- **Step 1 (The Foundation):** *"Diagnostic: You have 0 active territories."*
   -> The UI prompts you: `[Create First Sub-Franchise & Draw Geo-Fence Area]`
- **Step 2 (The Workforce):** *"Diagnostic: Your franchise has no talent pool."*
   -> The UI prompts you: `[Launch Caregiver & RN Recruitment Funnel]`. It guides you to set up onboarding documentation requirements.
- **Step 3 (The Market / B2B):** *"Diagnostic: You have operators, but 0 patient volume."*
   -> The UI prompts you: `[Open Hospital Relations CRM]`. The system guides you to input local hospital target accounts to begin B2B networking.

## 2. Real-Time Business Diagnostics (The "What is Going Wrong" Radar)
Once the business is running, the **Diagnostic Engine** shifts from an Onboarding Guide into an aggressive **Health Radar**. 

Every time you log into the Super Admin hub, the system evaluates millions of Prisma database rows natively and flags the *weakest link* in your corporate chain across 4 vectors:

### A. Operational Health Vector
* **Alert:** *"⚠️ Franchise A (Etobicoke) is failing to match 30% of its Weekend Night Shifts."*
* **Root Cause Analysis generated by AI:** "You do not have enough PSWs who have marked 'Weekend Nights' in their availability matrix."
* **System Recommendation:** Deploy a highly-targeted hiring campaign specifically for Weekend Night workers.

### B. Financial Health Vector
* **Alert:** *"⚠️ Franchise B (North York) is bleeding cash. Gross Margin dropped to 14%."*
* **Root Cause Analysis:** "Coordinator Sarah applied +1.5x Surge Pricing to 40 shifts this week."
* **System Recommendation:** Lock Coordination overrides and investigate regional staffing pools.

### C. Clinical / Liability Health Vector
* **Alert:** *"⚠️ Severe Legal Liability Exposure detected."*
* **Root Cause Analysis:** "3 Patient Incident Reports (Falls) were logged yesterday, but your RN Oversight has not acknowledged them."
* **System Recommendation:** Immediately ping the active RN to review the S.O.A.P notes.

### D. Hospital Relations (B2B Development) Vector
Instead of just matching shifts, PrimeCare acts as a **B2B Sales CRM** for your business model.
* **Alert:** *"⚠️ Referral Volume from Toronto General Hospital dropped by 18% this month."*
* **Root Cause Analysis:** "No Developer Relations (Sales) touchpoints have been logged with the Discharge Planners in 45 days."
* **System Recommendation:** Schedule a physical lunch meeting with the Hospital Admin layer immediately to rebuild the relationship.

---
**Summary:** You do not have to guess what is going wrong with your company. The PrimeCare ecosystem mathematically searches for the exact parameter that is failing locally and surfaces it to you as an urgent, actionable alert.
