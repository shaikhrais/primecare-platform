Open PSW-T04

Every shift

  1. Before: Check My Day, your schedule, the assigned plan, and current consent. Stop and ask if something is missing.
  2. During: Do only assigned work. Record what happened, including refusals and concerns.
  3. After: Submit the visit note and actual time, check outstanding work, then sign out.

Follow your employer’s urgent response procedure for immediate safety concerns. A training ticket is not an emergency channel.

PSW-T02Care and tasks

Steps

  1. Open the assigned visit. Review the current plan, readiness and precautions.
  2. Acknowledge the assignment. Confirm current consent before starting; report an exception when you cannot start.
  3. Record each actual outcome: done, refused, not done or concern.
  4. Submit the note and confirm its status. Use a signed addendum for a returned note; preserve the original.
  5. Record or acknowledge an approved handover when assigned and permitted.
