# Territory Acquisition & Management

For a Master Franchise, their day-to-day focus is securing B2B contracts with local Long-Term Care (LTC) facilities or Hospital networks within their designated geolocation territory.

## Demand Generation
1.  Master Franchise wins a contract with "Regional Hospital A".
2.  Regional Hospital A signals they need 50 nurses to cover weekends.
3.  The Master Franchise routes that massive demand volume directly into their branded platform.
4.  The system ingests the demand, and the Auto-Pilot begins matching it against the Master's pool of Child Agencies and direct Providers.

The Master Franchise focuses on sales and relationships; the platform handles the execution.
