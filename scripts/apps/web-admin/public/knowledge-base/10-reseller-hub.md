# The Reseller Hub: Spawning Child Agencies

This is the crown jewel mechanism of the Fractal SaaS.

## Overflow & The Spawner
When a Master Franchisee secures more B2B hospital contracts than their direct hires can possibly handle, they don't turn down the contract. Instead, they open the **Reseller Hub**.

From here, they act as the software provider. They click "Spawn Child Tenant", creating an entirely isolated mini-agency instance. 

They sell this instance to a local entrepreneur (e.g., a Nurse who wants to start her own tiny agency). The local entrepreneur recruits new nurses into the system. The Master Franchise broadcasts the overflow demand to the Child Agency, taking a cut of the revenue for sourcing the contract.
