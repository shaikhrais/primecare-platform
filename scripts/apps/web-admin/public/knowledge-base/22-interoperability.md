# Interoperability (FHIR/HL7) & Standards

The Fractal SaaS does not operate in a vacuum. To win massive government or hospital enterprise contracts, the platform must speak the global language of healthcare.

## Enterprise Integration
All patient records, care plans, and vital telemetry captured by Providers using the platform are seamlessly structured using global **HL7/FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources)** JSON models.

This means when a Master Franchise goes to bid for a $50M State Government contract, they can confidently state that their underlying technology (Your Platform) natively integrates directly into Epic, Cerner, and national health registries.
