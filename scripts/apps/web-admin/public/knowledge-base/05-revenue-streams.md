# Revenue Streams for HQ

The PrimeCare Platform generates revenue through two distinct, highly scalable channels:

## 1. Master Subscription Tiers (SaaS)
HQ charges Root Tenants (Master Franchises) a monthly base fee for access to the Reseller Hub and White-Labeling engine.
*   **Example:** $999/month for the Enterprise Tier, giving them the right to spawn up to 10 Child Agencies.

## 2. Transaction Micro-Fees (FinTech)
This is the golden goose. Because PrimeCare handles the physical routing of money via Stripe Connect (Instant Settlements), HQ takes a microscopic cut of volume.
*   **Example:** Every time a $200 shift is settled to a nurse, HQ takes 1.5% + $0.30.
*   As the fractal network scales to thousands of agencies and millions of shifts, this FinTech revenue eclipses the pure SaaS revenue exponentially.
