# The Master Agency Profile

Who are your ideal customers at the Master Franchise level?

They are large, established healthcare staffing firms, regional distributors, or highly capitalized serial entrepreneurs looking to conquer a regional territory (e.g., the State of Texas or the Province of Ontario).

## The Pain Point
To expand, they usually have to hire layers of middle management and manual schedulers. Legacy software costs them hundreds of thousands in licensing and still doesn't do automated payouts.

## The Solution
Instead of paying massive upfront fees, they sign up on PrimeCare HQ, get a "Business-in-a-Box", immediately custom-brand the portal, direct their local hospital contracts to it, and begin dispatching shifts via the Auto-Pilot. They transition from an "agency" into a "software-enabled staffing platform".
