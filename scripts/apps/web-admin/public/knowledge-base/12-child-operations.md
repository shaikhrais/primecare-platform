# Operating Within the Master Network

Child Tenants run lean operations.

Their only mandate is to recruit local Service Providers and ensure they are compliant. 

## The Workflow
1.  Child Agency recruits Nurse Jane.
2.  Nurse Jane uploads her licenses to the Child Agency portal.
3.  The Master Franchise broadcasts an "Overflow Shift" down into the Private Marketplace.
4.  Nurse Jane claims it.
5.  Nurse Jane completes the shift.

The platform automatically tracks the provenance of the labor. It routes the Provider Cut to Nurse Jane, the Margin Split to the Child Agency, the Master Fee to the Master Franchise, and the SaaS transaction fee up to PrimeCare HQ. All instantly.
