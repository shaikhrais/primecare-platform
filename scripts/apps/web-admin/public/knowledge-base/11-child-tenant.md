# The Local Entrepreneur (Child Tenant)

Who is the Child Tenant Profile?

They are typically small, agile local agencies. Most commonly, they are started by a veteran Registered Nurse who has 10-20 colleagues ready to work for them, but has absolutely no software, no hospital contracts, and no operating capital to float payroll.

## The Barrier to Entry Removed
By joining a Master Franchise network, the Child Tenant gets:
1.  **Instant Software:** Provided by the Master Franchise.
2.  **Instant Demand:** Ability to accept overflow shifts from the Master's massive hospital contracts.
3.  **Instant Payroll:** PrimeCare HQ handles the Stripe settlements, meaning the local RN entrepreneur never has to worry about payroll float.
