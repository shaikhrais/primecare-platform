# Network Effects: Franchise-Driven Growth

The brilliance of the Fractal SaaS model is its exponential, self-sustaining loop.

## The Multiplication Factor
When a single Master Franchise signs up 100 local sub-agencies, they do so fiercely and efficiently because **they take a cut of the revenue**. They are incentivized to grow their local volume more than you ever could from HQ.

Every time they spawn a Child Agency, and that Child Agency recruits 20 nurses, your Platform HQ user base multiplies automatically. 

Your transaction volume grows exponentially without you spending a single dollar on local marketing, purely because the Master Franchisees are defending and expanding their local empires on your rails.
