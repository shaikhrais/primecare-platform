# Clinical Auto-Pilot: Algorithmic Matching

The Auto-Pilot is the operational heartbeat of the Fractal SaaS.

## Eliminating the Manual Scheduler
Traditional agencies spend 80% of their overhead on operational staff manually calling and texting nurses to fill Friday night shifts.

The Auto-Pilot changes this:
1.  **Ingestion:** Hospital API or portal drops a list of 50 pending shifts.
2.  **Scoring:** The engine scores all active, compliant providers based on distance, clinical skill-match, and past reliability ratings.
3.  **Dispatch:** It instantly blasts "Shift Offers" to the top 20th percentile.
4.  **Confirmation:** First provider to accept locks the shift. 

What took 3 human schedulers 8 hours, the Auto-Pilot does in 3 seconds.
