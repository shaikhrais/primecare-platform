# Automated Fee Routing & Splits

The financial engine executes multi-party splits at the exact millisecond of fund capture, without human hands touching a ledger.

## Example Shift Breakdown
Imagine a hospital pays $100 for an hour of emergency coverage:

1.  **$100 Captured** from the Local Hospital on file.
2.  **$70 Instantly Settled** to the Service Provider (The Nurse doing the work).
3.  **$20 Split** to the Child Agency (Who recruited the Nurse).
4.  **$8 Split** to the Master Franchise (Who secured the Hospital Contract).
5.  **1.5% ($1.50) + $0.50** routed all the way up to Platform HQ (You) as the technology transaction fee.

Clean, seamless, and mathematically perfect.
