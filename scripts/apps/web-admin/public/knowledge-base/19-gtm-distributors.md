# Targeting the "Top 5" Distributors

To maximize the Fractal SaaS network effect, your Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy is completely inverted from typical B2B SaaS.

## The Anti-SMB Strategy
Do not conduct direct sales to single nurses. Do not run Facebook ads targeting standalone small clinics with 5 employees. The Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. Lifetime Value (LTV) ratio is terrible.

## The Whale Hunting Strategy
Focus 100% of your sales effort on securing the **Top 5** largest, most capitalized staffing agencies, distributors, or serial healthcare entrepreneurs in your target states.

Sell them on the "Reseller Hub". Once closed, they deploy their capital to recruit the thousands of sub-agencies and nurses. They bear the acquisition cost to grow the base, while you reap the systemic volume growth.
