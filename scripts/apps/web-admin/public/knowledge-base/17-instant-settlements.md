# The "Instant Settlements" Advantage

In the healthcare staffing industry, hospital invoices take 30 to 60 days to pay. Yet, nurses need to be paid weekly, creating massive cash-flow issues for small agencies.

PrimeCare solves this with **Instant Settlements**, offering unparalleled provider acquisition leverage.

## The Mechanism
When a nurse successfully completes an auto-pilot shift, their geo-fenced Check-out triggers an instant Stripe Payout to their attached debit card.

They receive their funds within 30 minutes of walking out of the hospital. By offering same-day pay powered by the platform's financial rails, Franchisees can poach the best talent from legacy competitors who force nurses to wait two weeks for a paycheck.
