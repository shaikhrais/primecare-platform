# Value Proposition by Stakeholder Level

Each layer of the Fractal SaaS receives a highly specialized value proposition, creating perfect alignment:

### 1. Platform HQ (You)
*   **Massive Network Effects:** You acquire thousands of end-users without doing local B2B sales.
*   **Revenue:** Software licensing fees combined with fractional transaction boundaries on billions of dollars in shift settlements.
*   **No Clinical Liability:** You are purely a technology rail.

### 2. Master Franchisees
*   **Build an Empire:** The ability to build a massive healthcare holding company by selling your own white-labeled software downline without hiring a single software engineer.
*   **Passive Income:** Taking a cut on the transactions of all your Child Agencies.

### 3. Child Agencies (Local Branches)
*   **Zero Setup Time:** No need to build an app or negotiate merchant accounts. They plug into the Master Franchise and start hiring.
*   **Instant Access:** Utilizing the Private Marketplaces of the Master Franchise for overflow shifts.

### 4. Providers (Nurses/PSWs)
*   **Total Autonomy:** Pick shifts seamlessly from the app.
*   **Instant Settlements:** Get paid minutes after clocking out, instead of waiting 30 days.
*   **Sovereign Identity:** Own your credentials in a portable wallet.
