# Data Sovereignty & Decentralized Identity

PrimeCare leads the way into the Sovereign Health Web.

## The Portable Wallet
Providers and Patients are not technically locked into any single Franchise database. Through W3C Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs), a Nurse owns their credentials in a cryptographically verifiable wallet.

If a Nurse works for Child Agency A, and moves to a different state to work for Child Agency B, their background checks, clinical ratings, and verified experience move with them seamlessly.

The PrimeCare backbone verifies the cryptographic signature of their history, eliminating the need to re-scan documents, and establishing the platform as the ultimate source of truth for clinical labor routing.
