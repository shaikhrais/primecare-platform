# Automated Risk Surveillance Engine

Platform HQ does not manage patient outcomes, but it must safeguard the ecosystem from catastrophic bad actors.

## The Silent Guardian
The Risk Surveillance Engine perpetually scans all spawned Tenants (Roots and Children alike).

It aggregates risk into a live home for Super Admins. It monitors:
*   "Percentage of Shifts Offered with Expired Credentials"
*   "Provider No-Show Ratios"
*   "Dispute Chargeback Frequencies"

If a Master Franchise becomes lazy and lets their Child Agencies run rampant with compliance violations, HQ receives automated alerts to step in, throttle functionality, and legally protect the software infrastructure.
