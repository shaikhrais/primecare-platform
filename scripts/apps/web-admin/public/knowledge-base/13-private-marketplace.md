# The Private Resource Marketplace

Master Franchisees can monetize their Child Agencies beyond just shift splitting by utilizing the **Private Marketplace**.

## The B2B Walled Garden
Each Master Tenant has an internal marketplace visible only to their spawned Sub-Agencies.

Instead of just shifting care hours, the Master can sell B2B assets:
*   **Proprietary Training:** E.g., "Advanced Dementia Protocol Certification ($500)"
*   **Compliance Services:** E.g., "Expedited Background Check Verifications ($50)"
*   **On-Call Pools:** Allowing Child Agencies to buy access to the Master's emergency staff float.

This keeps the entire sub-ecosystem reliant on, and profitable for, the Master Franchise.
