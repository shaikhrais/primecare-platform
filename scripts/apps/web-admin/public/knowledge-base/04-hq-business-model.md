# The Software Provider Business Model (HQ)

As the overarching technology provider, PrimeCare HQ sits entirely above the agency fray. 

## The Prime Directive
You do not coordinate care. You do not hire nurses. You do not hold patient liability or deal with clinical malpractice claims.

Your business model is **pure technology licensing and transaction routing**:
1. You license the overarching software rails to massive Master Franchises.
2. You monitor ecosystem health via the Risk Surveillance Engine.
3. You collect micro-transaction fees on the automated settlements flowing through the platform.

The HQ is a software development and infrastructure company. Your job is to keep the "Auto-Pilot" algorithms sharp and the compliance engines stringent.
