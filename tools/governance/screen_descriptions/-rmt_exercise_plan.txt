Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop and implement personalized treatment plans.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
7. Communicate effectively with clients about their treatment.
8. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.
9. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.
10. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High client cancellation or no-show rates.
2. Frequent complaints or negative feedback from clients.
3. Inconsistent treatment outcomes or client progress.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Lack of proper documentation or record-keeping.
6. Inadequate communication with clients or team members.
7. Signs of burnout or stress among staff.
8. Failure to maintain professional development and training.
9. Issues with appointment scheduling or overbooking.
10. Poor financial management or cash flow problems.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Overview of active clients and appointments.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
4. Treatment outcomes and progress tracking.
5. Financial performance indicators (e.g., revenue, expenses).
6. Staff performance metrics and workload distribution.
7. Alerts for compliance issues or operational red flags.
8. Access to educational resources and training opportunities.
9. Communication tools for client engagement.
10. Visualizations of key performance indicators (KPIs).
