Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits and reviews of clinical practices.
3. Manage clinical staff and ensure proper training and development.
4. Monitor patient care quality and implement improvements.
5. Collaborate with other departments to enhance service delivery.
6. Analyze clinical data and metrics to drive decision-making.
7. Ensure adherence to safety protocols and standards.
8. Address patient complaints and feedback effectively.
9. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of industry trends and regulations.
10. Prepare reports for stakeholders on clinical performance.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or negative feedback.
2. High staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
4. Declining patient care quality metrics.
5. Inconsistent clinical practices among staff.
6. Delays in audit processes or unresolved issues.
7. Lack of communication between clinical and administrative teams.
8. Insufficient training or resources for staff.
9. Poor documentation practices.
10. Unaddressed safety incidents or near misses.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Real-time compliance status and audit results.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care quality.
3. Staff performance metrics and training completion rates.
4. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
5. Incident reports and safety metrics.
6. Operational efficiency metrics (e.g., wait times, service delivery).
7. Financial performance indicators related to clinical operations.
8. Logs of clinical audits and compliance checks.
9. Alerts for any compliance breaches or operational issues.
10. Visualizations of trends in patient care and operational performance.
