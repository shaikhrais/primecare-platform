User Tasks:
1. Attend regional BDM meetings.
2. Review meeting agendas and materials prior to meetings.
3. Provide input and feedback during meetings.
4. Follow up on action items assigned during meetings.
5. Document key takeaways and decisions made during meetings.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent delays in meeting schedules.
2. Low attendance or participation rates.
3. Lack of clarity in meeting agendas.
4. Unresolved action items from previous meetings.
5. Poor communication regarding meeting outcomes.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Upcoming meeting schedule and agenda.
2. Attendance tracking for each meeting.
3. Action items list with assigned responsibilities and deadlines.
4. Summary of key decisions and takeaways from past meetings.
5. Notifications for upcoming meetings and overdue action items.
