Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Conduct compliance audits and scans.
2. Monitor and enforce governance policies.
3. Maintain operational audit logs.
4. Ensure multi-factor authentication is enforced.
5. Stream real-time system audit telemetry.
6. Manage secure event reporting ingestion.
7. Respond to compliance violations and incidents.
8. Collaborate with other departments for governance alignment.
9. Provide training and awareness on governance policies.
10. Report on compliance status and governance metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased frequency of compliance violations.
2. Delays in compliance audit execution.
3. Incomplete or missing operational logs.
4. Failure to enforce security policies (e.g., MFA).
5. Lack of real-time telemetry data.
6. Unresolved incidents or security breaches.
7. Low engagement in governance training sessions.
8. High turnover in governance-related roles.
9. Inconsistent reporting of compliance metrics.
10. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding governance processes.

Dashboard Needs for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance audit status and results.
2. Real-time telemetry data and alerts.
3. Operational audit logs with timestamps.
4. Metrics on policy enforcement (e.g., MFA compliance).
5. Incident reports and resolution status.
6. Training participation and effectiveness metrics.
7. Historical trends of compliance violations.
8. Summary of governance actions taken.
9. Notifications for upcoming audits or reviews.
10. Visual representation of compliance metrics (charts/graphs).
