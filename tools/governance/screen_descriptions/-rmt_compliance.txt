Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct thorough assessments of clients' physical conditions.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on assessments.
3. Perform various massage techniques to alleviate pain and promote relaxation.
4. Maintain accurate and confidential client records.
5. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
6. Stay updated with continuing education and industry standards.
7. Communicate effectively with clients regarding treatment progress.
8. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals when necessary.
9. Manage scheduling and appointment bookings.
10. Maintain a clean and organized workspace.

Red Flags for RMT Operations:
1. Inconsistent client records or documentation.
2. Frequent client complaints about treatment effectiveness.
3. Non-compliance with hygiene and safety protocols.
4. Lack of ongoing professional development or training.
5. High cancellation or no-show rates.
6. Inadequate communication with clients or other healthcare providers.
7. Failure to follow up on client progress or treatment outcomes.
8. Signs of burnout or decreased job satisfaction among staff.
9. Legal or ethical violations in practice.
10. Poor financial management or billing discrepancies.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Overview of current client appointments and schedules.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Client feedback and satisfaction ratings.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., treatment outcomes, client retention).
5. Alerts for upcoming training or certification renewals.
6. Summary of operational compliance checks.
7. Access to client records and treatment plans.
8. Notifications for policy updates or changes in regulations.
9. Financial overview (e.g., revenue, expenses).
10. Communication tools for client engagement and follow-up.
