User Tasks:
1. Input and track social determinants of health data.
2. Review and analyze health trends based on collected data.
3. Generate reports on social determinants affecting health outcomes.
4. Update personal or community health information as needed.
5. Collaborate with healthcare providers to address identified issues.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Incomplete or missing data entries.
2. Delays in data updates or reporting.
3. Low user engagement or interaction with the platform.
4. High error rates in data input or processing.
5. Lack of actionable insights derived from the data.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of key health metrics and trends.
2. Alerts for incomplete data or potential issues.
3. Visualizations of data trends over time.
4. User engagement statistics and feedback.
5. Quick access to report generation tools.
