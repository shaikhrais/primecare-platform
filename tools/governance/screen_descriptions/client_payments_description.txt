List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor client payment statuses.
2. Review and manage payment transactions.
3. Handle payment errors and exceptions.
4. Ensure data accuracy and integrity in payment records.
5. Provide feedback or report issues related to client payments.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent payment errors or failures.
2. Delays in payment processing times.
3. Inconsistent data or discrepancies in payment records.
4. Lack of user feedback or unresolved issues.
5. High volume of support requests related to payments.

What is needed on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of recent client payments and their statuses.
2. Alerts for failed or pending payments.
3. Summary of payment processing times.
4. Quick access to payment error logs.
5. User feedback section for reporting issues or suggestions.
