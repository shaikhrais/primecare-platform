List of work for Regional BDM:
1. Analyze regional sales data to identify trends and opportunities.
2. Develop and implement regional sales strategies.
3. Collaborate with marketing to align campaigns with regional goals.
4. Monitor competitor activities and market conditions.
5. Build and maintain relationships with key clients and stakeholders.
6. Prepare and present sales forecasts and reports to management.
7. Train and support regional sales teams.
8. Ensure compliance with company policies and regulations.
9. Conduct regular performance reviews and provide feedback to team members.
10. Identify areas for improvement and implement corrective actions.

Red flags of operation:
1. Declining sales figures in the region.
2. High turnover rates among sales staff.
3. Inconsistent communication between regional teams and headquarters.
4. Lack of engagement or feedback from clients.
5. Missed deadlines for reporting or project milestones.
6. Unresolved customer complaints or issues.
7. Poor performance metrics compared to regional targets.
8. Increased competition without strategic response.
9. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales initiatives.
10. Insufficient training or resources for sales teams.

Dashboard needs for Regional BDM:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics (e.g., revenue, growth rate).
2. Visualizations of sales trends over time.
3. Client engagement statistics (e.g., feedback, retention rates).
4. Competitor analysis and market share data.
5. Team performance metrics (e.g., individual sales achievements).
6. Alerts for red flags (e.g., declining sales, high turnover).
7. Integration with marketing campaign performance data.
8. Tools for tracking customer complaints and resolutions.
9. Forecasting tools for predicting future sales trends.
10. Resource allocation and training needs assessment.
