Compliance Manager Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits.
2. Monitor adherence to regulatory requirements.
3. Implement corrective actions for compliance failures.
4. Maintain documentation of compliance activities.
5. Train staff on compliance policies and procedures.
6. Report compliance status to management.
7. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance.
8. Stay updated on changes in regulations and standards.
9. Manage compliance-related incidents and investigations.
10. Develop and maintain compliance metrics and dashboards.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in corrective action implementation.
3. Lack of employee training on compliance matters.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. High turnover in compliance staff.
6. Poor communication between departments regarding compliance.
7. Failure to address audit findings in a timely manner.
8. Lack of updates on regulatory changes.
9. Insufficient resources allocated to compliance activities.
10. Negative feedback from compliance audits.

Dashboard Needs for Compliance Manager:
1. Compliance audit results and trends.
2. Status of corrective actions and timelines.
3. Training completion rates for staff.
4. Metrics on compliance violations and incidents.
5. Regulatory updates and changes.
6. Documentation status and accessibility.
7. Communication logs between departments.
8. Resource allocation for compliance activities.
9. Employee feedback on compliance training.
10. Visual representation of compliance metrics over time.
