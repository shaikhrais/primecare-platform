Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Responsibilities:
1. Set the vision and strategy for the organization.
2. Lead and manage the executive team.
3. Oversee the company's operations and resources.
4. Ensure the company meets its financial goals.
5. Communicate with stakeholders, including the board of directors, employees, and the public.
6. Foster a company culture that promotes ethical practices and encourages individual integrity.
7. Monitor industry trends and adapt strategies accordingly.
8. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations.
9. Make high-level decisions about policy and strategy.
10. Represent the company in legal and contractual matters.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining revenue or profits.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent operational disruptions or inefficiencies.
4. Lack of clear communication within the organization.
5. Negative feedback from customers or stakeholders.
6. Non-compliance with regulations or standards.
7. Poor performance metrics or KPIs.
8. Increased competition without a strategic response.
9. Inconsistent financial reporting or budgeting issues.
10. Resistance to change or innovation within the organization.

Dashboard Needs for the CEO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial performance.
2. Real-time operational metrics and analytics.
3. Employee engagement and satisfaction scores.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Customer feedback and satisfaction ratings.
6. Industry trends and competitive analysis.
7. Risk management indicators.
8. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
9. Strategic initiative progress tracking.
10. Communication tools for stakeholder engagement.
