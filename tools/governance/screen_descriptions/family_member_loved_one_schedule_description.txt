List of work the user must do:
1. View the schedule of family members or loved ones.
2. Interact with the schedule to add, edit, or remove appointments.
3. Monitor notifications or reminders related to the schedule.
4. Access detailed information about each family member or loved one.
5. Provide feedback or report issues regarding the schedule.

Red flags of operation:
1. Slow loading times when accessing the schedule.
2. Frequent errors when trying to load data.
3. Inconsistent data display or missing information.
4. Difficulty in navigating the interface or finding features.
5. Lack of responsiveness on different devices or screen sizes.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of upcoming appointments and schedules.
2. Quick access buttons for adding or editing appointments.
3. Notifications or alerts for upcoming events or changes.
4. Summary statistics (e.g., number of appointments, missed events).
5. User feedback section for reporting issues or suggestions.
