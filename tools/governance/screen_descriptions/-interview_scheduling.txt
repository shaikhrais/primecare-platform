Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
3. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, etc.).
4. Screen resumes and applications.
5. Conduct interviews and assessments.
6. Manage candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
7. Maintain relationships with candidates and hiring managers.
8. Analyze recruitment metrics and adjust strategies accordingly.
9. Ensure compliance with hiring policies and regulations.
10. Provide training and support to hiring teams.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates in new hires.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions.
3. Poor candidate experience feedback.
4. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
5. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
6. Frequent changes in hiring requirements.
7. Low engagement from hiring managers.
8. Inability to meet recruitment goals.
9. Over-reliance on a single sourcing channel.
10. Negative employer branding.

Dashboard Needs for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for recruitment (time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, etc.).
2. Candidate pipeline status and metrics.
3. Source of hire analysis.
4. Diversity metrics.
5. Candidate experience ratings.
6. Hiring manager satisfaction scores.
7. Compliance and audit logs.
8. Real-time updates on open positions.
9. Recruitment budget tracking.
10. Historical data trends for recruitment performance.
