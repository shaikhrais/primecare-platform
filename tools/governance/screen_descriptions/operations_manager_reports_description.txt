User Work List:
1. Monitor operational metrics and performance indicators.
2. Review reports on team productivity and efficiency.
3. Identify areas for improvement in operations.
4. Analyze data trends to forecast future performance.
5. Collaborate with team members to address operational challenges.
6. Ensure compliance with operational standards and procedures.
7. Provide feedback and recommendations based on report findings.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant deviations from expected performance metrics.
2. Increased error rates or operational failures.
3. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
4. High turnover rates or employee dissatisfaction.
5. Lack of communication or collaboration among team members.
6. Inconsistent data reporting or analysis.
7. Failure to meet compliance or regulatory requirements.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Visualizations of data trends and patterns.
3. Alerts for any red flag indicators.
4. Summary reports on team performance and productivity.
5. Tools for collaboration and communication among team members.
6. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
7. User-friendly interface for quick navigation and insights.
