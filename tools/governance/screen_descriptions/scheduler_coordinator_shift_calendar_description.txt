List of work the user must do:
1. View the shift calendar.
2. Manage and coordinate shifts for staff.
3. Monitor shift assignments and availability.
4. Update shifts as necessary.
5. Communicate with staff regarding shift changes.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors when loading data.
2. Delays in updating shifts.
3. Inconsistent shift assignments.
4. Lack of communication from staff about availability.
5. High number of unresolved conflicts in shift scheduling.

What we need on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of current shifts and assignments.
2. Notifications for upcoming shifts and changes.
3. Alerts for conflicts or issues in scheduling.
4. Performance metrics on shift management efficiency.
5. Quick access to communication tools for staff.
