User Tasks:
1. Monitor patient payment statuses.
2. Process incoming payments from patients.
3. Review payment history and transaction details.
4. Handle payment-related inquiries from patients.
5. Generate reports on payment trends and outstanding balances.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of payment errors or failed transactions.
2. Delays in payment processing times.
3. Increased patient complaints regarding payment issues.
4. Discrepancies in payment records or reports.
5. Lack of real-time updates on payment statuses.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of total payments received and pending.
2. Alerts for failed transactions or payment errors.
3. Graphical representation of payment trends over time.
4. Quick access to patient payment history.
5. Notifications for payment inquiries or issues needing attention.
