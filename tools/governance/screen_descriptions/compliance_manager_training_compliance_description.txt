List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor compliance training completion status.
2. Review training materials and updates.
3. Ensure all team members are enrolled in required training.
4. Track deadlines for training completion.
5. Generate reports on training compliance.
6. Address any issues or errors in training data.
7. Communicate with team members regarding training requirements.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low training completion rates among team members.
2. Frequent errors or issues reported during training sessions.
3. Delays in updating training materials or compliance requirements.
4. Lack of communication or follow-up with team members.
5. Inconsistent tracking of training deadlines and reports.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of training completion rates.
2. Alerts for upcoming deadlines or incomplete training.
3. Access to training materials and updates.
4. Reporting tools for generating compliance reports.
5. Communication tools for reaching out to team members.
6. Error logs or alerts for any issues encountered during training.
