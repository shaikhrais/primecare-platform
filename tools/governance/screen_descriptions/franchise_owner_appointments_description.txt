Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor and manage franchise operations.
2. Ensure compliance with operational standards and regulations.
3. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
4. Maintain communication with franchisees and stakeholders.
5. Analyze performance metrics and reports.
6. Implement corrective actions when necessary.
7. Provide training and support to franchisees.
8. Manage appointments and schedules effectively.
9. Address customer feedback and complaints.
10. Maintain accurate records and documentation.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decrease in appointment bookings or cancellations.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Non-compliance with operational standards.
4. High turnover rates among franchise staff.
5. Inconsistent performance metrics across franchises.
6. Delays in responding to customer inquiries.
7. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
8. Lack of communication from franchisees.
9. Failure to meet financial targets or budgets.
10. Unusual spikes in operational costs.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of active operations and performance metrics.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Appointment scheduling and management tools.
4. Customer feedback and complaint tracking.
5. Operational logs and historical data.
6. Alerts for red flags or compliance issues.
7. Financial performance indicators.
8. Training and support resources for franchisees.
9. Communication tools for franchisee engagement.
10. Real-time updates on operational changes or announcements.
