List of work the user must do:
1. Review and approve hiring staff documents.
2. Upload necessary documents for new hires.
3. Monitor the status of document submissions.
4. Ensure compliance with HR policies regarding documentation.
5. Communicate with candidates regarding document requirements.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in document submission from candidates.
2. Frequent errors in uploaded documents.
3. Lack of communication from candidates regarding document status.
4. Non-compliance with HR policies.
5. High turnover rates due to documentation issues.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of document submission status for each candidate.
2. Notifications for pending document approvals.
3. Alerts for overdue document submissions.
4. Compliance checklist for HR documentation.
5. Analytics on document submission trends and issues.
