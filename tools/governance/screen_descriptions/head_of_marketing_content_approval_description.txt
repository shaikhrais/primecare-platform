List of work for the User (Head of Marketing Content Approval):

1. Review marketing content submissions.
2. Approve or reject content based on brand guidelines.
3. Provide feedback to content creators.
4. Monitor the status of content approvals.
5. Collaborate with the marketing team for content strategy.
6. Analyze performance metrics of approved content.
7. Ensure timely approvals to meet marketing deadlines.

Red flags of operation:

1. Delays in content approval leading to missed deadlines.
2. High volume of rejected content indicating potential issues with guidelines or communication.
3. Lack of feedback from the Head of Marketing to content creators.
4. Inconsistent approval decisions that may confuse content creators.
5. Insufficient collaboration with the marketing team affecting content quality.
6. Low engagement metrics on approved content suggesting misalignment with audience expectations.

Dashboard needs for the User:

1. Overview of pending content approvals.
2. Metrics on approval times and rejection rates.
3. Feedback section for providing comments on submissions.
4. Collaboration tools for communication with the marketing team.
5. Performance analytics for approved content (e.g., engagement, reach).
6. Notifications for upcoming deadlines and overdue approvals.
7. Visual representation of content approval trends over time.
