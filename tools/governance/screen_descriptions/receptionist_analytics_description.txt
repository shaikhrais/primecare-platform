Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with budgeting and financial tracking.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Provide customer service and support.
8. Manage office supplies and inventory.
9. Perform data entry and database management.
10. Support team members with administrative tasks.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Missed deadlines or appointments.
2. Poor communication with team members.
3. Disorganized files and records.
4. Inaccurate data entry or reporting.
5. Frequent complaints from clients or staff.
6. Lack of follow-up on tasks or inquiries.
7. Inefficient use of office resources.
8. Low morale or high turnover in the administrative team.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview.
2. Calendar and scheduling integration.
3. Communication logs and correspondence tracking.
4. File and document management system.
5. Performance metrics and KPIs.
6. Budget tracking and financial reports.
7. Inventory management for office supplies.
8. Customer feedback and satisfaction metrics.
9. Alerts for upcoming deadlines and tasks.
10. Team collaboration tools and resources.
