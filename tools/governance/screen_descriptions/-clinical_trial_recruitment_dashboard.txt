User Tasks:
1. Monitor clinical trial recruitment metrics.
2. Analyze participant demographics and engagement.
3. Track recruitment timelines and milestones.
4. Identify and address recruitment challenges.
5. Generate reports on recruitment effectiveness.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low participant enrollment rates.
2. High dropout rates from trials.
3. Delays in recruitment timelines.
4. Inconsistent data reporting.
5. Lack of engagement from targeted demographics.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time recruitment metrics display.
2. Visual representation of participant demographics.
3. Timeline tracking for recruitment phases.
4. Alerts for recruitment challenges or delays.
5. Reporting tools for analyzing recruitment effectiveness.
