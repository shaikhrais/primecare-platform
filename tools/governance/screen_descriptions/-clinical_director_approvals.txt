Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
3. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics.
4. Manage clinical staff and ensure adequate training and resources.
5. Address any operational issues or concerns promptly.
6. Facilitate communication between clinical teams and administration.
7. Implement quality improvement initiatives.
8. Review and approve clinical protocols and policies.
9. Ensure patient safety and quality of care.
10. Maintain accurate records and documentation.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High rate of compliance failures or audit discrepancies.
2. Increased number of patient complaints or adverse events.
3. Low staff morale or high turnover rates.
4. Delays in clinical operations or service delivery.
5. Inconsistent reporting of operational metrics.
6. Lack of communication or collaboration among teams.
7. Frequent errors in patient documentation or records.
8. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Real-time compliance status and audit results.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for clinical operations.
3. Logs of operational activities and incidents.
4. Metrics on staff performance and patient outcomes.
5. Alerts for any compliance issues or operational red flags.
6. Visualizations of trends in patient care and safety metrics.
7. Access to historical data for trend analysis and reporting.
8. Tools for communication and collaboration with clinical teams.
