List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor field activities of sales representatives in their territory.
2. Track performance metrics and sales data.
3. Manage and assign tasks to team members.
4. Analyze customer interactions and feedback.
5. Report on sales trends and territory performance.
6. Coordinate with marketing and support teams for effective sales strategies.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low sales performance compared to targets.
2. High turnover rate of sales representatives.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Inconsistent reporting and data entry errors.
5. Lack of communication within the team.
6. Delays in task completion or follow-ups.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of sales performance metrics (e.g., total sales, conversion rates).
2. Task management section with assigned tasks and deadlines.
3. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
4. Alerts for low performance or other red flags.
5. Visualizations of sales trends over time.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Quick access to reports and analytics.
