Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health problems and needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
4. Monitor patient progress and response to treatment.
5. Educate patients and their families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to provide optimal patient care.
7. Document patient care and progress accurately in medical records.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Provide emotional support to patients and families.

Red Flags for RN Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
7. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
8. Unexplained changes in patient conditions.
9. Inadequate staffing levels leading to burnout.
10. Failure to follow up on critical lab results.

Dashboard Needs for RN:
1. Patient health status overview.
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Medication administration records.
4. Patient satisfaction metrics.
5. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
6. Incident reports and safety alerts.
7. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for nursing care.
8. Real-time telemetry data for patient monitoring.
9. Access to care plans and treatment protocols.
10. Communication tools for team collaboration.
