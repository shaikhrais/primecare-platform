User Tasks:
1. Monitor open territories for potential expansion.
2. Analyze data related to territory performance.
3. Identify and prioritize territories for expansion based on metrics.
4. Collaborate with sales and marketing teams to strategize territory development.
5. Update and maintain records of territories and their statuses.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in loading data or frequent errors when accessing territory information.
2. Inconsistent or outdated data displayed on the dashboard.
3. Lack of clear metrics or KPIs to evaluate territory performance.
4. Low user engagement or feedback indicating dissatisfaction with the tool.
5. High number of unresolved issues or bugs reported by users.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time data visualization of open territories.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each territory.
3. Alerts for territories that are underperforming or require immediate attention.
4. User feedback section for continuous improvement.
5. Easy navigation to access detailed reports and analytics.
