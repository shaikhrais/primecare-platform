Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor financial performance and key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Conduct regular compliance audits and scans.
3. Maintain operational logs and documentation.
4. Ensure security clearances and compliance with regulations.
5. Manage and optimize active operations.
6. Respond to alerts and issues in real-time.
7. Coordinate with other franchise owners and corporate management.
8. Provide feedback and suggestions for operational improvements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in key performance indicators (KPIs) such as sales or customer satisfaction.
2. Increased frequency of compliance issues or audit failures.
3. Unresolved operational logs indicating recurring problems.
4. Delays in responding to alerts or issues.
5. Low security clearance levels or exceptions logged.
6. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
7. Inconsistent financial reporting or discrepancies.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of financial performance metrics (sales, expenses, profit margins).
2. Compliance status and recent audit results.
3. Operational logs with timestamps and details of actions taken.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) visualized in charts or graphs.
5. Alerts for any critical issues or red flags.
6. Security clearance status and any exceptions.
7. Real-time telemetry data for operational monitoring.
8. Action buttons for executing compliance scans and other critical functions.
