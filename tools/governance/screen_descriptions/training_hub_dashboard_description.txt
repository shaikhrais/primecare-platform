List of work for Training Candidates:
1. Run compliance scans to ensure adherence to governance standards.
2. Sync security posture to maintain updated security measures.
3. Update policies based on the latest compliance requirements.
4. Export logs for auditing and review purposes.
5. Add logs for any significant actions or changes made during operations.

Red flags of operation:
1. Extended loading times during compliance scans indicating potential issues.
2. Frequent errors or failures in executing governance actions.
3. Inconsistent logs that may suggest lapses in operational procedures.
4. Low security clearance levels or exceptions logged during audits.
5. Decreased performance metrics in operational telemetry.

Dashboard requirements for Training Candidates:
1. Clear visibility of current operational status and compliance metrics.
2. Real-time telemetry data to monitor system performance.
3. Access to audit logs for transparency and accountability.
4. Notifications for any critical issues or required actions.
5. User-friendly interface for executing governance actions and viewing results.
