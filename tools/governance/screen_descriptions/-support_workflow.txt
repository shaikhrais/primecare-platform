List of work for Customer Support:

1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues via various channels (email, chat, phone).
2. Track and manage customer support tickets.
3. Provide troubleshooting assistance and solutions to customers.
4. Escalate complex issues to higher-level support or technical teams.
5. Maintain a knowledge base of common issues and solutions.
6. Gather customer feedback to improve services and products.
7. Train new support staff on processes and tools.
8. Monitor and report on support metrics (response time, resolution time, customer satisfaction).
9. Conduct follow-ups with customers after issue resolution.
10. Collaborate with other departments to resolve customer issues.

Red flags of operation:

1. High volume of unresolved tickets.
2. Increased response and resolution times.
3. Low customer satisfaction scores.
4. Frequent escalations to higher-level support.
5. Lack of updates or communication with customers.
6. Inconsistent knowledge base or outdated information.
7. High turnover rate among support staff.
8. Repeated issues from the same customers indicating unresolved problems.

Dashboard needs for Customer Support:

1. Ticket volume and status overview (open, closed, pending).
2. Average response and resolution times.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
4. Escalation rates and reasons for escalation.
5. Knowledge base usage statistics.
6. Staff performance metrics (individual and team).
7. Common issues reported by customers.
8. Real-time alerts for high-priority tickets or issues.
9. Historical data trends for support metrics.
10. Integration with other tools (CRM, project management) for seamless workflow.
