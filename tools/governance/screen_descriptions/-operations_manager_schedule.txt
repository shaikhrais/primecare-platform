List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor and manage the operations schedule.
2. Review and approve schedules submitted by team members.
3. Identify and address scheduling conflicts.
4. Analyze operational efficiency and make adjustments as needed.
5. Communicate with team members regarding schedule changes or updates.
6. Generate reports on operational performance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent scheduling conflicts or overlaps.
2. Delays in schedule approvals.
3. High levels of employee dissatisfaction with schedules.
4. Inconsistent operational performance metrics.
5. Lack of communication regarding schedule changes.
6. Increased number of errors in scheduling data.

What we need on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of the current operations schedule.
2. Notifications for pending approvals or conflicts.
3. Performance metrics and analytics related to scheduling efficiency.
4. A communication panel for team interactions.
5. Quick access to generate and view reports.
6. Visual indicators for red flags in operations (e.g., conflict alerts, approval delays).
