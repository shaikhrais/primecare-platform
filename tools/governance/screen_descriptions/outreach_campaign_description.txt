Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key clients and stakeholders.
4. Analyze market trends and competitor activities.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align strategies.
6. Monitor and report on the effectiveness of business development initiatives.
7. Lead negotiations and close deals.
8. Manage budgets and resources for business development activities.
9. Provide training and support to team members.
10. Ensure compliance with industry regulations and standards.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High client churn rate.
3. Lack of clear business development strategy.
4. Poor communication within the team.
5. Inconsistent reporting and tracking of performance metrics.
6. Low employee morale or high turnover rates.
7. Failure to meet compliance and regulatory standards.
8. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
9. Ineffective use of resources and budget.
10. Lack of innovation or adaptation to market changes.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue.
2. Client acquisition and retention metrics.
3. Market analysis and competitive landscape overview.
4. Progress tracking for business development initiatives.
5. Compliance status and audit logs.
6. Team performance metrics and employee engagement scores.
7. Financial forecasts and budget utilization.
8. Real-time updates on ongoing negotiations and deals.
9. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from clients.
10. Visualizations of market trends and opportunities.
