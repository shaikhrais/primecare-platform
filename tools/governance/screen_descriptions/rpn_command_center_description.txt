Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Provide direct patient care, including administering medications and treatments.
2. Monitor and record patient vital signs and health status.
3. Assist with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and feeding.
4. Collaborate with healthcare teams to develop and implement patient care plans.
5. Educate patients and families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
8. Participate in quality improvement initiatives and audits.
9. Respond to patient emergencies and provide first aid as necessary.
10. Support and mentor junior staff and students.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient falls or injuries.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
5. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
6. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
7. Delays in patient care or treatment.
8. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
9. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
10. Failure to meet regulatory compliance standards.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Practical Nurse (RPN):
1. Patient status overview with vital signs and alerts.
2. Compliance audit results and action items.
3. Logs of recent patient interactions and care provided.
4. Metrics on medication administration and errors.
5. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
6. Staff performance indicators and training needs.
7. Incident reports and follow-up actions.
8. Access to care plans and treatment protocols.
9. Real-time notifications for critical patient updates.
10. Tools for scheduling and managing daily tasks.
