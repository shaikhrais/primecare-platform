Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the full recruitment lifecycle from job posting to onboarding.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
6. Coordinate and schedule interviews with candidates and hiring teams.
7. Maintain candidate databases and track recruitment metrics.
8. Ensure a positive candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
9. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in talent acquisition.
10. Provide training and support to hiring managers on recruitment processes.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates in newly hired employees.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions.
3. Poor candidate feedback or experience ratings.
4. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
5. Inconsistent communication with candidates and hiring teams.
6. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or targets.
8. Unclear job descriptions leading to mismatched candidates.
9. Low engagement from hiring managers in the recruitment process.
10. Inadequate tracking of recruitment metrics and data.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Overview of open positions and their status.
2. Time-to-fill metrics for each position.
3. Candidate pipeline visualization (number of candidates at each stage).
4. Source of hire analytics to identify effective recruitment channels.
5. Diversity metrics of candidates and hires.
6. Candidate feedback scores and experience ratings.
7. Recruitment budget tracking and spending analysis.
8. Hiring manager engagement metrics.
9. Historical data on turnover rates and retention.
10. Alerts for positions that are taking longer than average to fill.
