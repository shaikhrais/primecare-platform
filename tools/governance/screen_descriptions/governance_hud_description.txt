List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor governance metrics and KPIs.
2. Review compliance and regulatory updates.
3. Analyze risk management reports.
4. Participate in governance meetings and discussions.
5. Provide feedback on governance policies and procedures.
6. Track action items from governance meetings.
7. Ensure alignment with organizational goals and objectives.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in compliance reporting.
2. Increased number of unresolved action items.
3. Lack of participation in governance meetings.
4. Frequent changes in regulatory requirements.
5. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in metrics.
6. High levels of risk identified in reports without mitigation plans.
7. Feedback from stakeholders indicating dissatisfaction with governance processes.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time governance metrics and KPIs.
2. Alerts for compliance deadlines and regulatory changes.
3. Summary of action items and their statuses.
4. Risk management dashboard with identified risks and mitigation strategies.
5. Calendar integration for governance meetings and deadlines.
6. Feedback section for stakeholders to provide input on governance processes.
7. Historical data trends for governance metrics to identify patterns over time.
