Pediatric Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct routine health assessments and screenings for children.
2. Diagnose and treat childhood illnesses and injuries.
3. Monitor growth and development milestones.
4. Provide immunizations and preventive care.
5. Educate parents and caregivers on child health and wellness.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care.
7. Maintain accurate medical records and documentation.
8. Stay updated on pediatric health trends and guidelines.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased number of missed appointments or cancellations.
2. Rising rates of vaccine hesitancy or refusal.
3. Significant changes in patient growth patterns or developmental milestones.
4. High incidence of emergency room visits among patients.
5. Frequent complaints or concerns from parents regarding care.
6. Delays in follow-up care or referrals to specialists.
7. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping practices.

Dashboard Requirements for Pediatric Specialist:
1. Real-time patient appointment status (scheduled, missed, canceled).
2. Immunization tracking and compliance rates.
3. Growth and development tracking metrics.
4. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Alerts for red flags in patient health or operational metrics.
6. Logs of recent activities and compliance checks.
7. Performance metrics against established pediatric health guidelines.
