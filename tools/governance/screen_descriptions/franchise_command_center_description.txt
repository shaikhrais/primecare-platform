Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor operational performance metrics.
2. Ensure compliance with governance standards.
3. Conduct regular audits and scans for compliance.
4. Review and analyze telemetry logs.
5. Manage security clearances and approvals.
6. Respond to operational alerts and issues.
7. Maintain communication with franchise support teams.
8. Implement improvements based on audit findings.
9. Train staff on compliance and operational procedures.
10. Participate in franchise meetings and strategy sessions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in operational performance metrics.
2. Increased number of compliance violations.
3. Delays in audit completion or unresolved issues.
4. Negative trends in telemetry data.
5. High turnover rates among staff.
6. Frequent security clearance issues.
7. Lack of communication or support from franchise teams.
8. Inconsistent application of operational procedures.
9. Unresolved customer complaints or feedback.
10. Failure to implement recommended improvements.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time performance metrics (e.g., active operations, productivity).
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Telemetry data visualization (e.g., charts, graphs).
4. Log of operational activities and alerts.
5. Security clearance status overview.
6. Notifications for compliance deadlines and issues.
7. Access to training resources and materials.
8. Communication tools for franchise support.
9. Historical data for trend analysis.
10. Actionable insights and recommendations based on data.
