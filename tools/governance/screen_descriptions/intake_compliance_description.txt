Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Conduct compliance audits and ensure adherence to governance policies.
2. Monitor and log operational activities and compliance status.
3. Manage secure event reporting and ingestion of operational data.
4. Enforce multi-factor authentication and other security measures.
5. Update and maintain real-time system audit telemetry.
6. Facilitate communication and coordination among clinical teams.
7. Provide training and support for compliance-related processes.
8. Analyze compliance data and generate reports for stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased frequency of compliance violations or audit failures.
2. Delays in processing secure event reports or ingestion forms.
3. Lack of timely updates to governance policies or security measures.
4. High volume of error logs or system alerts indicating operational issues.
5. Inconsistent communication among team members regarding compliance status.
6. Resistance to adopting new compliance protocols or technologies.
7. Failure to complete scheduled compliance audits on time.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators (e.g., compliant, non-compliant).
2. Logs of recent compliance audits and their outcomes.
3. Alerts for any compliance violations or operational issues.
4. Summary of secure event reports submitted and their statuses.
5. Metrics on the effectiveness of security measures (e.g., MFA adoption rate).
6. Historical data trends on compliance performance over time.
7. Quick access to governance directives and policy updates.
8. User-friendly interface for logging operational activities and notes.
