List of tasks a Scrum Master must do:
1. Facilitate Scrum ceremonies (Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective).
2. Remove impediments that hinder the team's progress.
3. Coach the team on Agile principles and practices.
4. Ensure effective communication and collaboration within the team and with stakeholders.
5. Monitor team performance and progress towards goals.
6. Foster a culture of continuous improvement.
7. Protect the team from external interruptions.
8. Support product backlog refinement and prioritization.
9. Assist in conflict resolution within the team.
10. Provide visibility into team progress through metrics and reporting.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work:
1. Frequent missed deadlines or sprint goals.
2. Low team morale or high turnover.
3. Lack of engagement during Scrum ceremonies.
4. Persistent unresolved impediments.
5. Poor communication among team members or with stakeholders.
6. Inconsistent adherence to Scrum practices.
7. High levels of technical debt or unresolved issues.
8. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities.
9. Resistance to change or improvement initiatives.
10. Negative feedback from stakeholders or customers.

Dashboard needs for a Scrum Master:
1. Sprint progress metrics (burn-down charts, velocity).
2. Team performance indicators (commitment vs. completed work).
3. Impediment tracking (status and resolution time).
4. Team engagement levels (participation in ceremonies).
5. Feedback from retrospectives and action items.
6. Backlog health (item prioritization and refinement status).
7. Stakeholder feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Team capacity and workload distribution.
9. Historical performance trends over multiple sprints.
10. Visual representation of team dynamics and collaboration.
