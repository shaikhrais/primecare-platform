List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the franchise pipeline for regional business development managers.
2. Analyze data related to franchise performance and opportunities.
3. Identify potential franchise leads and follow up on them.
4. Collaborate with other teams to ensure alignment on franchise strategies.
5. Report on pipeline status and progress to stakeholders.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in data loading or errors in fetching franchise information.
2. Inconsistent or missing data in the franchise pipeline.
3. Low engagement or follow-up rates on identified leads.
4. Lack of communication or collaboration with other teams.
5. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding pipeline reporting.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time data visualization of the franchise pipeline.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to franchise leads and conversions.
3. Alerts for any operational issues or red flags.
4. A summary of tasks and follow-ups required by the user.
5. Collaboration tools or links to communicate with other teams.
