Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on assessments.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a safe and hygienic treatment environment.
6. Educate clients on self-care and wellness practices.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
8. Communicate effectively with clients and healthcare professionals.
9. Manage scheduling and appointment bookings.
10. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.

Red Flags for RMT Operations:
1. Inconsistent client records or documentation.
2. Frequent client complaints or negative feedback.
3. High cancellation or no-show rates.
4. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
5. Inability to maintain professional boundaries.
6. Failure to stay updated with industry regulations and standards.
7. Poor communication with clients or team members.
8. Inadequate knowledge of contraindications for massage.
9. Signs of burnout or decreased job satisfaction.
10. Non-compliance with continuing education requirements.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Overview of active clients and appointments.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., number of sessions, revenue).
5. Alerts for upcoming certifications or training needs.
6. Access to client treatment plans and progress notes.
7. Real-time updates on hygiene and safety compliance.
8. Educational resources and industry news.
9. Communication tools for client follow-ups.
10. Visual representation of workload and productivity trends.
