Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
3. Assist in patient assessments and care planning.
4. Provide personal care and support to patients.
5. Document patient care and progress accurately.
6. Collaborate with healthcare teams to ensure quality care.
7. Educate patients and families about health management.
8. Maintain a safe and clean environment for patients.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Stay updated with nursing best practices and regulations.

Red Flags for RPN Operations:
1. Frequent medication errors or near misses.
2. High patient fall rates or injuries.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
4. Patient complaints about care or communication.
5. High staff turnover or low morale.
6. Delays in patient care or treatment.
7. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
8. Lack of timely communication with healthcare teams.
9. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
10. Inadequate training or resources for staff.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Real-time patient vital signs and alerts.
2. Medication administration records and compliance tracking.
3. Patient care documentation status.
4. Incident reports and safety metrics.
5. Staff performance and training records.
6. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Compliance audit results and action items.
8. Operational efficiency metrics (e.g., patient wait times).
9. Resource availability (e.g., staffing levels).
10. Access to educational resources and updates.
