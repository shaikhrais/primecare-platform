List of work for the user:
1. Monitor payment transactions.
2. Review payment statuses.
3. Manage payment disputes.
4. Generate payment reports.
5. Update payment settings.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of failed transactions.
2. Unresolved payment disputes.
3. Delays in payment processing.
4. Unusual spikes in transaction amounts.
5. Frequent system errors or crashes.

What we need on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of total transactions and their statuses.
2. Alerts for failed transactions and disputes.
3. Graphs showing transaction trends over time.
4. Quick access to payment settings.
5. Summary of recent payment activities.
