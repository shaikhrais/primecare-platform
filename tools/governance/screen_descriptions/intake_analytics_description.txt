Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming patient referrals and inquiries.
2. Schedule appointments and coordinate with clinical staff.
3. Collect and verify patient information and insurance details.
4. Maintain accurate records of patient interactions and follow-ups.
5. Communicate with patients regarding their intake process.
6. Collaborate with other departments to ensure smooth operations.
7. Monitor and report on intake metrics and performance.
8. Address patient concerns and resolve issues promptly.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and policies.
10. Provide training and support to new intake staff.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased wait times for patient appointments.
2. High volume of patient complaints regarding the intake process.
3. Inaccurate or incomplete patient information being collected.
4. Frequent scheduling errors or double bookings.
5. Low staff morale or high turnover rates in the intake team.
6. Lack of communication between intake and clinical staff.
7. Inconsistent reporting of intake metrics.
8. Delays in processing referrals or insurance verifications.
9. Failure to follow up with patients in a timely manner.
10. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.

Dashboard Needs for Intake Coordinator:
1. Real-time metrics on patient referrals and appointments.
2. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
3. Average wait times for appointments.
4. Number of completed intakes versus scheduled intakes.
5. Staff performance metrics and workload distribution.
6. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or pending actions.
7. Integration with scheduling and electronic health record systems.
8. Visualizations of trends in patient intake over time.
9. Compliance tracking for regulatory requirements.
10. Tools for communication and collaboration with clinical teams.
