Compliance Manager Responsibilities:
1. Monitor compliance with regulations and standards.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
3. Develop and implement compliance policies and procedures.
4. Provide training and support to staff on compliance matters.
5. Report compliance issues and breaches to management.
6. Maintain documentation related to compliance activities.
7. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance.
8. Stay updated on changes in laws and regulations.
9. Manage compliance-related investigations and corrective actions.
10. Prepare compliance reports for stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Increased number of compliance breaches or incidents.
2. Lack of timely reporting on compliance issues.
3. Inconsistent application of compliance policies.
4. High turnover rate in compliance staff.
5. Poor communication between compliance and other departments.
6. Failure to complete required training on compliance.
7. Inadequate documentation of compliance activities.
8. Resistance to compliance audits or assessments.
9. Lack of updates to compliance policies in response to regulatory changes.
10. Negative feedback from staff regarding compliance processes.

Dashboard Needs for Compliance Manager:
1. Compliance status overview (green, yellow, red indicators).
2. Number of compliance breaches reported.
3. Audit results and findings.
4. Training completion rates for staff.
5. Open compliance issues and their statuses.
6. Upcoming compliance deadlines and requirements.
7. Historical compliance data and trends.
8. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to compliance.
9. Alerts for overdue compliance tasks.
10. Integration with other systems for real-time data updates.
