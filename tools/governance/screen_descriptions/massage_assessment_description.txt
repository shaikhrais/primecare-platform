Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on assessments.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
7. Communicate effectively with clients regarding treatment progress.
8. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High client cancellation or no-show rates.
2. Frequent complaints or negative feedback from clients.
3. Inconsistent treatment outcomes or lack of improvement in clients.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Lack of proper documentation or incomplete client records.
6. High turnover rates among staff or therapists.
7. Insufficient training or knowledge in new techniques or practices.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Overview of active clients and treatment plans.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., number of sessions, revenue generated).
5. Alerts for any red flags or operational issues.
6. Access to educational resources and training opportunities.
7. Real-time updates on client appointments and schedules.
8. Visual representation of treatment outcomes and progress.
