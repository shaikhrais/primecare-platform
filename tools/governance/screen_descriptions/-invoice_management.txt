Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare and edit documents and reports.
5. Assist with invoicing and billing processes.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Manage office supplies and inventory.
8. Support team members with administrative tasks.
9. Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information.
10. Provide customer service and support.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Frequent errors in documentation or invoicing.
2. Missed deadlines or appointments.
3. Lack of communication among team members.
4. Inconsistent record-keeping practices.
5. High turnover rates in administrative roles.
6. Unresolved customer complaints or issues.
7. Poor time management leading to backlog.
8. Low morale or motivation among staff.
9. Inadequate training or onboarding processes.
10. Non-compliance with company policies or regulations.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview.
2. Calendar and scheduling integration.
3. Document management system.
4. Communication logs and correspondence tracking.
5. Performance metrics and KPIs.
6. Compliance tracking and audit logs.
7. Inventory management for office supplies.
8. Customer feedback and support ticket status.
9. Team collaboration tools.
10. Alerts for upcoming deadlines and tasks.
