List of work training candidates must do:
1. Complete onboarding training modules.
2. Participate in hands-on workshops.
3. Engage in peer-to-peer training sessions.
4. Attend regular feedback and review meetings.
5. Complete assessments and quizzes to evaluate understanding.
6. Collaborate on team projects to apply learned skills.
7. Stay updated with the latest training materials and resources.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low attendance in training sessions.
2. Poor performance in assessments and quizzes.
3. Lack of engagement during workshops and discussions.
4. Frequent absences or tardiness to scheduled training.
5. Inconsistent participation in team projects.
6. Negative feedback from peers or trainers.
7. Failure to complete required training modules on time.

Dashboard needs for training candidates:
1. Attendance tracking for training sessions.
2. Assessment scores and progress reports.
3. Feedback summaries from trainers and peers.
4. Completion status of training modules.
5. Engagement metrics (e.g., participation in discussions).
6. Upcoming training schedule and reminders.
7. Resources and materials for further learning.
