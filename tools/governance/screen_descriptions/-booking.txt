Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage patient intake processes.
2. Schedule appointments and follow-ups.
3. Collect and verify patient information.
4. Coordinate with clinical staff for patient needs.
5. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.
6. Maintain accurate records and documentation.
7. Communicate with patients regarding their status.
8. Handle inquiries and resolve issues related to bookings.
9. Monitor and report on operational metrics.
10. Update and maintain the booking system.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High number of appointment cancellations or no-shows.
2. Delays in processing patient information.
3. Frequent errors in patient data entry.
4. Increased patient complaints regarding scheduling.
5. Low compliance with regulatory audits.
6. Lack of communication between intake and clinical staff.
7. Inconsistent logging of operational activities.
8. System outages or technical issues affecting bookings.
9. High turnover rates among intake staff.
10. Inadequate training for new staff members.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Real-time appointment scheduling status.
2. Metrics on patient intake volume and trends.
3. Compliance audit results and alerts.
4. Logs of operational activities and issues.
5. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Alerts for high cancellation or no-show rates.
7. Performance indicators for staff efficiency.
8. System health status and error reports.
9. Communication logs with patients and staff.
10. Visualizations of key operational metrics (e.g., charts, graphs).
