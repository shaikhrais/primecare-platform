Chiropractor Work Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on assessments.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Unusual patterns in patient outcomes or treatment effectiveness.
3. High rates of patient dropouts or cancellations.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Failure to adhere to compliance and regulatory standards.
6. Lack of communication or collaboration with other healthcare providers.
7. Frequent equipment malfunctions or safety issues.
8. High turnover rates among staff or practitioners.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Overview of active operations and patient load.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
4. Patient satisfaction metrics and feedback.
5. Real-time telemetry data on operational performance.
6. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
7. Access to patient records and treatment history.
8. Tools for scheduling and managing appointments.
9. Educational resources for patient engagement and self-care.
