User Work List:
1. Monitor telehealth quality metrics.
2. Analyze patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
3. Review compliance with telehealth protocols.
4. Identify trends in telehealth service delivery.
5. Generate reports on telehealth performance.
6. Collaborate with healthcare providers to improve service quality.
7. Update and maintain quality metrics dashboard.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in patient satisfaction scores.
2. Increased number of complaints or negative feedback.
3. Non-compliance with established telehealth protocols.
4. Significant variations in service delivery metrics.
5. Lack of timely reporting or data analysis.
6. Inconsistent communication among healthcare providers.
7. Failure to implement recommended improvements.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time display of key quality metrics.
2. Visual representation of patient satisfaction trends.
3. Alerts for compliance issues or red flags.
4. Easy access to historical performance data.
5. Tools for generating custom reports.
6. Collaboration features for team communication.
7. User-friendly interface for quick navigation.
