Shift Supervisor Responsibilities:
1. Monitor and manage shift schedules.
2. Ensure staff assignments are appropriate and conflict-free.
3. Resolve scheduling conflicts promptly.
4. Maintain communication with staff regarding their shifts.
5. Track and report on shift performance metrics.
6. Optimize staff allocation based on patient needs and staff availability.
7. Document and log operational activities and incidents.
8. Provide support and guidance to staff during shifts.
9. Ensure compliance with operational policies and procedures.
10. Conduct regular audits of shift operations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of scheduling conflicts.
2. Staff shortages or overstaffing.
3. Low staff morale or high turnover rates.
4. Frequent patient complaints regarding care.
5. Inconsistent shift coverage leading to gaps in service.
6. Delays in resolving conflicts or issues.
7. Poor communication among staff members.
8. Inadequate documentation of shifts and incidents.
9. Failure to meet capacity buffer thresholds.
10. Negative trends in performance metrics.

Dashboard Needs for Shift Supervisor:
1. Overview of current shift assignments and statuses.
2. Alerts for scheduling conflicts and unresolved issues.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., staff utilization, patient satisfaction).
4. Logs of operational activities and incidents.
5. Capacity buffer percentage and alerts for exceeding thresholds.
6. Quick access to staff contact information.
7. Tools for resolving conflicts and optimizing schedules.
8. Historical data for trend analysis.
9. Visual indicators for staff performance and workload.
10. Feedback mechanisms for staff and patient input.
