User Work List:
1. Monitor patient flow in the virtual waiting room.
2. Manage patient check-in and check-out processes.
3. Track waiting times for patients.
4. Communicate with patients regarding their status.
5. Analyze usage statistics and patient feedback.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High patient wait times indicating potential bottlenecks.
2. Frequent patient complaints about communication delays.
3. Low patient check-in rates suggesting usability issues.
4. System errors or crashes during peak usage times.
5. Inconsistent data reporting on patient flow.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time patient count in the waiting room.
2. Average wait time metrics.
3. Patient check-in and check-out logs.
4. Alerts for high wait times or system errors.
5. Feedback summary from patients regarding their experience.
