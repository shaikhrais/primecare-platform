Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
3. Perform spinal adjustments and manipulations.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rate of treatment failures or lack of improvement.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
4. Non-compliance with regulatory standards or guidelines.
5. Unusual patterns in patient visits or cancellations.
6. Significant changes in patient demographics or health trends.
7. Negative feedback from staff or other healthcare providers.

Dashboard Needs for Chiropractor:
1. Patient management metrics (e.g., active patients, new patients).
2. Treatment outcome statistics (e.g., improvement rates).
3. Compliance audit results and logs.
4. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
5. Operational efficiency metrics (e.g., appointment wait times).
6. Financial performance indicators (e.g., revenue, expenses).
7. Alerts for red flags or compliance issues.
8. Access to educational resources and updates on best practices.
