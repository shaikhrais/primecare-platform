Pediatric Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular health assessments and check-ups for children.
2. Administer vaccinations and monitor immunization schedules.
3. Record and analyze growth metrics for pediatric patients.
4. Perform developmental screenings and assessments.
5. Provide guidance on nutrition and physical activity for children.
6. Diagnose and treat common childhood illnesses and conditions.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care.
8. Educate parents and caregivers on child health and safety.
9. Monitor and manage chronic conditions in pediatric patients.
10. Stay updated on pediatric healthcare guidelines and best practices.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Delays in administering vaccinations or growth metrics recording.
2. Increased number of adverse reactions reported post-vaccination.
3. Significant discrepancies in growth metrics compared to standard growth charts.
4. High rates of missed appointments or follow-ups.
5. Unusual patterns in patient complaints or symptoms.
6. Non-compliance with health and safety protocols.
7. Increased emergency room visits for preventable conditions.
8. Lack of communication or collaboration with other healthcare providers.
9. Inconsistent documentation of patient records.
10. Failure to address parental concerns or questions adequately.

Dashboard Requirements for Pediatric Specialist:
1. Overview of active operations and their status.
2. Real-time updates on vaccination schedules and compliance.
3. Growth metrics tracking and analysis tools.
4. Alerts for overdue vaccinations or assessments.
5. Logs of operational activities and audits.
6. Performance indicators for patient care quality.
7. Access to educational resources for parents and caregivers.
8. Integration with other healthcare systems for comprehensive data sharing.
9. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and data entry.
10. Tools for reporting and analyzing trends in pediatric health.
