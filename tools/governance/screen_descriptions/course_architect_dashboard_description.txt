Training Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Assess training needs through surveys, interviews, and consultations.
3. Design and deliver training sessions and workshops.
4. Evaluate the effectiveness of training programs.
5. Manage training budgets and resources.
6. Collaborate with other departments to align training with organizational goals.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in training and development.
8. Provide coaching and mentoring to staff.
9. Maintain training records and documentation.
10. Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements related to training.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low employee engagement in training programs.
2. High turnover rates indicating ineffective training.
3. Consistent negative feedback from training evaluations.
4. Lack of alignment between training programs and organizational goals.
5. Insufficient resources allocated for training.
6. Inability to track training effectiveness and outcomes.
7. Frequent changes in training requirements without proper updates.
8. Poor communication regarding training opportunities.
9. Resistance to training from employees or management.
10. Inconsistent training delivery across departments.

Dashboard Needs for Training Director:
1. Training program participation metrics.
2. Employee feedback and satisfaction scores.
3. Training effectiveness and outcomes data.
4. Budget utilization and resource allocation.
5. Compliance tracking for mandatory training.
6. Upcoming training sessions and schedules.
7. Employee skill assessments and development plans.
8. Industry trends and benchmarking data.
9. Training completion rates and timelines.
10. Logs of training sessions and participant attendance.
