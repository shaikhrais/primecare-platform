List of work the user must do:
1. Review financial data related to franchises.
2. Analyze performance metrics and trends.
3. Generate financial reports for stakeholders.
4. Monitor budget adherence and variances.
5. Collaborate with franchise owners for financial planning.
6. Ensure compliance with financial regulations and standards.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant deviations from budget forecasts.
2. Consistent negative cash flow reports.
3. Delays in financial reporting or data updates.
4. High levels of outstanding receivables.
5. Frequent errors in financial data entries.
6. Lack of communication or collaboration with franchise owners.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of key financial metrics (revenue, expenses, profit margins).
2. Visualizations of trends over time (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for budget variances or red flags.
4. Quick access to recent financial reports.
5. Collaboration tools for communication with franchise owners.
6. Compliance checklists and reminders.
