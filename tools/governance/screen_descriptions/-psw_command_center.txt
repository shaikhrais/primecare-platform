Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Monitor clients' health and report any changes to healthcare professionals.
5. Administer medications as prescribed.
6. Prepare meals and assist with feeding if necessary.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Document care provided and any observations in client records.
9. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare teams.
10. Participate in training and professional development activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or families about care quality.
2. High turnover rate of PSWs in the organization.
3. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
4. Missed appointments or scheduled visits.
5. Lack of communication between PSWs and healthcare professionals.
6. Unresolved incidents of safety concerns or accidents.
7. Non-compliance with care plans or protocols.
8. Signs of burnout or dissatisfaction among PSWs.

Dashboard Requirements for PSWs:
1. Overview of active operations and client assignments.
2. Compliance status and alerts for upcoming audits.
3. Real-time logs of care provided and incidents reported.
4. Performance metrics such as client satisfaction scores.
5. Notifications for training opportunities and compliance updates.
6. Access to client health records and care plans.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with healthcare teams.
8. Visual representation of workload and scheduling.
9. Emergency contact information and protocols.
10. Resources for mental health and wellness support for PSWs.
