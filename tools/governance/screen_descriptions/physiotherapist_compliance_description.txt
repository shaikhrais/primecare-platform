Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop individualized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic interventions.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment as necessary.
5. Educate patients about exercises and self-management techniques.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red Flags Indicating Potential Issues:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition.
2. Lack of improvement or deterioration in symptoms.
3. Unexplained pain or discomfort during treatment.
4. Signs of infection or unusual swelling.
5. Patient non-compliance with treatment plans.
6. Inconsistent patient history or documentation.
7. Adverse reactions to prescribed exercises or therapies.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapists:
1. Patient management system with easy access to patient records.
2. Compliance audit logs and status updates.
3. Real-time notifications for patient progress and alerts.
4. Tools for tracking treatment outcomes and effectiveness.
5. Access to educational resources and latest research.
6. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
7. Reporting tools for documenting compliance and governance actions.
