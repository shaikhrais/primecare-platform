List of work the user must do:
1. Conduct market research to identify potential territories for expansion.
2. Analyze demographic and economic data of target regions.
3. Evaluate competition in the identified territories.
4. Prepare reports summarizing findings and recommendations.
5. Collaborate with sales and marketing teams to align strategies.
6. Monitor market trends and adjust strategies accordingly.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent data sources leading to unreliable conclusions.
2. Delays in data collection or analysis impacting decision-making.
3. Lack of collaboration with other departments causing misalignment.
4. Ignoring market trends that could affect expansion strategies.
5. Insufficient resources or tools for effective research.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current market research projects and their statuses.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to market expansion.
3. Visualizations of demographic and economic data for quick insights.
4. Alerts for any red flags identified during research.
5. Collaboration tools for communication with sales and marketing teams.
6. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
