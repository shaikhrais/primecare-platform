List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) for the Ontario region.
2. Review operational reports and metrics.
3. Identify trends and areas for improvement.
4. Communicate with team members and stakeholders.
5. Make data-driven decisions based on dashboard insights.
6. Address any operational issues or red flags promptly.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant deviations from expected KPIs.
2. Increased error rates in operations.
3. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
4. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
5. Customer complaints or negative feedback trends.
6. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time KPIs and metrics display.
2. Visualizations (charts/graphs) for trends over time.
3. Alerts for any red flags or critical issues.
4. Summary of team performance and workload.
5. Access to detailed reports for deeper analysis.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
