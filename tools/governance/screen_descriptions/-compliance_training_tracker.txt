User Work List:
1. View compliance training status for employees.
2. Refresh the training data.
3. Send reminders for pending or overdue training.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Training modules marked as "overdue" indicate non-compliance risks.
2. Errors in loading training data may suggest issues with the API or data integrity.
3. A high number of overdue trainings could indicate a lack of engagement or awareness among employees.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of training completion status (e.g., completed, pending, overdue).
2. Quick access buttons for refreshing data and sending reminders.
3. Visual indicators (e.g., color-coded statuses) for overdue trainings.
4. Detailed view of each training module, including employee names and due dates.
5. Error messages or alerts for any loading issues.
