List of work the user must do:
1. Complete the compliance training modules.
2. Review any related documentation or resources provided.
3. Submit any required assessments or quizzes.
4. Track progress and ensure all modules are completed by the deadline.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent loading errors or issues when accessing the training modules.
2. Lack of user engagement or low completion rates.
3. Negative feedback from users regarding the training content or interface.
4. Delays in updating training materials or addressing user concerns.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Progress tracker showing completion percentage of training modules.
2. Notifications for upcoming deadlines or required assessments.
3. Access to resources and documentation related to compliance training.
4. Feedback section for users to report issues or suggest improvements.
5. Summary of completed modules and scores from assessments.
