General Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Manage budgets and financial performance.
3. Develop and implement business strategies.
4. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
5. Lead and motivate staff to achieve organizational goals.
6. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
8. Communicate with stakeholders and report on performance.
9. Address operational issues and implement solutions.
10. Foster a positive workplace culture.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decrease in productivity or performance metrics.
2. Increased employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
4. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
5. Poor communication and collaboration among teams.
6. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
7. Negative feedback from customers or stakeholders.
8. Lack of innovation or stagnation in business growth.

Dashboard Requirements for General Manager:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Real-time operational metrics and analytics.
3. Compliance status and audit results.
4. Employee performance and engagement metrics.
5. Financial performance indicators (budget vs. actual).
6. Recent operational logs and audit trails.
7. Alerts for any red flags or issues.
8. Tools for executing compliance scans and audits.
9. Options for manual synchronization and updates.
10. Visualizations of trends and forecasts.
