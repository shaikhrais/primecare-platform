List of caregiver tasks:
1. Monitor client health and well-being.
2. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, grooming).
3. Administer medications as prescribed.
4. Provide companionship and emotional support.
5. Maintain a clean and safe environment.
6. Document care provided and any changes in client condition.
7. Communicate with healthcare professionals and family members.
8. Run compliance scans and audits as required.
9. Log operational activities and incidents.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors in medication administration.
2. Unreported changes in client condition.
3. Lack of communication with healthcare professionals.
4. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
5. High turnover of caregivers.
6. Negative feedback from clients or family members.
7. Failure to follow safety protocols.
8. Delays in responding to client needs.

Dashboard needs for caregivers:
1. Real-time updates on client health status.
2. Alerts for medication schedules and compliance issues.
3. Access to logs of care provided and incidents.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., active operations, security clearance).
5. Compliance audit results and action items.
6. Communication tools for contacting healthcare professionals.
7. Training resources and guidelines for best practices.
