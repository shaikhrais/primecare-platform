Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health problems and needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments.
4. Monitor patient progress and response to treatments.
5. Educate patients and families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Provide emotional support to patients and families.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of medication errors or adverse events.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Poor communication among team members.
8. Lack of adherence to care plans or protocols.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Patient health status overview.
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Medication administration records.
4. Incident logs and trends.
5. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care.
6. Staff workload and patient assignments.
7. Real-time telemetry data for monitoring.
8. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
9. Access to educational resources and guidelines.
10. Communication tools for team collaboration.
