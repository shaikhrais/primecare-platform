User Tasks:
1. Review marketing performance reports.
2. Analyze data trends and insights.
3. Identify areas for improvement in marketing strategies.
4. Collaborate with team members to implement changes based on report findings.
5. Monitor ongoing marketing campaigns and their effectiveness.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in metrics.
2. Delays in report generation or access to data.
3. Lack of actionable insights from the reports.
4. Low engagement or response rates from marketing campaigns.
5. High customer acquisition costs without corresponding revenue growth.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs) for marketing campaigns.
2. Visualizations of data trends over time (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for any significant drops in performance metrics.
4. Comparison of current performance against historical data.
5. Ability to filter data by campaign, date range, and other relevant parameters.
