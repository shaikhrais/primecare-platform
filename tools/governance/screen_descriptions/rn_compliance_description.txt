Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient health and vital signs.
3. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
4. Educate patients and families about health conditions and care plans.
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members to provide optimal patient care.
6. Assist in diagnostic tests and procedures.
7. Maintain a safe and clean environment for patients.
8. Respond to patient emergencies and provide first aid.
9. Participate in patient assessments and care planning.
10. Advocate for patient needs and preferences.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient falls or injuries.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols or procedures.
8. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
9. Inadequate staffing levels leading to increased workload.
10. Failure to follow up on abnormal test results.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Patient health status overview (vital signs, alerts).
2. Medication administration records and schedules.
3. Compliance audit results and logs.
4. Incident reports and patient feedback.
5. Staffing levels and workload distribution.
6. Education resources for patient care.
7. Communication tools for team collaboration.
8. Access to patient care plans and assessments.
9. Alerts for critical lab results or changes in patient condition.
10. Performance metrics and quality indicators.
