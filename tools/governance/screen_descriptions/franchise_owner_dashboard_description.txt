List of work for the user:
1. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
2. Review sales data and trends.
3. Manage franchisee communications.
4. Access training and support resources.
5. Analyze customer feedback and satisfaction.
6. Track inventory levels and supply chain issues.
7. Evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness.
8. Set and review operational goals.

Red flags of operation:
1. Declining sales trends over consecutive months.
2. High levels of customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Inventory shortages or excess stock.
4. Low engagement from franchisees in training programs.
5. Inconsistent marketing performance across franchises.
6. Delays in communication or support requests from franchisees.
7. Unresolved operational issues or complaints.

Dashboard needs:
1. Real-time sales performance graphs.
2. Customer satisfaction metrics and feedback summaries.
3. Inventory status indicators.
4. Alerts for declining performance or red flags.
5. Communication logs with franchisees.
6. Access to training materials and resources.
7. Marketing campaign performance analytics.
8. Goal tracking and progress indicators.
