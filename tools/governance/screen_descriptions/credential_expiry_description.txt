HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement HR strategies aligned with business goals.
2. Oversee recruitment, selection, and onboarding processes.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Design and implement training and development programs.
6. Monitor employee performance and conduct evaluations.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
9. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Increased number of grievances or complaints.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
5. Delays in recruitment or high time-to-fill positions.
6. Poor performance management outcomes.
7. Lack of training and development opportunities.
8. Negative workplace culture indicators.
9. Inconsistent communication from management.
10. Insufficient data or metrics to make informed decisions.

Dashboard Needs for HR Director:
1. Employee turnover rate and retention metrics.
2. Employee engagement survey results.
3. Recruitment metrics (time-to-fill, quality of hire).
4. Compliance status with labor laws.
5. Training and development participation rates.
6. Performance management outcomes and trends.
7. Diversity and inclusion statistics.
8. Employee satisfaction and feedback.
9. Compensation and benefits analysis.
10. Key HR metrics visualized for quick insights.
