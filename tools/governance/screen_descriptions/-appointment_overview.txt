Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations of the franchise.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and regulations.
3. Manage staff and provide training as needed.
4. Monitor financial performance and manage budgets.
5. Implement marketing strategies to promote the franchise.
6. Maintain high levels of customer service and satisfaction.
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments of operations.
8. Communicate with the franchisor and report on performance.
9. Address any operational issues or challenges promptly.
10. Foster a positive work environment and culture.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue trends.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Inconsistent compliance with operational standards.
5. Poor inventory management or stock shortages.
6. Lack of engagement or motivation among staff.
7. Ineffective marketing efforts leading to low customer acquisition.
8. Unresolved operational issues or delays in addressing problems.
9. Inaccurate financial reporting or budgeting discrepancies.
10. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).

Dashboard Needs for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time sales and revenue metrics.
2. Employee performance and turnover statistics.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback summaries.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Inventory levels and management alerts.
6. Marketing campaign performance analytics.
7. Operational issue tracking and resolution status.
8. Financial performance overview including budgets and forecasts.
9. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking.
10. Logs of operational activities and compliance audits.
