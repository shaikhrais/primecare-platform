Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health and medical needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Monitor patient progress and adjust care plans as necessary.
4. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
5. Educate patients and families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to provide comprehensive care.
7. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
8. Advocate for patient needs and preferences.
9. Provide emotional support to patients and families.
10. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High patient-to-nurse ratio leading to inadequate care.
2. Frequent medication errors or missed doses.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
4. Delays in patient assessments or interventions.
5. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
6. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
7. Unclear or poorly defined care plans.
8. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
9. Inadequate training or resources for nursing staff.
10. Signs of burnout or stress among nursing staff.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Overview of active care plans and their statuses.
2. Patient demographics and health information.
3. Alerts for overdue assessments or interventions.
4. Metrics on medication administration and compliance.
5. Communication logs with healthcare team members.
6. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Tracking of nursing tasks and responsibilities.
8. Access to educational resources and guidelines.
9. Performance metrics and goals for nursing staff.
10. Tools for reporting incidents or red flags in care delivery.
