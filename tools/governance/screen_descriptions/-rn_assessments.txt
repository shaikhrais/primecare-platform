Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments (mobility, cognitive, nutritional).
2. Record and track assessment scores.
3. Submit assessments for documentation.
4. Monitor and update patient health status.
5. Communicate with healthcare team regarding patient care.
6. Maintain accurate assessment history.
7. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High variability in assessment scores indicating inconsistent evaluations.
2. Delays in submitting assessments leading to incomplete patient records.
3. Frequent errors in recorded data.
4. Lack of communication with the healthcare team.
5. Inability to access historical assessment data.
6. High number of patients with low scores in critical categories (mobility, cognitive, nutritional).

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Overview of current patient assessments and scores.
2. Quick access to assessment history.
3. Alerts for patients with critical scores.
4. Submission status of assessments.
5. Visual representation of trends in patient scores over time.
6. Communication tools for collaboration with the healthcare team.
7. Access to educational resources for improving assessment skills.
