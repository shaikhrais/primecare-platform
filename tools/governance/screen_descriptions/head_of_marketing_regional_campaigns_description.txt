User Tasks:
1. Monitor regional marketing campaigns.
2. Analyze campaign performance metrics.
3. Collaborate with regional teams for campaign strategies.
4. Adjust campaigns based on performance data.
5. Report on campaign outcomes to upper management.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low engagement rates on campaigns.
2. High customer acquisition costs.
3. Inconsistent branding across regions.
4. Delayed reporting on campaign performance.
5. Lack of collaboration between regional teams.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time campaign performance metrics (click-through rates, conversion rates).
2. Visual representation of engagement trends over time.
3. Alerts for campaigns underperforming against set KPIs.
4. Collaboration tools for communication with regional teams.
5. Summary reports of campaign outcomes and insights for future strategies.
