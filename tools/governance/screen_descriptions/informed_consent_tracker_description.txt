User Work Tasks:
1. Review and understand the informed consent process.
2. Track informed consent status for patients.
3. Update patient records with consent information.
4. Generate reports on consent status for compliance.
5. Communicate with patients regarding their consent choices.
6. Ensure all consent forms are properly signed and stored.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in updating consent status.
2. Missing or incomplete consent forms.
3. Inconsistent communication with patients.
4. Lack of visibility into consent tracking progress.
5. High error rates in data entry.
6. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of consent status for all patients.
2. Alerts for missing or overdue consent forms.
3. Metrics on consent completion rates.
4. Quick access to patient consent history.
5. Reporting tools for compliance audits.
6. Communication logs with patients regarding consent.
