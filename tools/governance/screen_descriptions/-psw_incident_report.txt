Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Monitor clients' health and report any changes to healthcare professionals.
5. Administer medications as prescribed.
6. Prepare and serve meals according to dietary requirements.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Document care provided and any incidents or changes in client condition.
9. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare teams.
10. Participate in training and professional development.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent incidents or accidents reported.
2. Delays in responding to client needs or emergencies.
3. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
4. Lack of communication among team members.
5. High turnover rates among staff.
6. Complaints from clients or families regarding care quality.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols or regulations.
8. Signs of caregiver burnout or stress.

Dashboard Needs for Personal Support Worker (PSW):
1. Real-time updates on client health status and care plans.
2. Incident reporting and tracking system.
3. Compliance audit results and alerts.
4. Metrics on daily activities and client engagement.
5. Communication tools for team collaboration.
6. Training and development resources.
7. Access to client feedback and satisfaction surveys.
8. Alerts for medication administration and scheduling.
9. Performance metrics for individual PSWs.
10. Resource allocation and staffing levels.
