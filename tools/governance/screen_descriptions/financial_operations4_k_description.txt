Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis: Develop budgets, forecasts, and financial models.
2. Financial Reporting: Prepare and present financial statements and reports to stakeholders.
3. Risk Management: Identify financial risks and implement strategies to mitigate them.
4. Compliance: Ensure adherence to financial regulations and standards.
5. Cash Flow Management: Monitor and manage cash flow to ensure liquidity.
6. Investment Management: Oversee investment strategies and portfolio management.
7. Strategic Planning: Collaborate with other executives to align financial goals with business strategy.
8. Cost Management: Analyze costs and implement cost-saving measures.
9. Team Leadership: Manage finance department staff and foster professional development.
10. Stakeholder Communication: Communicate financial performance and strategy to stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining Revenue: Consistent decrease in sales or revenue.
2. Increased Expenses: Unexplained rise in operational costs.
3. Cash Flow Issues: Frequent cash shortages or delays in payments.
4. Compliance Violations: Any instances of non-compliance with financial regulations.
5. Inaccurate Financial Reporting: Frequent errors or discrepancies in financial statements.
6. High Employee Turnover: Increased turnover in the finance team may indicate issues.
7. Lack of Financial Controls: Weaknesses in internal controls leading to potential fraud.
8. Poor Audit Results: Negative findings from internal or external audits.

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Metrics such as revenue growth, profit margins, and return on investment.
2. Financial Statements: Real-time access to income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements.
3. Budget vs. Actuals: Comparison of budgeted figures against actual performance.
4. Cash Flow Forecast: Projections of cash inflows and outflows.
5. Compliance Status: Overview of compliance with financial regulations.
6. Risk Assessment: Dashboard displaying identified financial risks and mitigation strategies.
7. Operational Metrics: Insights into operational efficiency and cost management.
8. Audit Logs: Access to logs of financial transactions and audits.
9. Investment Performance: Overview of investment portfolios and returns.
10. Team Performance: Metrics on finance team productivity and efficiency.
