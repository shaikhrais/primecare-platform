QA Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Review and analyze requirements and specifications.
2. Develop and execute test plans and test cases.
3. Perform manual and automated testing.
4. Identify, document, and track defects.
5. Collaborate with development teams to resolve issues.
6. Conduct regression testing after fixes and enhancements.
7. Maintain testing documentation and reports.
8. Ensure compliance with quality standards and best practices.
9. Participate in code reviews and design discussions.
10. Provide feedback on usability and user experience.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High number of defects reported post-release.
2. Frequent changes in requirements without proper documentation.
3. Lack of communication between QA and development teams.
4. Inconsistent testing processes and methodologies.
5. Insufficient test coverage for critical features.
6. Delays in the testing phase impacting release schedules.
7. Low morale or high turnover in the QA team.
8. Inadequate tools and resources for testing.
9. Poorly defined roles and responsibilities within the team.
10. Ignoring feedback from previous testing cycles.

Dashboard Requirements for QA Specialist:
1. Overview of current testing status (e.g., tests passed, failed, in progress).
2. Real-time defect tracking and status updates.
3. Metrics on test coverage and execution.
4. Historical data on defect trends and resolution times.
5. Compliance status and audit logs.
6. Performance indicators for testing efficiency.
7. Alerts for critical issues or bottlenecks in the testing process.
8. Integration with CI/CD pipelines for automated testing results.
9. User feedback and usability testing results.
10. Access to documentation and testing resources.
