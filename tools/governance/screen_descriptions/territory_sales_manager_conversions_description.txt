User Tasks:
1. Monitor sales conversions in their territory.
2. Analyze data trends and performance metrics.
3. Identify areas for improvement in sales strategies.
4. Collaborate with team members to optimize sales efforts.
5. Generate reports on sales performance.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant drop in conversion rates.
2. High customer churn or dissatisfaction.
3. Inconsistent sales data or reporting errors.
4. Lack of engagement from team members.
5. Delayed response times to customer inquiries.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time sales conversion metrics.
2. Visual representation of sales trends over time.
3. Alerts for significant changes in performance.
4. Access to customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Collaboration tools for team communication and strategy sharing.
