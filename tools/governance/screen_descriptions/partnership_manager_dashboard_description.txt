Partnership Manager Responsibilities:
1. Monitor partnership performance and compliance.
2. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
3. Update security policies and procedures.
4. Synchronize operational posture.
5. Maintain and export audit logs.
6. Facilitate communication between partners and internal teams.
7. Analyze partnership data for insights and improvements.
8. Manage partnership agreements and renewals.
9. Address partnership-related issues and conflicts.
10. Report on partnership metrics and KPIs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decreased partnership engagement or participation.
2. Increased number of compliance violations or audit failures.
3. Delays in communication or response times with partners.
4. Unresolved issues or conflicts with partners.
5. Negative trends in partnership performance metrics.
6. Lack of transparency in partnership activities.
7. High turnover rates in partnership management team.
8. Inconsistent reporting or data discrepancies.

Dashboard Requirements for Partnership Manager:
1. Overview of active partnerships and their statuses.
2. Compliance audit results and history.
3. Security clearance levels for partnerships.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for partnership success.
5. Real-time telemetry logs of partnership activities.
6. Operational audit logs with timestamps and details.
7. Alerts for compliance issues or red flags.
8. Metrics on partnership engagement and satisfaction.
9. Tools for exporting logs and reports.
10. Visualizations of partnership performance trends over time.
