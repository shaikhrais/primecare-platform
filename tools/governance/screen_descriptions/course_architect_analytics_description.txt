Training Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Assess training needs through surveys, interviews, and consultations.
3. Design and deliver training sessions, workshops, and seminars.
4. Evaluate training effectiveness and make improvements.
5. Manage training budgets and resources.
6. Collaborate with other departments to align training with organizational goals.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in training and development.
8. Maintain training records and documentation.
9. Provide coaching and support to trainers and facilitators.
10. Foster a culture of continuous learning within the organization.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low training participation rates.
2. Negative feedback from training participants.
3. High turnover rates among trained employees.
4. Inconsistent training quality across different sessions.
5. Lack of alignment between training programs and organizational goals.
6. Insufficient resources allocated for training.
7. Failure to track training outcomes and metrics.
8. Resistance to change from employees regarding new training initiatives.
9. Limited engagement from leadership in training programs.
10. Poor communication about training opportunities.

Dashboard Needs for Training Director:
1. Training participation metrics (enrollment, attendance).
2. Feedback and satisfaction scores from training sessions.
3. Training effectiveness metrics (pre- and post-training assessments).
4. Budget tracking for training programs.
5. Training completion rates and certifications achieved.
6. Trends in employee performance post-training.
7. Upcoming training schedules and deadlines.
8. Resource allocation and utilization statistics.
9. Employee engagement levels in training initiatives.
10. Reports on alignment of training programs with organizational objectives.
