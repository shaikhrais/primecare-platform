User Tasks:
1. Monitor compliance risks and register them in the system.
2. Review and update existing risk entries as necessary.
3. Generate reports on compliance status and risk assessments.
4. Collaborate with team members to address compliance issues.
5. Ensure timely communication of compliance updates to stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in updating risk entries may indicate a lack of engagement or awareness.
2. Frequent errors or issues reported during data loading may suggest underlying technical problems.
3. Low user interaction with the dashboard could indicate usability issues or lack of necessary features.
4. High turnover of compliance-related tasks may signal inefficiencies in processes or resource allocation.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of current compliance risks with status indicators (e.g., open, resolved).
2. Visual representation of risk trends over time (graphs/charts).
3. Notifications for pending updates or actions required on compliance risks.
4. Access to historical data for analysis and reporting purposes.
5. User activity logs to track engagement and identify areas for improvement.
