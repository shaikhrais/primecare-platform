List of tasks for an Infrastructure Auditor:
1. Review infrastructure configurations and compliance with standards.
2. Conduct risk assessments and identify vulnerabilities.
3. Monitor system performance and availability.
4. Evaluate security controls and access management.
5. Analyze incident response and recovery procedures.
6. Assess change management processes.
7. Document findings and recommendations.
8. Collaborate with IT teams for remediation efforts.
9. Prepare audit reports for stakeholders.
10. Stay updated on industry best practices and regulations.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent system outages or performance issues.
2. Unpatched vulnerabilities or outdated software.
3. Lack of documentation for changes and configurations.
4. Inconsistent access controls or unauthorized access attempts.
5. Delays in incident response or recovery.
6. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
7. Poor communication between teams.
8. High turnover rates in IT staff.
9. Insufficient training for personnel on security practices.
10. Lack of regular audits or assessments.

Dashboard needs for an Infrastructure Auditor:
1. Overview of system health and performance metrics.
2. Compliance status with regulatory standards.
3. Real-time alerts for security incidents or breaches.
4. Summary of audit findings and remediation status.
5. Risk assessment scores and vulnerability reports.
6. Change management logs and approval workflows.
7. Access control reports and user activity logs.
8. Historical data on incidents and resolutions.
9. Integration with monitoring tools for proactive alerts.
10. Visualizations for trends in system performance and security posture.
