Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming patient referrals and inquiries.
2. Schedule appointments and coordinate with clinical staff.
3. Collect and verify patient information and insurance details.
4. Communicate with patients regarding their intake process.
5. Maintain accurate records of patient interactions and data.
6. Collaborate with healthcare providers to ensure smooth transitions.
7. Monitor and follow up on pending referrals and appointments.
8. Provide support and guidance to patients throughout the intake process.
9. Address any issues or concerns raised by patients or staff.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of missed appointments or cancellations.
2. Delays in processing patient referrals.
3. Incomplete or inaccurate patient information.
4. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
5. Lack of communication between intake coordinators and clinical staff.
6. Difficulty in tracking referral status or follow-ups.
7. Inconsistent application of intake procedures.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Overview of pending referrals and their statuses.
2. Appointment scheduling calendar.
3. Metrics on appointment cancellations and no-shows.
4. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Integration status with healthcare providers.
6. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or pending actions.
7. Summary of completed intakes and processing times.
8. Access to patient demographics and insurance information.
