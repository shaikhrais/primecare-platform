VIP Client Manager Responsibilities:
1. Monitor VIP client interactions and satisfaction.
2. Execute compliance scans and quality verification sweeps.
3. Maintain operational logs and telemetry data.
4. Address any issues or red flags promptly.
5. Communicate with clients regarding their needs and feedback.
6. Analyze data to identify trends and areas for improvement.
7. Collaborate with other departments to enhance client experience.
8. Provide regular reports on VIP client status and metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased client complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Delays in compliance scan execution or reporting.
3. Unusual spikes in operational logs indicating potential issues.
4. Failure to meet service level agreements (SLAs).
5. Lack of communication or responsiveness to VIP clients.
6. Inconsistent data or discrepancies in reports.

Dashboard Requirements for VIP Client Manager:
1. Overview of VIP client satisfaction metrics.
2. Real-time compliance scan status and results.
3. Operational logs with timestamps and severity levels.
4. Alerts for any red flags or issues that require immediate attention.
5. Visualizations of trends in client interactions and satisfaction.
6. Metrics on service level agreements and compliance adherence.
7. Quick access to communication tools for client outreach.
8. Historical data analysis for trend identification and reporting.
