List of tasks for the Regional Manager USA:

1. Monitor active operations and ensure optimal productivity.
2. Conduct compliance audits and ensure all governance invariants are validated.
3. Synchronize security posture and update policies as needed.
4. Export audit logs for review and compliance purposes.
5. Review key performance indicators and telemetry data.
6. Address any operational issues or red flags identified during monitoring.
7. Communicate with team members and stakeholders regarding operational status.
8. Implement corrective actions based on audit findings or performance metrics.
9. Maintain documentation of operational procedures and compliance activities.
10. Provide training and support to team members on compliance and operational standards.

Red flags of operation:

1. Decrease in productivity metrics or active operations.
2. Unresolved compliance issues or audit findings.
3. Security clearance discrepancies or exceptions logged.
4. Inconsistent telemetry data indicating potential system issues.
5. Delays in policy updates or synchronization processes.
6. Negative feedback from team members regarding operational processes.
7. High volume of audit logs indicating frequent issues or incidents.
8. Lack of communication or collaboration within the team.

Dashboard requirements for the Regional Manager USA:

1. Overview of active operations and productivity metrics.
2. Compliance audit status and results.
3. Security clearance levels and any exceptions.
4. Real-time telemetry data and trends.
5. Audit logs with timestamps and descriptions of events.
6. Notifications for any red flags or issues that require attention.
7. Action buttons for running compliance scans and exporting logs.
8. Historical data for performance comparison and trend analysis.
9. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and access to information.
10. Visual representations of data (charts, graphs) for quick insights.
