List of work for the user:
1. Review and assess incoming assessments.
2. Monitor the status of assessments.
3. Communicate with other team members regarding assessment updates.
4. Document findings and decisions made during the assessment process.
5. Ensure all assessments are completed within the required timeframe.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in assessment completion.
2. High error rates in documented findings.
3. Lack of communication among team members.
4. Inconsistent data entry or documentation practices.
5. Frequent system errors or loading issues.

Dashboard needs for the user:
1. Overview of pending and completed assessments.
2. Notifications for overdue assessments.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., average assessment completion time).
4. Communication tools for team collaboration.
5. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
