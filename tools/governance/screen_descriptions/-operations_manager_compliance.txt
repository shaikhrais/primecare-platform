Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure compliance with policies.
2. Conduct regular audits and compliance checks.
3. Manage operational risks and implement mitigation strategies.
4. Coordinate with other departments for operational efficiency.
5. Analyze performance metrics and generate reports.
6. Facilitate training and development for staff.
7. Ensure resource allocation aligns with operational goals.
8. Address and resolve operational issues promptly.
9. Maintain communication with stakeholders regarding operational updates.
10. Implement process improvements for efficiency.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in operational processes or audits.
3. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
4. Inconsistent performance metrics.
5. Lack of communication or collaboration among teams.
6. Unresolved operational issues or complaints.
7. Failure to meet regulatory requirements.
8. Negative feedback from stakeholders or clients.
9. Increased operational costs without corresponding value.
10. Low engagement in training or compliance programs.

Dashboard Needs for Operations Manager:
1. Compliance status overview (pass/fail rates).
2. Audit logs and recent compliance checks.
3. Performance metrics (KPIs) for operational efficiency.
4. Incident reports and resolutions.
5. Employee engagement and satisfaction metrics.
6. Resource allocation and utilization statistics.
7. Risk assessment and mitigation status.
8. Real-time alerts for compliance violations.
9. Historical data trends for performance analysis.
10. Action items and follow-up tasks from audits or meetings.
