Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop individualized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy techniques and research.

Red Flags Indicating Need for Immediate Attention:
1. Sudden worsening of symptoms or new symptoms.
2. Signs of infection (fever, redness, swelling).
3. Unexplained weight loss or fatigue.
4. Neurological symptoms (numbness, weakness, coordination issues).
5. Severe pain not responding to treatment.
6. Changes in bowel or bladder function.
7. Patient expresses suicidal thoughts or severe emotional distress.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Patient list with current status and treatment plans.
2. Quick access to patient records and history.
3. Notifications for red flag alerts.
4. Progress tracking for each patient.
5. Integration with other healthcare systems for collaboration.
6. Access to educational resources and latest research.
7. Tools for scheduling and managing appointments.
8. Analytics on treatment outcomes and patient satisfaction.
