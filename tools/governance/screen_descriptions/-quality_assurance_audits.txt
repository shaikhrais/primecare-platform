User Work List:
1. Review quality assurance audit reports.
2. Conduct audits on various processes.
3. Document findings and recommendations.
4. Collaborate with team members to implement improvements.
5. Monitor the effectiveness of implemented changes.
6. Provide feedback on audit processes.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors or inconsistencies in audit reports.
2. Delays in completing audits or reporting findings.
3. Lack of communication among team members.
4. Resistance to implementing recommended changes.
5. Inconsistent follow-up on previous audits.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of current audit status (completed, in progress, pending).
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to audit effectiveness.
3. Alerts for overdue audits or follow-ups.
4. Summary of findings from recent audits.
5. User feedback section for continuous improvement suggestions.
