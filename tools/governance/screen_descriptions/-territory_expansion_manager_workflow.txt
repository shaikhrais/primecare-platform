List of responsibilities for a Territory Expansion Manager:
1. Identify potential markets for expansion.
2. Conduct market research and analysis.
3. Develop and implement territory expansion strategies.
4. Collaborate with sales and marketing teams.
5. Monitor performance metrics and adjust strategies accordingly.
6. Build relationships with local stakeholders and partners.
7. Prepare and present reports on territory performance.
8. Manage budgets and resources for expansion projects.
9. Ensure compliance with local regulations and company policies.
10. Train and support local teams in new territories.

Red flags of operation:
1. Consistent underperformance in new territories.
2. Lack of market research leading to poor decision-making.
3. High turnover rates in newly established teams.
4. Negative feedback from local stakeholders.
5. Budget overruns without clear justification.
6. Delays in project timelines.
7. Inconsistent communication between teams.
8. Failure to adapt strategies based on performance data.

Dashboard needs for a Territory Expansion Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each territory.
2. Market research insights and trends.
3. Budget tracking and financial performance.
4. Timeline and status updates on expansion projects.
5. Stakeholder engagement metrics.
6. Team performance and turnover rates.
7. Compliance status with local regulations.
8. Visualizations of market potential and growth opportunities.
