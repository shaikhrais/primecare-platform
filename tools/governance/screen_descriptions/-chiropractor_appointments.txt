Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
5. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Stay updated with the latest chiropractic research and techniques.
8. Ensure compliance with health regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High cancellation or no-show rates for appointments.
3. Frequent errors in patient documentation.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Inconsistent treatment outcomes or patient progress.
6. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.
7. Unusual spikes in operational costs or resource usage.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Overview of active appointments and patient status.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as patient satisfaction scores.
4. Operational audit logs for tracking issues and resolutions.
5. Real-time telemetry data for monitoring clinic performance.
6. Alerts for any compliance or operational issues.
7. Metrics on treatment effectiveness and patient progress.
8. Tools for scheduling and managing appointments efficiently.
