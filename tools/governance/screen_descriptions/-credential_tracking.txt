User Tasks:
1. Monitor credential statuses.
2. Update credential information as needed.
3. Review alerts or notifications related to credential expirations.
4. Generate reports on credential compliance.
5. Access historical data on credential tracking.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent loading errors indicating backend issues.
2. Delays in data updates or refreshes.
3. Inconsistent data displayed on the dashboard.
4. High frequency of user-reported issues or bugs.
5. Lack of user engagement or feedback on the dashboard features.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of credential statuses (active, expired, pending).
2. Notifications for upcoming expirations or required actions.
3. Quick access to update or edit credential information.
4. Visual representation of compliance metrics (charts or graphs).
5. Historical tracking of credential changes and user actions.
