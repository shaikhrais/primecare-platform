Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Monitor and record clients' health status and report changes to healthcare professionals.
5. Administer medications as prescribed and ensure compliance.
6. Prepare meals and assist with feeding if necessary.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Assist with household tasks such as laundry and light housekeeping.
9. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare teams.
10. Participate in training and professional development activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or families about care quality.
2. High turnover rates among PSWs.
3. Inconsistent documentation of client care.
4. Missed medication administration or errors in medication management.
5. Lack of communication between PSWs and healthcare professionals.
6. Unresolved incidents or accidents involving clients.
7. Low morale or dissatisfaction among PSWs.
8. Inadequate training or support for PSWs.
9. Failure to follow care plans or protocols.
10. Delays in responding to client needs or emergencies.

Dashboard Requirements for PSW:
1. Overview of active clients and their care plans.
2. Real-time updates on client health status and alerts for any changes.
3. Log of daily activities and care provided by PSWs.
4. Metrics on medication administration compliance.
5. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
6. Communication logs between PSWs and healthcare teams.
7. Training and certification status of PSWs.
8. Client satisfaction ratings and feedback.
9. Performance metrics for individual PSWs.
10. Alerts for upcoming training sessions or compliance audits.
