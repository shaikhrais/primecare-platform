List of work for the User:
1. Monitor and manage local marketing leads.
2. Analyze lead performance and conversion rates.
3. Update lead information and status.
4. Collaborate with the marketing team for campaign strategies.
5. Generate reports on lead metrics and effectiveness.
6. Identify trends and opportunities in local marketing.

Red flags of operation:
1. High lead drop-off rates indicating potential issues in the marketing funnel.
2. Low conversion rates suggesting ineffective lead engagement strategies.
3. Delays in updating lead statuses which can lead to outdated information.
4. Frequent errors or crashes when loading data, indicating potential bugs.
5. Lack of engagement from leads, suggesting a need for improved marketing tactics.

Dashboard needs for the User:
1. Overview of total leads and their current statuses.
2. Conversion rate metrics and trends over time.
3. Performance indicators for different marketing campaigns.
4. Alerts for leads that require immediate attention.
5. Visual representation of lead engagement and drop-off points.
6. Access to detailed reports for in-depth analysis of lead performance.
