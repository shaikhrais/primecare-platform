List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor operational issues reported by team members.
2. Analyze the data to identify trends and recurring problems.
3. Communicate with relevant departments to resolve issues.
4. Track the resolution status of reported issues.
5. Generate reports on operational performance and issues.

Red flags of operation:
1. High frequency of recurring issues indicating systemic problems.
2. Delays in issue resolution beyond acceptable timeframes.
3. Lack of communication or updates from team members on issue status.
4. Significant discrepancies in reported issues versus resolved issues.
5. Negative feedback from team members regarding issue handling.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current operational issues with status indicators.
2. Metrics on issue resolution times and trends.
3. Alerts for high-priority or overdue issues.
4. A summary of communication logs related to issue resolution.
5. Visual representations (charts/graphs) of operational performance over time.
