List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor daily operations and performance metrics.
2. Review and address any operational issues or bottlenecks.
3. Ensure team members are meeting their daily targets.
4. Communicate with other departments to align on operational goals.
5. Analyze data to identify trends and areas for improvement.
6. Prepare reports for upper management on operational performance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Consistent delays in project timelines.
2. High error rates in deliverables.
3. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
4. Frequent customer complaints or feedback indicating dissatisfaction.
5. Inability to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).
6. Lack of communication or collaboration among team members.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Alerts for any operational issues or red flags.
3. A summary of team member performance and productivity.
4. Visualizations of trends in operational data.
5. Quick access to reports and analytics.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
