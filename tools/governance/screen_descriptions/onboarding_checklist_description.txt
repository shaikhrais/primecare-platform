Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand job requirements.
3. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
4. Screen resumes and applications to shortlist candidates.
5. Conduct interviews and assessments.
6. Coordinate and schedule interviews with hiring teams.
7. Maintain candidate databases and track recruitment metrics.
8. Ensure a positive candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
9. Manage job postings and advertisements.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in talent acquisition.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill metrics suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative feedback from candidates about the hiring process.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates and hiring managers.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or targets.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as time-to-fill, quality of hire, and candidate satisfaction scores.
2. Visual representation of candidate pipeline stages.
3. Metrics on diversity and inclusion in hiring.
4. Source of hire analysis to identify the most effective recruitment channels.
5. Real-time updates on open positions and their status.
6. Logs of recruitment activities and candidate interactions.
7. Alerts for positions that are taking longer than average to fill.
8. Feedback mechanisms for candidates and hiring managers to assess the recruitment process.
