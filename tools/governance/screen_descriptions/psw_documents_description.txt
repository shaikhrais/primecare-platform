Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Administer medications as prescribed and monitor health conditions.
5. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
6. Document and report any changes in clients' health or behavior.
7. Communicate effectively with healthcare professionals and family members.
8. Assist with meal preparation and feeding if necessary.
9. Ensure clients' privacy and dignity are respected at all times.
10. Participate in training and professional development activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or family members about care quality.
2. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
3. High turnover rates among PSWs.
4. Missed appointments or late arrivals to client homes.
5. Lack of communication between PSWs and healthcare teams.
6. Signs of burnout or stress among PSWs.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols or regulations.
8. Inadequate training or support for PSWs.
9. Unresolved conflicts within the care team.
10. Failure to follow up on client health changes.

Dashboard Needs for Personal Support Worker (PSW):
1. Overview of active clients and their care plans.
2. Real-time updates on client health status and alerts for any changes.
3. Logs of daily activities and care provided for each client.
4. Metrics on PSW performance and client satisfaction.
5. Compliance status with health regulations and safety protocols.
6. Training and development resources for PSWs.
7. Communication tools for coordination with healthcare teams.
8. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
9. Visualizations of workload and scheduling for PSWs.
10. Access to client feedback and improvement suggestions.
