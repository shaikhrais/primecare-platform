Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess and diagnose patients' physical conditions.
2. Develop individualized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy techniques and research.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Unexplained patient deterioration.
2. Non-compliance with treatment protocols.
3. Significant changes in patient symptoms.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. High rates of patient dropouts or cancellations.
6. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.
7. Failure to adhere to safety and hygiene protocols.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Overview of active appointments and patient statuses.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
4. Real-time telemetry data on patient progress.
5. Alerts for any red flags or operational issues.
6. Access to patient history and treatment plans.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare professionals.
8. Metrics on patient satisfaction and feedback.
