User Tasks:
1. Monitor the sales pipeline for franchise opportunities.
2. Analyze sales data and trends.
3. Communicate with potential franchisees.
4. Update the status of leads in the pipeline.
5. Generate reports on sales performance.
6. Collaborate with marketing for lead generation strategies.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in updating lead statuses.
2. High number of leads with no follow-up.
3. Low conversion rates from leads to franchise agreements.
4. Inconsistent data entry or reporting.
5. Lack of communication with potential franchisees.
6. Overdue tasks or deadlines in the sales process.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Visual representation of the sales pipeline stages.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for lead conversion rates.
3. Alerts for overdue tasks or follow-ups.
4. Summary of active leads and their statuses.
5. Historical data trends for sales performance.
6. Integration with communication tools for tracking interactions with leads.
