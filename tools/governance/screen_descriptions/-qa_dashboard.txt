List of tasks for a System Verification Officer:

1. Conduct compliance scans to ensure adherence to regulations and standards.
2. Sync security posture to maintain up-to-date security measures.
3. Update security policies based on the latest compliance requirements.
4. Export audit logs for record-keeping and analysis.
5. Monitor operational metrics and performance indicators.
6. Review and analyze telemetry data for anomalies.
7. Document and report any discrepancies or issues found during audits.
8. Collaborate with other teams to address security vulnerabilities.
9. Maintain an up-to-date knowledge of industry standards and best practices.
10. Provide training and support to staff regarding compliance and security protocols.

Red flags of operation:

1. Increased number of compliance violations or exceptions.
2. Delays in executing compliance scans or audits.
3. Inconsistent or missing logs in the audit trail.
4. Anomalies in telemetry data indicating potential security breaches.
5. Lack of updates to security policies in response to new threats.
6. High number of manual interventions required for security posture updates.
7. Unresolved issues from previous audits or scans.
8. Negative trends in operational metrics or performance indicators.

Dashboard needs for a System Verification Officer:

1. Real-time status of compliance scans and their outcomes.
2. Metrics on active operations and their performance.
3. Alerts for any compliance violations or security issues.
4. Historical data on audit logs for trend analysis.
5. Visual representation of telemetry data for quick insights.
6. Quick access buttons for executing compliance scans and syncing posture.
7. Summary of recent logs and actions taken.
8. Notifications for policy updates and required actions.
9. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and data access.
10. Integration with other systems for comprehensive oversight.
