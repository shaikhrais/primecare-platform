User Tasks:
1. Review available grant funding options.
2. Allocate funding to specific projects or departments.
3. Monitor the status of allocated funds.
4. Generate reports on funding allocation and usage.
5. Communicate with stakeholders regarding funding decisions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in fund allocation processes.
2. Lack of transparency in funding distribution.
3. Inconsistent reporting on fund usage.
4. Insufficient communication with stakeholders.
5. Difficulty in tracking funding status.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of total available funding.
2. Breakdown of allocated vs. unallocated funds.
3. Status updates on each funding allocation.
4. Reporting tools for analyzing funding usage.
5. Alerts for any discrepancies or delays in funding processes.
