Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Responsibilities:
1. Set and communicate the organization's vision and mission.
2. Develop and implement strategic plans to achieve business goals.
3. Oversee the company's operations and ensure efficiency.
4. Manage the organization's resources, including finances and personnel.
5. Build and maintain relationships with stakeholders, including investors, customers, and employees.
6. Monitor industry trends and adapt strategies accordingly.
7. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations.
8. Foster a positive corporate culture and promote employee engagement.
9. Evaluate the performance of senior management and provide guidance.
10. Represent the organization in public and at industry events.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining revenue or profit margins.
2. High employee turnover or low morale.
3. Frequent operational disruptions or inefficiencies.
4. Negative customer feedback or declining satisfaction scores.
5. Inconsistent compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Lack of clear communication within the organization.
7. Poor financial management or cash flow issues.
8. Inability to adapt to market changes or competition.
9. Insufficient data or metrics to make informed decisions.
10. Lack of innovation or stagnation in product/service offerings.

Dashboard Needs for CEO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial performance (revenue, profit margins, expenses).
2. Employee engagement metrics (turnover rates, satisfaction scores).
3. Customer satisfaction and feedback scores.
4. Operational efficiency metrics (production rates, downtime).
5. Compliance status and audit results.
6. Market trends and competitive analysis.
7. Real-time data visualizations for decision-making.
8. Alerts for any red flags or issues that require immediate attention.
9. Updates on strategic initiatives and progress towards goals.
10. Risk management insights and mitigation strategies.
