User Work List:
1. Submit procurement requests including vendor name, service type, cost, and target hub.
2. Review and approve pending procurement orders.
3. Monitor total approved spend and pending approvals.
4. Update the status of procurement orders.
5. Access and analyze procurement data and trends.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of pending approvals indicating bottlenecks in the approval process.
2. Significant discrepancies in procurement costs compared to budget expectations.
3. Frequent errors in vendor information or service types leading to delays.
4. Low on-time delivery rates for procured supplies.
5. Lack of compliance with regional spending limits.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Total approved spend with a trend indicator.
2. Count of pending procurement requests awaiting approval.
3. Count of approved procurement orders.
4. Visualization of spending trends over time.
5. Alerts for high-value procurement requests or compliance issues.
