List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor quality metrics regularly.
2. Analyze data trends for quality improvements.
3. Identify areas needing attention based on metrics.
4. Collaborate with team members to address quality issues.
5. Report findings and suggest improvements.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant deviations from established quality benchmarks.
2. Frequent errors or defects reported in products/services.
3. Delays in addressing quality issues.
4. Lack of communication among team members regarding quality concerns.
5. Inconsistent data reporting or metrics tracking.

What we need on the dashboard of the user:
1. Real-time quality metrics display.
2. Alerts for any red flags or deviations.
3. Historical data trends for analysis.
4. Collaboration tools for team communication.
5. Reporting features for documenting findings and suggestions.
