List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor compliance policies.
2. Review and update existing policies.
3. Ensure policies are communicated to relevant stakeholders.
4. Track compliance with policies.
5. Generate reports on compliance status.
6. Identify areas for improvement in compliance processes.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in policy updates or reviews.
2. Lack of communication regarding policy changes.
3. High number of compliance violations.
4. Incomplete or outdated policy documentation.
5. Insufficient training for staff on compliance matters.
6. Low engagement from stakeholders in compliance discussions.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current compliance status.
2. Alerts for overdue policy reviews or updates.
3. Metrics on compliance violations and trends.
4. Quick access to policy documents.
5. Notifications for upcoming training sessions.
6. Feedback mechanism for stakeholders on compliance issues.
