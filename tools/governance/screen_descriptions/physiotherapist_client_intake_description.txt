Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct initial assessments of patients' physical conditions.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on assessments.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and techniques.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red Flags Indicating Need for Immediate Attention:
1. Sudden worsening of symptoms or pain.
2. Signs of infection (fever, swelling, redness).
3. Unexplained weight loss or fatigue.
4. Neurological symptoms (numbness, weakness, loss of coordination).
5. Changes in bowel or bladder function.
6. History of cancer with new symptoms.
7. Severe or persistent headaches.

Effective Work Strategies:
1. Utilize evidence-based practices in treatment.
2. Regularly communicate with patients about their progress.
3. Set clear, achievable goals for rehabilitation.
4. Use technology for tracking patient progress and outcomes.
5. Engage in continuous professional development.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapists:
1. Overview of patient caseload and status.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
3. Compliance audit results and logs.
4. Real-time telemetry data on patient progress.
5. Alerts for red flags or urgent patient needs.
6. Access to educational resources and latest research.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
