Nurse Practitioner (NP) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Diagnose and treat acute and chronic illnesses.
3. Order and interpret diagnostic tests.
4. Develop and manage treatment plans.
5. Provide patient education and counseling.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records.
8. Stay updated with medical guidelines and protocols.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.
10. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. High patient wait times.
2. Increased number of patient complaints.
3. Frequent medication errors.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Low patient satisfaction scores.
6. High staff turnover rates.
7. Non-compliance with treatment protocols.
8. Delays in test results or follow-ups.
9. Inadequate staffing levels.
10. Poor communication among team members.

Dashboard Needs for Nurse Practitioner (NP):
1. Patient load and demographics.
2. Compliance metrics and alerts.
3. Quality of care indicators.
4. Patient satisfaction scores.
5. Operational logs and incident reports.
6. Performance metrics (e.g., wait times, treatment outcomes).
7. Real-time access to patient records.
8. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or tests.
9. Resource allocation and staffing levels.
10. Integration with electronic health records (EHR) systems.
