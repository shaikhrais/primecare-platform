List of work for the User:
1. Monitor attendance of operations managers.
2. Review attendance data for accuracy.
3. Identify patterns or trends in attendance.
4. Address any discrepancies or issues with attendance records.
5. Generate reports on attendance for management review.
6. Ensure compliance with attendance policies.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent absenteeism or tardiness among operations managers.
2. Inconsistent attendance records or data discrepancies.
3. Lack of communication regarding attendance issues.
4. Patterns of attendance that may indicate underlying problems (e.g., burnout, dissatisfaction).
5. Delays in reporting or addressing attendance issues.

What is needed on the User dashboard:
1. Real-time attendance data overview.
2. Alerts for any attendance anomalies or red flags.
3. Visualizations of attendance trends over time.
4. Quick access to attendance reports.
5. Communication tools for addressing attendance issues.
6. Compliance tracking for attendance policies.
