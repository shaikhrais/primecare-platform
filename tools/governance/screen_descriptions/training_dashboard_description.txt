Training Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Coordinate training schedules and logistics.
3. Monitor and evaluate training effectiveness.
4. Maintain training records and documentation.
5. Collaborate with subject matter experts to create training content.
6. Provide support and guidance to trainees.
7. Ensure compliance with regulatory training requirements.
8. Conduct needs assessments to identify training gaps.
9. Facilitate training sessions and workshops.
10. Gather feedback from participants to improve training programs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low trainee engagement or participation rates.
2. High dropout rates from training programs.
3. Negative feedback from trainees regarding training quality.
4. Inconsistent training records or documentation.
5. Failure to meet compliance training deadlines.
6. Lack of collaboration with subject matter experts.
7. Inability to adapt training programs based on feedback.
8. Poor tracking of training effectiveness and outcomes.
9. Insufficient resources or budget for training initiatives.
10. High turnover rates among training staff.

Dashboard Requirements for Training Coordinator:
1. Overview of upcoming training sessions and schedules.
2. Metrics on trainee engagement and participation.
3. Compliance status for mandatory training requirements.
4. Feedback summary from trainees on training effectiveness.
5. Logs of training sessions conducted and attendance records.
6. Visual representation of training effectiveness metrics.
7. Alerts for upcoming compliance deadlines.
8. Resource allocation for training programs.
9. Collaboration tools for working with subject matter experts.
10. Historical data on training outcomes and improvements.
