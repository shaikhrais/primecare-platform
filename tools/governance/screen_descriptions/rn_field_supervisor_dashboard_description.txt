Registered Nurse (RN) Field Supervisor responsibilities:
1. Conduct field audits and assessments.
2. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and policies.
3. Monitor and evaluate nursing staff performance.
4. Provide training and support to nursing staff.
5. Document and report operational activities and incidents.
6. Coordinate with healthcare teams for patient care.
7. Manage and resolve issues related to nursing operations.
8. Maintain accurate records of nursing assessments and audits.
9. Communicate effectively with staff and management.
10. Implement quality improvement initiatives.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of compliance violations.
2. Increased incidents of patient complaints.
3. Low staff morale or high turnover rates.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Delays in reporting or addressing issues.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Failure to meet operational benchmarks.
8. Poor performance in audits or assessments.
9. Unresolved conflicts within the nursing team.
10. Inadequate training or support for staff.

Dashboard needs for Registered Nurse (RN) Field Supervisor:
1. Overview of active operations and compliance status.
2. Real-time logs of operational activities.
3. Metrics on staff performance and patient outcomes.
4. Alerts for compliance issues or operational red flags.
5. Access to audit logs and historical data.
6. Tools for submitting and tracking field audits.
7. Options for updating policies and procedures.
8. Visual representation of key performance indicators.
9. Communication tools for team collaboration.
10. Functionality for exporting logs and reports.
