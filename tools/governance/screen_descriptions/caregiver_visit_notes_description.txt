List of caregiver responsibilities:
1. Document visit notes accurately.
2. Conduct compliance scans as needed.
3. Maintain logs of operational activities.
4. Ensure security protocols are followed.
5. Report any discrepancies or issues encountered during visits.
6. Communicate effectively with team members and supervisors.
7. Participate in training and development sessions.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors in documentation.
2. Delays in compliance scan execution.
3. Inconsistent logging of activities.
4. Security breaches or lapses.
5. Lack of communication or collaboration with team members.
6. High turnover of caregivers or dissatisfaction reported.
7. Negative feedback from patients or families.

Dashboard needs for caregivers:
1. Real-time updates on compliance status.
2. Access to logs and historical data for reference.
3. Metrics on active operations and productivity levels.
4. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
5. A summary of security clearance levels.
6. Tools for easy documentation of visit notes.
7. A section for feedback and communication with supervisors.
