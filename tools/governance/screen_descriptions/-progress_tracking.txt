List of tasks a physiotherapist must perform:
1. Assess patient conditions and needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and techniques.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red flags for operations:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition.
2. Lack of progress in treatment.
3. Patient complaints of increased pain or discomfort.
4. Non-compliance with treatment plans.
5. Unusual side effects from treatments.
6. Incomplete or inaccurate patient records.
7. Failure to communicate effectively with the healthcare team.

Dashboard requirements for physiotherapists:
1. Patient progress tracking metrics.
2. Compliance audit logs.
3. Alerts for red flags in patient conditions.
4. Performance indicators for treatment effectiveness.
5. Access to patient history and treatment plans.
6. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
7. Educational resources for ongoing professional development.
