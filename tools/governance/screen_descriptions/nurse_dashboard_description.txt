List of work for the User:
1. Monitor patient status and updates.
2. Access patient records and history.
3. Manage medication schedules and administration.
4. Communicate with other healthcare professionals.
5. Track and report vital signs and other health metrics.
6. Update patient information as needed.
7. Review and respond to alerts or notifications.
8. Document care provided to patients.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in loading patient data or dashboard features.
2. Frequent errors when accessing patient records.
3. Inconsistent data displayed on the dashboard.
4. Lack of real-time updates on patient status.
5. Difficulty in navigating the dashboard or accessing features.
6. Unresponsive UI elements or slow performance.
7. Missing notifications or alerts for critical patient updates.

What is needed on the User dashboard:
1. Overview of patient assignments and statuses.
2. Quick access to frequently used features (e.g., medication management).
3. Real-time notifications for patient alerts and updates.
4. Visual indicators for patient health metrics (e.g., color-coded vital signs).
5. Search functionality for patient records.
6. Access to communication tools with other healthcare staff.
7. Summary of tasks or reminders for the day.
8. Performance metrics or reports on nursing activities.
