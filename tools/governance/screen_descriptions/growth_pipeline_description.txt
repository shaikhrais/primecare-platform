List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the growth pipeline metrics.
2. Analyze data trends and patterns.
3. Identify potential areas for improvement.
4. Collaborate with team members on growth strategies.
5. Report on progress and outcomes.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant delays in data loading or processing.
2. Frequent errors or crashes when accessing features.
3. Inconsistent or missing data in reports.
4. Lack of user engagement with the growth pipeline tools.
5. Negative feedback from team members regarding usability.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time metrics and KPIs related to growth.
2. Visualizations of data trends (charts, graphs).
3. Alerts for any anomalies or red flags.
4. Quick access to collaboration tools and resources.
5. Summary of recent activities and outcomes.
