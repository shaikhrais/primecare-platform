Training Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Coordinate training schedules and logistics.
3. Monitor and evaluate training effectiveness.
4. Maintain training records and documentation.
5. Collaborate with subject matter experts to create training content.
6. Provide support and resources for trainers and trainees.
7. Ensure compliance with training regulations and standards.
8. Communicate training opportunities to staff.
9. Gather feedback from participants for continuous improvement.
10. Report on training outcomes and metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High dropout rates in training programs.
2. Low participant engagement or feedback scores.
3. Inconsistent training records or documentation.
4. Frequent complaints about training quality or relevance.
5. Delays in training schedule or logistics.
6. Lack of communication regarding training updates.
7. Non-compliance with regulatory training requirements.
8. Insufficient resources or support for trainers.
9. Failure to meet training objectives or goals.
10. Negative trends in training effectiveness metrics.

Dashboard Needs for Training Coordinator:
1. Training program participation metrics.
2. Feedback and satisfaction scores from training participants.
3. Compliance status with training regulations.
4. Training completion rates and dropout statistics.
5. Upcoming training schedules and logistics.
6. Resource allocation for training sessions.
7. Performance metrics of trainers and training content.
8. Historical data on training effectiveness and improvements.
9. Alerts for any compliance issues or training gaps.
10. Visual representation of training trends over time.
