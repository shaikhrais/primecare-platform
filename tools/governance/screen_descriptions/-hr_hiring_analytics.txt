Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the full recruitment cycle from job posting to onboarding.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
6. Maintain candidate databases and track recruitment metrics.
7. Ensure a positive candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
8. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in talent acquisition.
9. Conduct market research to benchmark salaries and benefits.
10. Manage employer branding initiatives to attract top talent.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill metrics suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate engagement or feedback scores.
4. Inconsistent communication with candidates leading to a negative experience.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Over-reliance on a single sourcing channel.
7. Frequent changes in hiring requirements from managers.
8. Poor collaboration between HR and hiring departments.
9. Inadequate tracking of recruitment metrics.
10. Resistance to adopting new recruitment technologies or tools.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Overview of open positions and their status.
2. Time-to-fill metrics for each role.
3. Source of hire statistics to evaluate recruitment channels.
4. Candidate pipeline visualization.
5. Diversity metrics of applicants and hires.
6. Candidate feedback scores and experience ratings.
7. Recruitment budget tracking and spending analysis.
8. Performance metrics of recruitment team members.
9. Market salary benchmarks for various roles.
10. Upcoming recruitment events and deadlines.
