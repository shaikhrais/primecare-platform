Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor revenue performance and trends.
2. Ensure compliance with operational standards.
3. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
4. Manage and respond to operational logs.
5. Oversee security clearance levels.
6. Facilitate communication between staff and management.
7. Implement corrective actions based on audit findings.
8. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of industry regulations.
9. Provide training and support to staff as needed.
10. Analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) for decision-making.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining revenue trends over consecutive periods.
2. Increased frequency of compliance issues or audit failures.
3. Significant discrepancies in operational logs.
4. Low security clearance levels or exceptions logged.
5. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
6. Delays in responding to operational issues.
7. Lack of transparency in financial reporting.
8. Inconsistent communication from management.
9. Failure to implement corrective actions in a timely manner.
10. Negative customer feedback or reviews.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time revenue metrics and trends.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Operational logs with timestamps and actions taken.
4. Security clearance levels and any exceptions.
5. Key performance indicators (KPIs) visualizations.
6. Alerts for any red flags or issues needing attention.
7. Historical data for trend analysis.
8. User-friendly interface for quick navigation.
9. Access to training resources and support materials.
10. Communication tools for staff engagement and feedback.
