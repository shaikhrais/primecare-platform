User Work List:
1. Review and understand the purpose of the Crisis Protocol Trigger Screen.
2. Activate specific crisis workflows by selecting the appropriate protocol card.
3. Monitor the outcomes of activated protocols, including staff check-ins and message broadcasts.
4. Ensure that all necessary staff are informed and equipped to handle the crisis situation.
5. Provide feedback on the effectiveness of the protocols post-activation.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in activating protocols could lead to worsening crisis situations.
2. Lack of staff check-ins may indicate communication breakdowns.
3. Incomplete or unclear protocol descriptions could lead to improper execution.
4. Failure to reroute field staff or send alerts may compromise patient safety.
5. Inadequate response to system security breaches could lead to data vulnerabilities.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time status of activated protocols and their outcomes.
2. Alerts for any delays or issues in protocol activation.
3. Summary of staff check-ins and their locations.
4. Metrics on the effectiveness of each protocol based on past activations.
5. Visual indicators for ongoing crises and response times.
