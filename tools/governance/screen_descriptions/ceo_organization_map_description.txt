List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the organizational structure of the CEO's team.
2. Analyze the effectiveness of team members and their roles.
3. Identify any gaps or overlaps in responsibilities.
4. Review performance metrics associated with the organization.
5. Ensure that the data displayed is up-to-date and accurate.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent or outdated data displayed on the dashboard.
2. Slow loading times or failure to load the organizational map.
3. Errors in data retrieval that prevent users from accessing necessary information.
4. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities leading to confusion.
5. High turnover rates or dissatisfaction among team members indicated by feedback.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time updates on organizational changes.
2. Performance metrics for each team member.
3. Visual representation of the organizational structure (e.g., charts or graphs).
4. Alerts for any discrepancies or issues in the data.
5. User feedback section to gather insights on the effectiveness of the organization.
