Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Monitor and report changes in clients' health conditions.
5. Administer medications as prescribed.
6. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
7. Document care provided and any changes in clients' conditions.
8. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
9. Follow care plans and protocols established by healthcare providers.
10. Attend training and continuing education sessions as required.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent changes in clients' health conditions without proper documentation.
2. Lack of communication among team members regarding client care.
3. Inconsistent adherence to care plans.
4. High turnover rates among PSWs.
5. Complaints from clients or families about care quality.
6. Missed appointments or scheduled visits.
7. Inadequate training or support for PSWs.
8. Failure to report incidents or accidents involving clients.

Dashboard Requirements for PSWs:
1. Overview of active clients and their care plans.
2. Alerts for upcoming appointments and medication schedules.
3. Logs of daily activities and any incidents reported.
4. Compliance status and results from operational audits.
5. Metrics on client satisfaction and feedback.
6. Performance indicators for individual PSWs.
7. Access to training resources and updates on best practices.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with healthcare teams.
