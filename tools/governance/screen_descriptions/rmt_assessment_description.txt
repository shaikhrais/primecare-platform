List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) must perform:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans based on assessments.
3. Perform massage techniques to alleviate pain and improve mobility.
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Educate clients on self-care and wellness practices.
6. Stay updated on industry standards and continuing education.
7. Ensure a safe and hygienic treatment environment.
8. Communicate effectively with clients and other healthcare professionals.
9. Manage scheduling and appointment bookings.
10. Handle billing and insurance claims as necessary.

Red flags in operations for RMTs:
1. Inconsistent client records or documentation.
2. High cancellation or no-show rates.
3. Client complaints regarding treatment effectiveness or safety.
4. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
5. Inadequate communication with clients or healthcare team.
6. Failure to stay updated with continuing education requirements.
7. Signs of burnout or stress among staff.
8. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.

Dashboard needs for Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs):
1. Overview of active clients and appointments.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., treatment outcomes, client satisfaction).
4. Alerts for upcoming certifications or training requirements.
5. Client feedback and testimonials.
6. Financial overview (e.g., revenue, outstanding payments).
7. Access to educational resources and updates.
8. Quick links to essential tools (e.g., scheduling, documentation).
9. Visual representation of treatment effectiveness (e.g., charts or graphs).
10. Notifications for important operational updates or changes.
