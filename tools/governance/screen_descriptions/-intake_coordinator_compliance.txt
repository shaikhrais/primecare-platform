Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Conduct compliance audits.
2. Monitor operational logs for anomalies.
3. Ensure adherence to governance policies.
4. Manage secure event reporting.
5. Update and enforce security policies.
6. Facilitate communication between departments.
7. Provide training on compliance procedures.
8. Maintain documentation of compliance activities.
9. Respond to compliance-related inquiries.
10. Collaborate with IT for system security.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit completion.
3. Inconsistent logging of operational activities.
4. Lack of communication regarding policy changes.
5. High turnover in compliance staff.
6. Frequent system security breaches.
7. Low engagement in training sessions.
8. Unresolved compliance inquiries.
9. Missing documentation for audits.
10. Negative feedback from other departments.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Compliance audit status overview.
2. Real-time operational log updates.
3. Alerts for compliance violations.
4. Summary of recent policy changes.
5. Training session attendance metrics.
6. Secure event reporting submissions.
7. Historical compliance audit results.
8. User feedback on compliance processes.
9. System security incident reports.
10. Action items from governance meetings.
