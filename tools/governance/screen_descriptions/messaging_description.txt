List of caregiver responsibilities:
1. Provide personal care to clients, including bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Assist with mobility and transfers.
3. Administer medications as prescribed.
4. Monitor and record vital signs.
5. Prepare meals and assist with feeding.
6. Provide companionship and emotional support.
7. Assist with light housekeeping and laundry.
8. Help clients with daily living activities.
9. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
10. Maintain client confidentiality and dignity.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or families.
2. High turnover rate of caregivers.
3. Inconsistent care or missed appointments.
4. Lack of communication between caregivers and management.
5. Unresolved incidents or accidents.
6. Non-compliance with care plans or protocols.
7. Poor documentation of care provided.
8. Signs of caregiver burnout or stress.
9. Inadequate training or support for caregivers.
10. Failure to address client needs or preferences.

Dashboard needs for caregivers:
1. Overview of daily tasks and responsibilities.
2. Client profiles with care plans and preferences.
3. Communication logs with clients and families.
4. Alerts for medication schedules and appointments.
5. Performance metrics and feedback from clients.
6. Access to training resources and support.
7. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
8. Compliance status and audit logs.
9. Tools for scheduling and time management.
10. Resources for self-care and stress management.
