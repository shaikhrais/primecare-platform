Compliance Manager Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits.
2. Monitor compliance with regulations and internal policies.
3. Maintain documentation of compliance activities.
4. Train staff on compliance requirements and procedures.
5. Report compliance status to management.
6. Address compliance violations and implement corrective actions.
7. Stay updated on changes in laws and regulations.
8. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance.
9. Manage compliance-related risks.
10. Develop and implement compliance programs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delayed reporting of compliance issues.
3. Lack of staff training on compliance matters.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. High turnover in compliance staff.
6. Failure to address previous compliance findings.
7. Poor communication between departments regarding compliance.
8. Lack of updates on regulatory changes.
9. Insufficient resources allocated to compliance activities.
10. Negative feedback from audits or inspections.

Dashboard Requirements for Compliance Manager:
1. Overview of compliance status (green/yellow/red indicators).
2. List of recent compliance audits and their outcomes.
3. Log of compliance-related activities and incidents.
4. Metrics on staff training completion rates.
5. Alerts for upcoming regulatory deadlines.
6. Visual representation of compliance trends over time.
7. Summary of compliance violations and corrective actions taken.
8. Access to relevant documentation and policies.
9. Integration with incident reporting systems.
10. User-friendly interface for quick navigation and updates.
