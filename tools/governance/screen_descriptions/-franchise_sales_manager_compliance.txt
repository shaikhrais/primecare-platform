Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Ensure compliance with operational standards and policies.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments of franchise operations.
3. Maintain accurate records of compliance activities and logs.
4. Implement security measures, including multi-factor authentication.
5. Monitor and report on operational performance metrics.
6. Engage in continuous training and development for staff.
7. Address any compliance issues or breaches promptly.
8. Facilitate communication between franchisees and corporate management.
9. Participate in franchise meetings and governance discussions.
10. Utilize the compliance dashboard for real-time insights and reporting.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased frequency of compliance breaches or issues.
2. Delays in audit completion or reporting.
3. Lack of engagement or communication from franchisees.
4. High turnover rates among staff or franchisees.
5. Inconsistent application of policies across locations.
6. Negative feedback from customers regarding service or compliance.
7. Failure to implement recommended security measures.
8. Unresolved compliance logs or issues over extended periods.
9. Discrepancies in operational metrics or performance reports.
10. Resistance to change or improvement initiatives.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Audit logs and history of compliance checks.
3. Performance metrics and KPIs for franchise operations.
4. Alerts for any compliance breaches or issues.
5. Security policy compliance status (e.g., MFA enforcement).
6. Summary of recent audits and their outcomes.
7. Communication tools for engaging with franchisees.
8. Training and development resources for staff.
9. Reporting tools for operational performance.
10. Access to governance directives and updates.
