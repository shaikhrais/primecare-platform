Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the full recruitment cycle from job posting to onboarding.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Conduct interviews and assess candidates' qualifications.
6. Maintain relationships with candidates throughout the hiring process.
7. Track and analyze recruitment metrics to improve processes.
8. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
9. Develop employer branding initiatives to attract talent.
10. Provide training and support to hiring managers on best practices.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative candidate experience reflected in feedback.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or deadlines.
8. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
9. Poor collaboration with hiring managers.
10. Inadequate tracking of recruitment metrics.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, and candidate quality.
2. Real-time analytics on recruitment funnel stages.
3. Diversity metrics to track candidate demographics.
4. Source effectiveness to evaluate recruitment channels.
5. Candidate experience ratings and feedback.
6. Compliance status and audit logs.
7. Active job postings and their statuses.
8. Upcoming interviews and scheduled follow-ups.
9. Historical data trends for recruitment metrics.
10. Alerts for any operational issues or red flags.
