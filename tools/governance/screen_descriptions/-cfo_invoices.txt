Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Consistent budget overruns
2. Declining cash flow
3. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue
4. Delays in financial reporting
5. High employee turnover in finance department
6. Non-compliance with regulations
7. Unexplained discrepancies in financial statements
8. Lack of transparency in financial processes
9. Poor performance metrics
10. Inadequate risk management practices

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health
2. Real-time cash flow analysis
3. Budget vs. Actual performance tracking
4. Compliance status indicators
5. Audit logs and operational metrics
6. Risk assessment dashboard
7. Investment performance metrics
8. Revenue and expense trends
9. Employee performance metrics in finance
10. Alerts for financial anomalies or red flags
