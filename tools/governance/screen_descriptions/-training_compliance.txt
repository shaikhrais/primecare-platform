List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor training compliance status.
2. Review training completion rates.
3. Identify users who are non-compliant with training requirements.
4. Access training materials and resources.
5. Generate reports on training compliance.

Red flags of operation:
1. High percentage of non-compliance among users.
2. Frequent errors when loading training data.
3. Delays in accessing training materials.
4. Inconsistent reporting of training completion.
5. User feedback indicating confusion or difficulty navigating the training system.

What is needed on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of training compliance status (compliant vs. non-compliant users).
2. Visual representation of training completion rates (charts/graphs).
3. List of users who need to complete training.
4. Quick access to training materials and resources.
5. Option to generate and download compliance reports.
6. Notifications or alerts for upcoming training deadlines.
