List of work training candidates must do:
1. Complete compliance training modules.
2. Participate in hands-on workshops.
3. Attend regular compliance meetings.
4. Review and understand governance policies.
5. Conduct self-assessments on compliance knowledge.
6. Engage in scenario-based training exercises.
7. Provide feedback on training effectiveness.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of compliance audit failures.
2. Frequent updates to governance policies without training.
3. Low participation rates in training sessions.
4. Inconsistent logging of training activities.
5. Lack of follow-up on training feedback.
6. Delays in compliance scan execution.
7. Unresolved issues from previous audits.

Dashboard needs for training candidates:
1. Overview of training completion status.
2. Upcoming training sessions and deadlines.
3. Compliance audit results and trends.
4. Logs of training activities and participant feedback.
5. Notifications for policy updates and required actions.
6. Access to training resources and materials.
7. Metrics on training effectiveness and engagement.
