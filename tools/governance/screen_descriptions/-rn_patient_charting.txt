Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health problems and needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
4. Monitor patient progress and response to treatments.
5. Educate patients and their families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to provide optimal patient care.
7. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Participate in patient safety and quality improvement initiatives.
10. Provide emotional support and counseling to patients and families.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient readmissions.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols or regulations.
7. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
8. Inadequate communication among team members.
9. Lack of adherence to care plans.
10. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the healthcare team.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Patient health status overview (vital signs, lab results).
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Active operations metrics (patient load, staffing levels).
4. Alerts for critical patient conditions or changes.
5. Medication administration records.
6. Patient education resources and materials.
7. Communication tools for team collaboration.
8. Performance indicators (patient satisfaction, readmission rates).
9. Access to training and continuing education resources.
10. Real-time updates on policy changes and compliance requirements.
