List of tasks for HR Director:
1. Oversee recruitment and talent acquisition processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and conflict resolution.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Monitor employee performance and development.
6. Conduct training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red flags of operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Increased number of grievances or complaints.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
5. Poor performance metrics or productivity levels.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Ineffective communication within the organization.
8. Negative feedback from exit interviews.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for recruitment and retention.
2. Employee engagement and satisfaction metrics.
3. Compliance status with labor laws and regulations.
4. Training and development participation rates.
5. Performance metrics and appraisal results.
6. Diversity and inclusion statistics.
7. Real-time logs of HR activities and audits.
8. Alerts for any compliance issues or employee grievances.
