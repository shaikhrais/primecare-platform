List of tasks Nurse Practitioners (NPs) must perform:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Diagnose and treat acute and chronic conditions.
3. Order and interpret diagnostic tests.
4. Develop and implement treatment plans.
5. Prescribe medications and therapies.
6. Provide patient education and counseling.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
8. Maintain accurate patient records.
9. Perform minor procedures as needed.
10. Stay updated with continuing education and training.

Red flags of operation for effective work:
1. High patient wait times.
2. Increased number of patient complaints.
3. Inconsistent documentation practices.
4. Frequent medication errors.
5. Lack of follow-up on patient referrals.
6. Poor communication with other healthcare providers.
7. Inadequate patient education leading to misunderstandings.
8. Non-compliance with clinical guidelines or protocols.
9. High turnover rates among staff.
10. Insufficient resources or support for patient care.

Dashboard needs for Nurse Practitioners (NPs):
1. Real-time patient metrics (e.g., number of active patients, patient wait times).
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Logs of recent activities and actions taken.
4. Performance indicators (e.g., patient satisfaction scores).
5. Access to patient records and diagnostic results.
6. Notifications for follow-up appointments and referrals.
7. Tools for medication management and prescription tracking.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
9. Educational resources and updates on clinical guidelines.
10. Visualizations of operational metrics (e.g., charts, graphs).
