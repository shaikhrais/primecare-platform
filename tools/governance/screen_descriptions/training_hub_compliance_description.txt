List of work training candidates must do:
1. Complete compliance training modules.
2. Participate in security awareness sessions.
3. Submit secure event reporting forms.
4. Engage in regular compliance audits.
5. Review and acknowledge governance directives.
6. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of operational policies.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in compliance audit execution.
2. Incomplete or missing training module completions.
3. Low participation rates in security sessions.
4. Frequent errors in secure event reporting.
5. Non-compliance with governance directives.
6. Lack of timely updates in operational logs.

Dashboard needs for training candidates:
1. Real-time compliance status overview.
2. List of completed and pending training modules.
3. Summary of recent compliance audit results.
4. Notifications for upcoming training sessions.
5. Access to governance directives and updates.
6. Visual representation of operational audit logs.
