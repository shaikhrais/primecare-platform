Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the full recruitment lifecycle from job posting to offer acceptance.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews to assess candidate qualifications.
6. Maintain candidate databases and track recruitment metrics.
7. Ensure compliance with labor laws and company policies.
8. Build and maintain relationships with external recruitment agencies.
9. Conduct onboarding and orientation for new hires.
10. Provide training and support to hiring managers on recruitment best practices.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative feedback from candidates regarding the recruitment experience.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates and hiring managers.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or deadlines.
8. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.

Dashboard Needs for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, and candidate quality metrics.
2. Visual representation of candidate pipelines and stages in the hiring process.
3. Diversity metrics to track representation in candidate pools.
4. Source of hire analysis to evaluate the effectiveness of recruitment channels.
5. Candidate experience feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Compliance tracking for recruitment processes.
7. Real-time updates on open positions and hiring progress.
8. Audit logs of recruitment activities and decisions made.
