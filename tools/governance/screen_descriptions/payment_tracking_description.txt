Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with budgeting and financial tracking.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Maintain office supplies and inventory.
8. Support team members with administrative tasks.
9. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.
10. Manage travel arrangements.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Frequent delays in task completion.
2. Inconsistent communication among team members.
3. High error rates in documentation.
4. Lack of adherence to deadlines.
5. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
6. Insufficient tracking of tasks and responsibilities.
7. Low morale or engagement among staff.
8. Poor organization of files and records.
9. Inability to adapt to changes in procedures.
10. Lack of feedback or follow-up on tasks.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview.
2. Calendar and scheduling integration.
3. Communication logs and correspondence tracking.
4. Document management system.
5. Budget tracking and financial reports.
6. Meeting and event planning tools.
7. Performance metrics and KPIs.
8. Compliance tracking and audit logs.
9. Inventory management for office supplies.
10. Feedback and survey tools for team engagement.
