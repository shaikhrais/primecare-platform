User Work List:
1. View current prescriptions.
2. Dispense medications to patients.
3. Track inventory levels of medications.
4. Manage patient profiles and medication history.
5. Generate reports on dispensing activities.
6. Monitor medication expiration dates.
7. Communicate with healthcare providers regarding prescriptions.
8. Handle medication refills and renewals.
9. Address patient inquiries and concerns.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High rate of medication errors.
2. Frequent stockouts of critical medications.
3. Delays in prescription processing.
4. Increased patient complaints regarding service.
5. Inaccurate inventory records.
6. Low staff engagement or high turnover rates.
7. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
8. Lack of timely communication with healthcare providers.
9. Inconsistent tracking of medication expiration.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time inventory levels and alerts for low stock.
2. Overview of current prescriptions and their statuses.
3. Metrics on dispensing accuracy and error rates.
4. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Reports on medication expiration dates.
6. Alerts for pending refills and renewals.
7. Communication logs with healthcare providers.
8. Staff performance metrics and workload distribution.
9. Compliance checklists and audit trails.
