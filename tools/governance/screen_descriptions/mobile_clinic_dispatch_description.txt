User Work List:
1. Monitor mobile clinic dispatch operations.
2. Schedule and manage clinic routes.
3. Track clinic vehicle locations in real-time.
4. Assign staff to specific clinics or routes.
5. Review patient appointments and manage schedules.
6. Collect and analyze patient feedback.
7. Generate reports on clinic performance and efficiency.
8. Communicate with clinic staff and patients.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in dispatching clinics.
2. High number of missed appointments.
3. Frequent changes in staff assignments.
4. Inaccurate tracking of vehicle locations.
5. Low patient satisfaction scores.
6. Inconsistent reporting metrics.
7. Communication breakdowns between staff and patients.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time vehicle tracking map.
2. Appointment scheduling overview.
3. Staff assignment status.
4. Patient feedback summary.
5. Performance metrics and KPIs.
6. Alerts for missed appointments or delays.
7. Communication tools for staff and patients.
8. Reporting tools for generating performance reports.
