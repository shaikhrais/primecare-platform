Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budget Management
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Unexplained fluctuations in cash flow
2. Inconsistent financial reporting
3. High levels of debt or poor credit ratings
4. Non-compliance with regulations
5. Delays in financial audits
6. Increased operational costs without clear justification
7. Lack of transparency in financial processes
8. High employee turnover in finance department
9. Poor performance in key financial metrics
10. Negative feedback from stakeholders

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health
2. Real-time cash flow analysis
3. Budget vs. Actual performance tracking
4. Compliance status indicators
5. Risk assessment metrics
6. Audit logs and compliance reports
7. Investment performance overview
8. Operational efficiency metrics
9. Stakeholder feedback and satisfaction scores
10. Alerts for financial anomalies or red flags
