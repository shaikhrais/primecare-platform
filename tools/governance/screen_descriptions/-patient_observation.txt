Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, etc.).
4. Document patient care and observations accurately.
5. Communicate effectively with patients and healthcare team.
6. Participate in patient care planning and evaluation.
7. Maintain a safe and clean environment.
8. Educate patients and families about health management.
9. Assist in medical procedures as needed.
10. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Significant changes in patient vital signs.
2. Unexplained patient complaints or behaviors.
3. Medication errors or discrepancies.
4. Incomplete or inaccurate documentation.
5. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
6. High patient fall rates or safety incidents.
7. Delays in patient care or treatment.
8. Poor communication among team members.
9. Lack of patient engagement or understanding.
10. Inconsistent adherence to care plans.

Dashboard Requirements for RPN:
1. Patient vital signs monitoring display.
2. Medication administration tracking.
3. Daily living activities completion status.
4. Documentation compliance indicators.
5. Communication logs with patients and team.
6. Safety incident reporting and trends.
7. Patient education resources and tracking.
8. Compliance audit results and alerts.
9. Performance metrics for patient care.
10. Real-time updates on patient care plans and evaluations.
