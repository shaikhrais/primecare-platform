Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
3. Provide patient education and support.
4. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
5. Document patient care and progress accurately.
6. Assist with diagnostic tests and procedures.
7. Maintain a safe and clean environment.
8. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
9. Respond to patient emergencies and provide first aid.
10. Advocate for patient needs and preferences.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of medication errors.
3. Frequent staff turnover or absenteeism.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. Inconsistent documentation practices.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
8. Failure to follow safety protocols.
9. Inadequate staffing levels during peak times.
10. Poor compliance with regulatory standards.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Real-time patient monitoring metrics.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care.
4. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
5. Staff performance and workload statistics.
6. Medication administration records.
7. Patient satisfaction scores.
8. Incident reports and safety alerts.
9. Training and certification status of staff.
10. Access to clinical guidelines and protocols.
