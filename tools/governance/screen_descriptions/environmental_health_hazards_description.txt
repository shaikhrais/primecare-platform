User Tasks:
1. Monitor environmental health hazards.
2. Report any identified hazards.
3. Access data on previous reports and trends.
4. Collaborate with other users on hazard management.
5. Update hazard status and mitigation efforts.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in reporting hazards.
2. Lack of collaboration among users.
3. Incomplete or inaccurate data entries.
4. Low engagement with the dashboard features.
5. High number of unresolved hazards.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time updates on reported hazards.
2. Visual representation of hazard trends over time.
3. Alerts for new hazards reported.
4. Collaboration tools for user communication.
5. Summary of unresolved hazards and their statuses.
