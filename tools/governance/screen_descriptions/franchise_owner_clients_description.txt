Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor compliance with operational standards.
2. Conduct regular audits and scans for compliance.
3. Review key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics.
4. Manage client relationships and ensure satisfaction.
5. Address any operational issues or red flags promptly.
6. Maintain accurate logs of operational activities.
7. Ensure security clearance and adherence to safety protocols.
8. Provide training and support to franchise staff.
9. Implement improvements based on audit findings and feedback.
10. Communicate effectively with the corporate office and stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of compliance violations or audit failures.
2. Decrease in client satisfaction or increase in complaints.
3. Unexplained discrepancies in operational metrics.
4. Delays in addressing operational issues.
5. Lack of transparency in logs and documentation.
6. Security breaches or lapses in safety protocols.
7. Inconsistent communication with corporate office.
8. High turnover rates among staff.
9. Failure to meet established KPIs.
10. Negative trends in financial performance.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of compliance status and recent audit results.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking.
3. Real-time telemetry logs and operational metrics.
4. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
5. Historical data for trend analysis.
6. Client satisfaction scores and feedback summaries.
7. Security clearance status and any exceptions logged.
8. Operational audit logs for transparency.
9. Actionable insights and recommendations for improvement.
10. Quick access to initiate compliance scans and audits.
