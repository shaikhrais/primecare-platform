Clinical Nurse Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement care plans.
3. Provide education and support to patients and families.
4. Collaborate with healthcare teams to ensure quality care.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust care plans as necessary.
6. Advocate for patients' needs and preferences.
7. Participate in research and quality improvement initiatives.
8. Maintain accurate and up-to-date patient records.
9. Provide training and mentorship to nursing staff.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of readmission or complications.
3. Delays in patient care or treatment.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
6. Unusual patterns in patient outcomes or metrics.
7. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Nurse Specialist:
1. Patient assessment metrics and outcomes.
2. Compliance and quality assurance logs.
3. Real-time updates on patient care plans.
4. Alerts for red flags or operational issues.
5. Performance indicators for nursing staff.
6. Education and training resources for staff.
7. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
8. Collaboration tools for team communication.
