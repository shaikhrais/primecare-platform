Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
2. Build and maintain relationships with partners and stakeholders.
3. Develop and implement strategic plans for growth.
4. Analyze market trends and competitor activities.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align strategies.
6. Monitor and report on performance metrics.
7. Negotiate contracts and agreements with partners.
8. Ensure compliance with regulations and company policies.
9. Lead cross-functional teams to achieve business objectives.
10. Provide training and support to team members.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High partner turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Inconsistent communication with stakeholders.
4. Lack of alignment between business development and other departments.
5. Failure to meet compliance or regulatory standards.
6. Negative feedback from partners or customers.
7. Inability to adapt to market changes.
8. Poor performance metrics or KPIs.
9. High employee turnover in the business development team.
10. Insufficient resources or budget for initiatives.

Dashboard Needs for Head of Business Development:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue.
2. Partner satisfaction and feedback metrics.
3. Market analysis and trend data.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Progress on strategic initiatives and goals.
6. Team performance metrics and productivity levels.
7. Visualizations of sales pipeline and forecasts.
8. Logs of recent activities and engagements with partners.
9. Alerts for any compliance or operational issues.
10. Resource allocation and budget tracking.
