HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement HR strategies aligned with business goals.
2. Oversee recruitment, onboarding, and talent management processes.
3. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
4. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
5. Monitor and improve employee engagement and satisfaction.
6. Develop training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.
10. Ensure effective performance management processes.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Increased number of grievances or complaints.
3. Low employee engagement scores.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Poor performance management outcomes.
8. Ineffective communication within the organization.
9. High absenteeism rates.
10. Negative feedback from exit interviews.

HR Director Dashboard Needs:
1. Employee turnover rates and trends.
2. Recruitment metrics (time to fill, quality of hire).
3. Employee engagement scores and feedback.
4. Compliance status with labor laws.
5. Training and development participation rates.
6. Performance management outcomes and trends.
7. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
8. Employee satisfaction survey results.
9. Grievance and conflict resolution statistics.
10. Compensation and benefits analysis.
