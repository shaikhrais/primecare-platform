Territory Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement sales strategies for the assigned territory.
2. Build and maintain relationships with key clients and stakeholders.
3. Monitor market trends and competitor activities.
4. Conduct regular sales meetings and training sessions with the sales team.
5. Analyze sales data and prepare reports for management.
6. Set sales targets and ensure they are met.
7. Collaborate with marketing to create promotional campaigns.
8. Provide feedback to product development based on customer needs.
9. Manage budgets and allocate resources effectively.
10. Ensure compliance with company policies and regulations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Consistent failure to meet sales targets.
2. High turnover rate within the sales team.
3. Lack of communication between team members.
4. Negative feedback from clients regarding service or product quality.
5. Inconsistent reporting and data discrepancies.
6. Low engagement in training and development programs.
7. Increased competition without a strategic response.
8. Poor territory coverage leading to missed opportunities.
9. Delays in responding to customer inquiries or issues.
10. Lack of alignment between sales and marketing efforts.

Dashboard Needs for Territory Sales Manager:
1. Sales performance metrics (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2. Client relationship management tools.
3. Market analysis and competitor insights.
4. Sales pipeline visualization.
5. Team performance tracking and individual contributions.
6. Budget tracking and resource allocation.
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Training and development progress indicators.
9. Alerts for red flags in operations.
10. Integration with CRM and other sales tools for real-time data.
