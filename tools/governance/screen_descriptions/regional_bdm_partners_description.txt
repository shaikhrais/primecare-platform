List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the performance of regional business development managers (BDMs) and their partners.
2. Analyze data related to partner engagement and effectiveness.
3. Identify areas for improvement in partner relationships.
4. Provide feedback and support to BDMs based on performance metrics.
5. Collaborate with other departments to enhance partner strategies.

Red flags of operation:
1. Decrease in partner engagement or satisfaction scores.
2. Inconsistent performance metrics among different BDMs.
3. High turnover rates among partners or BDMs.
4. Delays in communication or response times with partners.
5. Lack of alignment between BDMs and company goals.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each BDM and their partners.
2. Visualizations of partner engagement trends over time.
3. Alerts for any red flags or performance issues.
4. Feedback mechanisms for BDMs to report challenges or successes.
5. Access to relevant resources and support tools for BDMs.
