Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with clients and partners.
4. Conduct market research and analysis.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals.
8. Negotiate contracts and agreements.
9. Track and report on business development metrics.
10. Lead and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High client churn rate.
3. Lack of clear business development strategy.
4. Poor communication within the team.
5. Inconsistent follow-up on leads and opportunities.
6. Negative feedback from clients or partners.
7. Missed deadlines on proposals or contracts.
8. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
9. Inability to adapt to market changes.
10. Insufficient training and resources for the team.

Dashboard Needs for Head of Business Development:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue.
2. Pipeline status of leads and opportunities.
3. Client engagement metrics.
4. Market trends and insights.
5. Team performance and productivity metrics.
6. Upcoming deadlines for proposals and contracts.
7. Feedback and satisfaction scores from clients.
8. Budget tracking for business development activities.
9. Visualizations of growth trends over time.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags.
