List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the progress of training coordinators.
2. Review training completion rates and participant feedback.
3. Identify areas for improvement in training programs.
4. Ensure all training materials are up to date.
5. Communicate with trainers and participants for feedback.
6. Analyze data for trends in training effectiveness.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low completion rates for training programs.
2. High dropout rates among participants.
3. Negative feedback from participants regarding training quality.
4. Delays in updating training materials.
5. Lack of communication from trainers or participants.
6. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in training metrics.

What is needed on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of training completion rates.
2. Participant feedback summaries.
3. Alerts for low performance indicators (e.g., completion rates, feedback).
4. Access to training material updates and version history.
5. Communication tools for reaching out to trainers and participants.
6. Data visualization for trends in training effectiveness over time.
