List of tasks for a Shift Supervisor:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure compliance with company policies.
2. Manage staff schedules and ensure adequate coverage.
3. Monitor performance metrics and address any issues promptly.
4. Conduct regular team meetings to communicate goals and expectations.
5. Provide training and support to team members as needed.
6. Handle customer inquiries and resolve complaints effectively.
7. Maintain a safe and productive work environment.
8. Report operational issues and suggest improvements to management.
9. Ensure proper documentation and record-keeping.

Red flags of operation:
1. High employee turnover or absenteeism.
2. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Inconsistent performance metrics or missed targets.
4. Lack of communication or collaboration among team members.
5. Safety incidents or violations of safety protocols.
6. Delays in service delivery or operational processes.
7. Unresolved conflicts or low morale within the team.

Dashboard needs for a Shift Supervisor:
1. Real-time performance metrics (e.g., sales, customer satisfaction).
2. Staff availability and scheduling information.
3. Incident reports and safety compliance status.
4. Customer feedback and complaint tracking.
5. Operational efficiency indicators (e.g., average service time).
6. Team member performance evaluations and training needs.
7. Alerts for any operational issues or red flags.
8. Historical data for trend analysis and decision-making.
