List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor territory expansion activities.
2. Analyze data related to territory performance.
3. Identify potential areas for expansion.
4. Collaborate with sales and marketing teams for strategic planning.
5. Update and maintain the territory map based on new data.
6. Generate reports on territory performance and expansion results.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent loading errors or slow performance of the application.
2. Inconsistent data displayed on the territory map.
3. Lack of updates or stale data leading to poor decision-making.
4. User complaints about navigation or usability issues.
5. High error rates when generating reports or accessing features.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time data visualization of territory performance.
2. Alerts for any errors or issues in data loading.
3. Quick access to reports and analytics tools.
4. A summary of recent activities and updates in territory expansion.
5. User feedback section for continuous improvement suggestions.
