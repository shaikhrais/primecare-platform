Community Outreach Lead Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement community outreach strategies.
2. Build and maintain relationships with community organizations and stakeholders.
3. Organize and participate in community events and workshops.
4. Collect and analyze feedback from the community to improve outreach efforts.
5. Monitor and report on outreach activities and their effectiveness.
6. Collaborate with internal teams to align outreach efforts with organizational goals.
7. Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and policies.
8. Manage outreach budgets and resources effectively.
9. Train and support volunteers and staff involved in outreach activities.
10. Create and distribute outreach materials and communications.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Lack of engagement or participation from the community.
2. Negative feedback or complaints from community members.
3. Inconsistent messaging or branding in outreach materials.
4. Failure to meet outreach goals or objectives.
5. High turnover or dissatisfaction among outreach staff or volunteers.
6. Insufficient data collection or analysis to inform outreach strategies.
7. Non-compliance with regulations or policies.
8. Poor communication between outreach teams and other departments.
9. Limited resources or budget constraints impacting outreach efforts.
10. Inability to adapt to changing community needs or demographics.

Dashboard Needs for Community Outreach Lead:
1. Overview of outreach activities and events.
2. Metrics on community engagement and participation rates.
3. Feedback and satisfaction scores from community members.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Budget tracking and resource allocation.
6. Log of outreach communications and materials distributed.
7. Performance indicators for outreach staff and volunteers.
8. Data visualization of trends in community needs and demographics.
9. Alerts for any compliance issues or operational red flags.
10. Tools for collaboration and communication with internal teams and stakeholders.
