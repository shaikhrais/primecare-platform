Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming patient referrals and inquiries.
2. Schedule appointments and coordinate with clinical staff.
3. Collect and verify patient information and insurance details.
4. Communicate with patients regarding their intake process.
5. Maintain accurate records and documentation of patient interactions.
6. Collaborate with healthcare providers to ensure smooth transitions.
7. Monitor and follow up on pending referrals and appointments.
8. Provide support and resources to patients as needed.
9. Analyze intake data to identify trends and areas for improvement.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of missed appointments or no-shows.
2. Delays in processing referrals or scheduling.
3. Frequent patient complaints regarding communication or service.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping practices.
5. Increased wait times for appointments.
6. Lack of follow-up on pending referrals.
7. High turnover rate among intake staff.

Dashboard Needs for Intake Coordinator:
1. Real-time metrics on referral volume and appointment scheduling.
2. Patient demographics and insurance information overview.
3. Tracking of missed appointments and no-show rates.
4. Status updates on pending referrals and follow-ups.
5. Performance indicators for response times and patient satisfaction.
6. Analytics on trends in patient inquiries and referrals.
7. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or pending actions.
