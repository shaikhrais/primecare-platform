General Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement business strategies.
3. Manage budgets and financial performance.
4. Lead and motivate staff to achieve goals.
5. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Analyze performance metrics and make data-driven decisions.
7. Foster relationships with stakeholders and partners.
8. Identify areas for improvement and implement changes.
9. Report to upper management on operational performance.
10. Manage risk and ensure business continuity.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased employee morale or high turnover rates.
2. Consistent missed deadlines or project delays.
3. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
4. Poor customer feedback or declining satisfaction.
5. Lack of communication among teams.
6. Inconsistent performance metrics or KPIs.
7. Increased operational costs without clear justification.
8. Non-compliance with industry regulations.
9. Resistance to change or innovation from staff.
10. Frequent conflicts or issues within teams.

Dashboard Requirements for General Manager:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) tracking.
2. Financial performance metrics (revenue, expenses).
3. Employee engagement and turnover statistics.
4. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
5. Project timelines and completion rates.
6. Compliance and regulatory status updates.
7. Operational efficiency metrics (productivity rates).
8. Risk management indicators.
9. Budget vs. actual spending reports.
10. Real-time alerts for critical issues or red flags.
