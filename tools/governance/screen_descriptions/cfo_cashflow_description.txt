Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining Revenue Trends
2. Increasing Expenses without Corresponding Revenue
3. Cash Flow Issues
4. Inaccurate Financial Reporting
5. Compliance Violations
6. High Employee Turnover in Finance Team
7. Unexplained Variances in Budget vs. Actuals
8. Lack of Financial Controls
9. Poor Audit Results
10. Delayed Financial Close Processes

Dashboard Needs for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Financial Health
2. Real-time Cash Flow Analysis
3. Budget vs. Actuals Comparison
4. Revenue and Expense Trends
5. Compliance Status and Audit Logs
6. Risk Assessment Metrics
7. Investment Performance Overview
8. Team Performance Metrics
9. Stakeholder Feedback and Communication Logs
10. Alerts for Financial Red Flags
