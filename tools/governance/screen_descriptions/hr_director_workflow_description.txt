List of tasks for HR Director:
1. Oversee recruitment and hiring processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Monitor employee performance and conduct evaluations.
6. Plan and implement training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red flags of operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Increased absenteeism or low employee morale.
3. Frequent complaints or grievances from employees.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
5. Lack of clear communication between management and staff.
6. Poor performance metrics or low productivity levels.
7. Inconsistent application of HR policies.
8. Resistance to change or innovation within the HR department.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Employee turnover rate and retention statistics.
2. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, candidate sources).
3. Employee satisfaction and engagement scores.
4. Compliance status with labor laws and regulations.
5. Performance evaluation results and trends.
6. Training and development participation rates.
7. Compensation and benefits analysis.
8. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
9. HR budget and expenditure tracking.
10. Real-time alerts for any HR-related issues or red flags.
