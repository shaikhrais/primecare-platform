Training Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Coordinate training schedules and logistics.
3. Monitor and evaluate training effectiveness.
4. Maintain training records and certifications.
5. Collaborate with subject matter experts to create training content.
6. Provide support and guidance to trainees.
7. Ensure compliance with regulatory training requirements.
8. Manage training budgets and resources.
9. Communicate training opportunities to staff.
10. Gather feedback to improve training programs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High dropout rates in training programs.
2. Low engagement or participation in training sessions.
3. Inconsistent training records or missing certifications.
4. Frequent complaints or negative feedback from trainees.
5. Lack of alignment between training content and job requirements.
6. Delays in training schedules or logistics.
7. Inability to track training effectiveness and outcomes.
8. Poor communication regarding training opportunities.
9. Non-compliance with regulatory training requirements.
10. Insufficient resources allocated for training programs.

Dashboard Needs for Training Coordinator:
1. Overview of upcoming training sessions and schedules.
2. Metrics on training participation and completion rates.
3. Feedback and evaluation results from trainees.
4. Compliance status for required certifications.
5. Budget tracking for training programs.
6. Logs of training sessions conducted and attendance records.
7. Alerts for upcoming certification expirations.
8. Resource allocation for training materials and venues.
9. Performance indicators for training effectiveness.
10. Communication tools for announcements and updates.
