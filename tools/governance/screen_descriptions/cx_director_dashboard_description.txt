List of tasks a CX Director must perform:
1. Oversee customer experience strategy and implementation.
2. Analyze customer feedback and data to identify trends and areas for improvement.
3. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance customer journey.
4. Develop and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) related to customer satisfaction.
5. Conduct regular compliance audits and ensure adherence to policies.
6. Manage customer service operations and ensure high service standards.
7. Lead initiatives to improve customer engagement and loyalty.
8. Train and mentor team members on best practices in customer experience.
9. Report on customer experience metrics to executive leadership.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in customer experience.

Red flags in operations that indicate ineffective performance:
1. Declining customer satisfaction scores.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. High employee turnover in customer-facing roles.
4. Inconsistent service delivery across channels.
5. Lack of actionable insights from customer data.
6. Delays in responding to customer inquiries or issues.
7. Poor compliance with established policies and procedures.
8. Low engagement rates in customer loyalty programs.
9. Ineffective communication between departments regarding customer issues.
10. Failure to meet established KPIs related to customer experience.

Key elements to include on the CX Director dashboard:
1. Real-time customer satisfaction scores.
2. Trends in customer feedback and complaints.
3. KPIs for customer engagement and loyalty metrics.
4. Compliance audit results and action items.
5. Operational performance metrics (e.g., response times, resolution rates).
6. Logs of recent customer interactions and resolutions.
7. Alerts for any significant drops in performance metrics.
8. Visualizations of customer journey mapping.
9. Employee performance metrics related to customer service.
10. Upcoming initiatives and their expected impact on customer experience.
