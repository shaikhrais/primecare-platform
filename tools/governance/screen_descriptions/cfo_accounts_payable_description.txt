User Tasks:
1. Review and approve invoices.
2. Manage vendor payments.
3. Track payment statuses.
4. Generate financial reports related to accounts payable.
5. Ensure compliance with financial regulations.
6. Communicate with vendors regarding payment inquiries.
7. Monitor cash flow related to accounts payable.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in invoice approvals.
2. High volume of payment disputes.
3. Frequent errors in payment processing.
4. Lack of visibility into payment statuses.
5. Non-compliance with financial regulations.
6. Inconsistent communication with vendors.
7. Unusual spikes in accounts payable aging.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of pending invoices and their statuses.
2. Alerts for overdue payments and approvals.
3. Visual representation of cash flow related to accounts payable.
4. Reports on payment disputes and resolutions.
5. Compliance tracking metrics.
6. Vendor communication logs.
7. Performance metrics for accounts payable processing times.
