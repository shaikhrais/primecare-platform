List of work user must do:
1. Monitor quality assurance metrics.
2. Review scorecards for team performance.
3. Identify areas for improvement based on scorecard data.
4. Collaborate with team members to address quality issues.
5. Provide feedback and suggestions for process enhancements.

Red flags of operation:
1. Consistent low scores in specific metrics.
2. High error rates in deliverables.
3. Delays in project timelines due to quality issues.
4. Lack of communication regarding quality standards.
5. Repeated issues that have not been resolved.

What we need on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of quality assurance metrics.
2. Visual representation of scorecards (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for red flag operations.
4. Historical data for trend analysis.
5. Actionable insights and recommendations for improvement.
