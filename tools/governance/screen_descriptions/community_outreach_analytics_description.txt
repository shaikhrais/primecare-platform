Community Outreach Lead Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement community outreach strategies.
2. Build and maintain relationships with community organizations and stakeholders.
3. Organize and participate in community events and workshops.
4. Collect and analyze data on community needs and outreach effectiveness.
5. Collaborate with internal teams to align outreach efforts with organizational goals.
6. Prepare reports and presentations on outreach activities and outcomes.
7. Manage outreach budgets and resources effectively.
8. Train and supervise outreach staff and volunteers.
9. Monitor and evaluate the impact of outreach programs.
10. Address community feedback and concerns promptly.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in community engagement or participation in events.
2. Negative feedback from community members or stakeholders.
3. Inconsistent data collection or reporting practices.
4. Lack of collaboration with internal teams.
5. Budget overruns or mismanagement of resources.
6. High turnover rates among outreach staff or volunteers.
7. Failure to meet outreach goals or objectives.
8. Limited visibility or awareness of outreach programs within the community.

Dashboard Needs for Community Outreach Lead:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for outreach effectiveness.
2. Community engagement metrics (event attendance, feedback scores).
3. Budget tracking and resource allocation overview.
4. Data visualization of outreach program impacts.
5. Reports on community needs and demographics.
6. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from community members.
7. Collaboration metrics with internal teams and stakeholders.
8. Alerts for any red flags or issues in outreach operations.
9. Historical data comparison to track progress over time.
10. Tools for planning and scheduling upcoming outreach events.
