List of work for the User:
1. Monitor sales pipeline progress.
2. Analyze territory performance metrics.
3. Identify potential leads and opportunities.
4. Collaborate with team members on sales strategies.
5. Update and maintain customer information.
6. Generate sales reports for management review.
7. Attend training sessions for new tools and techniques.

Red flags of operation:
1. Declining sales numbers over consecutive periods.
2. High turnover rates among sales staff.
3. Inconsistent data entry or updates in the system.
4. Lack of communication within the sales team.
5. Missed deadlines for follow-ups with leads.
6. Low engagement with customers or prospects.
7. Frequent errors in reporting or data analysis.

What is needed on the User dashboard:
1. Real-time sales pipeline overview.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for territories.
3. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or tasks.
4. Visualizations of sales trends and forecasts.
5. Quick access to customer profiles and history.
6. Collaboration tools for team communication.
7. Reporting tools for generating insights and summaries.
