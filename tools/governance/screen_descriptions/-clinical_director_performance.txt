Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Monitor and evaluate clinical performance metrics.
3. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
4. Manage clinical staff and resources effectively.
5. Ensure patient safety and quality of care.
6. Implement clinical protocols and best practices.
7. Facilitate communication between clinical teams and administration.
8. Address and resolve operational issues promptly.
9. Maintain accurate and up-to-date clinical documentation.
10. Provide training and support to clinical staff.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High rates of patient complaints or adverse events.
2. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
3. Decreased staff morale or high turnover rates.
4. Inconsistent clinical performance metrics.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Lack of communication among clinical teams.
7. Insufficient resources or staffing levels.
8. Frequent errors in clinical documentation.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for clinical operations.
2. Real-time telemetry logs and compliance standings.
3. Audit logs of operational activities.
4. Metrics on patient safety and quality of care.
5. Staff performance and engagement statistics.
6. Alerts for compliance issues or operational red flags.
7. Visualizations of clinical performance trends over time.
8. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
