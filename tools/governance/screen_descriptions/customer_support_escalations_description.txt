User Tasks:
1. Monitor customer support escalations.
2. Review and respond to escalated customer issues.
3. Analyze trends in escalations for process improvement.
4. Collaborate with other departments to resolve escalated issues.
5. Document resolutions and feedback for future reference.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of escalated issues indicating potential underlying problems.
2. Delays in response times to escalated issues.
3. Repeated escalations from the same customers.
4. Lack of communication between departments regarding escalations.
5. Inconsistent documentation of escalated issues and resolutions.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of current escalated issues with status updates.
2. Metrics on response times and resolution times for escalated issues.
3. Trend analysis of escalations over time.
4. Alerts for high-priority escalations or repeated issues.
5. Feedback loop for customer satisfaction post-resolution.
