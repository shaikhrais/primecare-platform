Responsibilities of a Territory Expansion Manager:
1. Identify potential new territories for expansion.
2. Conduct market research and analysis to assess viability.
3. Develop and implement territory expansion strategies.
4. Collaborate with sales and marketing teams to align efforts.
5. Monitor performance metrics and adjust strategies as needed.
6. Build relationships with local stakeholders and partners.
7. Ensure compliance with local regulations and company policies.
8. Report on progress and outcomes to senior management.

Red flags in operations:
1. Consistent failure to meet expansion targets.
2. High turnover rates in newly expanded territories.
3. Negative feedback from local stakeholders or partners.
4. Compliance issues or regulatory violations.
5. Lack of market research leading to poor decision-making.
6. Insufficient resources allocated for expansion efforts.
7. Poor communication between teams involved in expansion.

Dashboard needs for a Territory Expansion Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for expansion success.
2. Market analysis data and insights.
3. Compliance status and audit logs.
4. Stakeholder feedback and engagement metrics.
5. Resource allocation and budget tracking.
6. Progress tracking against expansion timelines.
7. Alerts for any compliance or operational issues.
