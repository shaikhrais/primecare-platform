Infrastructure Auditor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular audits of infrastructure components.
2. Assess compliance with security policies and standards.
3. Evaluate performance and capacity of infrastructure.
4. Identify vulnerabilities and risks in the infrastructure.
5. Review configuration management processes.
6. Ensure proper documentation of infrastructure changes.
7. Monitor and report on infrastructure health and performance.
8. Collaborate with IT teams to implement improvements.
9. Provide recommendations for optimization and cost reduction.
10. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of industry best practices.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent outages or downtime.
2. Unpatched vulnerabilities or outdated software.
3. Lack of documentation for infrastructure changes.
4. Inconsistent configuration across environments.
5. Poor performance metrics or capacity issues.
6. Unauthorized access attempts or security breaches.
7. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
8. High operational costs without clear justification.
9. Lack of monitoring or alerting systems.
10. Insufficient backup and disaster recovery plans.

Dashboard Requirements for Infrastructure Auditor:
1. Overview of infrastructure health and performance metrics.
2. Compliance status with security policies and regulations.
3. Alerts for critical issues or anomalies.
4. Audit trail of changes made to infrastructure.
5. Visualization of resource utilization and capacity.
6. Reports on vulnerabilities and remediation status.
7. Historical data on outages and incidents.
8. Summary of ongoing and completed audits.
9. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for infrastructure.
10. Integration with incident management and ticketing systems.
