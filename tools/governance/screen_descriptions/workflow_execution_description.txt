Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess governance frameworks.
3. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
4. Maintain accurate logs of operations and audits.
5. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
6. Provide training and resources on governance policies.
7. Analyze data and report on governance performance.
8. Identify and mitigate risks associated with governance.
9. Implement corrective actions for non-compliance.
10. Stay updated on changes in regulations and best practices.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit processes or reporting.
3. Lack of transparency in operations.
4. Inconsistent or missing documentation.
5. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
6. Poor communication between departments.
7. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding governance.
8. Failure to implement corrective actions.
9. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
10. Inadequate training or resources for staff.

Dashboard needs for a Governance Officer:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Audit logs and historical data visualization.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for governance.
4. Risk assessment metrics and trends.
5. Alerts for compliance violations or issues.
6. Summary of completed and pending audits.
7. Stakeholder feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Training and resource utilization statistics.
9. Regulatory updates and changes.
10. Action items and follow-up tasks tracker.
