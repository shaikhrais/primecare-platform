Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
3. Assist with patient hygiene and daily living activities.
4. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members for patient care.
6. Conduct wound care and assessments.
7. Perform basic diagnostic tests and procedures.
8. Educate patients and families about health management.
9. Maintain a clean and safe environment for patients.
10. Participate in compliance audits and quality assurance activities.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased medication errors or discrepancies.
2. Unusual changes in patient vital signs.
3. High rates of patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Incomplete or inaccurate documentation.
5. Delays in medication administration or treatments.
6. Frequent staff turnover or absenteeism.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
8. Lack of communication among team members.
9. Inconsistent patient assessments or care plans.
10. Failure to report critical incidents or near misses.

Dashboard Requirements for LPN:
1. Real-time patient vital signs monitoring.
2. Medication administration logs and alerts.
3. Compliance audit results and action items.
4. Patient care documentation status.
5. Communication tools for team collaboration.
6. Incident reporting and tracking system.
7. Performance metrics and KPIs for patient care.
8. Access to educational resources for patient management.
9. Alerts for overdue assessments or treatments.
10. Summary of patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
