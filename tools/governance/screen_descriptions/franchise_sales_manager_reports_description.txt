List of work the user must do:
1. Review franchise sales data.
2. Analyze performance metrics of franchises.
3. Generate reports on sales trends.
4. Identify top-performing franchises.
5. Monitor franchise compliance with sales standards.
6. Provide feedback and support to franchisees.
7. Collaborate with marketing for promotional strategies.
8. Attend meetings to discuss franchise performance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant drop in sales for any franchise.
2. High number of customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Delayed reporting or data submission from franchises.
4. Inconsistent performance metrics across similar franchises.
5. Lack of engagement from franchisees in training or support programs.
6. Frequent turnover of franchise staff.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time sales data visualization.
2. Alerts for any red flags identified.
3. Performance comparison charts for franchises.
4. Customer feedback summary.
5. Compliance tracking indicators.
6. Access to historical sales data for trend analysis.
7. Quick links to generate reports.
8. Notifications for upcoming meetings or deadlines.
