Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Monitor patient health and vital signs.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Assist with daily living activities for patients.
4. Document patient care and progress.
5. Collaborate with healthcare teams.
6. Educate patients and families about health management.
7. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
8. Participate in patient assessments and care planning.
9. Respond to patient emergencies and provide first aid.
10. Maintain a clean and safe environment.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient falls or injuries.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Signs of burnout or low morale among staff.
7. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
8. Unusual patterns in patient vital signs or health status.
9. Lack of communication among team members.
10. Inadequate staffing levels affecting patient care.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Patient health status overview (vital signs, alerts).
2. Medication administration records and compliance tracking.
3. Incident reporting and compliance audit logs.
4. Patient satisfaction metrics and feedback.
5. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
6. Training and certification status of staff.
7. Real-time alerts for critical patient conditions.
8. Compliance with health and safety standards.
9. Historical data on patient outcomes and incidents.
10. Communication tools for team collaboration.
