User Work List:
1. Track outpatient prescriptions.
2. View prescription details.
3. Update prescription status.
4. Manage patient information related to prescriptions.
5. Generate reports on prescription usage.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in updating prescription status.
2. Inaccurate patient information.
3. Missing prescription details.
4. Low user engagement with the tracker.
5. High error rates in prescription entries.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of current prescriptions and their statuses.
2. Alerts for overdue prescriptions or updates needed.
3. Analytics on prescription trends and usage.
4. User activity logs to monitor engagement.
5. Quick access to patient information and prescription history.
