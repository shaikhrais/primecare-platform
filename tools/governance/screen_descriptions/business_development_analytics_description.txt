Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key clients and partners.
4. Conduct market research and competitive analysis.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align strategies.
6. Monitor industry trends and adjust strategies accordingly.
7. Prepare and present business proposals and presentations.
8. Track and report on business development metrics and KPIs.
9. Lead negotiations and close deals.
10. Manage and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High client churn rate.
3. Lack of clear business development strategy.
4. Poor communication within the team or with clients.
5. Inconsistent tracking of metrics and KPIs.
6. Resistance to change or innovation.
7. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
8. Inability to adapt to market changes.
9. Missed deadlines or project delays.
10. Lack of collaboration with other departments.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Sales performance metrics (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2. Client acquisition and retention rates.
3. Pipeline status and deal progress.
4. Market trends and competitive analysis data.
5. Team performance metrics (individual and team goals).
6. Revenue forecasts and projections.
7. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Key business development initiatives and their status.
9. Budget and resource allocation overview.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
