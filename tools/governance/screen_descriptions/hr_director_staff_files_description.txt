List of tasks for HR Director:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Conduct performance evaluations and manage talent development.
6. Monitor employee engagement and satisfaction.
7. Implement training and development programs.
8. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
9. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.
11. Oversee health and safety programs.
12. Manage HR budgets and resources.

Red flags of operation:
1. High turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Frequent conflicts or grievances among staff.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Negative feedback from exit interviews.
7. Inconsistent performance evaluations.
8. Lack of training and development opportunities.
9. Poor communication within the organization.
10. Budget overruns in HR initiatives.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for recruitment, retention, and employee satisfaction.
2. Compliance status with labor laws and regulations.
3. Real-time metrics on employee engagement and feedback.
4. Overview of training and development programs and participation rates.
5. Financial overview of HR budgets and expenditures.
6. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
7. Audit logs of HR activities and compliance checks.
8. Alerts for any red flags or issues that need immediate attention.
9. Visualizations of trends in employee performance and engagement over time.
10. Summary of ongoing HR initiatives and their status.
