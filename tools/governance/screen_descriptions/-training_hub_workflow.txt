List of work training candidates must do:
1. Complete onboarding training modules.
2. Participate in hands-on workshops.
3. Engage in peer-to-peer learning sessions.
4. Complete assessments and quizzes.
5. Provide feedback on training materials.
6. Attend regular check-ins with mentors.
7. Document progress and challenges faced during training.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low engagement in training sessions.
2. Frequent absences or tardiness.
3. Inconsistent performance in assessments.
4. Lack of communication with mentors.
5. Negative feedback on training materials.
6. Failure to meet training milestones.
7. Resistance to feedback or improvement suggestions.

Dashboard needs for training candidates:
1. Progress tracking for each training module.
2. Assessment scores and feedback.
3. Upcoming training sessions and events calendar.
4. Communication log with mentors.
5. Resource library for training materials.
6. Feedback submission section.
7. Visual indicators for milestones achieved and areas needing improvement.
