Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and evaluate their effectiveness.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Conduct market research to identify trends and opportunities.
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams to align marketing efforts.
6. Monitor and analyze performance metrics to optimize campaigns.
7. Ensure brand consistency across all marketing channels.
8. Lead and mentor the marketing team.
9. Manage relationships with external partners and vendors.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining engagement or conversion rates.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Inconsistent brand messaging across channels.
4. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
5. Lack of clear marketing objectives or KPIs.
6. High turnover rates within the marketing team.
7. Failure to adapt to market changes or consumer preferences.
8. Poor communication between marketing and other departments.
9. Inadequate tracking of campaign performance.
10. Over-reliance on a single marketing channel.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their performance.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as conversion rates, ROI, and customer acquisition costs.
3. Real-time analytics on website traffic and engagement metrics.
4. Social media performance metrics including reach, engagement, and follower growth.
5. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
6. Market research insights and trend analysis.
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Team performance metrics and workload distribution.
9. Competitor analysis and benchmarking data.
10. Alerts for any significant changes in performance or market conditions.
