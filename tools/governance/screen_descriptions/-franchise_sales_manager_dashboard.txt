Franchise Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee franchise sales operations.
2. Develop and implement sales strategies.
3. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
4. Conduct market research and analysis.
5. Manage relationships with franchisees.
6. Provide training and support to franchisees.
7. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements.
8. Prepare sales reports and forecasts.
9. Collaborate with marketing for promotional activities.
10. Attend franchise expos and networking events.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales figures.
2. High franchisee turnover rate.
3. Frequent complaints from franchisees.
4. Non-compliance with franchise standards.
5. Poor customer feedback or ratings.
6. Inconsistent communication with franchisees.
7. Lack of training or support for franchisees.
8. Delays in responding to franchisee inquiries.
9. Unclear or outdated franchise policies.
10. Insufficient market research leading to poor decision-making.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Sales Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and franchisee performance.
2. Real-time sales data and trends.
3. Compliance status and audit results.
4. Franchisee satisfaction metrics.
5. Market analysis and competitive positioning.
6. Logs of operational activities and communications.
7. Alerts for any red flags or issues.
8. Tools for tracking training and support provided to franchisees.
9. Visualizations of sales forecasts and targets.
10. Access to historical performance data for trend analysis.
