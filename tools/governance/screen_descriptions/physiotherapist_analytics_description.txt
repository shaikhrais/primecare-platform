Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy techniques and research.

Red Flags Indicating Need for Immediate Attention:
1. Severe pain or discomfort reported by the patient.
2. Sudden changes in patient condition or mobility.
3. Signs of infection or unusual swelling.
4. Lack of improvement in patient progress.
5. Patient reports dizziness or fainting during treatment.
6. Unexplained bruising or skin changes.
7. Non-compliance with treatment plan by the patient.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Patient assessment summary and history.
2. Treatment plan overview and progress tracking.
3. Alerts for red flag indicators.
4. Patient feedback and satisfaction metrics.
5. Integration with electronic health records (EHR).
6. Performance metrics and analytics on treatment outcomes.
7. Access to educational resources and latest research.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
