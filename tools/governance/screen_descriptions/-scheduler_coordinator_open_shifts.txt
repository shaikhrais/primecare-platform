User Tasks:
1. Monitor open shifts and their statuses.
2. Assign shifts to available staff.
3. Review and manage shift requests from employees.
4. Communicate with team members regarding shift changes.
5. Analyze shift coverage and identify gaps.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors in loading data indicating potential backend issues.
2. Delays in shift assignment leading to understaffing.
3. High number of unassigned shifts indicating poor communication or engagement.
4. Repeated complaints from staff about shift management.
5. Inconsistent data displayed on the dashboard.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time overview of open shifts and their statuses.
2. Notifications for unassigned shifts and pending requests.
3. Performance metrics for shift assignments and employee engagement.
4. Visual representation of shift coverage across different time periods.
5. Quick access to communication tools for team coordination.
