HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement HR strategies aligned with business goals.
2. Oversee recruitment, onboarding, and talent management processes.
3. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
4. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
5. Design and implement training and development programs.
6. Monitor and improve employee performance and engagement.
7. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
8. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and environment.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Frequent compliance issues or audits.
4. Delays in recruitment processes.
5. Increased number of employee grievances or conflicts.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Poor performance management outcomes.
8. Inconsistent application of HR policies.
9. Negative feedback from employee surveys.
10. Ineffective communication between departments.

HR Director Dashboard Needs:
1. Employee engagement metrics.
2. Recruitment and turnover statistics.
3. Compliance audit results.
4. Training and development participation rates.
5. Performance management outcomes.
6. Employee satisfaction survey results.
7. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
8. Compensation and benefits analysis.
9. Real-time HR operational logs.
10. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to HR initiatives.
