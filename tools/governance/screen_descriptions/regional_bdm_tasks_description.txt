User Tasks:
1. Monitor regional sales performance.
2. Manage and assign tasks to team members.
3. Review and analyze sales reports.
4. Communicate with clients and stakeholders.
5. Set and track sales targets.
6. Attend regional meetings and training sessions.
7. Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales figures in specific regions.
2. High turnover rates among team members.
3. Delays in task completion or project deadlines.
4. Lack of communication or collaboration within the team.
5. Frequent customer complaints or dissatisfaction.
6. Inconsistent reporting or data discrepancies.
7. Low engagement in meetings or training sessions.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics.
2. Task assignment and completion status.
3. Visual representation of sales trends and forecasts.
4. Client feedback and satisfaction ratings.
5. Team member performance and productivity analytics.
6. Alerts for overdue tasks or deadlines.
7. Summary of recent communications with clients.
