User Work List:
1. Monitor and manage scheduling conflicts.
2. Review and resolve any identified conflicts.
3. Communicate with team members regarding scheduling issues.
4. Update the scheduling system with resolved conflicts.
5. Generate reports on scheduling conflicts and resolutions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent conflicts in scheduling indicating poor planning.
2. Delays in resolving conflicts leading to operational inefficiencies.
3. Lack of communication among team members regarding scheduling.
4. Inconsistent updates to the scheduling system.
5. High volume of unresolved conflicts over time.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of current scheduling conflicts.
2. Status indicators for conflict resolution (e.g., resolved, pending).
3. Communication log for team interactions regarding conflicts.
4. Historical data on conflicts and resolutions for trend analysis.
5. Alerts for high-priority conflicts that require immediate attention.
