Customer Support must perform the following tasks:

1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Maintain a knowledge base for common issues and solutions.
3. Conduct regular compliance audits to ensure adherence to policies.
4. Log all customer interactions and resolutions.
5. Provide feedback to product teams based on customer insights.
6. Monitor and analyze support metrics for continuous improvement.
7. Train and update team members on new policies and tools.
8. Manage escalations effectively and ensure timely resolutions.
9. Ensure customer satisfaction through follow-ups and surveys.
10. Collaborate with other departments for complex issues.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work include:

1. High response times to customer inquiries.
2. Increased number of unresolved tickets.
3. Frequent escalations to higher management.
4. Negative customer feedback or low satisfaction scores.
5. Lack of updated knowledge base articles.
6. Inconsistent logging of customer interactions.
7. Poor communication within the support team.
8. High turnover rates among support staff.
9. Failure to meet compliance audit requirements.
10. Lack of proactive measures to address recurring issues.

Dashboard needs for Customer Support:

1. Real-time metrics on ticket volume and response times.
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
3. Compliance audit results and status.
4. Logs of customer interactions and resolutions.
5. Escalation rates and resolution times.
6. Knowledge base usage statistics.
7. Team performance metrics and individual contributions.
8. Alerts for high-priority tickets or issues.
9. Trends in customer inquiries and common issues.
10. Training and development needs for support staff.
