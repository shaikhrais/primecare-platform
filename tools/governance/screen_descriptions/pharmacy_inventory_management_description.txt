User Tasks:
1. Monitor inventory levels of medications and supplies.
2. Update stock quantities after receiving new shipments.
3. Track expiration dates of medications.
4. Generate reports on inventory usage and trends.
5. Set reorder alerts for low stock items.
6. Manage supplier information and contact details.
7. Conduct regular audits of inventory for accuracy.
8. Handle returns and disposals of expired or damaged items.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent stockouts of critical medications.
2. High number of expired items in inventory.
3. Discrepancies between physical counts and system records.
4. Delayed updates to inventory after receiving shipments.
5. Lack of timely reports on inventory usage.
6. Inconsistent supplier performance or delivery issues.
7. Unclear or missing documentation for inventory transactions.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time inventory levels for all medications and supplies.
2. Alerts for low stock and expiration dates.
3. Visual reports on inventory trends and usage statistics.
4. Audit trail of inventory changes and transactions.
5. Supplier performance metrics.
6. Quick access to reorder and supplier contact information.
7. Search functionality for easy item lookup.
8. User-friendly interface for updating inventory records.
