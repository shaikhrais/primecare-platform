Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and initiatives.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Analyze market trends and customer insights.
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams.
6. Monitor and report on marketing performance metrics.
7. Lead and mentor the marketing team.
8. Ensure brand consistency across all channels.
9. Manage social media presence and engagement.
10. Conduct market research to identify opportunities.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining engagement rates on social media.
2. Inconsistent brand messaging across platforms.
3. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
4. Low conversion rates from marketing campaigns.
5. Negative feedback or reviews from customers.
6. High employee turnover within the marketing team.
7. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales teams.
8. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).
9. Delays in campaign execution or project timelines.
10. Insufficient data analysis leading to uninformed decisions.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their status.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as engagement rates, conversion rates, and ROI.
3. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
4. Social media metrics including follower growth and engagement.
5. Market research insights and trends.
6. Customer feedback and sentiment analysis.
7. Team performance metrics and productivity levels.
8. Timeline for upcoming campaigns and initiatives.
9. Compliance and governance status related to marketing activities.
10. Real-time analytics and reporting capabilities.
