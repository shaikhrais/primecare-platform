List of tasks Personal Support Workers (PSWs) must perform:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, grooming).
2. Monitor and record vital signs (blood pressure, temperature).
3. Administer medications as prescribed.
4. Provide companionship and emotional support.
5. Help with mobility and physical exercises.
6. Prepare meals and assist with feeding if necessary.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Communicate with healthcare professionals about client status.
9. Document care provided and any changes in client condition.
10. Report any concerns or emergencies to supervisors.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work:
1. Missed scheduled visits or appointments.
2. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
3. Lack of communication with clients or their families.
4. Failure to follow care plans or medication schedules.
5. Signs of neglect or abuse in client care.
6. High turnover rates among PSWs.
7. Frequent complaints from clients or families.
8. Inadequate training or support for PSWs.
9. Poor time management leading to rushed care.
10. Lack of adherence to safety protocols.

Dashboard needs for Personal Support Workers (PSWs):
1. Client list with essential details (name, age, address, care level).
2. Search functionality for quick access to client profiles.
3. Filter options (e.g., by visit status: today, pending).
4. Notifications for upcoming visits or tasks.
5. Summary of vital signs and health status for each client.
6. Documentation section for recording care provided.
7. Alerts for any red flags or concerns regarding clients.
8. Communication tools for contacting healthcare professionals.
9. Training resources and support materials.
10. Performance metrics to track PSW effectiveness and client satisfaction.
