Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the end-to-end recruitment process.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
6. Coordinate and schedule interviews with candidates and hiring teams.
7. Provide feedback to candidates throughout the hiring process.
8. Maintain applicant tracking system and ensure data accuracy.
9. Analyze recruitment metrics and report on hiring progress.
10. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
11. Build and maintain relationships with external recruitment agencies.
12. Conduct onboarding for new hires.
13. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in talent acquisition.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative feedback from candidates about the hiring process.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates and hiring managers.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or targets.
8. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
9. Poor candidate experience reflected in surveys or reviews.
10. Inability to adapt to changing market conditions or talent needs.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, and candidate quality metrics.
2. Real-time data on open positions and their status.
3. Candidate pipeline visualization showing stages of the recruitment process.
4. Diversity metrics to track the inclusivity of hiring practices.
5. Source of hire analysis to evaluate the effectiveness of recruitment channels.
6. Candidate feedback and experience ratings.
7. Compliance and audit logs to ensure adherence to regulations.
8. Recruitment team performance metrics.
9. Historical data trends for recruitment metrics over time.
10. Alerts for positions that are taking longer than average to fill.
