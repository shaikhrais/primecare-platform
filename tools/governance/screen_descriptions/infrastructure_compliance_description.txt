Infrastructure Auditor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits of infrastructure.
2. Monitor and validate governance invariants.
3. Ensure adherence to security policies and protocols.
4. Maintain logs of all compliance activities and audits.
5. Report on compliance status and any identified issues.
6. Implement and manage security measures such as MFA.
7. Provide real-time telemetry for system audits.
8. Facilitate secure event reporting and ingestion.
9. Update governance directives as necessary.
10. Trigger state actions for compliance and governance.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent compliance failures or non-compliance reports.
2. Delays in executing compliance audits.
3. Inconsistent logging of compliance activities.
4. Unauthorized access attempts or security breaches.
5. Lack of adherence to security policies (e.g., MFA not enforced).
6. High volume of manual interventions required.
7. Negative trends in compliance audit results over time.
8. Incomplete or missing telemetry data.
9. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
10. Poor response times to compliance-related incidents.

Dashboard Requirements for Infrastructure Auditor:
1. Real-time compliance status overview.
2. Recent audit logs and compliance history.
3. Alerts for compliance failures or security breaches.
4. Metrics on the number of audits conducted and their outcomes.
5. Visual representation of compliance trends over time.
6. Quick access to governance directives and their statuses.
7. Secure event reporting submissions and their statuses.
8. Notifications for required actions or policy updates.
9. Summary of operational audit logs.
10. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and interaction.
