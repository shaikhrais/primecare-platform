List of tasks a QA Specialist must do:
1. Conduct regular compliance scans to ensure adherence to quality standards.
2. Review and analyze audit logs for discrepancies or issues.
3. Collaborate with development teams to identify and resolve quality issues.
4. Monitor and report on key performance indicators (KPIs).
5. Maintain documentation of quality assurance processes and findings.
6. Provide training and support to team members on quality standards.
7. Participate in continuous improvement initiatives.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors or failures in compliance scans.
2. Inconsistent or missing documentation of quality processes.
3. High number of discrepancies in audit logs.
4. Delays in addressing identified quality issues.
5. Lack of communication between QA and development teams.
6. Low engagement or feedback from team members regarding quality processes.

Dashboard needs for a QA Specialist:
1. Real-time compliance scan results and status.
2. Visual representation of audit logs and discrepancies.
3. KPIs tracking for quality metrics (e.g., error rates, resolution times).
4. Alerts for critical issues or failures in quality processes.
5. Historical data trends for compliance and quality metrics.
6. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and access to information.
