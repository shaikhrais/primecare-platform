Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Monitor patient vital signs and report any abnormalities.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
4. Collaborate with healthcare team members to develop patient care plans.
5. Educate patients and families about health conditions and treatments.
6. Assist in diagnostic tests and procedures.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for patients.
8. Respond to patient calls and provide necessary assistance.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Stay updated on best practices and continuing education.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient readmissions.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
8. Poor communication among team members.
9. Lack of adherence to infection control measures.
10. Inadequate staffing levels during peak times.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Real-time patient vital signs and alerts.
2. Compliance audit logs and results.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care.
4. Medication administration records and alerts.
5. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
6. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
7. Access to educational resources and training updates.
8. Incident reporting and tracking system.
9. Communication tools for team collaboration.
10. Visualizations of patient outcomes and trends.
