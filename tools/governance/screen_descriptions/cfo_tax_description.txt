Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Regulatory Oversight
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining Revenue Trends
2. Increasing Expenses without Corresponding Revenue Growth
3. Cash Flow Issues
4. High Employee Turnover in Finance Team
5. Compliance Violations or Audit Issues
6. Inaccurate Financial Reporting
7. Unexplained Variances in Budget vs Actual
8. Lack of Clear Financial Strategy
9. Poor Communication with Stakeholders
10. Delayed Financial Close Processes

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Overview
2. Revenue and Expense Trends
3. Cash Flow Projections
4. Budget vs Actual Analysis
5. Compliance Status Indicators
6. Risk Management Metrics
7. Audit Log Summary
8. Investment Performance Overview
9. Team Performance Metrics
10. Real-time Financial Alerts and Notifications
