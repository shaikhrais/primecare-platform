Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient conditions and needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red Flags Indicating Need for Immediate Attention:
1. Sudden worsening of symptoms.
2. Signs of infection (fever, redness, swelling).
3. Unexplained weight loss or fatigue.
4. Neurological symptoms (numbness, weakness).
5. Severe pain not relieved by treatment.
6. Changes in bowel or bladder function.
7. Patient expresses suicidal thoughts or self-harm.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Patient progress tracking metrics.
2. Compliance audit results.
3. Operational audit logs.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
5. Alerts for red flags and critical patient updates.
6. Real-time telemetry data for treatment adjustments.
7. Access to patient history and treatment plans.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
