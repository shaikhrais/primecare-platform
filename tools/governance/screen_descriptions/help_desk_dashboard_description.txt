List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor incoming help desk tickets.
2. Track ticket resolution times.
3. Analyze user feedback on resolved tickets.
4. Identify recurring issues and trends.
5. Communicate with team members regarding ticket status.
6. Update ticket statuses as they progress.
7. Generate reports on help desk performance.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of unresolved tickets.
2. Increased average resolution time.
3. Frequent escalations to higher support tiers.
4. Low user satisfaction ratings.
5. Recurring issues that are not being addressed.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Inconsistent ticket updates.

What is needed on the dashboard:
1. Real-time ticket status overview (open, in progress, resolved).
2. Average resolution time metrics.
3. User satisfaction ratings and feedback summaries.
4. Alerts for tickets that exceed expected resolution times.
5. Visual representation of recurring issues and trends.
6. Team member workload distribution.
7. Quick access to communication tools for team collaboration.
