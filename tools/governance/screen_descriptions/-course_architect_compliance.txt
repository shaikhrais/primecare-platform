Training Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Assess training needs through surveys, interviews, and consultations.
3. Design and deliver training materials and resources.
4. Evaluate the effectiveness of training programs.
5. Monitor and report on training outcomes and performance metrics.
6. Collaborate with other departments to align training with organizational goals.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in training and development.
8. Manage training budgets and resources.
9. Provide coaching and support to trainers and facilitators.
10. Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational training requirements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High turnover rates among trainees or trainers.
2. Low engagement or participation in training sessions.
3. Inconsistent feedback from trainees regarding training quality.
4. Lack of measurable outcomes or improvements post-training.
5. Delays in training program implementation.
6. Insufficient resources allocated for training initiatives.
7. Non-compliance with training regulations or standards.
8. Poor communication between departments regarding training needs.
9. Negative trends in employee performance metrics post-training.
10. Limited access to training materials or resources.

Dashboard Needs for Training Director:
1. Training program participation rates.
2. Trainee feedback and satisfaction scores.
3. Training effectiveness metrics (e.g., pre- and post-training assessments).
4. Compliance status with training regulations.
5. Budget utilization for training programs.
6. Upcoming training sessions and schedules.
7. Trainer performance evaluations.
8. Industry trends and benchmarks for training effectiveness.
9. Resource availability and allocation for training.
10. Logs of training sessions and outcomes for accountability.
