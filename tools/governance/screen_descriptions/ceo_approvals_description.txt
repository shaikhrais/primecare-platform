List of work the user must do:
1. Review pending approvals.
2. Approve or reject requests.
3. Monitor the status of approvals.
4. Provide feedback on requests if necessary.
5. Access historical approval data for analysis.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in approval times.
2. High number of rejected requests.
3. Frequent errors or issues during the approval process.
4. Lack of feedback from users on the approval process.
5. Inconsistent data or discrepancies in historical approval records.

What is needed on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of pending approvals with timestamps.
2. Statistics on approval times and rejection rates.
3. Notifications for overdue approvals.
4. Quick access to historical approval data.
5. Feedback section for users to report issues or suggestions.
