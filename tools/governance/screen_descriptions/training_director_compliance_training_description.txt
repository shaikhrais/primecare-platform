User Tasks:
1. Monitor compliance training completion rates.
2. Review training materials and updates.
3. Analyze user feedback on training effectiveness.
4. Ensure all training modules are up-to-date.
5. Generate reports on training compliance.
6. Address any issues or concerns raised by users.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low completion rates for training modules.
2. Frequent user complaints about training content.
3. Delays in updating training materials.
4. High number of errors or issues reported during training.
5. Lack of engagement or participation in training sessions.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of training completion rates (percentage and numbers).
2. List of upcoming training deadlines.
3. User feedback summary and trends.
4. Alerts for any compliance issues or overdue training.
5. Access to training materials and updates.
6. Reporting tools for generating compliance reports.
