Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Performance Monitoring

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining Revenue Trends
2. Increased Operating Costs
3. Cash Flow Issues
4. High Employee Turnover
5. Compliance Violations
6. Inaccurate Financial Reporting
7. Poor Risk Management Practices
8. Lack of Strategic Alignment
9. Inefficient Processes
10. Low Employee Engagement

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
   - Revenue Growth Rate
   - Profit Margins
   - Operating Expenses
   - Cash Flow Metrics
   - Return on Investment (ROI)
2. Compliance Status
3. Audit Logs and Findings
4. Budget vs. Actual Performance
5. Forecast Accuracy
6. Risk Assessment Metrics
7. Operational Efficiency Metrics
8. Stakeholder Feedback
9. Market Trends and Analysis
10. Real-time Financial Data Visualization
