Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations of the franchise.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and standards.
3. Manage staff recruitment, training, and performance.
4. Monitor financial performance and manage budgets.
5. Implement marketing strategies to drive sales.
6. Maintain customer satisfaction and handle complaints.
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments of operations.
8. Communicate with the franchisor and attend training sessions.
9. Manage inventory and supply chain logistics.
10. Develop and maintain relationships with local businesses and community.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue trends.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Inconsistent adherence to franchise standards.
5. Poor inventory management leading to stockouts or excess.
6. Lack of engagement or communication with staff.
7. Failure to implement marketing initiatives.
8. Unresolved operational issues or inefficiencies.
9. Inadequate financial reporting or budgeting practices.
10. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.

Dashboard Needs for Franchise Owner:
1. Sales performance metrics (daily, weekly, monthly).
2. Employee performance and turnover statistics.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
4. Inventory levels and supply chain status.
5. Financial reports (profit and loss, budget vs. actual).
6. Marketing campaign performance analytics.
7. Compliance checklists and audit results.
8. Operational efficiency indicators.
9. Alerts for any red flags or issues needing attention.
10. Communication tools for staff and franchisor engagement.
