List of work for the user in the HR Onboarding screen:

1. View the list of applicants with their details (name, role, stage, compliance score, contact information).
2. Filter applicants by different stages (All Applicants, Interviewing, Background check, Hired Roster).
3. Select an applicant to view more details and manage their onboarding process.
4. Toggle checklist items for each applicant (e.g., CPR certification, criminal record check).
5. Promote an applicant to the roster once they meet all compliance requirements.

Red flags of operation that may indicate inefficiencies:

1. Low compliance scores (below a certain threshold) for applicants, indicating potential issues with their onboarding process.
2. A high number of applicants stuck in the same stage for an extended period, suggesting bottlenecks in the hiring process.
3. Frequent toggling of checklist items without progress, indicating confusion or lack of clarity in the onboarding requirements.
4. Delays in promoting applicants to the roster, which could affect staffing and operational capacity.

Dashboard needs for the user:

1. Overview of the total number of applicants and their distribution across different stages.
2. Visual representation of compliance scores for each applicant, highlighting those who need attention.
3. Alerts or notifications for applicants who have been in a stage for too long or have low compliance scores.
4. Quick access buttons for common actions (e.g., promote to roster, view details).
5. Summary statistics on the onboarding process, such as average time spent in each stage and the number of applicants per stage.
