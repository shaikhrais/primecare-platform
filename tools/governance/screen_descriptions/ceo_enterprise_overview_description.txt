List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor enterprise performance metrics.
2. Review operational efficiency indicators.
3. Analyze financial reports and trends.
4. Evaluate team performance and productivity.
5. Identify areas for improvement and growth.
6. Make strategic decisions based on data insights.
7. Communicate with stakeholders about enterprise status.

Red flags of operation:
1. Declining performance metrics over time.
2. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
3. Low team productivity or high turnover rates.
4. Frequent errors or issues reported in operations.
5. Negative feedback from stakeholders or customers.
6. Inconsistent data reporting or analysis results.
7. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Visualizations of financial trends (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for any operational red flags.
4. Team performance statistics and feedback.
5. Summary of recent operational issues and resolutions.
6. Strategic recommendations based on data analysis.
7. Communication tools for stakeholder engagement.
