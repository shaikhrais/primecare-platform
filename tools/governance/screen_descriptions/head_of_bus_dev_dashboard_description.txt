Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify new market opportunities and potential clients.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders.
4. Conduct market research and competitive analysis.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align strategies.
6. Monitor industry trends and adjust strategies accordingly.
7. Prepare and present business proposals to potential clients.
8. Negotiate contracts and agreements with clients.
9. Track and report on business development metrics and KPIs.
10. Lead and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High client churn rate.
3. Lack of communication within the team.
4. Missed deadlines on proposals or projects.
5. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
6. Inconsistent tracking of performance metrics.
7. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
8. Failure to adapt to market changes.
9. Poor collaboration with other departments.
10. Lack of clear goals and objectives.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue.
2. Client acquisition and retention metrics.
3. Market analysis and trends overview.
4. Active opportunities and pipeline status.
5. Team performance and productivity metrics.
6. Recent client feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Compliance and audit logs.
8. Notifications for upcoming deadlines and tasks.
9. Visual representation of sales trends over time.
10. Access to relevant market research data.
