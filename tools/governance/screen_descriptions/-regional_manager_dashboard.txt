List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor regional performance metrics.
2. Analyze sales data and trends.
3. Review team performance and productivity.
4. Identify areas for improvement in operations.
5. Communicate with team members and stakeholders.
6. Set goals and objectives for the region.
7. Prepare reports for upper management.
8. Implement strategies to enhance regional performance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Declining sales figures over consecutive months.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Consistent delays in project timelines.
4. Negative customer feedback or complaints.
5. Low engagement levels among team members.
6. Failure to meet set targets or KPIs.
7. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics.
2. Visualizations of trends and forecasts.
3. Team performance indicators and metrics.
4. Alerts for any red flags or issues.
5. A section for customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Goal tracking and progress indicators.
7. Quick access to reports and analytics tools.
8. Communication tools for team collaboration.
