List of work the patient must do:
1. Review billing statements and ensure accuracy.
2. Provide any necessary documentation for billing inquiries.
3. Respond to any requests for additional information from the billing department.
4. Attend any scheduled appointments related to billing disputes or clarifications.

Red flags of operation:
1. Unexplained charges on billing statements.
2. Delays in receiving billing statements or responses to inquiries.
3. Frequent errors in billing that require correction.
4. Lack of transparency in billing processes or communication from the billing department.

Effectiveness of work:
1. Timely resolution of billing inquiries.
2. Accurate billing statements with minimal errors.
3. Clear communication between patients and the billing department.
4. Efficient processing of payments and claims.

Dashboard needs for the patient:
1. Overview of current billing status and outstanding balances.
2. Access to detailed billing statements and transaction history.
3. Notifications for any updates or changes in billing.
4. A section for logging inquiries or disputes related to billing.
5. Metrics on the average time taken to resolve billing issues.
