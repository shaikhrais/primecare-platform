List of tasks a Scrum Master must do:
1. Facilitate Scrum ceremonies (Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective).
2. Remove impediments for the team.
3. Coach the team on Agile principles and practices.
4. Ensure effective communication within the team and with stakeholders.
5. Monitor team progress and performance metrics.
6. Foster a culture of continuous improvement.
7. Support product owner in managing the backlog.
8. Help the team maintain focus on their goals.
9. Conduct training sessions for team members on Agile methodologies.
10. Ensure adherence to Scrum processes and practices.

Red flags of operation indicating ineffective work:
1. Frequent missed deadlines or sprint goals.
2. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
3. Lack of communication or collaboration within the team.
4. Increased number of impediments that are not being resolved.
5. Poor quality of deliverables or high defect rates.
6. Resistance to change or lack of engagement in Scrum ceremonies.
7. Over-reliance on the Scrum Master for decision-making.
8. Inconsistent attendance in Scrum ceremonies.
9. Lack of transparency in progress tracking.
10. Team members not taking ownership of their tasks.

Key elements needed on the Scrum Master dashboard:
1. Current sprint progress (burndown chart).
2. Team velocity metrics.
3. List of impediments and their status.
4. Upcoming Scrum ceremonies and attendance.
5. Team member workload and task assignments.
6. Quality metrics (defect rates, code review status).
7. Feedback from sprint retrospectives.
8. Compliance audit results and logs.
9. Historical performance data for trend analysis.
10. Notifications for upcoming deadlines or important events.
