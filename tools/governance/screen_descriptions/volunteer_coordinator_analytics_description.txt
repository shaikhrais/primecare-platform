List of work volunteers must do:
1. Attend scheduled training sessions.
2. Participate in community outreach programs.
3. Assist in organizing events and activities.
4. Provide support to staff and other volunteers.
5. Maintain communication with the volunteer coordinator.
6. Complete necessary paperwork and documentation.
7. Report any issues or concerns promptly.

Red flags of operation:
1. Lack of communication from volunteers.
2. Frequent absenteeism or tardiness.
3. Negative feedback from community members or staff.
4. Incomplete or missing documentation.
5. Disengagement or lack of enthusiasm during activities.
6. Failure to follow safety protocols.
7. Conflicts between volunteers or with staff.

Dashboard needs for volunteers:
1. Volunteer attendance tracking.
2. Event participation statistics.
3. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from community members.
4. Task completion status.
5. Communication logs between volunteers and coordinators.
6. Alerts for upcoming training or events.
7. Recognition and rewards tracking for volunteer contributions.
