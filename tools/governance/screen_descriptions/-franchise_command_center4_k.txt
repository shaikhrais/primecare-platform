Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor daily operations and performance metrics.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and regulations.
3. Conduct regular audits and assessments of franchise operations.
4. Manage staff training and development.
5. Oversee marketing and promotional activities.
6. Maintain financial records and manage budgets.
7. Address customer feedback and service quality issues.
8. Implement operational improvements and best practices.
9. Communicate with the franchisor and report on performance.
10. Participate in franchisee meetings and training sessions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue trends.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative reviews.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Inconsistent inventory management.
6. Poor financial management or cash flow issues.
7. Lack of engagement or motivation among staff.
8. Failure to meet franchise standards or guidelines.
9. Ineffective marketing strategies leading to low customer acquisition.
10. Unresolved operational issues or recurring problems.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time performance metrics (sales, customer satisfaction, etc.).
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Employee performance and training progress.
4. Financial overview (expenses, revenue, profit margins).
5. Customer feedback and complaint tracking.
6. Inventory levels and management alerts.
7. Marketing campaign performance metrics.
8. Operational logs and incident reports.
9. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
10. Tools for communication with the franchisor and other franchisees.
