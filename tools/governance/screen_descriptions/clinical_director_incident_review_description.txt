Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits and reviews of clinical practices.
3. Manage incident reports and ensure timely resolution.
4. Coordinate with other departments to enhance patient care.
5. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) related to clinical outcomes.
6. Facilitate training and development for clinical staff.
7. Implement and review clinical policies and procedures.
8. Engage in strategic planning for clinical services.
9. Ensure effective communication within the clinical team.
10. Address any operational issues that arise promptly.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Increased number of incident reports or complaints.
2. Decline in patient satisfaction scores.
3. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
4. High staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
5. Delays in incident resolution or audit processes.
6. Inconsistent reporting of clinical outcomes.
7. Lack of engagement or communication among clinical staff.
8. Unresolved issues from previous audits or reviews.
9. Poor performance on key metrics or KPIs.
10. Failure to implement recommended changes from audits.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Real-time monitoring of incident reports and resolutions.
2. Overview of compliance status and audit results.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to clinical outcomes.
4. Staff performance metrics and satisfaction levels.
5. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
6. Operational efficiency metrics (e.g., wait times, service delivery).
7. Logs of recent activities and audits conducted.
8. Alerts for any compliance issues or operational red flags.
9. Visualizations of trends in clinical performance over time.
10. Access to training and development resources for staff.
