List of tasks a physician must perform:
1. Monitor patient health metrics.
2. Review patient compliance with treatment plans.
3. Analyze operational logs for anomalies.
4. Execute quality verification sweeps.
5. Maintain communication with healthcare teams.
6. Update patient records and documentation.
7. Participate in continuous education and training.
8. Ensure adherence to regulatory standards.

Red flags for operations:
1. High number of exceptions flagged in compliance checks.
2. Delays in patient treatment or follow-up.
3. Inconsistent patient health metrics.
4. Network or API errors during data retrieval.
5. Lack of synchronization in operational logs.
6. Unusual trends in telemetry data.

Effective work strategies:
1. Regularly review and update dashboards.
2. Utilize automated alerts for compliance issues.
3. Foster collaboration among healthcare teams.
4. Implement feedback loops for continuous improvement.
5. Prioritize patient-centered care in decision-making.

Dashboard needs for physicians:
1. Real-time health metrics display.
2. Compliance status indicators.
3. Operational logs and alerts.
4. Telemetry charts for trend analysis.
5. Quick access to patient records.
6. Task execution buttons for operational workflows.
7. Log history for accountability and review.
