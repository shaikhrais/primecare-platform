List of tasks for Regional BDM:
1. Monitor active operations and ensure optimal productivity.
2. Conduct compliance audits and scans regularly.
3. Synchronize security posture and update policies as needed.
4. Export and manage operational audit logs.
5. Review key performance indicators and telemetry data.
6. Address any governance required actions promptly.
7. Maintain communication with team members and stakeholders.
8. Analyze trends and make data-driven decisions.

Red flags of operation:
1. Decrease in active operations or productivity.
2. Security clearance issues or exceptions logged.
3. Delays in compliance audits or scans.
4. Inconsistent telemetry data or performance metrics.
5. Lack of timely updates on policies or security posture.
6. Unresolved governance actions or issues.
7. Negative trends in operational metrics.

Dashboard requirements for Regional BDM:
1. Display active operations and productivity metrics.
2. Show security clearance status and exceptions.
3. Provide real-time telemetry data and trends.
4. Include logs of compliance audits and operational activities.
5. Allow for manual refresh and synchronization actions.
6. Highlight any governance actions that need attention.
7. Enable export functionality for audit logs and reports.
