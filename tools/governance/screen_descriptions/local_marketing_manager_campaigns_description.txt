List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor campaign performance metrics.
2. Create and manage local marketing campaigns.
3. Analyze customer engagement and feedback.
4. Adjust marketing strategies based on data insights.
5. Collaborate with local businesses for promotions.
6. Report on campaign outcomes and effectiveness.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low engagement rates on campaigns.
2. High customer churn or dissatisfaction.
3. Delays in campaign launches or updates.
4. Inconsistent data reporting or analytics.
5. Lack of collaboration with local businesses.
6. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.

What we need on the dashboard for the user:
1. Campaign performance metrics (click-through rates, conversion rates).
2. Engagement statistics (likes, shares, comments).
3. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
4. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
5. Timeline for upcoming campaigns and deadlines.
6. Alerts for any red flags or issues that need immediate attention.
