Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations of the franchise.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and regulations.
3. Manage staff recruitment, training, and performance.
4. Monitor financial performance and manage budgets.
5. Implement marketing strategies to promote the franchise.
6. Maintain customer service standards and address customer complaints.
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments of operations.
8. Communicate with the franchisor and report on performance metrics.
9. Manage inventory and supply chain logistics.
10. Ensure adherence to health and safety regulations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Consistent negative customer feedback or complaints.
3. Declining sales or revenue trends.
4. Frequent operational errors or compliance issues.
5. Lack of engagement or motivation among staff.
6. Inconsistent adherence to brand standards.
7. Poor inventory management leading to stockouts or excess stock.
8. Failure to meet financial targets or budget overruns.
9. Delays in reporting or communication with the franchisor.
10. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales, expenses, and profits.
2. Staff performance metrics and training progress.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback summaries.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Inventory levels and turnover rates.
6. Operational logs and incident reports.
7. Marketing campaign performance and ROI.
8. Financial health indicators (cash flow, profit margins).
9. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
10. Real-time data visualization for quick decision-making.
