User Work List:
1. Review quality assurance reports.
2. Analyze data trends and patterns.
3. Identify areas for improvement in quality processes.
4. Collaborate with team members to address quality issues.
5. Document findings and recommendations.
6. Monitor the implementation of quality improvements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors or bugs reported in the quality assurance reports.
2. Delays in report generation or data retrieval.
3. Lack of communication among team members regarding quality issues.
4. Inconsistent data or discrepancies in reports.
5. Low engagement or feedback from users on quality processes.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of key quality metrics (e.g., defect rates, test coverage).
2. Real-time status of ongoing quality assurance activities.
3. Alerts for critical issues or anomalies in quality data.
4. Historical data trends for comparison and analysis.
5. User feedback section for continuous improvement suggestions.
