Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
3. Monitor operational performance and risk management.
4. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
5. Maintain documentation and records of governance activities.
6. Provide training and support on governance issues.
7. Analyze data and generate reports for decision-making.
8. Implement governance frameworks and best practices.
9. Address and resolve governance-related issues.
10. Stay updated on changes in laws and regulations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit processes or reporting.
3. Lack of communication or transparency among teams.
4. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
5. Inconsistent application of policies and procedures.
6. Negative feedback from stakeholders or employees.
7. Failure to meet regulatory deadlines.
8. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
9. Lack of training or awareness among staff.
10. Incomplete or inaccurate documentation.

Dashboard Requirements for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance status overview (pass/fail rates).
2. Audit logs and findings summary.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to governance.
4. Real-time alerts for compliance issues.
5. Historical data trends for audits and compliance.
6. Stakeholder feedback and satisfaction metrics.
7. Risk assessment and management reports.
8. Training and development progress tracking.
9. Operational performance metrics.
10. Action items and follow-up status from previous meetings.
