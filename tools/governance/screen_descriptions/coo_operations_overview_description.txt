Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Foster a positive company culture and employee engagement.
7. Collaborate with other executives to align operational goals with business strategy.
8. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
9. Manage risk and ensure business continuity.
10. Communicate operational updates to stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased productivity or efficiency metrics.
2. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
3. High employee turnover or low morale.
4. Frequent compliance issues or regulatory violations.
5. Poor customer feedback or service complaints.
6. Lack of clear communication within teams.
7. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
8. Inconsistent performance across departments.
9. Unresolved conflicts or issues within teams.
10. Failure to meet strategic goals or objectives.

Dashboard Requirements for COO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) overview.
2. Operational efficiency metrics.
3. Financial performance indicators (revenue, costs, profit margins).
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Employee engagement and satisfaction scores.
6. Customer feedback and service metrics.
7. Risk management status and issues.
8. Project timelines and deliverable status.
9. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
10. Real-time operational logs and alerts.
