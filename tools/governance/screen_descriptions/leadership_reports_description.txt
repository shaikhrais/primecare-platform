List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor leadership reports for insights and performance metrics.
2. Analyze data presented on the Leadership Reports screen.
3. Identify trends and areas for improvement based on the reports.
4. Collaborate with team members to discuss findings from the reports.
5. Provide feedback or suggestions based on the report data.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent loading errors indicating issues with data retrieval.
2. Inconsistent or missing data in the reports.
3. Slow response times when accessing the Leadership Reports screen.
4. Lack of user engagement or interaction with the reports.
5. Negative feedback from users regarding the usability of the reports.

What is needed on the dashboard of the user:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to leadership metrics.
2. Visual representations of data (charts, graphs) for easier analysis.
3. Alerts or notifications for significant changes in data trends.
4. A summary section highlighting critical insights or recommendations.
5. User-friendly navigation to access different sections of the reports easily.
