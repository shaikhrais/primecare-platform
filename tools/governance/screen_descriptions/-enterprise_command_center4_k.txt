Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Responsibilities:
1. Set and communicate the organization's vision and mission.
2. Develop and implement strategic plans.
3. Oversee company operations and resource management.
4. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations.
5. Build and maintain relationships with stakeholders.
6. Monitor industry trends and adjust strategies accordingly.
7. Lead and motivate the executive team.
8. Manage the company's financial performance.
9. Represent the company in public and at industry events.
10. Foster a positive corporate culture.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining financial performance or profitability.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Poor customer feedback or declining sales.
4. Compliance issues or legal challenges.
5. Lack of innovation or stagnation in product development.
6. Ineffective communication within the organization.
7. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
8. Failure to meet strategic goals or milestones.
9. Negative media coverage or public relations issues.
10. Inconsistent performance metrics across departments.

Dashboard Requirements for CEO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial performance.
2. Employee satisfaction and turnover rates.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Operational efficiency metrics.
6. Sales performance and market trends.
7. Innovation metrics (e.g., new product development timelines).
8. Real-time data on industry trends and competitive analysis.
9. Visualizations of financial forecasts and budgets.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or compliance issues.
