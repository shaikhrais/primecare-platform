Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Document dressing changes.
2. Administer vaccines and maintain immunization logs.
3. Monitor patient conditions and report changes.
4. Provide patient education and support.
5. Collaborate with healthcare teams.
6. Maintain accurate patient records.
7. Ensure compliance with health regulations and protocols.
8. Assist in patient assessments and care planning.
9. Manage medication reminders and verifications.
10. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Delays in logging patient care activities.
2. Incomplete or inaccurate documentation.
3. High rates of patient complaints or adverse events.
4. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
5. Lack of communication among team members.
6. Inconsistent patient monitoring and follow-up.
7. Failure to adhere to medication administration guidelines.
8. Insufficient training or knowledge gaps among staff.
9. Overwhelming workload leading to burnout.
10. Poor patient outcomes or satisfaction scores.

Dashboard Requirements for RPN:
1. Overview of pending and completed dressing logs.
2. Immunization log summary with recent entries.
3. Alerts for overdue vaccinations or follow-ups.
4. Patient feedback and satisfaction metrics.
5. Compliance tracking for protocols and guidelines.
6. Performance metrics for individual RPNs.
7. Notifications for training and development opportunities.
8. Access to patient education resources.
9. Real-time updates on patient conditions.
10. Integration with electronic health records for seamless data entry.
