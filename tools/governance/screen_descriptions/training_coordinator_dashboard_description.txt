List of work for Training Candidates:
1. Attend training sessions.
2. Complete assigned training modules.
3. Participate in compliance audits.
4. Engage in practical exercises.
5. Provide feedback on training effectiveness.
6. Maintain training logs and documentation.
7. Collaborate with peers for group activities.
8. Update personal training progress on the dashboard.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of unresolved compliance issues.
2. Frequent system errors or downtime.
3. Low engagement in training sessions.
4. Inconsistent training log entries.
5. Delays in completing training modules.
6. Negative feedback from participants.
7. Lack of updates on training progress.

Dashboard needs for Training Candidates:
1. Overview of training progress and completion rates.
2. Real-time updates on compliance status.
3. Access to training materials and resources.
4. Summary of feedback from training sessions.
5. Notifications for upcoming training events.
6. Visual representation of performance metrics.
7. Logs of completed training activities and audits.
