List of tasks Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) must perform:
1. Conduct wound assessments and document findings.
2. Monitor and log vital signs.
3. Administer immunizations and track immunization status.
4. Update wound care plans based on patient progress.
5. Communicate with patients regarding their care and treatment plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare teams to ensure comprehensive patient care.
7. Educate patients and families about wound care and prevention.
8. Maintain accurate and up-to-date patient records.
9. Report any changes in patient conditions to the supervising nurse or physician.
10. Participate in quality improvement initiatives related to patient care.

Red flags of operation for RPN effectiveness:
1. High number of unhealed or deteriorating wounds.
2. Low compliance rates in vital signs logging.
3. Decreased immunization rates compared to targets.
4. Frequent patient complaints regarding care or communication.
5. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
6. Delays in dressing changes or wound assessments.
7. Lack of collaboration or communication with the healthcare team.
8. Increased incidence of pressure injuries or complications.

Dashboard needs for Registered Practical Nurse (RPN):
1. Active wound cases count.
2. Vitals logging compliance percentage.
3. Total immunizations administered.
4. Immunization status breakdown by type.
5. Wound assessment details including patient name, location, stage, status, dimensions, and dressing age.
6. Alerts for overdue dressing changes or assessments.
7. Patient education resources and materials.
8. Performance metrics and trends over time for quality improvement.
9. Communication tools for team collaboration.
10. Access to patient records and history for informed decision-making.
