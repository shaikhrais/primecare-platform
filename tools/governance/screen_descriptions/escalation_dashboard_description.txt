List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor escalation cases and their statuses.
2. Review metrics related to escalations.
3. Identify trends in escalations over time.
4. Respond to escalations in a timely manner.
5. Collaborate with team members on unresolved escalations.
6. Provide feedback on the escalation process for continuous improvement.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased number of escalations over a specific period.
2. Delays in response times to escalations.
3. High volume of escalations from specific teams or departments.
4. Recurring issues that lead to escalations.
5. Lack of follow-up on resolved escalations.

What is needed on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of current escalations with status indicators.
2. Metrics on response times and resolution times.
3. Historical data on escalations to identify trends.
4. Notifications for new escalations and updates on existing ones.
5. A section for team collaboration and comments on escalations.
6. A feedback mechanism for users to suggest improvements to the escalation process.
