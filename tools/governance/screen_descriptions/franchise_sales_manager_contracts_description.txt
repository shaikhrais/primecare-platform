List of work the user must do:
1. Review and manage franchise contracts.
2. Monitor contract statuses and updates.
3. Communicate with franchisees regarding contract details.
4. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements.
5. Analyze contract performance and metrics.
6. Update contract templates as necessary.
7. Collaborate with legal and finance teams for contract-related issues.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in contract approvals or renewals.
2. High number of contract disputes or complaints from franchisees.
3. Inconsistent contract terms across different franchises.
4. Lack of communication or feedback from franchisees.
5. Missing or incomplete documentation related to contracts.
6. Frequent changes in contract terms without proper notification.

What is needed on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of active contracts and their statuses.
2. Alerts for contracts nearing expiration or requiring action.
3. Performance metrics of franchises based on contract compliance.
4. Communication logs with franchisees regarding contracts.
5. Quick access to contract templates and legal resources.
6. Summary of any ongoing disputes or issues related to contracts.
7. Visual representation of contract timelines and milestones.
