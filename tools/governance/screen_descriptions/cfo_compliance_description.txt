Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Regulatory Oversight
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Inconsistent Financial Reporting
2. High Levels of Debt
3. Cash Flow Issues
4. Regulatory Non-Compliance
5. Poor Budget Management
6. Lack of Financial Controls
7. Unexplained Variances in Financial Statements
8. Declining Profit Margins
9. Ineffective Risk Management Practices
10. High Employee Turnover in Finance Team

Dashboard Needs for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Overview
2. Financial Health Metrics (e.g., liquidity ratios, profitability ratios)
3. Budget vs. Actual Performance
4. Cash Flow Projections
5. Compliance Status Indicators
6. Risk Management Dashboard
7. Audit Trail and Logs
8. Investment Performance Metrics
9. Revenue and Expense Trends
10. Stakeholder Engagement Metrics
