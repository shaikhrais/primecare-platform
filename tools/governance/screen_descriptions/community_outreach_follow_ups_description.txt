List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor community outreach follow-ups.
2. Review the status of outreach initiatives.
3. Analyze feedback from community members.
4. Update follow-up records as necessary.
5. Coordinate with team members for outreach efforts.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in follow-up responses from community members.
2. Low engagement rates in outreach initiatives.
3. Inconsistent updates to follow-up records.
4. Negative feedback trends from community members.
5. Lack of communication among team members.

What we need on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of ongoing outreach initiatives.
2. Status indicators for follow-ups (e.g., pending, completed).
3. Engagement metrics (e.g., response rates, feedback scores).
4. Notifications for overdue follow-ups.
5. Team collaboration tools or updates.
