List of tasks for HR Director:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and conflict resolution.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Conduct performance management and employee evaluations.
6. Design and implement training and development programs.
7. Monitor employee engagement and satisfaction.
8. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
9. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.
11. Develop succession planning strategies.
12. Collaborate with other departments to align HR strategies with business goals.

Red flags of operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Frequent complaints or grievances.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Inconsistent performance evaluations.
7. Lack of training and development opportunities.
8. Poor communication between HR and other departments.
9. Negative feedback from exit interviews.
10. Insufficient diversity in hiring practices.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for recruitment and retention.
2. Employee engagement and satisfaction metrics.
3. Compliance status with labor laws.
4. Training and development participation rates.
5. Diversity and inclusion statistics.
6. Performance management overview.
7. Compensation and benefits analysis.
8. Real-time operational audit logs.
9. Alerts for any compliance issues or grievances.
10. Visualizations of HR metrics trends over time.
