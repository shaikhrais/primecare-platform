Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
3. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, etc.).
4. Screen resumes and applications.
5. Conduct interviews and assessments.
6. Manage the offer process and negotiate salaries.
7. Maintain relationships with candidates throughout the hiring process.
8. Track and analyze recruitment metrics.
9. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
10. Enhance employer branding and promote company culture.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions.
3. Poor candidate experience feedback.
4. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
5. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
6. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
7. Frequent changes in hiring requirements.
8. Low acceptance rates for job offers.
9. Inadequate tracking of recruitment metrics.
10. Unclear roles and responsibilities within the recruitment team.

Dashboard Needs for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Recruitment metrics (time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, source of hire).
2. Candidate pipeline status (number of candidates at each stage).
3. Diversity metrics (gender, ethnicity, etc.).
4. Offer acceptance rates.
5. Candidate experience feedback scores.
6. Active job postings and their status.
7. Compliance status and audit logs.
8. Recruitment budget tracking.
9. Team performance metrics.
10. Upcoming recruitment events or job fairs.
