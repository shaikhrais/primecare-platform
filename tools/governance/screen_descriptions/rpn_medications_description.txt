Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
3. Assist in patient assessments and care planning.
4. Provide patient education regarding medications and treatments.
5. Document patient care and medication administration accurately.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
7. Maintain a safe and clean environment for patients.
8. Respond to patient needs and concerns promptly.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Stay updated on best practices and changes in healthcare regulations.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased medication errors.
2. Unexplained changes in patient conditions.
3. High rates of patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Delays in medication administration.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Failure to follow infection control protocols.
8. Inadequate patient education leading to non-compliance.
9. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
10. Insufficient staffing levels impacting patient care.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Real-time medication administration logs.
2. Patient vital signs monitoring dashboard.
3. Compliance audit results and alerts.
4. Patient satisfaction metrics.
5. Incident reporting and tracking system.
6. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
7. Training and certification status of staff.
8. Access to clinical guidelines and protocols.
9. Communication tools for team collaboration.
10. Performance metrics for quality improvement initiatives.
