Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess governance frameworks.
3. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
4. Conduct risk assessments and manage risks.
5. Develop and implement governance strategies.
6. Provide training and support on governance issues.
7. Maintain accurate records and documentation.
8. Prepare reports for management and stakeholders.
9. Oversee audits and compliance checks.
10. Engage in continuous improvement of governance processes.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent non-compliance incidents.
2. Lack of clear communication among stakeholders.
3. Inconsistent documentation practices.
4. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
5. Delays in decision-making processes.
6. Insufficient training on governance policies.
7. Unresolved audit findings.
8. Increased risk exposure without mitigation plans.
9. Low engagement in governance initiatives.
10. Poor stakeholder feedback on governance effectiveness.

Dashboard Needs for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance status indicators.
2. Risk assessment metrics.
3. Stakeholder communication logs.
4. Audit findings and resolutions tracker.
5. Training completion rates.
6. Governance strategy implementation progress.
7. Incident reporting and resolution timelines.
8. Performance metrics for governance initiatives.
9. Document management and version control.
10. Feedback mechanisms from stakeholders.
