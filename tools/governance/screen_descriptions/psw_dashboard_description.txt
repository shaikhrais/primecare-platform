List of tasks Personal Support Workers (PSWs) must do:
1. Check-in and check-out for shifts.
2. Complete Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) for residents.
3. Log incidents and care activities.
4. Monitor and respond to safety alerts.
5. Communicate with clinical staff regarding resident care.
6. Trigger emergency alerts when necessary.
7. Participate in compliance audits and ensure adherence to standards.
8. Maintain accurate documentation of care provided.

Red flags of operation for effective work:
1. High number of active alerts indicating potential safety issues.
2. Low completion rate of tasks compared to total assigned tasks.
3. Frequent check-outs without proper documentation.
4. Delays in logging incidents or care activities.
5. Lack of communication or updates in logs.
6. Emergency alerts triggered frequently without resolution.
7. Non-compliance with care standards during audits.

Dashboard needs for Personal Support Workers (PSWs):
1. Shift status indicator (checked in or off-duty).
2. Progress tracker for ADL tasks (completed vs. total).
3. Active wing information for care assignments.
4. Safety alerts count and status.
5. Logs of recent activities and incidents.
6. Emergency alert button for immediate action.
7. Compliance audit results and reminders.
8. Refresh button for manual updates.
