List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) must perform:
1. Conduct patient assessments.
2. Develop treatment plans.
3. Perform massage therapy techniques.
4. Document SOAP notes for each session.
5. Schedule and manage appointments.
6. Communicate with patients about their treatment.
7. Maintain cleanliness and organization of the therapy space.
8. Stay updated on industry standards and practices.
9. Handle billing and insurance claims.
10. Participate in continuing education and training.

Red flags of operation for RMTs:
1. High cancellation or no-show rates.
2. Frequent patient complaints or negative feedback.
3. Inconsistent documentation of treatments.
4. Delays in submitting insurance claims.
5. Lack of compliance with health and safety regulations.
6. Poor communication with patients or staff.
7. Inability to manage appointment schedules effectively.
8. Signs of burnout or fatigue in the therapist.
9. Failure to keep up with continuing education requirements.
10. Inadequate record-keeping practices.

Dashboard requirements for Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs):
1. Overview of active appointments and their statuses.
2. Log of recent activities and compliance checks.
3. Metrics on patient satisfaction and feedback.
4. Alerts for upcoming appointments and follow-ups.
5. Summary of submitted SOAP notes and treatment plans.
6. Insurance claim submission status.
7. Performance metrics (e.g., number of treatments performed).
8. Compliance audit results and action items.
9. Access to patient history and treatment records.
10. Notifications for required training or certifications.
