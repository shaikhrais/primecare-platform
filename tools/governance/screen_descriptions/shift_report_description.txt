Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health problems and needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
4. Monitor patient progress and response to treatments.
5. Educate patients and their families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to provide optimal patient care.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Provide emotional support and counseling to patients and families.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of medication errors or adverse events.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols or regulations.
7. Poor communication among healthcare team members.
8. Lack of timely response to patient needs or emergencies.
9. Inadequate staffing levels leading to burnout.
10. Failure to meet quality metrics or performance indicators.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Patient health status overview (vital signs, alerts).
2. Compliance metrics (medication administration, documentation).
3. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
4. Staffing levels and workload distribution.
5. Incident reports and safety alerts.
6. Performance indicators (medication errors, readmission rates).
7. Access to care plans and treatment protocols.
8. Communication tools for team collaboration.
9. Training and education resources.
10. Real-time updates on patient assignments and responsibilities.
