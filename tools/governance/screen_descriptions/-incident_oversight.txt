Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Manage incident oversight and response protocols.
3. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
4. Review and analyze operational audit logs.
5. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics.
6. Facilitate communication between clinical staff and administration.
7. Implement improvements based on audit findings and performance metrics.
8. Ensure staff training and adherence to clinical protocols.
9. Address any incidents or issues that arise in clinical operations.
10. Maintain a focus on patient safety and quality of care.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased number of incidents or near misses reported.
2. Delays in compliance scan results or audit findings.
3. Low compliance rates with established protocols.
4. High turnover rates among clinical staff.
5. Negative trends in key performance indicators.
6. Frequent errors in patient care or documentation.
7. Lack of timely communication regarding incidents or operational changes.
8. Inconsistent training or onboarding processes for new staff.
9. Insufficient resources or support for clinical staff.
10. Poor patient feedback or satisfaction scores.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Real-time incident reporting and oversight metrics.
2. Compliance scan results and audit findings.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking (e.g., patient outcomes, staff performance).
4. Operational audit logs with timestamps and action items.
5. Alerts for any red flags or areas needing immediate attention.
6. Visualizations of trends in incidents and compliance over time.
7. Staff training and certification status.
8. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
9. Patient satisfaction and feedback summaries.
10. Communication logs between clinical staff and administration.
