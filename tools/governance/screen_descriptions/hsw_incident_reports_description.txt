Home Support Worker Responsibilities:
1. Provide personal care to clients, including bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Assist with mobility and transfers.
3. Administer medications as prescribed.
4. Prepare meals and assist with feeding.
5. Perform light housekeeping tasks.
6. Provide companionship and emotional support.
7. Monitor and report changes in clients' health status.
8. Document care provided and any incidents or changes in condition.
9. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
10. Follow care plans and protocols established by healthcare providers.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent incidents or accidents reported.
2. Delays in reporting incidents or changes in client condition.
3. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
4. Lack of communication between team members.
5. High turnover rate of staff.
6. Complaints from clients or families regarding care quality.
7. Non-compliance with care plans or protocols.
8. Insufficient training or support for staff.

Dashboard Needs for Home Support Worker:
1. Incident reporting and tracking system.
2. Client health status updates and alerts.
3. Care plan access and compliance tracking.
4. Communication tools for team collaboration.
5. Training and resource access for staff development.
6. Performance metrics and reporting tools.
7. Feedback system for clients and families.
8. Scheduling and time management tools.
