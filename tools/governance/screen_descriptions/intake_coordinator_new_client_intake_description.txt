Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Recruit and onboard volunteers.
2. Train volunteers on roles and responsibilities.
3. Schedule volunteer shifts and manage availability.
4. Communicate with volunteers regarding updates and events.
5. Track volunteer hours and contributions.
6. Organize volunteer appreciation events.
7. Maintain volunteer records and databases.
8. Evaluate volunteer performance and provide feedback.
9. Collaborate with other departments for volunteer needs.
10. Ensure compliance with organizational policies and procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rate.
2. Lack of communication with volunteers.
3. Inconsistent scheduling leading to missed shifts.
4. Low volunteer engagement or participation.
5. Incomplete or inaccurate volunteer records.
6. Negative feedback from volunteers regarding their experience.
7. Insufficient training leading to confusion in roles.
8. Failure to recognize or appreciate volunteer contributions.
9. Over-reliance on a few key volunteers.
10. Difficulty in recruiting new volunteers.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Volunteer recruitment metrics (number of new volunteers).
2. Volunteer retention rates.
3. Shift scheduling overview (filled vs. unfilled shifts).
4. Volunteer engagement statistics (event participation).
5. Training completion rates for volunteers.
6. Hours logged by volunteers.
7. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from volunteers.
8. Upcoming events and volunteer opportunities.
9. Recognition and appreciation highlights.
10. Compliance and policy adherence status.
