List of work for the User:
1. Monitor local marketing performance metrics.
2. Analyze reports on marketing campaigns.
3. Identify trends and areas for improvement.
4. Collaborate with local teams for strategy adjustments.
5. Review customer feedback and engagement data.
6. Prepare presentations for stakeholders.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant drop in engagement metrics.
2. Negative customer feedback trends.
3. Delays in report generation or data availability.
4. Inconsistent data across different reports.
5. Low conversion rates from marketing campaigns.
6. High churn rates in customer retention.

Dashboard needs for the User:
1. Real-time performance metrics (e.g., engagement, conversion rates).
2. Visualizations of campaign effectiveness (e.g., graphs, charts).
3. Alerts for significant changes in key metrics.
4. Customer feedback summaries and sentiment analysis.
5. Comparison tools for different marketing strategies.
6. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
