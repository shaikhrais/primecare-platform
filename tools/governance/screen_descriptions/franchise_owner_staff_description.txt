Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor operational performance and compliance.
2. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
3. Review and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs).
4. Manage staff and ensure proper training and adherence to protocols.
5. Address any operational issues or red flags promptly.
6. Maintain communication with franchise headquarters.
7. Implement marketing strategies to boost sales and customer engagement.
8. Ensure customer satisfaction and handle complaints effectively.
9. Track financial performance and manage budgets.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and regulations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or customer footfall.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Inconsistent operational processes or protocols.
6. Poor inventory management leading to stockouts or excess.
7. Lack of engagement in staff training and development.
8. Unresolved operational issues or delays in response.
9. Failure to meet compliance audit standards.
10. Negative financial trends or budget overruns.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of active operations and performance metrics.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Real-time telemetry logs and operational updates.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) visualizations.
5. Alerts for any red flags or operational issues.
6. Staff performance and training status.
7. Financial performance summaries and budget tracking.
8. Customer satisfaction metrics and feedback summaries.
9. Marketing campaign performance and engagement metrics.
10. Historical data trends for sales and operational performance.
