Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Recruit and onboard volunteers.
2. Train volunteers on their roles and responsibilities.
3. Schedule volunteer shifts and manage availability.
4. Communicate with volunteers regarding updates and events.
5. Track volunteer hours and contributions.
6. Organize volunteer appreciation events.
7. Address volunteer concerns and feedback.
8. Collaborate with other departments to align volunteer efforts with organizational goals.
9. Maintain volunteer records and databases.
10. Evaluate volunteer programs and suggest improvements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rates.
2. Lack of communication with volunteers.
3. Inconsistent scheduling leading to gaps in coverage.
4. Low volunteer engagement or participation in events.
5. Negative feedback from volunteers regarding their experience.
6. Difficulty in recruiting volunteers for key roles.
7. Incomplete or inaccurate volunteer records.
8. Failure to recognize or appreciate volunteer contributions.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Volunteer recruitment metrics (number of new volunteers, retention rates).
2. Volunteer engagement statistics (event participation, feedback scores).
3. Shift scheduling overview (upcoming shifts, gaps in coverage).
4. Volunteer hours logged and contributions made.
5. Communication logs (messages sent, responses received).
6. Recognition and appreciation tracker (events held, volunteer awards).
7. Feedback and concerns tracker (issues raised, resolutions provided).
8. Program evaluation metrics (success rates, areas for improvement).
