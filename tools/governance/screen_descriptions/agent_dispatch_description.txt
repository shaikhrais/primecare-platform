Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess operational risks.
3. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
4. Maintain accurate logs of operational activities.
5. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
6. Provide training and support on governance policies.
7. Analyze data and generate reports on governance metrics.
8. Identify and address any governance-related issues.
9. Collaborate with other departments to ensure alignment with governance standards.
10. Stay updated on changes in laws and regulations affecting governance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased frequency of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit completion or reporting.
3. Lack of transparency in operational logs.
4. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
5. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding governance processes.
6. Inconsistent application of policies across departments.
7. Failure to address identified risks or issues.
8. Poor communication regarding governance changes or updates.
9. Low engagement in governance training sessions.
10. Unresolved discrepancies in operational data.

Dashboard needs for a Governance Officer:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Audit log summaries and trends.
3. Risk assessment metrics and alerts.
4. Performance metrics for governance initiatives.
5. Visualizations of operational data and trends.
6. Access to historical compliance scan results.
7. Notifications for upcoming audits or compliance deadlines.
8. Stakeholder feedback summaries.
9. Training participation rates and outcomes.
10. Integration with other operational dashboards for holistic insights.
