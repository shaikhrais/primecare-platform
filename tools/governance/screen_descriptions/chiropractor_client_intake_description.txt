List of work Chiropractor must do:
1. Conduct client intake assessments.
2. Review patient history and current health status.
3. Perform physical examinations.
4. Develop treatment plans based on assessments.
5. Educate patients about chiropractic care and wellness.
6. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
7. Document patient interactions and treatment outcomes.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals when necessary.
10. Maintain a clean and safe treatment environment.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent patient records or documentation.
2. High rate of patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent cancellations or no-shows by patients.
4. Lack of compliance with health regulations.
5. Unusual patterns in patient treatment outcomes.
6. Insufficient follow-up on patient progress.
7. Poor communication with patients or staff.
8. Inadequate training or knowledge of chiropractic practices.
9. Equipment malfunctions or safety hazards in the clinic.
10. Financial discrepancies or billing issues.

Dashboard needs for Chiropractor:
1. Overview of active operations and patient load.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Patient satisfaction metrics and feedback.
4. Treatment plan effectiveness and patient progress tracking.
5. Real-time telemetry data on clinic operations.
6. Security clearance status and any exceptions logged.
7. Alerts for any red flags or operational issues.
8. Access to patient history and treatment records.
9. Metrics on appointment scheduling and cancellations.
10. Tools for communication and collaboration with other healthcare providers.
