List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor training programs and their effectiveness.
2. Review feedback from training participants.
3. Analyze training completion rates.
4. Schedule and manage training sessions.
5. Collaborate with trainers and participants for improvements.
6. Access reports on training outcomes and performance metrics.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low training completion rates.
2. Negative feedback trends from participants.
3. High dropout rates during training sessions.
4. Inconsistent scheduling of training sessions.
5. Lack of engagement from participants.
6. Delays in accessing training reports or data.

What is needed on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of current training programs and their statuses.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for training effectiveness.
3. Feedback summary from participants.
4. Completion rates and trends over time.
5. Upcoming training sessions and schedules.
6. Alerts for any red flags identified in training operations.
