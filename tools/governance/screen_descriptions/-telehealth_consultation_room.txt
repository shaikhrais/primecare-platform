User Work List:
1. Access the Telehealth Consultation Room.
2. Initiate a telehealth consultation.
3. Communicate with healthcare providers via video/audio.
4. View patient information and medical history.
5. Schedule follow-up appointments.
6. Provide feedback on the consultation experience.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Inability to connect to video/audio during consultations.
2. Delays in loading patient information.
3. Frequent disconnections during sessions.
4. Poor audio/video quality affecting communication.
5. User interface issues that hinder navigation.
6. Lack of timely notifications for upcoming appointments.

Dashboard Requirements for User:
1. Real-time status of ongoing consultations.
2. Notifications for scheduled appointments and reminders.
3. Access to previous consultation records.
4. Feedback submission option for consultations.
5. Quick access to technical support or help resources.
6. Overview of upcoming appointments and follow-ups.
