Local Marketing Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement local marketing strategies.
2. Conduct market research to identify local trends and customer needs.
3. Manage local advertising campaigns across various channels.
4. Collaborate with sales teams to align marketing efforts with sales goals.
5. Monitor and analyze the performance of local marketing initiatives.
6. Build and maintain relationships with local businesses and community organizations.
7. Organize and participate in local events and promotions.
8. Manage social media presence and engage with local audiences.
9. Create and manage budgets for local marketing activities.
10. Report on marketing performance metrics and adjust strategies as needed.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining engagement rates on local marketing campaigns.
2. Inconsistent messaging across different marketing channels.
3. Lack of collaboration with sales teams leading to misalignment.
4. Poor performance metrics compared to local competitors.
5. Negative feedback from the community or local businesses.
6. High turnover in marketing staff affecting continuity.
7. Inadequate budget allocation for local marketing efforts.
8. Failure to adapt to changing local market conditions.

Dashboard Needs for Local Marketing Manager:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their performance metrics.
2. Real-time analytics on customer engagement and conversion rates.
3. Budget tracking for local marketing initiatives.
4. Insights from market research and customer feedback.
5. Social media engagement metrics and audience demographics.
6. Calendar of upcoming local events and promotions.
7. Collaboration tools for communication with sales and other teams.
8. Alerts for any significant changes in market trends or performance issues.
