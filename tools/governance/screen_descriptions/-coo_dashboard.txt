Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
3. Monitor performance metrics and operational productivity.
4. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
5. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
6. Lead and mentor operational teams.
7. Coordinate with other executives to align operations with company goals.
8. Analyze data to identify areas for improvement.
9. Facilitate communication between departments.
10. Conduct risk assessments and manage operational risks.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining operational productivity metrics.
2. Increased number of security clearance exceptions.
3. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
4. High employee turnover in operational roles.
5. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
6. Negative feedback from team members or stakeholders.
7. Inconsistent communication across departments.
8. Lack of alignment between operational goals and company strategy.

Dashboard Requirements for COO:
1. Active Operations Count: Display the number of ongoing operations.
2. Operational Productivity: Show metrics related to productivity levels.
3. Security Clearance Level: Indicate the current security clearance status.
4. Clearance Exceptions: Highlight any exceptions to security clearance.
5. Telemetry Data: Provide real-time data points relevant to operations.
6. Audit Logs: Maintain a log of compliance and operational audits.
7. Performance Trends: Visual representation of productivity trends over time.
8. Quick Actions: Buttons for immediate actions like running compliance scans or syncing data.
9. Insights and Recommendations: Suggestions for operational improvements.
10. Error Handling: Display error messages and retry options if data fails to load.
