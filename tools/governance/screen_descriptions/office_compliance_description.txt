Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage office communications and correspondence.
2. Schedule and coordinate meetings and appointments.
3. Maintain filing systems and organize documents.
4. Prepare reports, presentations, and other documents.
5. Assist with bookkeeping and financial record keeping.
6. Handle office supplies and inventory management.
7. Support team members with administrative tasks.
8. Ensure compliance with office policies and procedures.
9. Manage travel arrangements and itineraries.
10. Assist in onboarding new employees.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent missed deadlines or delays in task completion.
2. Lack of communication or unclear instructions.
3. High turnover rate among administrative staff.
4. Inconsistent filing and documentation practices.
5. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
6. Poor time management leading to backlog of tasks.
7. Inadequate training or resources for staff.
8. Low employee morale or engagement.
9. Frequent errors in reports or documents.
10. Lack of adherence to compliance and governance standards.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview with deadlines and priorities.
2. Communication log for tracking correspondence.
3. Calendar view for meetings and appointments.
4. Document management system for easy access to files.
5. Compliance checklist to ensure adherence to policies.
6. Performance metrics to track efficiency and productivity.
7. Inventory tracker for office supplies.
8. Feedback mechanism for team members to report issues.
9. Training resources and materials for skill development.
10. Audit trail for financial and operational records.
