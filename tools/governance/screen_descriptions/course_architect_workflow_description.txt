Training Director Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Assess training needs through surveys, interviews, and consultations.
3. Design and deliver training sessions and workshops.
4. Evaluate training effectiveness and make improvements.
5. Manage training budgets and resources.
6. Collaborate with other departments to align training with organizational goals.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in training and development.
8. Maintain training records and documentation.
9. Provide coaching and support to trainers and facilitators.
10. Foster a culture of continuous learning within the organization.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High turnover rates among trained employees.
2. Low engagement or participation in training programs.
3. Negative feedback from training participants.
4. Inconsistent training delivery across departments.
5. Lack of measurable outcomes or improvements post-training.
6. Insufficient resources allocated for training initiatives.
7. Failure to adapt training programs based on feedback or changing needs.
8. Poor communication regarding training opportunities.
9. Limited access to training materials or resources.
10. Inability to track training progress and effectiveness.

Dashboard Needs for Training Director:
1. Training program participation rates.
2. Feedback scores from training sessions.
3. Completion rates of training modules.
4. Budget utilization for training initiatives.
5. Employee performance metrics post-training.
6. Trends in training needs assessments.
7. Engagement levels in training activities.
8. Resource allocation for training programs.
9. Upcoming training schedules and deadlines.
10. Reports on training effectiveness and ROI.
