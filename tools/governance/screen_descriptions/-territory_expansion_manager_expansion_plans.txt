List of work the user must do:
1. Review the current expansion plans for territories.
2. Analyze data related to market potential and competition in new territories.
3. Collaborate with the sales and marketing teams to align strategies.
4. Monitor the implementation of expansion plans and adjust as necessary.
5. Report on the progress and outcomes of expansion efforts.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in data retrieval or loading times, indicating potential performance issues.
2. Frequent errors when loading features, suggesting instability in the application.
3. Lack of user engagement or feedback on the expansion plans.
4. Inconsistencies in data presented, which could lead to poor decision-making.
5. High turnover rates in the territories being expanded into, indicating potential market challenges.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. A summary of current expansion plans and their statuses.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to territory performance.
3. Visualizations of market analysis data (e.g., charts or graphs).
4. Notifications for any issues or updates regarding expansion plans.
5. A feedback mechanism for users to report issues or suggest improvements.
