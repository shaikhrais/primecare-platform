Customer Support Work Responsibilities:
1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance.
3. Manage customer complaints and escalate when necessary.
4. Document customer interactions and resolutions in the system.
5. Conduct follow-ups to ensure customer satisfaction.
6. Collaborate with other departments to resolve complex issues.
7. Maintain knowledge of products and services to assist customers effectively.
8. Train new support staff on processes and tools.
9. Analyze customer feedback for service improvement.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased response times to customer inquiries.
2. High volume of unresolved tickets or complaints.
3. Frequent escalations to higher management.
4. Negative customer feedback or low satisfaction scores.
5. Lack of communication among team members.
6. Inconsistent documentation of customer interactions.
7. High turnover rates within the support team.
8. Repeated issues that indicate a lack of product knowledge.

Dashboard Requirements for Customer Support:
1. Real-time metrics on ticket volume and response times.
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
3. Status of open and closed tickets.
4. Escalation rates and reasons for escalations.
5. Team performance metrics, including individual response times.
6. Common issues reported by customers.
7. Training and knowledge base resources for staff.
8. Alerts for high-priority tickets or customer complaints.
