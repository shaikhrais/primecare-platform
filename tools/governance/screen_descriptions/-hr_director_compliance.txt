List of tasks for the HR Director:

1. Oversee compliance with labor laws and regulations.
2. Manage employee relations and conflict resolution.
3. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
4. Monitor and report on HR metrics and analytics.
5. Ensure effective recruitment and onboarding processes.
6. Conduct performance management and employee development programs.
7. Facilitate training and development initiatives.
8. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
9. Ensure diversity and inclusion initiatives are in place.
10. Conduct regular audits of HR processes and systems.
11. Maintain employee records and HR documentation.
12. Lead organizational change initiatives.
13. Ensure workplace safety and health compliance.
14. Foster a positive organizational culture.

Red flags of operation:

1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Increased number of employee grievances or complaints.
3. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
4. Poor employee engagement scores.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Inconsistent performance evaluations.
8. Low participation in diversity and inclusion programs.
9. High absenteeism rates.
10. Negative feedback from exit interviews.

Dashboard needs for the HR Director:

1. Employee turnover rate.
2. Compliance audit results.
3. Employee engagement scores.
4. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, cost per hire).
5. Training and development participation rates.
6. Performance management statistics.
7. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
8. Employee grievance statistics.
9. Health and safety compliance status.
10. Overall HR operational efficiency metrics.
