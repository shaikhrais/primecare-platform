Home Support Worker Responsibilities:
1. Access and review personalized patient care plans.
2. Monitor dietary outlines and restrictions.
3. Provide medication support and reminders.
4. Assist with mobility aids and transfer instructions.
5. Document patient progress and changes in care needs.
6. Communicate effectively with patients and their families.
7. Report any changes in patient condition to healthcare professionals.
8. Ensure compliance with safety and health regulations.
9. Participate in training and professional development.
10. Maintain confidentiality of patient information.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Inconsistent documentation of patient care.
2. Frequent missed medication reminders.
3. Lack of communication with patients or families.
4. Unreported changes in patient condition.
5. Non-compliance with dietary restrictions.
6. Inadequate training on mobility aids.
7. High turnover of Home Support Workers.
8. Negative feedback from patients or families.
9. Delays in responding to patient needs.
10. Poor collaboration with healthcare teams.

Dashboard Needs for Home Support Worker:
1. Overview of patient care plans and status.
2. Alerts for medication reminders and dietary restrictions.
3. Communication log with patients and families.
4. Documentation section for recording patient progress.
5. Training and compliance tracking.
6. Performance metrics and feedback.
7. Incident reporting and follow-up actions.
8. Resource links for additional support and training.
9. Calendar for scheduling visits and appointments.
10. Access to emergency contact information.
