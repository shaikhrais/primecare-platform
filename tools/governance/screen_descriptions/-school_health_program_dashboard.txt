User Work Tasks:
1. Monitor health program metrics.
2. Review student health records.
3. Analyze program effectiveness.
4. Manage health resources and supplies.
5. Communicate with health professionals and educators.
6. Update program policies and procedures.
7. Conduct health assessments and screenings.
8. Organize health education workshops.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low participation rates in health programs.
2. High incidence of health issues among students.
3. Delayed reporting of health data.
4. Inconsistent communication with stakeholders.
5. Lack of resources or supplies for health initiatives.
6. Poor feedback from program participants.
7. Non-compliance with health regulations.
8. Insufficient training for staff on health protocols.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for health programs.
2. Visual representation of health metrics (charts/graphs).
3. Alerts for low participation or high incidence rates.
4. Quick access to student health records.
5. Resource management tools for tracking supplies.
6. Communication tools for stakeholder engagement.
7. Feedback collection mechanism from program participants.
8. Compliance tracking for health regulations.
