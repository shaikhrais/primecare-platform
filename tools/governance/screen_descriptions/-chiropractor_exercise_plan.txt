List of work Chiropractor must do:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Educate patients on exercises and lifestyle changes.
5. Monitor patient progress and modify treatment plans as necessary.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and research.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Unusual patterns in patient outcomes or recovery times.
3. High rates of patient dropouts or cancellations.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Non-compliance with safety and regulatory standards.
6. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.
7. Signs of burnout or decreased staff morale.

Dashboard needs for Chiropractor:
1. Active operations metrics (e.g., number of patients treated, ongoing treatments).
2. Compliance status indicators (e.g., security clearance levels).
3. Performance trends (e.g., patient recovery rates, satisfaction scores).
4. Telemetry logs for operational audits.
5. Notifications for upcoming compliance scans or audits.
6. Patient feedback and satisfaction ratings.
7. Visualizations of treatment outcomes over time.
