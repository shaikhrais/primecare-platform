User Work List:
1. Conduct community health needs assessments.
2. Collect and analyze health data from various sources.
3. Engage with community stakeholders for input and feedback.
4. Identify health disparities and priority health issues.
5. Develop action plans based on assessment findings.
6. Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of health interventions.
7. Report findings to relevant health authorities and stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Lack of stakeholder engagement leading to incomplete assessments.
2. Insufficient data collection methods resulting in unreliable data.
3. Ignoring community feedback which may lead to ineffective solutions.
4. Failure to identify and address health disparities.
5. Inadequate monitoring of implemented health interventions.
6. Poor communication of findings to stakeholders.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of assessment progress and timelines.
2. Data visualization of health metrics and disparities.
3. Stakeholder engagement metrics (e.g., number of participants).
4. Action plan tracking and status updates.
5. Feedback collection mechanism from community members.
6. Reports on the effectiveness of health interventions.
7. Alerts for any identified red flags in the assessment process.
