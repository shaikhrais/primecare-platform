Finance Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee financial planning and analysis.
2. Manage budgeting processes and forecasts.
3. Ensure compliance with financial regulations and standards.
4. Monitor financial performance and report on variances.
5. Develop financial strategies to support organizational goals.
6. Manage cash flow and working capital.
7. Supervise financial reporting and audits.
8. Collaborate with other departments to align financial goals.
9. Provide financial insights to support decision-making.
10. Lead financial risk management efforts.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant variances in budget vs. actual performance.
2. Delays in financial reporting or audits.
3. Increased frequency of compliance issues or violations.
4. Unexplained fluctuations in cash flow.
5. Lack of transparency in financial processes.
6. High turnover in finance team or key positions.
7. Inconsistent financial data across reports.
8. Poor communication with stakeholders regarding financial matters.
9. Failure to meet financial targets or KPIs.
10. Resistance to implementing financial controls or policies.

Dashboard Needs for Finance Director:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health.
2. Real-time cash flow analysis.
3. Budget vs. actual performance tracking.
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Financial forecasts and trend analysis.
6. Risk assessment metrics.
7. Variance analysis reports.
8. Summary of recent financial transactions.
9. Alerts for compliance issues or financial discrepancies.
10. Visualizations of financial data for quick insights.
