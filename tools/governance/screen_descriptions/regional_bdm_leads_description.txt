User Work List:
1. Monitor regional business development leads.
2. Analyze lead data for trends and insights.
3. Collaborate with team members on lead strategies.
4. Update lead statuses and follow up on actions.
5. Report on lead performance metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in loading data or features.
2. Frequent errors when accessing lead information.
3. Inconsistent data presentation or updates.
4. Lack of user engagement or feedback.
5. High bounce rates on lead follow-up actions.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of active leads and their statuses.
2. Performance metrics (conversion rates, follow-up success).
3. Visualizations of lead trends over time.
4. Notifications for overdue follow-ups or actions.
5. Quick access to team collaboration tools and resources.
