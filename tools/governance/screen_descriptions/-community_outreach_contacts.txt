List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor community outreach contacts.
2. Review and update contact information as necessary.
3. Engage with community members through outreach efforts.
4. Analyze outreach effectiveness and gather feedback.
5. Report on outreach activities and outcomes.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors loading features or data.
2. Delays in updating contact information.
3. Low engagement rates with community members.
4. Negative feedback from community outreach efforts.
5. Inconsistent data displayed on the dashboard.

What we need on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of community outreach contacts.
2. Status indicators for data loading and updates.
3. Engagement metrics (e.g., number of interactions, feedback scores).
4. Alerts for any errors or issues encountered.
5. Quick access to update contact information and outreach reports.
