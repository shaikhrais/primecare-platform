Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage volunteer recruitment and onboarding.
2. Coordinate volunteer training and development.
3. Schedule volunteer shifts and assignments.
4. Maintain communication with volunteers.
5. Track volunteer hours and contributions.
6. Organize volunteer appreciation events.
7. Ensure compliance with organizational policies and regulations.
8. Monitor volunteer performance and provide feedback.
9. Address volunteer concerns and resolve conflicts.
10. Report on volunteer program outcomes and metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rates.
2. Inconsistent communication with volunteers.
3. Lack of engagement or participation from volunteers.
4. Missed deadlines for volunteer-related tasks.
5. Negative feedback from volunteers regarding their experience.
6. Compliance issues or failure to meet regulatory requirements.
7. Insufficient tracking of volunteer hours and contributions.
8. Low attendance at volunteer training sessions.
9. Difficulty in scheduling or filling volunteer shifts.
10. Limited recognition or appreciation for volunteer efforts.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Overview of active volunteers and their roles.
2. Metrics on volunteer hours logged and contributions.
3. Compliance status and audit results.
4. Communication logs with volunteers.
5. Upcoming volunteer events and training sessions.
6. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from volunteers.
7. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
8. Historical data on volunteer engagement and turnover.
9. Tools for scheduling and shift management.
10. Resources for volunteer training and development.
