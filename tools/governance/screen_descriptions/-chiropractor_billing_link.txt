Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Monitor patient progress and modify treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and preventive care.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rate of treatment failures or lack of improvement.
3. Unusual patterns in billing or insurance claims.
4. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
5. Frequent errors in patient documentation.
6. Staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
7. Inconsistent treatment protocols among practitioners.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Overview of active patient cases and treatment statuses.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as patient satisfaction scores.
4. Operational audit logs for tracking actions taken.
5. Real-time telemetry data on clinic operations.
6. Security clearance status and any exceptions logged.
7. Quick access buttons for compliance scans and operational actions.
8. Notifications for any urgent issues or compliance alerts.
