Clinical Nurse Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement care plans.
3. Provide education and support to patients and families.
4. Collaborate with healthcare teams to optimize patient outcomes.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust care plans as necessary.
6. Advocate for patients' needs and preferences.
7. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
8. Stay updated on best practices and clinical guidelines.
9. Provide mentorship and training to nursing staff.
10. Document patient care and outcomes accurately.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of readmission or complications.
3. Delays in patient care or treatment.
4. Inconsistent documentation or care plans.
5. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
6. Unusual patterns in patient outcomes or metrics.
7. Staff burnout or turnover.
8. Non-compliance with clinical guidelines or protocols.
9. Insufficient resources or support for patient care.
10. Failure to address identified patient safety concerns.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Nurse Specialist:
1. Patient demographics and statistics.
2. Compliance metrics and performance indicators.
3. Real-time patient monitoring data.
4. Logs of patient interactions and care plans.
5. Alerts for critical patient conditions or changes.
6. Quality improvement metrics and outcomes.
7. Staff performance and training records.
8. Resource allocation and availability.
9. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
10. Trends in patient outcomes and operational efficiency.
