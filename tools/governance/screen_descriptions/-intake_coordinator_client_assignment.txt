List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor client assignments and ensure they are correctly allocated.
2. Review incoming client requests and prioritize them based on urgency.
3. Communicate with clients to gather necessary information for assignments.
4. Update the status of client assignments in the system.
5. Collaborate with other team members to resolve any issues related to client assignments.
6. Generate reports on client assignment metrics and performance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in client assignment processing.
2. High volume of client complaints or inquiries.
3. Frequent errors in assignment allocations.
4. Lack of communication or collaboration among team members.
5. Inconsistent data or metrics reported in the system.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current client assignments and their statuses.
2. Alerts for any overdue assignments or pending requests.
3. Performance metrics such as average assignment time and client satisfaction scores.
4. Communication tools for easy interaction with clients and team members.
5. Access to reports and analytics for informed decision-making.
