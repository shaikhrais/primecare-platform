Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor active operations and ensure optimal productivity.
2. Maintain security clearance and compliance with regulations.
3. Review operational audit logs for any discrepancies or issues.
4. Execute compliance scans and audits regularly.
5. Manage and respond to any alerts or notifications from the system.
6. Refresh and synchronize dashboard telemetry data.
7. Document and log any significant events or changes in operations.
8. Communicate with staff and stakeholders regarding operational status.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in active operations or productivity levels.
2. Security clearance issues or exceptions logged.
3. Frequent errors or failures during compliance scans.
4. Unusual patterns in telemetry data indicating potential problems.
5. Lack of timely updates in operational audit logs.
6. High number of manual refreshes indicating system instability.
7. Delays in responding to alerts or notifications.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of active operations with productivity metrics.
2. Security clearance status and any exceptions.
3. Real-time telemetry data and historical trends.
4. Operational audit logs with timestamps and details.
5. Compliance scan results and any issues identified.
6. Notifications for alerts or required actions.
7. Button to manually trigger compliance scans or refresh data.
8. Log entry feature for documenting significant events.
