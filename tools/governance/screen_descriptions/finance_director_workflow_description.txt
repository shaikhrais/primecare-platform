Finance Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee financial planning and analysis.
2. Manage budgeting and forecasting processes.
3. Ensure compliance with financial regulations and standards.
4. Monitor financial performance and report on variances.
5. Develop financial strategies to support business objectives.
6. Manage cash flow and working capital.
7. Supervise financial reporting and audits.
8. Collaborate with other departments to align financial goals.
9. Provide financial insights to support decision-making.
10. Lead financial risk management efforts.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Significant variances between budgeted and actual performance.
2. Delays in financial reporting or compliance submissions.
3. Increased frequency of financial errors or discrepancies.
4. Unexplained fluctuations in cash flow.
5. High levels of debt or poor credit ratings.
6. Lack of transparency in financial processes.
7. Inconsistent financial metrics across departments.
8. Frequent changes in financial regulations impacting operations.
9. Low employee morale in finance teams.
10. High turnover rates in finance-related positions.

Dashboard Requirements for Finance Director:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health.
2. Real-time cash flow monitoring.
3. Budget vs. actual performance tracking.
4. Compliance status and alerts.
5. Financial forecasting and trend analysis.
6. Audit trail of financial transactions.
7. Risk assessment metrics.
8. Operational logs and activity summaries.
9. Visualizations of financial data (charts, graphs).
10. Alerts for any anomalies or red flags in financial operations.
