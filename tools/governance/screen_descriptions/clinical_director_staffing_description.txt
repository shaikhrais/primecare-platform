List of work for the User:
1. Monitor staffing levels and ensure adequate coverage.
2. Review and approve staffing requests.
3. Analyze staffing data to identify trends and needs.
4. Communicate with team members regarding staffing changes.
5. Address any staffing-related issues or concerns.
6. Generate reports on staffing efficiency and effectiveness.

Red flags of operation:
1. High turnover rates among staff.
2. Frequent understaffing or overstaffing situations.
3. Delays in staffing requests being processed.
4. Increased number of complaints related to staffing.
5. Lack of communication regarding staffing changes.
6. Inconsistent data reporting on staffing levels.

What is needed on the User dashboard:
1. Real-time staffing levels and availability.
2. Alerts for understaffing or overstaffing situations.
3. Quick access to staffing request approvals.
4. Visualizations of staffing trends over time.
5. Communication tools for team updates.
6. Summary reports on staffing efficiency and issues.
