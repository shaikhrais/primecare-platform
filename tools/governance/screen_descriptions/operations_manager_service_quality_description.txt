List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor service quality metrics.
2. Analyze operational data for trends and anomalies.
3. Identify areas for improvement in service delivery.
4. Collaborate with team members to address service quality issues.
5. Generate reports on service quality performance.
6. Implement corrective actions based on findings.
7. Communicate with stakeholders regarding service quality status.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased number of customer complaints.
2. Decline in service delivery speed.
3. Frequent service interruptions or outages.
4. Low employee engagement or morale.
5. Inconsistent service quality across different teams or locations.
6. High turnover rates among service staff.
7. Failure to meet service level agreements (SLAs).

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time service quality metrics (e.g., customer satisfaction scores, response times).
2. Alerts for any red flags or anomalies detected.
3. Visualizations of trends over time (e.g., graphs showing service quality changes).
4. Summary of recent customer feedback and complaints.
5. Action items or recommendations based on data analysis.
6. Performance comparison against established benchmarks or SLAs.
7. Access to detailed reports for deeper analysis.
