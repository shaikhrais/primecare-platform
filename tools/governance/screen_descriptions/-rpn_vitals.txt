Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Assist in patient assessments and care planning.
4. Provide personal care and support to patients.
5. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
7. Educate patients and families about health management.
8. Ensure compliance with safety and infection control protocols.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Respond to patient emergencies and provide first aid.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Inconsistent or inaccurate vital sign readings.
2. Delays in medication administration.
3. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Frequent errors in documentation.
5. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
6. High turnover rates among staff.
7. Lack of communication within the healthcare team.
8. Unresolved patient issues or concerns.
9. Inadequate staffing levels.
10. Failure to follow up on patient care plans.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Real-time vital sign monitoring.
2. Medication administration tracking.
3. Patient care plan status updates.
4. Compliance audit logs and results.
5. Alerts for critical patient changes.
6. Performance metrics (e.g., patient satisfaction scores).
7. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
8. Access to educational resources for patients.
9. Communication tools for team collaboration.
10. Historical data for trend analysis and reporting.
