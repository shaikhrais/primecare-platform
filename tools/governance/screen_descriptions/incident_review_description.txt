Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health problems and needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments.
4. Monitor patient progress and responses to treatment.
5. Educate patients and their families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Provide emotional support to patients and families.
10. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of medication errors.
3. Inconsistent documentation practices.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. Frequent staff turnover or burnout.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Poor communication among team members.
8. Lack of adherence to care plans.
9. Unresolved conflicts within the team.
10. Inadequate training or resources for staff.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Patient health status overview.
2. Medication administration records.
3. Compliance tracking metrics.
4. Incident and error logs.
5. Patient satisfaction scores.
6. Staffing levels and availability.
7. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
8. Performance metrics for nursing interventions.
9. Access to educational resources and training.
10. Communication tools for team collaboration.
