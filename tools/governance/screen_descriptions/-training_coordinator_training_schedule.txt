User Tasks:
1. Review and manage the training schedule.
2. Monitor training sessions and participant attendance.
3. Update training materials and resources as needed.
4. Communicate with trainers and participants regarding schedule changes.
5. Analyze training effectiveness and gather feedback.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors or loading issues when accessing the training schedule.
2. Delays in updating training materials or schedules.
3. Low participant attendance or engagement in training sessions.
4. Inconsistent communication with trainers and participants.
5. Lack of feedback collection or analysis on training effectiveness.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of upcoming training sessions and participant lists.
2. Notifications for schedule changes or updates.
3. Attendance tracking and reporting features.
4. Feedback collection tools for participants.
5. Performance metrics on training effectiveness and engagement levels.
