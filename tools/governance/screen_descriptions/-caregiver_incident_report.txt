Caregiver Responsibilities:
1. Monitor patient health and report any changes.
2. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, grooming).
3. Administer medications as prescribed.
4. Provide companionship and emotional support.
5. Maintain a clean and safe environment.
6. Document care provided and any incidents.
7. Communicate effectively with healthcare professionals and family members.
8. Attend training and stay updated on best practices.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent incidents reported in logs.
2. Delays in compliance scans or audits.
3. High turnover of caregivers.
4. Negative feedback from patients or families.
5. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Unresolved errors or issues in the system.

Dashboard Needs for Caregiver:
1. Overview of active operations and patient statuses.
2. Real-time telemetry logs and compliance standings.
3. Alerts for any red flags or incidents.
4. Access to operational audit logs.
5. Metrics on caregiver performance and patient satisfaction.
6. Quick access buttons for compliance scans and incident reporting.
7. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and updates.
