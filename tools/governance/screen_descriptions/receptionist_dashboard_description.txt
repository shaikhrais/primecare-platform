Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage scheduling and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Maintain filing systems and records.
4. Assist with data entry and document preparation.
5. Organize meetings and take minutes.
6. Manage office supplies and inventory.
7. Provide customer service and support.
8. Prepare reports and presentations.
9. Coordinate travel arrangements.
10. Support team members with administrative tasks.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors in data entry or documentation.
2. Missed deadlines or appointments.
3. Poor communication with team members or clients.
4. Inconsistent filing and record-keeping practices.
5. Lack of responsiveness to inquiries or requests.
6. High turnover of administrative staff.
7. Inability to adapt to new tools or technologies.
8. Overreliance on manual processes instead of automation.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task and appointment calendar.
2. Communication log (emails, calls, messages).
3. Document management system for easy access to files.
4. Performance metrics (tasks completed, deadlines met).
5. Inventory tracker for office supplies.
6. Customer service feedback and response times.
7. Compliance checklists and audit logs.
8. Quick access buttons for frequently used tools or reports.
9. Notifications for upcoming deadlines or meetings.
10. A summary of team members' tasks and responsibilities.
