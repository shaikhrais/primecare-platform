List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor claims submitted by users.
2. Review and approve or reject claims based on predefined criteria.
3. Generate reports on claims status and trends.
4. Communicate with users regarding their claims.
5. Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and policies.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of rejected claims may indicate issues with user submissions or unclear guidelines.
2. Delays in processing claims could signal resource shortages or inefficiencies.
3. Frequent errors in claims data may suggest a need for better user training or system improvements.
4. Lack of communication from users regarding their claims can indicate dissatisfaction or confusion.

What is needed on the dashboard of the user:
1. Overview of claims status (pending, approved, rejected).
2. Metrics on average processing time for claims.
3. Alerts for claims that are approaching deadlines.
4. User feedback section to gather insights on the claims process.
5. Visualizations of claims trends over time (e.g., monthly submissions, approval rates).
