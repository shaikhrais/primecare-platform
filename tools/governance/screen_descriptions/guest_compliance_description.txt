List of work the Guest must do:
1. Execute compliance audits by running the compliance scan.
2. Monitor operational audit logs for any anomalies or issues.
3. Update governance directives as necessary (e.g., enforce MFA, audit telemetry).
4. Submit secure event reports through the ingestion form.
5. Review recent compliance verification audits for status updates.
6. Trigger state actions as required for governance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Any errors displayed during compliance scans or audits.
2. Logs indicating failed governance actions or policy updates.
3. Non-compliant statuses in recent compliance verification audits.
4. Delays or failures in submitting secure event reports.
5. High frequency of manual refreshes or triggered actions indicating potential issues.

Dashboard needs for the Guest:
1. Real-time compliance status overview.
2. Summary of recent audit logs and their statuses.
3. Notifications for any compliance failures or required actions.
4. Quick access to governance directives and their current settings.
5. Visual indicators for the health of operational processes (e.g., successful submissions, compliance scan results).
