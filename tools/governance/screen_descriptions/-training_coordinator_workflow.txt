List of work training candidates must do:
1. Attend scheduled training sessions.
2. Complete assigned training modules.
3. Participate in hands-on exercises.
4. Engage in group discussions and feedback sessions.
5. Submit assessments and evaluations on time.
6. Track progress on training goals.
7. Provide feedback on training effectiveness.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent absences from training sessions.
2. Low engagement during training activities.
3. Incomplete training modules or assessments.
4. Lack of communication or feedback from candidates.
5. Consistent failure to meet training deadlines.
6. Negative feedback from peers or trainers.
7. Resistance to participate in group activities.

Dashboard needs for training candidates:
1. Progress tracking for each training module.
2. Upcoming training session calendar.
3. Notifications for deadlines and assessments.
4. Feedback section for trainers and peers.
5. Performance metrics and evaluation scores.
6. Resources and materials for additional learning.
7. Communication tools for candidate-trainer interaction.
