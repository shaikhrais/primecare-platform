Territory Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Monitor sales performance in assigned territories.
2. Develop and implement sales strategies to achieve targets.
3. Conduct market research to identify new opportunities.
4. Build and maintain relationships with key clients.
5. Analyze sales data and prepare reports for management.
6. Collaborate with marketing to align promotional efforts.
7. Train and support sales team members.
8. Ensure compliance with company policies and regulations.
9. Manage budgets and allocate resources effectively.
10. Provide feedback to product development based on customer insights.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales figures over consecutive periods.
2. High turnover rates within the sales team.
3. Increased customer complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Failure to meet compliance standards or audit findings.
5. Lack of communication or collaboration within the team.
6. Inconsistent reporting or data discrepancies.
7. Low engagement in training or development programs.
8. Unclear sales strategies or objectives.
9. Poor territory coverage or missed opportunities.
10. Resistance to change or adaptation to market trends.

Dashboard Requirements for Territory Sales Manager:
1. Sales performance metrics (e.g., revenue, growth rate).
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
3. Compliance status and audit results.
4. Territory coverage maps and analysis.
5. Team performance indicators (e.g., individual sales figures).
6. Market trends and competitive analysis.
7. Training and development progress for team members.
8. Budget tracking and resource allocation.
9. Real-time alerts for critical issues or red flags.
10. Historical data for trend analysis and forecasting.
