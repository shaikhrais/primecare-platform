User Tasks:
1. Monitor community outreach activities.
2. Generate reports on outreach effectiveness.
3. Analyze data trends over time.
4. Identify areas for improvement in outreach efforts.
5. Collaborate with team members on outreach strategies.
6. Provide feedback on community engagement initiatives.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in outreach metrics.
2. Delays in report generation or data retrieval.
3. High error rates in data entry or processing.
4. Lack of user engagement or participation in outreach activities.
5. Negative feedback from the community regarding outreach efforts.
6. Insufficient resources or support for outreach initiatives.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of key outreach metrics (e.g., number of events, participant feedback).
2. Visual representation of data trends (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for any operational red flags (e.g., low engagement).
4. Quick access to generate and download reports.
5. Collaboration tools for team communication.
6. Feedback section for community input on outreach efforts.
