User Tasks:
1. Monitor training coordinator reports.
2. Analyze training data for trends and insights.
3. Generate and export reports for stakeholders.
4. Address any discrepancies or issues in training data.
5. Collaborate with other departments for training needs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors or loading issues when accessing reports.
2. Inconsistent data or discrepancies in training metrics.
3. Delays in report generation or export functionality.
4. Lack of user engagement or feedback on the reports.
5. High bounce rates or low interaction with the dashboard.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time data visualization of training metrics.
2. Alerts for any discrepancies or issues in data.
3. User feedback section for continuous improvement.
4. Quick access to frequently used reports.
5. Performance metrics to track user engagement with the dashboard.
