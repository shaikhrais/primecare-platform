Franchise Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee franchise sales operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Monitor franchise performance metrics and sales trends.
3. Conduct regular audits of franchise operations.
4. Provide training and support to franchisees.
5. Develop and implement sales strategies to increase franchise sales.
6. Maintain communication with franchisees to address concerns and provide updates.
7. Analyze market trends and competitor activities.
8. Prepare reports on franchise performance and compliance status.
9. Manage franchise recruitment and onboarding processes.
10. Collaborate with marketing teams to promote franchise opportunities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales figures or performance metrics.
2. Increased number of compliance violations or complaints from franchisees.
3. High turnover rate among franchisees.
4. Lack of communication or engagement from franchisees.
5. Negative feedback from customers regarding franchise operations.
6. Inconsistent application of franchise policies and procedures.
7. Delays in franchisee onboarding or support processes.
8. Unresolved issues or disputes with franchisees.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Sales Manager:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics (e.g., sales volume, growth rate).
2. Compliance status indicators (e.g., number of violations, resolution status).
3. Franchisee engagement metrics (e.g., communication frequency, feedback scores).
4. Market analysis data (e.g., competitor performance, market trends).
5. Operational logs and audit results.
6. Training and support requests from franchisees.
7. Recruitment status of potential franchisees.
8. Alerts for any red flags or issues that require immediate attention.
