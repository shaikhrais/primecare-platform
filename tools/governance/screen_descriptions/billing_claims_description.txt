User Tasks:
1. Review outstanding claims and their statuses.
2. Select claims for batch submission.
3. Submit selected claims for reimbursement.
4. Manually reconcile claims as necessary.
5. Monitor claims rejection rate and outstanding claims value.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of claims in 'auditing' or 'rejected' status.
2. Delays in processing claims leading to increased outstanding claims value.
3. Frequent manual reconciliations indicating potential issues in the claims process.
4. Low reimbursement amounts compared to outstanding claims.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Display total outstanding claims value.
2. Show total reimbursed amount for the current month.
3. Highlight claims rejection rate.
4. Provide visual indicators for claims status (e.g., auditing, reimbursed, rejected).
5. Allow for bulk selection and submission of claims.
6. Include a progress indicator during batch submission.
7. Show selected claims count and provide an option to clear selection.
