Registered Nurse (RN) Field Supervisor Responsibilities:
1. Oversee nursing staff and ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.
2. Conduct regular field visits to assess patient care quality.
3. Provide training and support to nursing staff.
4. Monitor patient outcomes and report findings.
5. Collaborate with healthcare teams to improve patient care.
6. Manage staffing schedules and assignments.
7. Address patient and staff concerns promptly.
8. Ensure proper documentation and reporting of patient care activities.
9. Implement quality improvement initiatives.
10. Maintain communication with healthcare providers and stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High patient complaint rates.
2. Increased staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent errors in patient documentation.
4. Non-compliance with healthcare regulations.
5. Poor patient outcomes or adverse events.
6. Lack of timely communication among staff.
7. Inconsistent adherence to care protocols.
8. Insufficient training or support for nursing staff.
9. Delays in patient care or response times.
10. Inadequate staffing levels during peak times.

Dashboard Requirements for RN Field Supervisor:
1. Real-time patient care metrics (e.g., patient satisfaction scores).
2. Compliance tracking indicators.
3. Staffing levels and availability.
4. Incident reporting and resolution status.
5. Training and development progress for nursing staff.
6. Patient outcome statistics (e.g., readmission rates).
7. Logs of field visits and assessments.
8. Alerts for any operational red flags.
9. Communication logs with healthcare teams.
10. Quality improvement initiative tracking.
