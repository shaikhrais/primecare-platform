Responsibilities of Community Outreach Lead:
1. Develop and implement community outreach strategies.
2. Build and maintain relationships with community organizations and stakeholders.
3. Organize and facilitate community events and workshops.
4. Collect and analyze feedback from community members.
5. Monitor and report on outreach program effectiveness.
6. Collaborate with internal teams to align outreach efforts with organizational goals.
7. Manage outreach budget and resources.
8. Train and supervise outreach staff and volunteers.
9. Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and policies.
10. Create and distribute outreach materials and communications.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low community engagement or participation in events.
2. Negative feedback or complaints from community members.
3. Inconsistent communication with stakeholders.
4. Lack of measurable outcomes or impact from outreach efforts.
5. Budget overruns or mismanagement of resources.
6. High turnover or dissatisfaction among outreach staff and volunteers.
7. Failure to meet compliance or regulatory requirements.
8. Limited collaboration with other departments or organizations.
9. Inadequate tracking and reporting of outreach activities.
10. Resistance to change or innovation in outreach strategies.

Dashboard needs for Community Outreach Lead:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for outreach effectiveness.
2. Community engagement metrics (e.g., event attendance, feedback scores).
3. Budget tracking and resource allocation overview.
4. Logs of outreach activities and events.
5. Compliance status and audit results.
6. Visualizations of community demographics and needs.
7. Real-time updates on ongoing outreach initiatives.
8. Feedback collection tools for community members.
9. Collaboration tools for internal and external stakeholders.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
