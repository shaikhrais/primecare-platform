Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and evaluate their effectiveness.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Conduct market research to identify trends and opportunities.
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams to align marketing efforts.
6. Monitor and analyze campaign performance metrics.
7. Build and maintain relationships with external partners and vendors.
8. Lead and mentor the marketing team.
9. Ensure brand consistency across all marketing channels.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining engagement or conversion rates in campaigns.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Inconsistent messaging across marketing channels.
4. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
5. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales teams.
6. High turnover rates within the marketing team.
7. Failure to meet campaign deadlines or objectives.
8. Insufficient data analysis leading to uninformed decisions.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of active marketing campaigns with performance metrics.
2. Real-time analytics on customer engagement and conversion rates.
3. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
4. Market research insights and competitor analysis.
5. Team performance metrics and workload distribution.
6. Customer feedback and sentiment analysis.
7. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for brand awareness and reach.
8. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
