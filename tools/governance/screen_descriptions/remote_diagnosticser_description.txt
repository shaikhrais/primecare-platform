List of work for the User:
1. Access the Remote Diagnostics Viewer.
2. View diagnostic data and reports.
3. Analyze trends and patterns in diagnostics.
4. Generate and export reports.
5. Collaborate with team members on findings.
6. Provide feedback on the diagnostic tools and features.

Red flags of operation:
1. Slow loading times for diagnostic data.
2. Inaccurate or outdated information displayed.
3. Difficulty in navigating the interface.
4. Lack of collaboration features or tools.
5. Inability to export data in desired formats.
6. Frequent crashes or bugs in the application.

What is needed on the User dashboard:
1. Quick access to recent diagnostic reports.
2. Visualizations of key metrics and trends.
3. Alerts for critical issues or anomalies.
4. Collaboration tools for team discussions.
5. Export options for reports in multiple formats.
6. User feedback section for continuous improvement.
