Chiropractor Work Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Perform spinal adjustments and manipulations.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and research.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rate of treatment failures or lack of progress.
3. Unusual spikes in patient cancellations or no-shows.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Non-compliance with safety and health regulations.
6. Staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
7. Financial discrepancies or billing issues.
8. Lack of communication among team members.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Active Operations Metrics: Display current patient treatments and status.
2. Security Clearance Level: Show compliance with healthcare regulations.
3. System Latency: Monitor system performance and response times.
4. Data Integrity: Ensure accuracy and security of patient data.
5. Telemetry Logs: Provide real-time updates on patient interactions and treatments.
6. Audit Logs: Maintain a record of operational activities and compliance checks.
7. Action Buttons: Quick access to execute compliance scans and other critical functions.
8. Refresh and Sync Options: Allow for manual updates and synchronization of data.
