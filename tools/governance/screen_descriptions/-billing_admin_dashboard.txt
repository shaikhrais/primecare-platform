Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage billing invoices and ensure accuracy.
2. Reconcile discrepancies in billing.
3. Update billing rates for services.
4. Maintain logs of operations and changes.
5. Monitor and report on billing statuses.
6. Handle client inquiries related to billing.
7. Ensure compliance with tax regulations.
8. Provide support for financial audits.
9. Assist in preparing financial reports.
10. Coordinate with other departments for billing-related issues.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. High number of discrepancies in invoices.
2. Delays in invoice reconciliation.
3. Frequent client complaints regarding billing.
4. Inconsistent billing rates applied.
5. Lack of updated logs or documentation.
6. Errors in financial reports.
7. Non-compliance with tax regulations.
8. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
9. Low productivity metrics.
10. High turnover in billing staff.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Overview of current billing statuses (e.g., outstanding, underpaid).
2. Metrics on discrepancies and resolutions.
3. Logs of recent activities and changes.
4. Alerts for overdue invoices or discrepancies.
5. Financial performance indicators (e.g., revenue trends).
6. Compliance status with tax regulations.
7. Client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
8. Quick access to frequently used functions (e.g., update rates, reconcile invoices).
9. Visual representation of workload and productivity.
10. Historical data for trend analysis and forecasting.
