List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor patient care activities.
2. Access patient records and updates.
3. Coordinate with other healthcare team members.
4. Track patient progress and outcomes.
5. Report any issues or concerns regarding patient care.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in accessing patient records.
2. Inconsistent updates on patient status.
3. Lack of communication among team members.
4. High number of unresolved patient issues.
5. Frequent errors in patient data entry.

What we need on the dashboard for the user:
1. Quick access to patient records.
2. Notifications for updates or changes in patient status.
3. Communication tools for team collaboration.
4. Visual indicators for patient progress (e.g., charts or graphs).
5. Alerts for any red flags or urgent issues that need attention.
