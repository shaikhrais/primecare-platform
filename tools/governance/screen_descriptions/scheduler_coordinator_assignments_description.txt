List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor and manage assignments for coordinators.
2. Review the status of ongoing assignments.
3. Update or modify assignments as necessary.
4. Communicate with team members regarding assignment details.
5. Analyze performance metrics related to assignments.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors or failures in loading assignment data.
2. Delays in assignment updates or modifications.
3. Lack of communication or feedback from team members.
4. High turnover or dissatisfaction among coordinators.
5. Inconsistent performance metrics or unexpected results.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current assignments and their statuses.
2. Notifications for any errors or issues with assignments.
3. Performance metrics and analytics for assignments.
4. Communication tools for interacting with team members.
5. Quick access to modify or update assignments.
