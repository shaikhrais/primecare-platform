Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Perform spinal adjustments and manipulations.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle and wellness strategies.
5. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rate of treatment failures or complications.
3. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
4. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
5. Unusual patterns in patient visits or cancellations.
6. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.
7. Failure to follow up on patient progress.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Patient management system with appointment scheduling.
2. Compliance audit logs and reports.
3. Real-time updates on treatment plans and patient progress.
4. Alerts for compliance issues or red flags.
5. Access to educational resources and updates on best practices.
6. Performance metrics and patient satisfaction scores.
7. Secure event reporting and incident management tools.
8. Integration with billing and insurance processing systems.
