List of work Customer Support must do:
1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Provide accurate information and solutions to customer problems.
3. Maintain a knowledge base for common issues and solutions.
4. Track customer interactions and feedback.
5. Escalate unresolved issues to higher-level support or management.
6. Follow up with customers to ensure satisfaction.
7. Document all customer interactions for future reference.
8. Train new support staff on processes and tools.
9. Analyze customer feedback to improve service quality.
10. Collaborate with other departments to resolve complex issues.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of unresolved tickets.
2. Increased response time to customer inquiries.
3. Frequent escalations to higher-level support.
4. Negative customer feedback or low satisfaction scores.
5. Lack of documentation for customer interactions.
6. High turnover rate among support staff.
7. Inconsistent information provided to customers.
8. Failure to follow up with customers after issue resolution.
9. Poor integration with other departments or tools.
10. Lack of training and resources for support staff.

Dashboard needs for Customer Support:
1. Real-time ticket status and resolution metrics.
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
3. Average response and resolution times.
4. Volume of incoming and outgoing customer interactions.
5. Escalation rates and reasons for escalation.
6. Knowledge base usage statistics.
7. Staff performance metrics and workload distribution.
8. Trends in common customer issues and inquiries.
9. Alerts for high-priority or unresolved tickets.
10. Integration with other tools and systems for seamless operations.
