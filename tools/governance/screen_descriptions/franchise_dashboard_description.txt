Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor operational performance metrics.
2. Conduct compliance audits regularly.
3. Synchronize security postures.
4. Update operational policies as needed.
5. Export and review audit logs.
6. Address any operational issues or discrepancies.
7. Ensure staff training and adherence to franchise standards.
8. Maintain communication with the franchisor.
9. Implement marketing strategies to boost sales.
10. Manage financial records and budgets.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in sales or customer footfall.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
4. Inconsistent operational procedures.
5. Negative customer feedback or reviews.
6. Delays in inventory replenishment.
7. Increased operational costs without clear justification.
8. Lack of engagement in training or development programs.
9. Poor communication with the franchisor.
10. Failure to implement recommended changes or strategies.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time operational performance metrics.
2. Compliance audit status and history.
3. Security posture overview.
4. Recent logs of operational activities.
5. Alerts for any red flags or issues.
6. Financial performance indicators (sales, expenses).
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction ratings.
8. Training and staff performance metrics.
9. Marketing campaign effectiveness.
10. Quick access to export logs and reports.
