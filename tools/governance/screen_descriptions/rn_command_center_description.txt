Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Patient assessment and monitoring.
2. Administering medications and treatments.
3. Collaborating with healthcare teams.
4. Educating patients and families about health conditions.
5. Documenting patient care and progress.
6. Ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations.
7. Participating in quality improvement initiatives.
8. Responding to patient emergencies.
9. Managing patient care plans.
10. Maintaining a safe and clean environment.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors.
3. High rates of patient falls or injuries.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. Inconsistent documentation practices.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
8. Lack of communication among team members.
9. Unresolved conflicts within the team.
10. Inadequate training or resources for staff.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Real-time patient status updates.
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care.
4. Logs of operational activities and incidents.
5. Access to patient education materials.
6. Metrics on medication administration accuracy.
7. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
8. Staff workload and availability overview.
9. Tools for communication with the healthcare team.
10. Resources for ongoing professional development.
