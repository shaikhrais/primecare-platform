User Work List:
1. Monitor daily metrics such as residents, staff on shift, open shifts, falls, incidents, and hospital transfers.
2. Track staff attendance and active outbreaks.
3. Analyze fall rates, pressure ulcers, wandering alerts, missed repositionings, weight loss risks, and dehydration alerts.
4. Review high-risk residents and ensure appropriate care ratios.
5. Manage overtime hours, sick calls, agency staff usage, and training completion rates.
6. Address expiring certifications and staff workload.
7. Ensure timely ADL charting, incident reports, and care plans.
8. Investigate privacy breaches and active abuse cases.
9. Monitor active infections and isolation counts.
10. Manage PPE inventory and hand hygiene audit scores.
11. Address active complaints and family calls.
12. Analyze mood trends, profit margins, payroll ratios, and outstanding payments.
13. Review revenue by service and therapist, room utilization, and inventory alerts.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of falls or incidents.
2. Increased open shifts or low staff attendance.
3. Elevated fall rates or pressure ulcers.
4. High counts of missed repositionings or dehydration alerts.
5. Expiring certifications or high sick calls.
6. Delays in ADL charting or overdue care plans.
7. Active complaints or investigations.
8. Low hand hygiene audit scores or inadequate PPE inventory.
9. Increased active infections or isolation counts.
10. Low satisfaction rates or high pending family calls.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time metrics for all key performance indicators.
2. Alerts for red flags and critical incidents.
3. Visualizations for trends in falls, incidents, and staff workload.
4. Summary of high-risk residents and their care needs.
5. Reports on staff attendance, overtime, and agency usage.
6. Notifications for expiring certifications and overdue tasks.
7. Financial metrics including profit margins and outstanding payments.
8. Inventory status for PPE and other critical supplies.
9. Feedback mechanisms for resident satisfaction and complaints.
10. Historical data for trend analysis and decision-making.
