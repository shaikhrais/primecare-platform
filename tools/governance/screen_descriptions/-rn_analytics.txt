Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement care plans.
3. Monitor and document patient progress.
4. Administer medications and treatments.
5. Educate patients and families about health conditions and care plans.
6. Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams.
7. Respond to patient needs and emergencies.
8. Maintain patient records and documentation.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Stay updated with medical knowledge and best practices.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High number of escalated alerts indicating potential patient risk.
2. Low average MMSE scores suggesting cognitive decline.
3. Delays in completing intakes or care plans.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
5. Increased frequency of patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Failure to follow up on escalated cases.
8. Inadequate training or resources for staff.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Average MMSE Score display.
2. Count of completed intakes.
3. Number of active care plans.
4. Count of high-risk escalations.
5. Evaluation logs with patient status and last tested dates.
6. Category filter for evaluations (All, Escalated, Stable).
7. Alerts for overdue assessments or follow-ups.
8. Performance metrics for care plans and patient outcomes.
9. Access to educational resources and updates.
10. Communication tools for team collaboration.
