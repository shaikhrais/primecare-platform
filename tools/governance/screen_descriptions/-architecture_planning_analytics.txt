Infrastructure Auditor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular audits of infrastructure components.
2. Assess compliance with security policies and regulations.
3. Identify vulnerabilities and risks in the infrastructure.
4. Review and analyze system performance metrics.
5. Ensure proper documentation of infrastructure configurations.
6. Evaluate disaster recovery and business continuity plans.
7. Collaborate with IT teams to implement corrective actions.
8. Maintain an inventory of all infrastructure assets.
9. Report findings and recommendations to management.
10. Stay updated on industry best practices and emerging technologies.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent system outages or downtime.
2. Unpatched vulnerabilities in software or hardware.
3. Inconsistent documentation or lack of documentation.
4. Unauthorized access attempts or security breaches.
5. Poor performance metrics indicating resource bottlenecks.
6. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
7. Lack of a clear disaster recovery plan.
8. High turnover rates in IT staff.
9. Inadequate training for personnel on infrastructure management.
10. Insufficient monitoring of infrastructure components.

Dashboard Requirements for Infrastructure Auditor:
1. Overview of infrastructure health and performance metrics.
2. Alerts for security incidents or compliance violations.
3. Visualization of system uptime and downtime statistics.
4. Inventory of infrastructure assets with status indicators.
5. Reports on audit findings and remediation actions.
6. Historical data trends for performance and security metrics.
7. Integration with monitoring tools for real-time data.
8. Access to documentation and policies related to infrastructure.
9. User activity logs for auditing access and changes.
10. Summary of ongoing and completed audit activities.
