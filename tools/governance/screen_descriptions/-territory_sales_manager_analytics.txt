Territory Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement sales strategies for assigned territories.
2. Identify and target potential customers within the territory.
3. Build and maintain relationships with existing clients.
4. Monitor market trends and competitor activities.
5. Analyze sales data to forecast future sales and set targets.
6. Collaborate with marketing teams to create promotional materials.
7. Conduct sales presentations and product demonstrations.
8. Provide training and support to sales representatives.
9. Prepare regular reports on sales performance and territory status.
10. Attend industry events and trade shows to network and promote products.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales figures over consecutive months.
2. High turnover rate among sales staff.
3. Lack of communication between sales and marketing teams.
4. Inconsistent reporting and data inaccuracies.
5. Low customer satisfaction and high complaint rates.
6. Failure to meet sales targets and quotas.
7. Poor territory coverage and missed opportunities.
8. Resistance to adopting new sales technologies or tools.
9. Limited engagement with key accounts.
10. Inadequate training and development for sales staff.

Dashboard Requirements for Territory Sales Manager:
1. Sales performance metrics (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2. Customer acquisition and retention rates.
3. Territory coverage maps and customer segmentation.
4. Sales pipeline status and forecasts.
5. Competitor analysis and market trends.
6. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Activity logs of sales representatives.
8. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking.
9. Integration with CRM systems for real-time data.
10. Alerts for red flags and operational issues.
