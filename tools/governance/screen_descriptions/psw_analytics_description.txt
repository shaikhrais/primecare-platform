List of tasks Personal Support Workers (PSWs) must perform:
1. Assist with activities of daily living (ADLs) such as bathing, grooming, meal preparation, and transferring assistance.
2. Monitor and document clients' hydration levels.
3. Conduct mood observations and report any significant changes in clients' emotional states.
4. Maintain accurate records of visits and mileage.
5. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare teams.
6. Follow care plans and ensure compliance with healthcare protocols.
7. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
8. Report any concerns or changes in clients' health to the appropriate healthcare professionals.

Red flags of operation for effective work:
1. Low ADL completion rates indicating potential issues in care delivery.
2. High levels of client anxiety or agitation reported in mood observations.
3. Significant discrepancies in recorded visits or mileage.
4. Inconsistent hydration levels among clients.
5. Frequent changes in mood observations that may indicate underlying issues.
6. Lack of communication or documentation regarding client care.

Dashboard requirements for Personal Support Workers (PSWs):
1. ADL completion rate metrics.
2. Total number of visits and shifts completed.
3. Average hydration levels logged for clients.
4. Total mileage traveled during client visits.
5. Breakdown of ADL performance by category (e.g., bathing, meal preparation).
6. Mood observation logs with alerts for caution or significant changes.
7. Filters to view data by different time frames (e.g., today, this week, this month).
8. Quick logging feature for mood observations.
9. Alerts or notifications for any red flags identified in client care.
