Infrastructure Auditor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits to ensure adherence to governance policies.
2. Monitor and analyze operational logs for anomalies and compliance status.
3. Validate security measures, including multi-factor authentication and real-time telemetry.
4. Report findings and recommend improvements based on audit results.
5. Maintain an up-to-date inventory of infrastructure components and their compliance status.
6. Collaborate with other teams to ensure security policies are enforced.
7. Document all audit processes and outcomes for accountability and future reference.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent non-compliance issues reported in audits.
2. Delays in executing compliance scans or audits.
3. Inconsistent logging of operational activities.
4. Unauthorized access attempts or security breaches.
5. Lack of updates to governance policies or security measures.
6. High volume of manual interventions required to maintain compliance.
7. Negative trends in compliance status over time.

Dashboard Requirements for Infrastructure Auditor:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators for all infrastructure components.
2. Logs of recent compliance audits and their outcomes.
3. Alerts for any non-compliance issues or security breaches.
4. Visual representation of trends in compliance over time.
5. Summary of governance policies and their current status.
6. Access to operational logs with filtering options for analysis.
7. Quick action buttons for executing compliance scans and refreshing data.
