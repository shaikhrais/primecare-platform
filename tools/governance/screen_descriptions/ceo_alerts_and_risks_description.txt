List of work for the user:
1. Monitor alerts and risks related to the organization's operations.
2. Review the data presented on the dashboard for insights into potential issues.
3. Respond to alerts in a timely manner to mitigate risks.
4. Collaborate with relevant teams based on alerts received.
5. Provide feedback on the effectiveness of the alerts and dashboard features.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors loading data on the dashboard.
2. Delays in receiving alerts or notifications.
3. Inconsistent data presentation or unclear information.
4. Lack of actionable insights from the alerts.
5. User feedback indicating difficulty in navigating the dashboard.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time alerts and notifications for critical risks.
2. Clear visual indicators for the status of alerts (e.g., resolved, pending).
3. Historical data trends to identify recurring issues.
4. User-friendly navigation and layout for quick access to information.
5. Feedback mechanism for users to report issues or suggest improvements.
