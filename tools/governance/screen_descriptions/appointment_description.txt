List of work the patient must do:
1. Schedule an appointment through the AppointmentScreen.
2. Review and confirm appointment details.
3. Complete any required pre-appointment paperwork.
4. Arrive on time for the appointment.
5. Follow any pre-appointment instructions provided by the healthcare provider.

Red flags of operation:
1. Failure to complete required paperwork before the appointment.
2. Arriving late or missing the appointment.
3. Not following pre-appointment instructions, which could affect the appointment outcome.
4. Any signs of distress or emergency that require immediate medical attention.

Effective work strategies:
1. Ensure clear communication of appointment details and requirements to the patient.
2. Provide reminders for appointments and necessary preparations.
3. Monitor patient compliance with pre-appointment instructions.
4. Offer support for any issues that may arise before the appointment.

Dashboard needs for the patient:
1. Overview of upcoming appointments and their statuses.
2. Notifications for any required actions or paperwork.
3. Access to educational materials related to the appointment.
4. A log of past appointments and any follow-up actions required.
5. Contact information for the healthcare provider for any questions or concerns.
