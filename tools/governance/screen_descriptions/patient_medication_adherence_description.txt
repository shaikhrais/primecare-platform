User Work List:
1. Monitor patient medication adherence.
2. Input and update patient medication schedules.
3. Review patient adherence reports.
4. Identify patients with low adherence rates.
5. Communicate with patients regarding their medication.
6. Set reminders for patients to take their medications.
7. Analyze trends in medication adherence over time.
8. Collaborate with healthcare providers to improve adherence.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High percentage of patients with low adherence rates.
2. Frequent missed medication reminders.
3. Lack of patient engagement or communication.
4. Inconsistent data entry or updates on medication schedules.
5. Delays in reporting adherence metrics.
6. Increased patient inquiries about medication schedules.
7. Patterns of non-adherence linked to specific medications.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of patient adherence rates (percentage and trends).
2. List of patients with low adherence and their medication details.
3. Alerts for missed medication reminders.
4. Communication logs with patients regarding adherence.
5. Graphical representation of adherence trends over time.
6. Quick access to update patient medication schedules.
7. Summary of recent patient interactions and follow-ups.
8. Integration with healthcare provider notes and recommendations.
