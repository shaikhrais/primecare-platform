Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and staff performance.
2. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
3. Conduct regular audits and performance evaluations.
4. Implement quality improvement initiatives.
5. Manage staff training and development.
6. Coordinate with other departments for integrated care.
7. Monitor patient outcomes and satisfaction.
8. Address and resolve operational issues.
9. Maintain accurate records and documentation.
10. Communicate effectively with stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High staff turnover rates.
2. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
4. Declining patient outcomes or safety incidents.
5. Inefficient workflows leading to delays in care.
6. Lack of staff engagement or morale issues.
7. Inconsistent performance metrics across teams.
8. Unresolved operational issues or conflicts.
9. Insufficient training or resources for staff.
10. Poor communication between departments.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for staff performance.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
4. Operational efficiency metrics (e.g., wait times).
5. Staff training and development progress.
6. Incident reports and resolution status.
7. Real-time telemetry data for patient care.
8. Logs of operational audits and compliance scans.
9. Alerts for any red flags or issues needing attention.
10. Visualizations of trends in patient outcomes and safety.
