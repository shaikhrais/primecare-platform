Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Recruit and onboard volunteers.
2. Schedule and coordinate volunteer activities.
3. Provide training and support to volunteers.
4. Maintain communication with volunteers.
5. Track volunteer hours and contributions.
6. Organize volunteer appreciation events.
7. Evaluate volunteer performance and provide feedback.
8. Manage volunteer records and data.
9. Collaborate with other departments for volunteer needs.
10. Ensure compliance with organizational policies and procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rates.
2. Lack of engagement or communication from volunteers.
3. Inconsistent tracking of volunteer hours.
4. Low attendance at scheduled volunteer events.
5. Negative feedback from volunteers regarding their experience.
6. Difficulty in recruiting volunteers for specific roles.
7. Incomplete or inaccurate volunteer records.
8. Failure to recognize or appreciate volunteer contributions.
9. Overlapping or conflicting volunteer schedules.
10. Insufficient training or support for volunteers.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Volunteer recruitment metrics (number of new volunteers).
2. Volunteer engagement statistics (event attendance, feedback).
3. Hours tracked per volunteer and total hours contributed.
4. Upcoming volunteer events and schedules.
5. Volunteer performance evaluations and feedback summaries.
6. Communication logs with volunteers.
7. Recognition and appreciation initiatives.
8. Compliance status with organizational policies.
9. Trends in volunteer turnover and retention.
10. Resource allocation for volunteer training and support.
