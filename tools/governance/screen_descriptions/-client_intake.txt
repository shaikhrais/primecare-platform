Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage client intake process.
2. Conduct compliance scans and audits.
3. Maintain accurate logs of operations.
4. Monitor security clearances.
5. Coordinate with other departments for client onboarding.
6. Provide updates and reports on intake status.
7. Address client inquiries and concerns.
8. Ensure adherence to governance and compliance standards.
9. Facilitate communication between clients and clinical staff.
10. Analyze intake metrics and performance indicators.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased error rates in compliance scans.
2. Delays in client onboarding processes.
3. High volume of client complaints or inquiries.
4. Inconsistent logging of operational activities.
5. Security clearance issues or exceptions.
6. Lack of communication between departments.
7. Low engagement with intake metrics or dashboards.
8. Frequent system outages or technical issues.
9. Non-compliance with governance standards.
10. High turnover rates in the intake team.

Dashboard Needs for Intake Coordinator:
1. Real-time status of client intake processes.
2. Compliance scan results and audit logs.
3. Metrics on active operations and productivity.
4. Security clearance levels and exceptions.
5. Telemetry data for operational performance.
6. Log of recent activities and actions taken.
7. Alerts for any compliance or operational issues.
8. Historical data trends for analysis.
9. Client feedback and satisfaction ratings.
10. Quick access to communication tools for client interaction.
