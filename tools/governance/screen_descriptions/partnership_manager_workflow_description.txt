Partnership Manager Responsibilities:
1. Identify potential partners and establish relationships.
2. Negotiate partnership agreements and contracts.
3. Monitor partnership performance and ensure compliance with agreements.
4. Coordinate with internal teams to align partnership goals with company objectives.
5. Develop and implement partnership strategies to maximize value.
6. Conduct regular reviews and assessments of partnerships.
7. Manage communication and reporting with partners.
8. Address any issues or conflicts that arise in partnerships.
9. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities.
10. Provide training and support to internal teams regarding partnerships.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Lack of communication or responsiveness from partners.
2. Decreased performance metrics or KPIs related to partnerships.
3. Frequent conflicts or issues arising in partnership agreements.
4. Inconsistent reporting or data from partners.
5. Changes in partner management or key personnel.
6. Negative feedback from internal teams regarding partnerships.
7. Failure to meet partnership goals or objectives.
8. Lack of alignment between partnership strategies and company goals.
9. High turnover rates in partner organizations.
10. Unclear or vague partnership agreements.

Dashboard Requirements for Partnership Manager:
1. Overview of active partnerships with performance metrics.
2. Alerts for any red flags or issues in partnerships.
3. KPIs tracking for each partnership (e.g., revenue generated, engagement levels).
4. Communication logs with partners.
5. Upcoming renewal dates or contract expirations.
6. Summary of partnership goals and progress towards them.
7. Integration of feedback from internal teams regarding partnerships.
8. Industry trends and competitor analysis related to partnerships.
9. Visual representation of partnership performance over time.
10. Tools for scheduling and managing partnership meetings or reviews.
