List of work Chiropractor must do:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Monitor patient progress and modify treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.

Red flags for operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Unexplained changes in patient progress or outcomes.
3. High rates of patient dropouts or cancellations.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Failure to adhere to compliance and regulatory standards.
6. Signs of burnout or stress among staff.
7. Equipment malfunctions or safety concerns.

Dashboard needs for Chiropractor:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient outcomes.
2. Live telemetry logs of operational metrics.
3. Compliance standings and audit results.
4. Active operations status and productivity metrics.
5. Security clearance levels and exceptions logged.
6. Operational audit logs for transparency.
7. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Alerts for any red flags or compliance issues.
