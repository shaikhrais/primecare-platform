List of tasks a Social Worker must perform:
1. Conduct assessments and evaluations of clients.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Provide counseling and support to clients.
4. Coordinate services and resources for clients.
5. Advocate for clients' needs and rights.
6. Maintain accurate and confidential records.
7. Monitor client progress and adjust plans as necessary.
8. Collaborate with other professionals and agencies.
9. Participate in training and professional development.
10. Stay informed about community resources and services.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work:
1. High caseloads leading to burnout.
2. Lack of communication with clients or team members.
3. Incomplete or inaccurate documentation.
4. Missed appointments or follow-ups with clients.
5. Failure to meet compliance or regulatory standards.
6. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
7. Inconsistent application of policies and procedures.
8. Limited access to resources or support services.
9. High turnover rates within the team.
10. Difficulty in tracking client outcomes and progress.

Essential elements for the Social Worker dashboard:
1. Active operations metrics (e.g., number of active cases).
2. Compliance status indicators (e.g., audit results).
3. Security clearance levels.
4. System performance metrics (e.g., latency, uptime).
5. Data integrity status (e.g., accuracy of records).
6. Telemetry logs for monitoring activities.
7. Audit logs for tracking compliance and operational history.
8. Notifications for upcoming deadlines or important events.
9. Client progress tracking tools.
10. Resource allocation and availability indicators.
