Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and strategies.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Foster a positive company culture and employee engagement.
7. Collaborate with other executives to align operations with business goals.
8. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
9. Lead operational audits and compliance checks.
10. Communicate operational updates to stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining performance metrics or KPIs.
2. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
3. High employee turnover or low morale.
4. Frequent compliance issues or regulatory violations.
5. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
6. Lack of clear communication among teams.
7. Customer complaints or dissatisfaction trends.
8. Inefficient resource utilization or waste.
9. Inconsistent reporting or data discrepancies.
10. Resistance to change or innovation within the team.

Dashboard Needs for COO:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Financial summaries and budget tracking.
4. Employee engagement and turnover statistics.
5. Operational efficiency indicators.
6. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Project timelines and status updates.
8. Resource allocation and utilization reports.
9. Risk management and incident reports.
10. Logs of operational activities and audits.
