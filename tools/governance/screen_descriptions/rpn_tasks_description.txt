Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Tasks:
1. Conduct patient assessments.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Monitor and document patient vital signs.
4. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, etc.).
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
6. Provide patient education and support.
7. Maintain patient records and documentation.
8. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
9. Participate in care planning and evaluation.
10. Respond to patient emergencies and provide first aid.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
4. High staff turnover or low morale.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Unusual patterns in patient vital signs.
8. Lack of communication among team members.
9. Inadequate staffing levels.
10. Failure to follow up on patient concerns.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Practical Nurse (RPN):
1. Real-time patient monitoring metrics (vital signs, medication administration).
2. Compliance status indicators (audit results, safety checks).
3. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
4. Staffing levels and workload distribution.
5. Alerts for critical patient conditions or red flags.
6. Access to patient care plans and documentation.
7. Logs of daily activities and interventions.
8. Communication tools for team collaboration.
9. Training and compliance resources.
10. Performance metrics and goals tracking.
