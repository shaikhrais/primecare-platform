Legal Counsel Responsibilities:
1. Review and draft legal documents.
2. Provide legal advice to the organization.
3. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations.
4. Manage legal risks and disputes.
5. Conduct legal research.
6. Liaise with external legal counsel when necessary.
7. Monitor changes in legislation that may affect the organization.
8. Develop and implement policies and procedures.
9. Train staff on legal compliance and best practices.
10. Maintain records of legal documents and correspondence.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Inconsistent compliance with regulations.
2. High volume of legal disputes or litigation.
3. Delays in contract approvals or renewals.
4. Lack of documentation for legal processes.
5. Poor communication between departments regarding legal matters.
6. Frequent changes in legislation affecting operations.
7. Insufficient training for staff on legal issues.
8. Negative feedback from external legal counsel.
9. Unresolved legal risks identified in audits.
10. Increased legal costs without clear justification.

Dashboard Requirements for Legal Counsel:
1. Compliance status indicators.
2. Active legal disputes and their statuses.
3. Key performance metrics related to legal operations.
4. Recent legal documents drafted or reviewed.
5. Audit logs of legal activities.
6. Alerts for upcoming deadlines or renewals.
7. Training completion rates for staff on legal compliance.
8. Summary of recent legal research findings.
9. External counsel engagement metrics.
10. Visual representation of legal risks and their mitigation status.
