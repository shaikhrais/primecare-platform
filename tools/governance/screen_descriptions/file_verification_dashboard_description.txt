Governance Officer Responsibilities:
1. Oversee compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
3. Monitor operational performance and risk management.
4. Ensure data integrity and security.
5. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
6. Maintain documentation and records of governance activities.
7. Provide training and support on governance policies.
8. Analyze and report on governance metrics.
9. Implement corrective actions for compliance failures.
10. Stay updated on regulatory changes and best practices.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit completion or reporting.
3. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
4. Lack of communication or transparency in operations.
5. Inconsistent data or reporting discrepancies.
6. Negative feedback from stakeholders or employees.
7. Failure to implement corrective actions from previous audits.
8. Unresolved security incidents or breaches.
9. Low engagement in governance training sessions.
10. High levels of operational downtime or inefficiency.

Dashboard Requirements for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance status indicators (green/yellow/red).
2. Audit logs and history of compliance scans.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to governance.
4. Real-time telemetry data and operational metrics.
5. Alerts for compliance violations or red flags.
6. Summary of recent governance activities and actions taken.
7. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and reporting.
8. Access to training resources and documentation.
9. Visualizations of trends in compliance and operational performance.
10. Feedback mechanisms for stakeholders to report issues or concerns.
