User Tasks:
1. Draft research publications.
2. Edit and revise drafts.
3. Collaborate with co-authors.
4. Submit drafts for review.
5. Track submission status.
6. Incorporate feedback from reviewers.
7. Finalize and publish research papers.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Lack of collaboration tools leading to communication issues.
2. Delays in feedback from co-authors or reviewers.
3. Difficulty in tracking submission status.
4. Inconsistent formatting or citation styles.
5. Limited access to necessary research resources.
6. Technical issues with the drafting platform.
7. Insufficient version control for drafts.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Task progress tracker for each publication.
2. Collaboration status indicators (e.g., pending feedback).
3. Submission status overview (submitted, under review, accepted, rejected).
4. Notifications for deadlines and feedback.
5. Access to research resources and citation tools.
6. Version history of drafts.
7. Performance metrics (e.g., average time to publication).
