Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess governance frameworks.
3. Conduct audits and compliance scans.
4. Maintain logs of operational activities.
5. Identify and report risks and issues.
6. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
7. Develop and implement governance strategies.
8. Provide training and support on governance matters.
9. Review and update governance documentation.
10. Analyze performance metrics and compliance standings.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent compliance failures or audit issues.
2. Inconsistent or incomplete documentation.
3. Lack of timely communication regarding governance matters.
4. High number of unresolved risks or incidents.
5. Low engagement or participation in governance activities.
6. Delays in executing compliance scans or audits.
7. Negative trends in performance metrics.
8. Unclear roles and responsibilities among team members.
9. Insufficient training or resources for governance practices.
10. Poor stakeholder feedback on governance processes.

Dashboard Needs for Governance Officer:
1. Overview of compliance status and audit results.
2. Real-time logs of operational activities.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to governance.
4. Alerts for compliance failures or red flags.
5. Visual representation of risk assessments.
6. Metrics on stakeholder engagement and training effectiveness.
7. Historical data on governance performance trends.
8. Action items and follow-up tasks for governance initiatives.
9. Summary of recent governance meetings and decisions.
10. Access to relevant documentation and resources.
