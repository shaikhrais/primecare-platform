Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess operational risks.
3. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
4. Conduct audits and compliance scans.
5. Maintain accurate records and logs of governance activities.
6. Provide training and support on governance-related matters.
7. Develop and implement governance frameworks and policies.
8. Report on governance performance and issues to senior management.
9. Identify and address governance-related red flags.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit processes or compliance scans.
3. Lack of communication or transparency among stakeholders.
4. Unresolved operational risks or issues.
5. Inconsistent or incomplete documentation.
6. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
7. Negative feedback from audits or assessments.

Dashboard needs for a Governance Officer:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Audit logs and historical compliance data.
3. Risk assessment metrics and trends.
4. Stakeholder communication logs.
5. Performance metrics for governance activities.
6. Alerts for compliance violations or operational issues.
7. Visualizations of audit results and compliance scans.
8. Access to training resources and governance frameworks.
