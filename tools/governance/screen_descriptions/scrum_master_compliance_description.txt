List of tasks a Scrum Master must do:
1. Facilitate daily stand-ups and ensure they are effective.
2. Remove impediments that hinder the team's progress.
3. Coach the team on Agile principles and practices.
4. Ensure the team adheres to Scrum ceremonies (sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives).
5. Foster a collaborative team environment.
6. Protect the team from external interruptions.
7. Help the Product Owner manage the product backlog.
8. Track and report team progress and metrics.
9. Conduct regular retrospectives to improve team processes.
10. Support continuous improvement initiatives.

Red flags of operation indicating ineffective work:
1. Frequent missed deadlines or sprint goals.
2. Low team morale or high turnover.
3. Lack of communication or collaboration within the team.
4. Repeatedly unresolved impediments.
5. Poor quality of deliverables or high defect rates.
6. Resistance to change or Agile practices.
7. Inconsistent attendance at Scrum ceremonies.
8. Lack of engagement from team members.
9. Over-reliance on the Scrum Master for decision-making.
10. Confusion about roles and responsibilities within the team.

Dashboard needs for a Scrum Master:
1. Sprint progress metrics (burn-down charts, velocity).
2. Impediment tracking and resolution status.
3. Team member engagement levels.
4. Quality metrics (defect rates, code quality).
5. Backlog health (items ready for the next sprint).
6. Attendance and participation in Scrum ceremonies.
7. Feedback from retrospectives and action items.
8. Overall team performance trends over time.
9. Compliance with Agile principles and practices.
10. Visual representation of team dynamics and collaboration levels.
