List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor accounts receivable balances.
2. Review outstanding invoices.
3. Follow up on overdue payments.
4. Generate accounts receivable reports.
5. Analyze cash flow related to receivables.
6. Communicate with clients regarding payment issues.

Red flags of operation:
1. High percentage of overdue invoices.
2. Significant fluctuations in accounts receivable balances.
3. Increased customer complaints regarding billing.
4. Delays in payment processing.
5. Unusually high write-offs or bad debt.

What we need on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of total accounts receivable.
2. Aging report of receivables (current, 30 days, 60 days, 90 days).
3. Alerts for overdue invoices.
4. Payment trends and cash flow forecasts.
5. Quick access to generate reports.
6. Client communication history regarding payments.
