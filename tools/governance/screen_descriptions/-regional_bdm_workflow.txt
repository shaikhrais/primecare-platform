List of tasks for Regional BDM:
1. Monitor regional sales performance.
2. Develop and implement sales strategies.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key clients.
4. Conduct market research to identify new opportunities.
5. Collaborate with marketing to align campaigns with sales goals.
6. Provide training and support to sales teams.
7. Analyze sales data and prepare reports for management.
8. Attend industry events and conferences to network.
9. Address customer inquiries and resolve issues.
10. Set and track sales targets for the region.

Red flags in operations:
1. Declining sales figures over consecutive months.
2. High turnover rates within the sales team.
3. Lack of communication between regional and corporate teams.
4. Inconsistent reporting of sales data.
5. Customer complaints increasing in frequency.
6. Failure to meet sales targets.
7. Low engagement in training programs.
8. Delays in responding to client inquiries.
9. Poor feedback from client satisfaction surveys.
10. Unclear or conflicting sales strategies.

Dashboard needs for Regional BDM:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics.
2. Visual representation of sales trends over time.
3. Client relationship management tools.
4. Market analysis and opportunity identification features.
5. Training and support resources for sales teams.
6. Alerts for declining performance or red flags.
7. Integration with marketing campaign data.
8. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
9. Sales target tracking and forecasting tools.
10. Collaboration features for team communication.
