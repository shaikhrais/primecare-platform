List of work the User must do:
1. Monitor client inquiries and issues.
2. Respond to client communications in a timely manner.
3. Track and document client interactions and resolutions.
4. Collaborate with other teams to resolve client issues.
5. Analyze client feedback for service improvement.
6. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of products and services.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased response times to client inquiries.
2. High volume of unresolved client issues.
3. Frequent client complaints or negative feedback.
4. Lack of communication between team members.
5. Inconsistent documentation of client interactions.
6. Low client satisfaction scores.

What we need on the dashboard of User:
1. Real-time metrics on response times and resolution rates.
2. Overview of open and closed client issues.
3. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
4. Alerts for high-priority client issues.
5. Team collaboration tools and communication logs.
6. Performance trends over time for continuous improvement.
