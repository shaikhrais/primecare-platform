User Work Tasks:
1. Monitor the inpatient pharmacy queue for new prescriptions.
2. Review and verify prescription details.
3. Prepare medications for inpatient delivery.
4. Communicate with healthcare providers regarding any prescription issues.
5. Update the status of prescriptions in the system.
6. Track medication inventory levels.
7. Generate reports on prescription processing times.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in prescription processing.
2. High error rates in prescription verification.
3. Inconsistent communication with healthcare providers.
4. Low medication inventory levels leading to stockouts.
5. Increased patient complaints regarding medication delivery times.
6. Lack of visibility into the status of prescriptions.

Dashboard Requirements for User:
1. Real-time updates on the status of the pharmacy queue.
2. Alerts for delayed prescriptions or inventory shortages.
3. Metrics on prescription processing times and error rates.
4. Communication logs with healthcare providers.
5. Inventory levels and alerts for low stock items.
6. User-friendly interface for quick access to critical information.
