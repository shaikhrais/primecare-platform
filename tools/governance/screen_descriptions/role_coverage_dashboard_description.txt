Governance Officer Responsibilities:
1. Monitor compliance with governance policies and regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments of operational processes.
3. Maintain and update governance documentation and frameworks.
4. Facilitate training and awareness programs on governance practices.
5. Report on governance performance metrics to stakeholders.
6. Identify and mitigate risks associated with governance failures.
7. Collaborate with other departments to ensure alignment with governance objectives.
8. Manage governance-related incidents and investigations.
9. Ensure timely execution of compliance scans and audits.
10. Maintain logs of governance activities and decisions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations or audit findings.
2. Delays in executing compliance scans or audits.
3. Lack of engagement or awareness among staff regarding governance policies.
4. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
5. Inconsistent documentation of governance activities.
6. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding governance effectiveness.
7. Failure to address identified risks or issues in a timely manner.
8. Unclear roles and responsibilities related to governance tasks.
9. Poor communication regarding governance changes or updates.
10. Lack of metrics or data to assess governance performance.

Dashboard Needs for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance status indicators (e.g., percentage of compliance achieved).
2. Audit findings and trends over time.
3. Logs of governance activities and actions taken.
4. Risk assessment metrics and identified risks.
5. Training completion rates for governance-related programs.
6. Incident reports and resolutions related to governance.
7. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for governance effectiveness.
8. Real-time updates on compliance scan results.
9. Stakeholder feedback and satisfaction scores.
10. Visualizations of operational metrics related to governance.
