Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
3. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, feeding).
4. Provide wound care and change dressings.
5. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to develop care plans.
7. Educate patients and families about health management.
8. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
9. Participate in patient assessments and evaluations.
10. Maintain a clean and safe environment for patients.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Unexplained changes in patient vital signs.
2. Increase in medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. Frequent patient complaints about care or treatment.
4. Signs of neglect or inadequate patient supervision.
5. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
6. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
7. Non-compliance with infection control protocols.
8. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
9. Delays in patient care or treatment.
10. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the nursing team.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Practical Nurse (RPN):
1. Real-time patient vital signs monitoring.
2. Medication administration records and alerts.
3. Daily task lists and reminders for patient care.
4. Compliance tracking for health and safety regulations.
5. Incident reporting and tracking system.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Patient education resources and materials.
8. Performance metrics and quality indicators.
9. Access to patient medical history and care plans.
10. Log of completed tasks and patient interactions.
