Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence (emails, phone calls, etc.).
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with budgeting and financial tracking.
6. Coordinate office activities and events.
7. Provide support to other staff members.
8. Maintain office supplies and inventory.
9. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.
10. Assist in onboarding new employees.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Missed deadlines or appointments.
2. Poor communication with team members.
3. Inconsistent record-keeping.
4. Lack of organization in files and documents.
5. Frequent errors in reports or correspondence.
6. Low morale or dissatisfaction among team members.
7. High turnover rates in administrative staff.
8. Inability to adapt to new tools or processes.
9. Over-reliance on manual processes.
10. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Calendar view for appointments and deadlines.
2. Task list with priority levels.
3. Communication log (emails, calls, messages).
4. Document management system for easy access to files.
5. Performance metrics (e.g., tasks completed, deadlines met).
6. Inventory tracker for office supplies.
7. Budget overview and expense tracking.
8. Feedback and survey tools for team satisfaction.
9. Alerts for upcoming deadlines or important events.
10. Integration with other tools (e.g., email, project management software).
