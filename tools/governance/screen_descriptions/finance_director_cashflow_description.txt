List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor cash flow reports.
2. Analyze financial data for trends.
3. Prepare financial forecasts.
4. Review budget allocations.
5. Identify discrepancies in cash flow.
6. Communicate with other departments regarding financial needs.
7. Ensure compliance with financial regulations.
8. Make strategic decisions based on cash flow analysis.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant fluctuations in cash flow.
2. Delays in financial reporting.
3. Unexplained discrepancies in financial data.
4. Increased frequency of budget overruns.
5. Lack of communication with other departments.
6. Non-compliance with financial regulations.
7. Inability to meet financial obligations.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time cash flow metrics.
2. Alerts for significant cash flow changes.
3. Visualizations of cash flow trends.
4. Budget vs. actual spending reports.
5. Compliance status indicators.
6. Communication tools for inter-departmental collaboration.
7. Access to historical financial data for analysis.
8. Forecasting tools for future cash flow predictions.
