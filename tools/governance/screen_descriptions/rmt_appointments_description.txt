List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) must perform:
1. Conduct client assessments and gather medical history.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on client needs.
3. Perform various types of massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate and confidential client records.
5. Educate clients on self-care and wellness practices.
6. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
8. Comply with local regulations and ethical standards.
9. Manage appointment scheduling and client communications.
10. Handle billing and insurance claims as necessary.

Red flags of operation for RMTs:
1. High cancellation or no-show rates.
2. Frequent client complaints or negative feedback.
3. Inconsistent treatment outcomes or client satisfaction.
4. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
5. Incomplete or inaccurate client records.
6. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
7. Poor communication with clients or team members.
8. Burnout or high turnover rates among staff.
9. Inadequate training or professional development opportunities.
10. Failure to keep up with industry standards and practices.

Dashboard needs for Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs):
1. Overview of upcoming appointments and client schedules.
2. Compliance and audit logs for tracking operational integrity.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., client satisfaction scores, treatment outcomes).
4. Real-time updates on client feedback and complaints.
5. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
6. Access to educational resources and training opportunities.
7. Financial metrics (e.g., revenue, billing status).
8. Health and safety compliance indicators.
9. Communication tools for client engagement.
10. Reporting tools for analyzing trends and operational efficiency.
