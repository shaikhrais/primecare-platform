List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the growth pipeline metrics.
2. Analyze data trends and performance indicators.
3. Identify areas for improvement in the growth strategy.
4. Collaborate with team members to implement changes.
5. Review and adjust goals based on performance data.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant drop in key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
3. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
4. Lack of engagement in team meetings or discussions.
5. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in metrics.

What we need on the dashboard for the user:
1. Real-time growth metrics and KPIs.
2. Visualizations of data trends (graphs and charts).
3. Alerts for any red flags or anomalies in performance.
4. Task management features for tracking team responsibilities.
5. Collaboration tools for team communication and feedback.
