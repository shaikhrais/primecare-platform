User Tasks:
1. Schedule appointments for intake coordination.
2. Monitor and manage incoming requests.
3. Communicate with clients regarding their intake status.
4. Update client information as needed.
5. Generate reports on scheduling efficiency and client intake.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of missed appointments.
2. Delays in responding to client requests.
3. Frequent errors in client data entry.
4. Low client satisfaction ratings.
5. Inconsistent scheduling patterns.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of scheduled appointments and availability.
2. Alerts for missed or upcoming appointments.
3. Client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
4. Performance metrics on response times and data accuracy.
5. Visual representation of scheduling trends over time.
