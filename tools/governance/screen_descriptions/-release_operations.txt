Governance Officer Responsibilities:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor operational performance and governance metrics.
3. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
4. Maintain logs of operational activities and incidents.
5. Facilitate communication between stakeholders regarding governance issues.
6. Provide training and resources on governance best practices.
7. Analyze data to identify trends and areas for improvement.
8. Report on governance status and issues to senior management.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased frequency of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit completion or reporting.
3. Unresolved incidents or issues in operational logs.
4. Low engagement or participation in governance training.
5. Significant changes in operational metrics or performance indicators.
6. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding governance processes.
7. High turnover or dissatisfaction among governance staff.

Dashboard Requirements for Governance Officer:
1. Real-time compliance status and metrics.
2. Summary of recent audit findings and actions taken.
3. Operational logs with filtering options for quick access.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to governance.
5. Alerts for any compliance violations or red flags.
6. Visualizations of trends in operational performance.
7. Access to training resources and documentation.
8. Communication tools for stakeholder engagement.
