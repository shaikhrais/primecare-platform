Home Support Worker Responsibilities:
1. Conduct home visits for patient care.
2. Check-in and check-out of visits via the system.
3. Trigger emergency alerts when necessary.
4. Perform compliance audits and scans.
5. Update policies and procedures as required.
6. Maintain accurate logs of activities and incidents.
7. Sync security posture and ensure compliance with regulations.
8. Export logs for audits and reviews.
9. Communicate effectively with patients and healthcare teams.
10. Document patient progress and any changes in condition.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in check-in/check-out processes.
2. Frequent network or API errors during operations.
3. Inconsistent logging of activities.
4. High number of compliance issues or audit failures.
5. Unresolved emergency alerts or incidents.
6. Lack of synchronization with security protocols.
7. Low engagement or response from patients.
8. Missing or incomplete documentation of visits.

Dashboard Requirements for Home Support Worker:
1. Real-time status of active operations.
2. Security clearance level and compliance status.
3. Logs of recent activities and incidents.
4. Metrics on visit check-ins and check-outs.
5. Alerts for any unresolved issues or emergencies.
6. Compliance audit results and history.
7. Visual representation of telemetry data (e.g., charts).
8. Quick access buttons for key actions (e.g., check-in, check-out, trigger alert).
9. Notifications for policy updates or required actions.
10. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and operation.
