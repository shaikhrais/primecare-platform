Partnership Manager Responsibilities:
1. Conduct compliance audits.
2. Monitor operational compliance checks.
3. Manage security policies and ensure they are up to date.
4. Facilitate secure event reporting and ingestion.
5. Maintain logs of operational activities and compliance status.
6. Trigger necessary governance actions.
7. Update and enforce governance directives.
8. Collaborate with other departments for compliance-related issues.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent compliance audit failures.
2. Delays in executing compliance scans.
3. Inconsistent logging of operational activities.
4. High number of security policy violations.
5. Lack of real-time telemetry data.
6. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
7. Low engagement in secure event reporting.

Dashboard Requirements for Partnership Manager:
1. Real-time compliance audit status.
2. Summary of recent compliance verification audits.
3. Logs of operational activities with timestamps.
4. Alerts for any compliance failures or issues.
5. Status of governance directives (enabled/disabled).
6. Metrics on the number of secure event reports submitted.
7. Visual representation of compliance trends over time.
8. Quick access buttons for executing compliance scans and triggering governance actions.
