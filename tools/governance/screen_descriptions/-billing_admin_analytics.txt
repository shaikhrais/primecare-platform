Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with billing and invoicing.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Maintain office supplies and inventory.
8. Support team members with administrative tasks.
9. Manage databases and data entry.
10. Ensure compliance with company policies.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Missed deadlines or appointments.
2. Inconsistent communication with team members.
3. Disorganized files and records.
4. Frequent errors in billing or invoicing.
5. Lack of responsiveness to requests.
6. High turnover of administrative staff.
7. Poor time management and prioritization.
8. Inadequate tracking of tasks and projects.
9. Low morale or dissatisfaction among team members.
10. Inefficient use of office resources.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview.
2. Calendar and scheduling integration.
3. Communication logs and correspondence tracking.
4. Billing and invoicing status updates.
5. File and document management system.
6. Performance metrics and KPIs.
7. Inventory and supply tracking.
8. Meeting and event coordination tools.
9. Compliance checklists and reminders.
10. Feedback and support request system.
