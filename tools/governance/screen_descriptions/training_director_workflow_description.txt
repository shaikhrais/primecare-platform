List of work for Training Candidates:
1. Execute compliance scans to ensure adherence to training standards.
2. Monitor operational logs for any anomalies or issues.
3. Review and validate telemetry data for accuracy.
4. Trigger manual sweeps for additional verification.
5. Maintain clear communication regarding the status of training operations.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent API errors during compliance scans.
2. High number of network errors indicating connectivity issues.
3. Inconsistencies in telemetry data or operational logs.
4. Delays in executing scheduled tasks or scans.
5. Low clearance status indicating potential exceptions flagged.

Dashboard needs for Training Candidates:
1. Real-time status of compliance scans and their results.
2. Operational logs with timestamps for tracking issues.
3. Metrics on active training streams and their performance.
4. Alerts for any red flags or issues that require immediate attention.
5. Historical data trends for telemetry to identify patterns over time.
