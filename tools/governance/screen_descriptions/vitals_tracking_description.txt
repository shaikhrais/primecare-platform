Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Monitor and record vital signs.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Assist with patient hygiene and mobility.
4. Provide emotional support to patients and families.
5. Document patient care and progress.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
7. Educate patients about health management.
8. Ensure compliance with safety and health regulations.
9. Participate in patient assessments and care planning.
10. Respond to patient needs and emergencies.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Inconsistent or abnormal vital signs.
2. Delays in medication administration.
3. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Lack of communication among team members.
5. Frequent errors in documentation.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. High turnover rates among staff.
8. Insufficient training or resources for staff.
9. Unresolved patient issues or complaints.
10. Inadequate follow-up on patient care plans.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Real-time vital sign monitoring.
2. Medication administration tracking.
3. Patient care logs and documentation.
4. Alerts for abnormal vital signs or patient status changes.
5. Communication tools for team collaboration.
6. Compliance tracking for safety protocols.
7. Patient satisfaction metrics.
8. Training resources and updates.
9. Access to patient care plans and assessments.
10. Reporting tools for operational audits and performance metrics.
