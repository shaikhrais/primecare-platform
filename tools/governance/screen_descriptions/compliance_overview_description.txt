Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Ensure compliance with operational standards.
2. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
3. Conduct regular audits and compliance checks.
4. Provide training and support to staff.
5. Manage inventory and supply chain effectively.
6. Maintain customer service quality.
7. Implement marketing strategies to drive sales.
8. Handle financial reporting and budgeting.
9. Address customer feedback and complaints.
10. Stay updated on industry regulations and best practices.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or customer footfall.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative reviews.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Inconsistent inventory levels or stockouts.
6. Poor financial performance or cash flow issues.
7. Lack of staff training or development.
8. Failure to meet franchise standards or guidelines.
9. Unresolved operational issues or delays.
10. Negative trends in key performance indicators.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time sales and revenue metrics.
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
3. Inventory levels and supply chain status.
4. Employee performance and turnover rates.
5. Compliance audit results and logs.
6. Marketing campaign performance metrics.
7. Financial health indicators (profit margins, expenses).
8. Operational efficiency metrics (service times, wait times).
9. Alerts for any compliance or operational issues.
10. Historical performance trends for analysis.
