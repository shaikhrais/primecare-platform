Chiropractor Work Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Perform spinal manipulations and adjustments.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
5. Document treatment notes and patient progress.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and research.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Unexplained changes in treatment outcomes.
3. High rates of patient dropouts or cancellations.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing treatment notes.
5. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
6. Frequent errors in billing or coding.
7. Staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
8. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Overview of active patient cases and treatment plans.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to patient outcomes.
4. Real-time telemetry logs of treatment effectiveness.
5. Alerts for any red flags or compliance issues.
6. Access to patient feedback and satisfaction metrics.
7. Summary of operational metrics (e.g., active operations, security clearance).
8. Functionality for running compliance scans and audits.
