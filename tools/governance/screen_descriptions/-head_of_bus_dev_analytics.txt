Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key clients and partners.
4. Conduct market research and analysis to inform strategies.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align efforts.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals and reports.
8. Negotiate contracts and agreements with clients.
9. Track and analyze performance metrics to assess effectiveness.
10. Lead and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High client churn rate.
3. Lack of alignment between business development and other departments.
4. Inconsistent communication with clients or partners.
5. Delays in project delivery or execution.
6. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
7. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).
8. Limited market presence or brand awareness.
9. High employee turnover within the business development team.
10. Ineffective use of resources or budget overruns.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Sales performance metrics (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2. Client acquisition and retention rates.
3. Market analysis and competitive landscape overview.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking.
5. Pipeline status and forecasted revenue.
6. Team performance metrics and individual contributions.
7. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Budget utilization and financial performance.
9. Upcoming opportunities and potential risks.
10. Integration with CRM and other relevant tools for real-time data.
