Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budget Management
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Regulatory Oversight
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Consistent budget overruns
2. Declining cash flow
3. Increasing debt levels
4. Inaccurate financial reporting
5. Non-compliance with regulations
6. High employee turnover in finance team
7. Lack of financial controls
8. Poor forecasting accuracy
9. Unexplained variances in financial statements
10. Delays in financial reporting

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health
2. Budget vs. Actuals analysis
3. Cash flow projections
4. Profit and loss statements
5. Balance sheet overview
6. Risk assessment metrics
7. Compliance status indicators
8. Investment performance tracking
9. Team performance metrics
10. Stakeholder feedback and engagement levels
