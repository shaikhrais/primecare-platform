List of tasks Registered Nurse (RN) must do:
1. Assess patient health and medical histories.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments.
4. Monitor patient progress and vital signs.
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
6. Educate patients and families on health management.
7. Document patient care and progress.
8. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.
10. Provide emotional support to patients and families.

Red flags of operation for effective work:
1. Delays in patient care or treatment.
2. High rates of medication errors.
3. Incomplete or inaccurate documentation.
4. Poor communication among team members.
5. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
6. Frequent changes in patient conditions without proper monitoring.
7. Lack of adherence to care protocols.
8. Inadequate staffing levels leading to burnout.
9. Unresolved conflicts within the healthcare team.
10. Failure to follow up on critical lab results.

Dashboard needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Patient list with current status and alerts.
2. Medication administration records.
3. Care plan revisions and approval statuses.
4. Goal-setting and tracking tools.
5. Communication logs with other healthcare providers.
6. Performance metrics and quality indicators.
7. Patient education resources and materials.
8. Access to clinical guidelines and protocols.
9. Incident reporting and tracking system.
10. Tools for scheduling and managing patient appointments.
