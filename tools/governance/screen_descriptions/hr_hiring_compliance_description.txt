Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
3. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, etc.).
4. Screen resumes and applications.
5. Conduct interviews and assessments.
6. Coordinate and facilitate the hiring process.
7. Maintain relationships with candidates and hiring managers.
8. Ensure compliance with hiring policies and regulations.
9. Analyze recruitment metrics and adjust strategies accordingly.
10. Provide training and support to hiring teams.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates in recent hires.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions.
3. Poor candidate experience feedback.
4. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
5. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
6. Frequent changes in hiring requirements.
7. Low acceptance rates of job offers.
8. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
9. Insufficient tracking of recruitment metrics.
10. Non-compliance with hiring regulations.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Overview of open positions and their status.
2. Time-to-fill metrics for each position.
3. Candidate pipeline status (number of candidates at each stage).
4. Diversity metrics of candidates.
5. Source of hire analysis (which channels yield the best candidates).
6. Candidate experience feedback scores.
7. Offer acceptance rates.
8. Compliance checklist status.
9. Recruitment budget tracking.
10. Historical data on hiring trends and metrics.
