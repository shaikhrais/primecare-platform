List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor incoming customer support tickets.
2. Respond to customer inquiries and issues.
3. Track the status of each ticket (open, in progress, resolved).
4. Escalate tickets that require higher-level support.
5. Analyze ticket trends and customer feedback for improvements.
6. Update ticket statuses and add notes as necessary.
7. Collaborate with other departments to resolve complex issues.
8. Provide feedback on the support process for continuous improvement.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of unresolved tickets indicating potential staffing issues.
2. Long response times to customer inquiries.
3. Frequent escalations suggesting inadequate first-level support.
4. Repeated issues from customers indicating a lack of product knowledge or training.
5. Negative customer feedback or low satisfaction ratings.
6. Lack of communication between support staff and other departments.
7. Inconsistent ticket updates leading to confusion.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of open, in-progress, and resolved tickets.
2. Average response time and resolution time metrics.
3. Alerts for tickets that have been open for too long.
4. Customer satisfaction ratings and feedback summaries.
5. Quick access to frequently asked questions or knowledge base articles.
6. Visual representation of ticket trends over time.
7. Collaboration tools for communicating with other departments.
8. Performance metrics for individual support agents.
