List of tasks for HR Director:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Monitor employee performance and conduct evaluations.
6. Design and implement training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.
11. Conduct exit interviews and analyze turnover data.
12. Collaborate with other departments to align HR strategies with business goals.

Red flags of operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Frequent complaints or grievances.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Poor performance management practices.
8. Ineffective communication within the organization.
9. Inconsistent application of HR policies.
10. Insufficient diversity and inclusion efforts.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for recruitment, retention, and employee satisfaction.
2. Real-time employee engagement metrics.
3. Compliance status with labor laws and regulations.
4. Performance evaluation summaries.
5. Training and development program participation rates.
6. Diversity and inclusion statistics.
7. Audit logs of HR activities and decisions.
8. Alerts for potential issues (e.g., high turnover, low engagement).
9. Visualizations of HR metrics (charts, graphs).
10. Access to employee feedback and survey results.
