List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMT) must perform:
1. Conduct client assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Stay updated on industry best practices and continuing education.
7. Communicate effectively with clients regarding their treatment.
8. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.
9. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.
10. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.

Red flags of operation for RMTs:
1. Inconsistent or incomplete client records.
2. Client complaints about treatment effectiveness or discomfort.
3. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
4. High cancellation or no-show rates.
5. Failure to obtain informed consent from clients.
6. Inadequate follow-up on client progress.
7. Poor communication with clients or team members.
8. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
9. Signs of burnout or stress among staff.
10. Negative feedback from client surveys or reviews.

Dashboard needs for Registered Massage Therapists (RMT):
1. Client appointment scheduling and management.
2. Client progress tracking and treatment outcomes.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., number of sessions, client retention).
4. Financial overview (e.g., revenue, expenses).
5. Compliance tracking (e.g., certifications, licenses).
6. Client feedback and satisfaction ratings.
7. Integration with electronic health records (EHR).
8. Access to continuing education resources and updates.
9. Alerts for upcoming renewals or expirations of licenses.
10. Communication tools for client interaction and follow-up.
