Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with budgeting and financial tracking.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Manage office supplies and inventory.
8. Provide customer service and support.
9. Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information.
10. Perform data entry and database management.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent missed deadlines.
2. High volume of errors in documents.
3. Poor communication among team members.
4. Lack of organization in files and records.
5. Inconsistent follow-up on tasks.
6. Low employee morale or high turnover.
7. Inefficient use of office resources.
8. Negative feedback from clients or customers.
9. Inability to adapt to new tools or processes.
10. Overreliance on manual processes.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview.
2. Calendar and scheduling integration.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., tasks completed, deadlines met).
4. Communication logs (emails, calls).
5. Document management status.
6. Budget tracking and financial reports.
7. Inventory levels for office supplies.
8. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
9. Compliance and audit logs.
10. Alerts for upcoming deadlines and meetings.
