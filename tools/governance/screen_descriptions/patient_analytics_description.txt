List of work for the Patient:
1. Complete any required pre-operative assessments.
2. Attend scheduled follow-up appointments.
3. Adhere to prescribed medication regimens.
4. Participate in any necessary rehabilitation or therapy sessions.
5. Maintain a healthy lifestyle, including diet and exercise as advised.

Red flags for operation:
1. Sudden increase in pain or discomfort.
2. Unexplained swelling or redness at the surgical site.
3. Fever or signs of infection.
4. Unusual bleeding or discharge.
5. Difficulty breathing or chest pain.

Effective work strategies:
1. Regular monitoring of patient progress.
2. Clear communication of expectations and responsibilities.
3. Timely follow-up on any concerns raised by the patient.
4. Use of technology for reminders and updates.

Dashboard needs for the Patient:
1. Overview of upcoming appointments and tasks.
2. Alerts for any red flags or concerns.
3. Summary of medications and adherence tracking.
4. Access to educational resources related to their condition.
5. Communication tools for reaching healthcare providers.
