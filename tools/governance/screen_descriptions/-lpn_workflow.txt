Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and injections as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes to the healthcare team.
3. Assist with patient hygiene and daily living activities.
4. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
5. Collaborate with registered nurses and other healthcare professionals.
6. Provide basic wound care and dressing changes.
7. Educate patients and families about health management and care plans.
8. Assist in the development and implementation of patient care plans.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
10. Participate in patient assessments and evaluations.

Red Flags for Operation:
1. High patient-to-nurse ratio leading to potential burnout.
2. Inconsistent documentation practices.
3. Frequent medication errors or near misses.
4. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
5. Delays in patient care or response times.
6. Unresolved patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
7. Inadequate training or resources for LPNs.
8. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
9. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
10. Insufficient follow-up on patient care plans.

Dashboard Needs for LPN:
1. Real-time patient status updates and alerts.
2. Metrics on medication administration accuracy.
3. Logs of patient interactions and care provided.
4. Compliance tracking for healthcare regulations.
5. Performance indicators for patient outcomes.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Access to training resources and guidelines.
8. Visualizations of patient care trends and patterns.
9. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
10. Feedback mechanisms for continuous improvement.
