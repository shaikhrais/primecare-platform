Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Perform spinal manipulations and adjustments.
4. Monitor patient progress and modify treatment as necessary.
5. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and research.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Unusual patterns in patient progress or outcomes.
3. High cancellation or no-show rates.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Non-compliance with treatment protocols.
6. Failure to follow up on patient referrals or consultations.
7. Signs of burnout or stress among staff.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Active operations metrics.
2. Security clearance status.
3. Compliance audit logs.
4. Patient progress tracking.
5. Treatment plan effectiveness indicators.
6. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Telemetry charts for operational performance.
8. Notifications for upcoming audits or compliance checks.
