Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Unexplained Variances in Financial Statements
2. Declining Cash Flow
3. Increased Debt Levels
4. Inconsistent Financial Reporting
5. Compliance Issues or Regulatory Violations
6. High Employee Turnover in Finance Team
7. Lack of Transparency in Financial Processes
8. Poor Internal Controls
9. Inaccurate Forecasting
10. Delayed Financial Reporting

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Overview
2. Real-time Financial Metrics (Revenue, Expenses, Profit Margins)
3. Budget vs Actual Analysis
4. Cash Flow Projections
5. Compliance Status Indicators
6. Risk Assessment Metrics
7. Audit Logs and Compliance Reports
8. Operational Efficiency Metrics
9. Investment Performance Overview
10. Team Performance and Development Metrics
