List of tasks Personal Support Workers (PSWs) must do:
1. Clock in and clock out for shifts.
2. Complete assigned tasks for each client.
3. Record the duration of active shifts.
4. Update shift status (completed, pending).
5. Communicate with clients and their families.
6. Document any changes in client condition or needs.
7. Report any incidents or concerns to supervisors.
8. Maintain confidentiality and privacy of client information.
9. Participate in training and development sessions.
10. Follow health and safety protocols.

Red flags of operation for effective work:
1. Frequent clocking in/out without completing shifts.
2. Inconsistent task completion rates.
3. High number of pending shifts.
4. Lack of communication with clients or families.
5. Unreported incidents or changes in client condition.
6. Non-compliance with health and safety protocols.
7. Low engagement in training and development.
8. High turnover of PSWs in a specific area or team.

Dashboard needs for Personal Support Workers (PSWs):
1. Current clock-in status and active shift details.
2. List of today's shifts with status updates.
3. Task completion tracking (completed vs. total tasks).
4. Notifications for upcoming shifts and reminders.
5. Summary of active duration for each shift.
6. Alerts for any red flags (e.g., pending tasks, incidents).
7. Access to client information (name, address, needs).
8. Reporting tools for incidents or concerns.
9. Training and development resources.
10. Health and safety compliance checklists.
