Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop and implement personalized treatment plans.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate treatment notes and documentation.
5. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
6. Communicate effectively with clients regarding treatment progress.
7. Stay updated on industry best practices and continuing education.
8. Manage client scheduling and follow-up appointments.
9. Maintain a clean and safe treatment environment.
10. Handle client inquiries and concerns professionally.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Inconsistent or incomplete treatment notes.
2. Frequent client complaints or dissatisfaction.
3. Non-compliance with health regulations or standards.
4. High cancellation or no-show rates.
5. Lack of ongoing professional development.
6. Poor communication with clients or team members.
7. Signs of burnout or fatigue among staff.
8. Inadequate response to client feedback.
9. Failure to maintain equipment and facilities.
10. Low client retention rates.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Overview of active clients and appointments.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
4. Client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
5. Operational audit logs for transparency.
6. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
7. Access to treatment plan templates and resources.
8. Metrics on client retention and referral rates.
9. Summary of ongoing professional development activities.
10. Real-time updates on scheduling and cancellations.
