Franchise Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Manage franchise sales operations and strategies.
2. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
3. Conduct market analysis to identify growth opportunities.
4. Develop and maintain relationships with franchisees.
5. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and regulations.
6. Provide training and support to franchisees.
7. Analyze sales data and generate reports.
8. Coordinate marketing efforts for franchise promotions.
9. Address franchisee concerns and resolve issues.
10. Implement best practices for franchise operations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales trends over consecutive periods.
2. High turnover rates among franchisees.
3. Frequent complaints or disputes from franchisees.
4. Non-compliance with franchise agreements.
5. Inconsistent performance metrics across franchises.
6. Delayed responses to franchisee inquiries.
7. Lack of engagement in training and support programs.
8. Poor customer feedback or satisfaction ratings.
9. Unclear communication of policies and procedures.
10. Insufficient marketing support for franchises.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Sales Manager:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics.
2. Compliance status indicators for each franchise.
3. Franchisee satisfaction ratings and feedback.
4. Market analysis reports and trends.
5. Training and support engagement metrics.
6. Alerts for declining performance or compliance issues.
7. Historical sales data comparisons.
8. Marketing campaign effectiveness tracking.
9. Communication logs with franchisees.
10. Operational logs and activity history.
