List of work for the user:
1. Understand the access restrictions on the No Access screen.
2. Identify the required permissions to gain access to the restricted content.
3. Use the "Go Back" button to navigate away from the No Access screen.
4. If applicable, request the necessary permissions from an administrator or support team.

Red flags of operation:
1. Users encountering the No Access screen may indicate a lack of proper permissions management.
2. Frequent access restriction messages could suggest that users are not adequately informed about their permissions.
3. If users are unable to resolve access issues independently, it may lead to frustration and decreased productivity.

Dashboard needs for the user:
1. A clear overview of current permissions and access levels.
2. Notifications or alerts for permission changes or requests.
3. A quick link to request additional permissions or support.
4. A history of access attempts and their outcomes for better tracking.
