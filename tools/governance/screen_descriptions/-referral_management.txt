Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming referrals and ensure proper documentation.
2. Coordinate communication between patients, healthcare providers, and insurance companies.
3. Schedule appointments and follow-up visits.
4. Maintain accurate and up-to-date patient records.
5. Monitor referral status and ensure timely processing.
6. Conduct compliance scans and audits to ensure adherence to regulations.
7. Provide support and guidance to patients throughout the referral process.
8. Collaborate with clinical teams to address any issues related to referrals.
9. Track and report on key performance indicators related to referral management.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of referral rejections or delays.
2. High volume of patient complaints regarding the referral process.
3. Frequent errors in patient documentation or scheduling.
4. Lack of communication between departments involved in the referral process.
5. Inconsistent tracking of referral status and outcomes.
6. Low compliance audit scores or findings.
7. High turnover rate among staff handling referrals.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Overview of active referrals and their statuses.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as average processing time, referral acceptance rates, and patient satisfaction scores.
3. Real-time logs of referral activities and communications.
4. Compliance audit results and alerts for any issues.
5. Metrics on appointment scheduling and follow-up visits.
6. Alerts for overdue referrals or pending actions.
7. Visualizations of referral trends over time.
8. Access to patient feedback and complaints related to the referral process.
