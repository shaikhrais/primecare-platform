List of tasks a Social Worker must perform:
1. Assess client needs and situations.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Provide counseling and support to clients.
4. Coordinate with other professionals and agencies.
5. Maintain accurate and confidential records.
6. Advocate for client rights and services.
7. Monitor client progress and adjust plans as necessary.
8. Conduct home visits and community outreach.
9. Participate in training and professional development.
10. Stay informed about social services and resources.

Red flags in operations that may indicate ineffective work:
1. High caseloads leading to burnout.
2. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
3. Lack of communication with clients or team members.
4. Missed deadlines for reports or assessments.
5. Failure to follow up with clients.
6. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
7. Increased number of client complaints.
8. Difficulty in accessing necessary resources or services.
9. Frequent changes in treatment plans without justification.
10. Lack of engagement in professional development.

Dashboard needs for a Social Worker:
1. Overview of current caseload and client status.
2. Alerts for upcoming deadlines and appointments.
3. Quick access to client records and documentation.
4. Metrics on client outcomes and progress.
5. Communication tools for collaboration with other professionals.
6. Resource directory for local services and support.
7. Training and development opportunities.
8. Feedback and evaluation tools for self-assessment.
9. Reporting features for tracking performance and compliance.
10. Visualizations of workload and time management.
