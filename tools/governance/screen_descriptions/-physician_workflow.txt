Physician Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient examinations and assessments.
2. Diagnose medical conditions.
3. Develop treatment plans.
4. Prescribe medications and therapies.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
6. Document patient interactions and treatment outcomes.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
8. Stay updated with medical advancements and best practices.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
10. Provide patient education and support.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient readmissions.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. Non-compliance with treatment protocols.
6. Inconsistent documentation practices.
7. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
8. Unusual patterns in patient outcomes or metrics.
9. Failure to follow up on test results or referrals.
10. Inadequate response to patient emergencies.

Dashboard Needs for Physicians:
1. Real-time patient status updates.
2. Compliance metrics and alerts.
3. Operational logs and audit trails.
4. Performance indicators (e.g., patient satisfaction scores).
5. Task management tools for tracking patient care activities.
6. Access to clinical guidelines and protocols.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with other providers.
8. Telemetry data for monitoring patient health trends.
9. Alerts for red flags or operational issues.
10. Summary of ongoing and completed tasks.
