List of tasks a Scrum Master must do:
1. Facilitate Scrum ceremonies (Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective).
2. Remove impediments that hinder the team's progress.
3. Coach the team on Agile principles and practices.
4. Ensure the team adheres to Scrum processes.
5. Foster collaboration and communication within the team and with stakeholders.
6. Track and report on team progress and performance metrics.
7. Support product backlog refinement and prioritization.
8. Promote a culture of continuous improvement.
9. Assist in conflict resolution within the team.
10. Engage with stakeholders to ensure alignment and transparency.

Red flags indicating ineffective operations:
1. Frequent missed deadlines or sprint goals.
2. Low team morale or high turnover rates.
3. Lack of engagement during Scrum ceremonies.
4. Persistent unresolved impediments.
5. Poor communication within the team or with stakeholders.
6. Inconsistent adherence to Scrum practices.
7. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities.
8. Resistance to change or improvement initiatives.
9. Over-reliance on the Scrum Master for decision-making.
10. Inadequate feedback loops or retrospectives.

Dashboard needs for a Scrum Master:
1. Sprint progress (burn-down charts, velocity).
2. Impediment tracking and resolution status.
3. Team capacity and workload distribution.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for team effectiveness.
5. Feedback from retrospectives and action items.
6. Stakeholder engagement metrics.
7. Backlog health (items ready for development, prioritization).
8. Team morale indicators (surveys, feedback).
9. Historical performance data for trend analysis.
10. Visual representation of Scrum ceremonies participation.
