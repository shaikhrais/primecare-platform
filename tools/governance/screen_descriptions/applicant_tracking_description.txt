Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
3. Manage the full recruitment cycle from job posting to onboarding.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
6. Coordinate and schedule interviews with candidates and hiring teams.
7. Maintain candidate database and track recruitment metrics.
8. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
9. Build and maintain relationships with external recruitment agencies.
10. Provide training and support to hiring managers on best practices.
11. Monitor and report on recruitment KPIs.
12. Enhance employer branding and candidate experience.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative feedback from candidates about the recruitment process.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or deadlines.
8. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
9. Poor collaboration with hiring managers.
10. Inadequate tracking of recruitment metrics.

Dashboard Needs for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Overview of open positions and their status.
2. Time-to-fill metrics for each role.
3. Candidate pipeline visualization.
4. Source of hire analysis to identify effective recruitment channels.
5. Diversity metrics of candidates and hires.
6. Candidate experience feedback and ratings.
7. Recruitment KPIs such as offer acceptance rates and turnover rates.
8. Compliance status and audit logs.
9. Real-time updates on recruitment activities and progress.
10. Alerts for positions that are taking longer than average to fill.
