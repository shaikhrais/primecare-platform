Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Unusual spikes in treatment failures or complications.
3. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
4. Failure to adhere to compliance and regulatory standards.
5. High staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Inadequate follow-up on patient care.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Active Operations Metric: Display current active treatments.
2. Security Clearance Status: Indicate compliance level.
3. Telemetry Chart: Show patient progress and treatment effectiveness over time.
4. Operational Audit Logs: List recent activities and compliance checks.
5. Compliance Scan Button: Allow for manual compliance checks.
6. Refresh Button: Enable manual refresh of dashboard data.
7. Notifications for red flags or alerts in operations.
