User Work List:
1. Review compliance checklists for each clinic.
2. Schedule audits for clinics that require them.
3. Update compliance statuses in the checklist.
4. Monitor overall readiness percentage across clinics.
5. Address any critical violations reported.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low readiness percentage (below 45%).
2. Clinics with multiple compliance checklist items marked as false.
3. Clinics with audits scheduled far in the future or not scheduled at all.
4. High number of critical violations reported.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Display overall readiness average percentage.
2. Show number of scheduled audits and their dates.
3. Highlight critical violations and their status.
4. Provide a detailed view of compliance checklists for each clinic.
5. Allow for easy scheduling of audits and updating of compliance statuses.
