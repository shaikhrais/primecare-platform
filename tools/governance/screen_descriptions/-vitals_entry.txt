Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide medication reminders and assistance with medication administration.
3. Help clients with mobility and transfers.
4. Monitor and record vital signs and health changes.
5. Provide companionship and emotional support.
6. Assist with meal preparation and feeding if necessary.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
9. Document care provided and report any concerns to the supervising nurse or healthcare provider.
10. Participate in training and continuing education to enhance skills.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors in medication administration.
2. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
3. Lack of communication with healthcare team members.
4. Signs of neglect or abuse in client care.
5. High turnover rates among staff.
6. Unresolved complaints from clients or families.
7. Inadequate training or knowledge of care protocols.
8. Failure to follow safety protocols leading to accidents or injuries.

Dashboard Needs for Personal Support Worker (PSW):
1. Overview of daily tasks and responsibilities.
2. Alerts for medication schedules and reminders.
3. Vital signs tracking and trends.
4. Client care logs and documentation access.
5. Communication tools for team collaboration.
6. Training and compliance tracking.
7. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
8. Performance metrics and feedback from supervisors.
9. Access to resources and educational materials.
10. Emergency contact information and protocols.
