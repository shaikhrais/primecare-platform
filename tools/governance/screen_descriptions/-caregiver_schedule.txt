List of work a Caregiver must do:
1. Monitor patient health and well-being.
2. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, eating).
3. Administer medications as prescribed.
4. Maintain accurate records of patient care.
5. Communicate effectively with healthcare professionals and family members.
6. Ensure a safe and clean environment for patients.
7. Provide emotional support and companionship.
8. Report any changes in patient condition to the healthcare team.
9. Participate in training and continuing education.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent medication errors or missed doses.
2. Unexplained changes in patient behavior or health.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
4. High turnover rates among caregivers.
5. Complaints from patients or families about care quality.
6. Lack of communication among the care team.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols.

Dashboard needs for Caregiver:
1. Overview of patient assignments and schedules.
2. Alerts for medication administration times.
3. Log of patient health updates and incidents.
4. Compliance scan results and audit logs.
5. Performance metrics (e.g., patient satisfaction scores).
6. Access to training resources and updates.
7. Communication tools for team collaboration.
8. Visual representation of workload and tasks.
