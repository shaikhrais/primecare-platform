List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor area performance metrics for sales.
2. Analyze sales data to identify trends and opportunities.
3. Address any discrepancies or issues in sales reporting.
4. Collaborate with team members to strategize improvements.
5. Review and adjust sales targets based on performance data.
6. Provide feedback and insights to upper management.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant drop in sales performance compared to previous periods.
2. High variance in sales data across different territories.
3. Frequent errors or discrepancies in reported sales figures.
4. Low engagement or participation from team members in sales activities.
5. Delays in reporting or data updates.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Visualizations of sales trends over time.
3. Alerts for any anomalies or red flags in sales data.
4. Comparison tools for different territories or time periods.
5. Collaboration features for team communication and feedback.
6. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
