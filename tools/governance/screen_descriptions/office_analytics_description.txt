Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Organize and maintain files and records.
3. Handle correspondence and communications.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with project management tasks.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Maintain office supplies and inventory.
8. Support team members with administrative tasks.
9. Manage databases and data entry.
10. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Missed deadlines or appointments.
2. Inconsistent communication with team members.
3. Disorganization in file management.
4. Frequent errors in reports or data entry.
5. Lack of responsiveness to requests.
6. Poor time management leading to backlog of tasks.
7. Inability to prioritize tasks effectively.
8. High turnover of administrative staff.
9. Negative feedback from team members or clients.
10. Unclear roles and responsibilities within the team.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview with deadlines.
2. Calendar integration for scheduling.
3. Communication log for correspondence tracking.
4. File management system status.
5. Performance metrics (e.g., tasks completed, response times).
6. Inventory levels for office supplies.
7. Project status updates.
8. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from team members.
9. Compliance checklists for policies and procedures.
10. Alerts for upcoming deadlines or important events.
