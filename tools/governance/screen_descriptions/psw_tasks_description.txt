Personal Support Worker (PSW) Tasks:

1. Prepare Breakfast & Tea
2. Assist with Morning Walk (15 mins)
3. Confirm Medication Compliance
4. Check Blood Glucose Levels
5. Provide Range of Motion Exercises
6. Assist with Evening Bathing

Red Flags of Operation:
- High-priority tasks not completed
- Repeatedly missed tasks
- Lack of communication regarding client needs
- Inconsistent documentation of tasks
- Failure to report changes in client condition

Dashboard Needs for PSW:
- Overview of pending and completed tasks
- Progress tracking (percentage of tasks completed)
- Alerts for high-priority tasks
- Client-specific notes and requirements
- Communication log with other healthcare professionals
- Performance metrics (e.g., average time spent on tasks)
