Training Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement training programs.
2. Coordinate training schedules and logistics.
3. Monitor and evaluate training effectiveness.
4. Maintain training records and documentation.
5. Collaborate with subject matter experts for content development.
6. Provide support and resources for trainers and trainees.
7. Ensure compliance with training regulations and standards.
8. Facilitate onboarding and orientation programs.
9. Collect feedback from participants for continuous improvement.
10. Report on training outcomes and metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High dropout rates in training programs.
2. Consistent negative feedback from participants.
3. Lack of engagement or participation in training sessions.
4. Incomplete or inaccurate training records.
5. Failure to meet compliance requirements.
6. Delays in training schedule or logistics.
7. Insufficient resources or support for trainers.
8. Poor communication regarding training updates or changes.
9. Inability to track training effectiveness or outcomes.
10. Repeated issues with technology or training tools.

Dashboard Needs for Training Coordinator:
1. Training program overview and status.
2. Participant engagement metrics.
3. Feedback and evaluation results.
4. Compliance tracking indicators.
5. Training completion rates.
6. Resource allocation and availability.
7. Upcoming training schedules and logistics.
8. Historical training data for trend analysis.
9. Alerts for any operational red flags.
10. Visualizations of training effectiveness and outcomes.
