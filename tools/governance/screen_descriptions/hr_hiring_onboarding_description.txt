Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the full recruitment cycle from job posting to onboarding.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, etc.).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
6. Coordinate and schedule interviews with candidates and hiring teams.
7. Maintain candidate database and track recruitment metrics.
8. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
9. Build and maintain relationships with external recruitment agencies.
10. Provide training and support to hiring managers on recruitment processes.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative feedback from candidates about the recruitment experience.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates and hiring managers.
7. Failure to meet recruitment targets or KPIs.
8. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, and candidate quality metrics.
2. Real-time candidate pipeline status and progress.
3. Source of hire analysis to evaluate recruitment channels.
4. Diversity metrics to track representation in candidate pools.
5. Feedback scores from candidates and hiring managers.
6. Compliance status and audit logs for recruitment processes.
7. Upcoming recruitment events and deadlines.
8. Historical data trends for recruitment performance over time.
