Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Answering phone calls and directing them to the appropriate personnel.
2. Managing schedules and appointments for staff.
3. Handling correspondence, including emails and letters.
4. Maintaining filing systems and databases.
5. Preparing reports and presentations.
6. Assisting with the onboarding of new employees.
7. Coordinating office activities and events.
8. Ordering and managing office supplies.
9. Providing support to other departments as needed.
10. Ensuring compliance with company policies and procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent missed calls or unanswered inquiries.
2. Overlapping appointments or scheduling conflicts.
3. Delays in responding to emails or correspondence.
4. Disorganization in filing systems or databases.
5. Inaccurate or incomplete reports.
6. High turnover of new employees due to poor onboarding.
7. Lack of communication between departments.
8. Insufficient office supplies leading to operational delays.
9. Low morale or dissatisfaction among staff.
10. Non-compliance with company policies.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Call log and response tracking.
2. Calendar view for appointments and meetings.
3. Email response time metrics.
4. Filing system status and organization metrics.
5. Report generation and accuracy tracking.
6. Onboarding progress tracker for new employees.
7. Office supply inventory and reorder alerts.
8. Task management and prioritization tool.
9. Communication logs between departments.
10. Compliance checklist for company policies.
