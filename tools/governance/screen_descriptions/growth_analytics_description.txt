Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement growth strategies.
2. Identify new business opportunities and partnerships.
3. Conduct market research and analysis.
4. Build and maintain relationships with clients and stakeholders.
5. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
6. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align strategies.
7. Set and track performance metrics for business development initiatives.
8. Prepare and present reports on business development activities.
9. Manage budgets and resources for business development projects.
10. Lead and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High customer churn rates.
3. Lack of engagement or feedback from clients.
4. Inconsistent communication within the team.
5. Missed deadlines or project delays.
6. Poor performance metrics or KPIs.
7. Increased competition without a response strategy.
8. Negative feedback from stakeholders or clients.
9. High employee turnover in the business development team.
10. Lack of alignment between business development and other departments.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue growth.
2. Customer acquisition and retention metrics.
3. Market analysis and competitor insights.
4. Progress tracking for ongoing business development initiatives.
5. Visual representation of sales pipeline and forecasts.
6. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Team performance metrics and individual contributions.
8. Budget utilization and resource allocation.
9. Alerts for potential red flags in operations.
10. Summary of recent business development activities and outcomes.
