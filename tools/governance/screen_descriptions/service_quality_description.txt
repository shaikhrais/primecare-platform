Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Lead and mentor department heads and teams.
6. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
7. Foster a culture of continuous improvement.
8. Collaborate with other executives on strategic planning.
9. Analyze market trends and adapt operations accordingly.
10. Communicate operational strategies to stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining performance metrics or KPIs.
2. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue.
3. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
4. Frequent compliance issues or audits.
5. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
6. Poor communication between departments.
7. Lack of innovation or resistance to change.
8. Customer complaints or declining satisfaction rates.
9. Inconsistent reporting or data discrepancies.
10. Over-reliance on key personnel without succession planning.

Dashboard Needs for COO:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Financial dashboards showing budget vs. actuals.
3. Compliance status and audit results.
4. Employee engagement and turnover statistics.
5. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
6. Project timelines and status updates.
7. Operational efficiency indicators.
8. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
9. Risk management and incident reports.
10. Market trends and competitive analysis.
