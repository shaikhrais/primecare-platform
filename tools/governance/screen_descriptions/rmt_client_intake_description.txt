Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client intake assessments.
2. Develop treatment plans based on client needs.
3. Perform massage therapy techniques.
4. Maintain accurate client records.
5. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
6. Communicate effectively with clients regarding their treatment.
7. Stay updated on industry best practices and continuing education.
8. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.
9. Handle billing and insurance claims as necessary.
10. Maintain a clean and safe work environment.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High client cancellation or no-show rates.
2. Frequent complaints or negative feedback from clients.
3. Inconsistent documentation of client treatments.
4. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
5. Lack of communication or follow-up with clients.
6. Inability to manage appointment schedules effectively.
7. High turnover rate of staff or therapists.
8. Insufficient training or knowledge of new techniques.
9. Poor financial management or billing errors.
10. Failure to maintain professional boundaries with clients.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Client intake metrics (number of new clients, completed assessments).
2. Treatment plan effectiveness (client progress tracking).
3. Appointment scheduling overview (upcoming appointments, cancellations).
4. Compliance status (health and safety checks, audit results).
5. Client feedback and satisfaction ratings.
6. Financial overview (revenue, billing status, insurance claims).
7. Operational logs (notes on client sessions, compliance scans).
8. Training and development tracking (completed courses, certifications).
9. Performance metrics (number of sessions performed, client retention).
10. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
