Shareholder Responsibilities:
1. Monitor compliance with regulations and operational standards.
2. Review and analyze performance metrics.
3. Engage in strategic decision-making based on analytics.
4. Ensure effective communication with stakeholders.
5. Participate in governance and risk management activities.
6. Oversee financial performance and investment strategies.
7. Provide feedback on operational processes and improvements.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased frequency of compliance errors or exceptions.
2. Delays in reporting or response times.
3. Significant deviations from expected performance metrics.
4. Negative trends in operational logs or telemetry data.
5. Lack of transparency in communication or reporting.
6. High turnover rates or dissatisfaction among key personnel.
7. Unresolved issues from previous audits or assessments.

Dashboard Needs for Shareholders:
1. Real-time compliance status and alerts.
2. Performance metrics and trend analysis.
3. Operational logs and historical data access.
4. Visual representation of key performance indicators (KPIs).
5. Summary of recent activities and actions taken.
6. Risk assessment and mitigation strategies.
7. Tools for scenario analysis and forecasting.
