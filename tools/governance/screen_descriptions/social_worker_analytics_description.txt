List of tasks a Social Worker must do:
1. Conduct assessments and evaluations of clients.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans.
3. Provide counseling and support to clients and families.
4. Advocate for clients' needs and rights.
5. Coordinate services and resources for clients.
6. Maintain accurate and confidential records.
7. Collaborate with other professionals and agencies.
8. Monitor client progress and adjust plans as needed.
9. Participate in training and professional development.
10. Stay informed about community resources and services.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work:
1. High caseloads leading to burnout and reduced quality of service.
2. Inconsistent documentation and record-keeping.
3. Lack of communication with clients or other professionals.
4. Failure to follow up on client progress.
5. Inadequate knowledge of available resources and services.
6. Poor time management and prioritization of tasks.
7. Negative feedback from clients or colleagues.
8. Increased number of client crises or emergencies.
9. Lack of engagement in professional development opportunities.
10. Resistance to feedback or change in practice.

Dashboard needs for Social Worker:
1. Client assessment and progress tracking.
2. Task and appointment management.
3. Resource and service directory.
4. Communication log with clients and other professionals.
5. Performance metrics and analytics.
6. Alerts for follow-ups and deadlines.
7. Training and professional development resources.
8. Feedback and satisfaction surveys from clients.
9. Case load overview and management tools.
10. Integration with other systems for seamless data sharing.
