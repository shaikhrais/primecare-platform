List of tasks for a Physiotherapist:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red flags for operations:
1. Unexplained patient deterioration.
2. Non-compliance with treatment plans.
3. Significant changes in patient symptoms.
4. Adverse reactions to treatments.
5. Incomplete or inaccurate patient records.
6. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.
7. High rates of patient dropouts or dissatisfaction.

Dashboard needs for a Physiotherapist:
1. Patient progress tracking metrics.
2. Compliance audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
4. Real-time telemetry data for patient monitoring.
5. Alerts for red flags in patient care.
6. Access to patient history and treatment plans.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare professionals.
