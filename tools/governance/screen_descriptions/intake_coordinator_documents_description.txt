Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Recruit and onboard volunteers.
2. Schedule and coordinate volunteer activities.
3. Provide training and support to volunteers.
4. Maintain communication with volunteers.
5. Track volunteer hours and contributions.
6. Organize volunteer recognition and appreciation events.
7. Evaluate volunteer performance and provide feedback.
8. Ensure compliance with organizational policies and regulations.
9. Manage volunteer records and databases.
10. Collaborate with other departments to identify volunteer needs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rate.
2. Lack of engagement or communication from volunteers.
3. Inconsistent tracking of volunteer hours.
4. Negative feedback from volunteers regarding their experience.
5. Difficulty in filling volunteer positions.
6. Inadequate training or support for volunteers.
7. Poor coordination of volunteer activities.
8. Non-compliance with safety or legal regulations.
9. Insufficient recognition of volunteer contributions.
10. Low participation in volunteer events.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Volunteer recruitment metrics (number of new volunteers, retention rates).
2. Volunteer activity scheduling and participation rates.
3. Training completion rates for volunteers.
4. Communication logs with volunteers.
5. Volunteer hours tracked and reported.
6. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from volunteers.
7. Recognition and appreciation event participation.
8. Compliance status with policies and regulations.
9. Volunteer performance evaluations.
10. Collaboration requests and responses from other departments.
