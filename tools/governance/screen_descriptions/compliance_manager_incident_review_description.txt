User Tasks:
1. Review compliance incidents reported in the system.
2. Analyze incident data for patterns or recurring issues.
3. Document findings and actions taken for each incident.
4. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance standards are met.
5. Generate reports on compliance incidents for management review.
6. Stay updated on compliance regulations and best practices.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High frequency of incidents in a specific area or department.
2. Delays in incident reporting or resolution.
3. Lack of documentation for incidents and actions taken.
4. Repeated incidents of the same nature without corrective actions.
5. Inconsistent compliance with regulatory requirements.
6. Low engagement from team members in compliance discussions.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of current incidents with status (open, resolved, pending).
2. Metrics on incident frequency and types over time.
3. Alerts for overdue incidents or unresolved issues.
4. Documentation status for each incident.
5. Compliance score or rating based on incident management.
6. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
7. Collaboration tools for communication with other departments.
