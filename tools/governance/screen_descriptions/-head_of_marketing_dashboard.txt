Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and initiatives.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Analyze market trends and customer insights.
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams to align marketing efforts.
6. Monitor and report on the performance of marketing activities.
7. Lead and mentor the marketing team.
8. Ensure brand consistency across all marketing channels.
9. Manage relationships with external partners and agencies.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining engagement or conversion rates.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
4. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales teams.
5. Inconsistent brand messaging across channels.
6. High employee turnover within the marketing team.
7. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).
8. Delays in campaign execution or project timelines.
9. Insufficient data analysis leading to uninformed decisions.
10. Poor communication within the team or with stakeholders.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their performance metrics.
2. Real-time analytics on website traffic, engagement, and conversion rates.
3. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
4. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Social media performance metrics.
6. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking.
7. Market trends and competitive analysis summaries.
8. Team performance and productivity metrics.
9. Audit logs of marketing activities and decisions.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
