User Tasks:
1. Monitor formulary compliance metrics.
2. Review and analyze compliance reports.
3. Identify non-compliance issues.
4. Collaborate with stakeholders to address compliance gaps.
5. Update formulary information as needed.
6. Generate compliance-related documentation.
7. Participate in training sessions on formulary updates.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of non-compliance reports.
2. Delays in updating formulary information.
3. Lack of communication among stakeholders.
4. Frequent changes in regulatory requirements.
5. Inconsistent data across compliance reports.
6. Low user engagement with compliance tools.
7. High turnover of compliance staff.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Compliance metrics overview (e.g., compliance rates, trends).
2. Alerts for non-compliance issues.
3. Summary of recent updates to formulary information.
4. Collaboration tools for stakeholder communication.
5. Access to historical compliance reports.
6. Training resources and schedules.
7. User engagement statistics and feedback options.
