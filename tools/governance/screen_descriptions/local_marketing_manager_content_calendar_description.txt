List of work the user must do:
1. Review and manage the content calendar for local marketing campaigns.
2. Schedule posts and promotional content.
3. Collaborate with team members on content ideas and strategies.
4. Analyze performance metrics of past campaigns.
5. Adjust content strategies based on performance data.
6. Ensure all content aligns with brand guidelines and marketing goals.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in content approval processes.
2. Inconsistent posting schedules.
3. Low engagement rates on published content.
4. Lack of collaboration or communication among team members.
5. Missing deadlines for campaign launches.
6. High volume of errors in scheduled content.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of upcoming scheduled posts and campaigns.
2. Performance metrics for past content (engagement, reach, etc.).
3. Notifications for pending approvals or deadlines.
4. Collaboration tools for team discussions and feedback.
5. A calendar view for easy visualization of content schedules.
6. Alerts for any red flags or issues that need immediate attention.
