Chiropractor Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Review and interpret X-ray images.
3. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
4. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
6. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and preventive care.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
9. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Unexplained patient symptoms or worsening conditions.
2. Inconsistent patient progress or lack of improvement.
3. Adverse reactions to treatments.
4. High patient turnover or dissatisfaction.
5. Compliance issues with regulations or standards.
6. Incomplete or inaccurate patient records.
7. Equipment malfunctions or safety concerns.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Patient management system with easy access to patient records.
2. X-ray review and interpretation tools.
3. Compliance audit logs and results.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., patient progress, treatment outcomes).
5. Real-time telemetry data for operational efficiency.
6. Alerts for red flags or compliance issues.
7. Educational resources for patient care and treatment options.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
