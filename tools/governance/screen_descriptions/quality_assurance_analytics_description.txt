List of tasks a QA Specialist must do:
1. Develop and implement test plans and test cases.
2. Conduct manual and automated testing.
3. Identify, document, and track defects.
4. Collaborate with development teams to resolve issues.
5. Review requirements and specifications for testability.
6. Perform regression testing after fixes and enhancements.
7. Analyze test results and provide feedback.
8. Maintain testing documentation and reports.
9. Ensure compliance with quality standards and best practices.
10. Participate in code reviews and design discussions.

Red flags in operations that indicate ineffective work:
1. High number of unresolved defects.
2. Frequent regression issues after releases.
3. Inconsistent testing coverage across features.
4. Lack of communication between QA and development teams.
5. Delays in testing timelines affecting release schedules.
6. Poor documentation of test cases and results.
7. Low test automation coverage.
8. Repeated issues that were previously resolved.
9. Lack of adherence to testing processes and methodologies.
10. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding quality.

Dashboard needs for a QA Specialist:
1. Overview of current testing status (in progress, completed, blocked).
2. Defect tracking metrics (open, closed, severity levels).
3. Test coverage reports (percentage of requirements tested).
4. Automation test results and coverage statistics.
5. Historical data on defect trends over time.
6. Performance metrics of testing processes (time taken, resources used).
7. Alerts for critical defects or testing delays.
8. Integration with project management tools for visibility.
9. Feedback loop from stakeholders on quality perceptions.
10. Access to documentation and testing resources.
