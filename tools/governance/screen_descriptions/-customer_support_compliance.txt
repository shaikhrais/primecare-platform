Customer Support must perform the following tasks:

1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and transactions.
3. Conduct compliance audits regularly to ensure adherence to policies.
4. Monitor and analyze customer feedback for service improvement.
5. Provide training and resources for team members on compliance and customer service best practices.
6. Implement security measures such as multi-factor authentication.
7. Manage and update operational audit logs.
8. Facilitate secure event reporting and ingestion of logs.
9. Ensure real-time system audit telemetry is functioning correctly.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work include:

1. Increased response times to customer inquiries.
2. A rise in unresolved customer complaints.
3. Frequent errors in record-keeping or transaction logs.
4. Non-compliance issues identified during audits.
5. Lack of updates or maintenance on security measures.
6. Low customer satisfaction scores or negative feedback trends.
7. Inconsistent training and knowledge among team members.
8. Delays in processing secure event reports.

The dashboard for Customer Support should include:

1. Real-time metrics on response times and resolution rates.
2. A summary of customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
3. Compliance audit results and any identified issues.
4. Logs of customer interactions and transactions.
5. Alerts for any non-compliance or security issues.
6. Status of operational audit logs and recent updates.
7. Metrics on team performance and training completion rates.
8. A section for recent customer complaints and their resolution status.
