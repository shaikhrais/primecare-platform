Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Manage staff and delegate tasks effectively.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Conduct regular audits and compliance checks.
5. Implement process improvements and best practices.
6. Handle budgeting and resource allocation.
7. Communicate with stakeholders and report on performance.
8. Resolve operational issues and conflicts.
9. Ensure adherence to safety and regulatory standards.
10. Train and develop team members.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased productivity or performance metrics.
2. Increased error rates or compliance issues.
3. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
4. Delayed project timelines or missed deadlines.
5. Poor communication among team members.
6. Lack of adherence to safety protocols.
7. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
8. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
9. Inconsistent reporting or data inaccuracies.
10. Resistance to change or process improvements.

Dashboard Requirements for Operations Manager:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Employee attendance and engagement levels.
4. Financial overview including budget vs. actuals.
5. Operational issues and resolutions log.
6. Project timelines and milestones tracking.
7. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
8. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
9. Safety incident reports and trends.
10. Alerts for any red flags or critical issues.
