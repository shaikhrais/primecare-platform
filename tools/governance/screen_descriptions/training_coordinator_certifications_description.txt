List of work for the user:
1. Review the training coordinator certifications.
2. Monitor the status of certifications.
3. Update certification details as necessary.
4. Generate reports on certification progress.
5. Communicate with trainees regarding their certification status.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in certification processing.
2. High error rates in certification data.
3. Lack of communication from trainees.
4. Incomplete certification records.
5. Low engagement from trainees in certification activities.

Dashboard needs for the user:
1. Overview of certification statuses (completed, pending, failed).
2. Notifications for upcoming certification deadlines.
3. Metrics on trainee engagement and performance.
4. Quick access to certification update forms.
5. Visual representation of certification progress over time.
