Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming requests for services.
2. Assess and prioritize requests based on urgency and need.
3. Coordinate with clinical staff to schedule appointments.
4. Maintain accurate records of all intake activities.
5. Communicate with clients to gather necessary information.
6. Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
7. Monitor and report on intake metrics and performance.
8. Facilitate communication between departments.
9. Provide support and guidance to clients during the intake process.
10. Conduct follow-ups to ensure client satisfaction.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of missed appointments.
2. Delays in processing intake requests.
3. High volume of client complaints.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Frequent errors in client information.
6. Low staff engagement or high turnover rates.
7. Lack of compliance with policies and procedures.
8. Poor communication between departments.
9. Inability to meet service demand.
10. Unresolved issues from previous intakes.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Overview of active intake requests.
2. Metrics on appointment scheduling and cancellations.
3. Compliance status indicators.
4. Client satisfaction ratings.
5. Logs of recent activities and communications.
6. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or actions.
7. Performance metrics against established KPIs.
8. Visual representation of workload distribution.
9. Access to historical data for trend analysis.
10. Quick action buttons for common tasks (e.g., schedule, follow-up).
