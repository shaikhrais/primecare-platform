Compliance Manager Responsibilities:
1. Conduct compliance audits and scans.
2. Monitor governance policies and ensure adherence.
3. Manage operational compliance checks.
4. Maintain logs of compliance activities and audits.
5. Update and enforce security policies (e.g., MFA).
6. Handle secure event reporting and ingestion.
7. Provide real-time system audit telemetry.
8. Respond to compliance-related incidents and issues.
9. Communicate compliance status to stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased frequency of compliance violations.
2. Delays in compliance audit execution.
3. Inconsistent logging of compliance activities.
4. Failure to enforce security policies (e.g., MFA not enabled).
5. Lack of real-time telemetry data.
6. High number of manual interventions required.
7. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding compliance processes.

Dashboard Requirements for Compliance Manager:
1. Overview of compliance audit status (e.g., last scan date, results).
2. Real-time logs of compliance activities.
3. Alerts for compliance violations or issues.
4. Summary of governance policies and their enforcement status.
5. Metrics on the effectiveness of compliance measures (e.g., number of audits completed, incidents reported).
6. Visual representation of compliance trends over time.
7. Quick access to secure event reporting and ingestion forms.
8. Action buttons for executing compliance scans and updating policies.
