Pediatric Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular health assessments and screenings for children.
2. Diagnose and treat pediatric illnesses and conditions.
3. Monitor growth and development milestones.
4. Provide immunizations and preventive care.
5. Educate parents and caregivers on child health and wellness.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals for comprehensive care.
7. Maintain accurate medical records and documentation.
8. Stay updated on pediatric healthcare advancements and guidelines.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Significant delays in patient wait times.
2. Increased number of missed appointments or cancellations.
3. High rates of emergency room visits for previously treated conditions.
4. Unusual spikes in specific illness diagnoses.
5. Patient complaints regarding care or service quality.
6. Inconsistent follow-up on treatment plans.
7. Lack of communication with caregivers or parents.

Dashboard Requirements for Pediatric Specialist:
1. Real-time patient metrics (e.g., number of active patients, appointment schedules).
2. Compliance and quality assurance indicators.
3. Operational logs and alerts for red flags.
4. Telemetry data on patient outcomes and treatment effectiveness.
5. Visualizations of growth and development trends.
6. Access to educational resources for parents and caregivers.
7. Integration with electronic health records for seamless data management.
