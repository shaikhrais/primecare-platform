Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess and diagnose patients' physical conditions.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and techniques.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated on the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition.
2. Lack of improvement in patient progress.
3. High rate of patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Equipment malfunctions or safety hazards.
6. Non-compliance with treatment protocols.
7. Unusual patterns in patient attendance or cancellations.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient outcomes.
2. Real-time logs of patient interactions and treatments.
3. Compliance audit results and alerts.
4. Metrics on operational efficiency (e.g., appointment scheduling, patient wait times).
5. Notifications for upcoming patient appointments and follow-ups.
6. Access to educational resources and latest research.
7. Tools for manual synchronization of patient data.
8. Export functionality for audit logs and reports.
