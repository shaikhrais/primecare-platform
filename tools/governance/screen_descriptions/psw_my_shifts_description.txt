Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Administer medications as prescribed and monitor health conditions.
5. Prepare meals and assist with feeding if necessary.
6. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
7. Document client care and report any changes in health status.
8. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
9. Follow care plans and ensure compliance with health regulations.
10. Participate in training and development programs.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or families about care quality.
2. High turnover rates among PSWs.
3. Inconsistent documentation of client care.
4. Missed appointments or late arrivals.
5. Lack of communication among team members.
6. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.
7. Signs of burnout or stress among PSWs.
8. Inadequate training or support for PSWs.
9. Poor client health outcomes or increased hospitalizations.
10. Failure to follow up on client needs or changes in condition.

Dashboard Needs for PSW:
1. Overview of active shifts and schedules.
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Client health status updates and care plans.
4. Performance metrics such as client satisfaction scores.
5. Log of operational audit entries and issues.
6. Notifications for upcoming training or compliance requirements.
7. Real-time communication tools for team collaboration.
8. Access to resources and support materials for PSWs.
9. Metrics on PSW workload and hours worked.
10. Visual representation of client health trends and outcomes.
