List of work the user must do:
1. Complete the intake forms as required.
2. Review submitted forms for accuracy and completeness.
3. Follow up with clients if additional information is needed.
4. Ensure all necessary documentation is collected and stored securely.
5. Monitor the status of submitted forms and track progress.
6. Communicate with team members regarding any issues or updates.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of incomplete or inaccurate forms submitted.
2. Delays in processing forms beyond expected timeframes.
3. Frequent follow-ups required with clients for missing information.
4. Lack of communication or updates from team members.
5. Repeated errors in data entry or form submissions.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of submitted forms with status indicators (e.g., pending, completed, error).
2. Notifications for forms that require follow-up or additional information.
3. Metrics on form submission accuracy and processing times.
4. Quick access to frequently used resources or documentation.
5. Communication tools for team collaboration and updates.
