Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on client assessments.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue, sports).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Communicate effectively with clients regarding treatment progress and recommendations.
7. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
8. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.
9. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.
10. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals when necessary.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High client cancellation or no-show rates.
2. Frequent complaints or negative feedback from clients.
3. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
4. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
5. Difficulty in maintaining professional boundaries with clients.
6. Failure to stay current with certifications and training.
7. Poor communication within the team or with clients.
8. Inability to manage time effectively during appointments.
9. Signs of burnout or stress among staff.
10. Non-compliance with regulatory standards or guidelines.

Dashboard Needs for RMT:
1. Client appointment scheduling and reminders.
2. Client assessment and treatment plan tracking.
3. Performance metrics (e.g., number of sessions, client retention rates).
4. Compliance and audit logs for treatments performed.
5. Client feedback and satisfaction ratings.
6. Continuing education tracking and certification status.
7. Operational metrics (e.g., revenue, expenses).
8. Alerts for upcoming certifications or training requirements.
9. Communication tools for client follow-ups.
10. Resource library for techniques and best practices.
