Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Consistent budget overruns
2. Declining revenue trends
3. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue
4. High employee turnover in finance department
5. Delayed financial reporting
6. Compliance issues or audit findings
7. Inaccurate financial forecasts
8. Lack of cash reserves
9. Poor investment returns
10. Ineffective communication with stakeholders

Dashboard Needs for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health
2. Real-time revenue and expense tracking
3. Cash flow analysis
4. Budget vs. actual performance metrics
5. Compliance status and audit logs
6. Risk assessment indicators
7. Investment performance overview
8. Employee productivity metrics
9. Stakeholder engagement metrics
10. Visualizations for trends and forecasts
