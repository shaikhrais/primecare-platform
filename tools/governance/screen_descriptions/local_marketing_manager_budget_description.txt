List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor and manage the local marketing budget effectively.
2. Analyze the performance of marketing campaigns.
3. Adjust budget allocations based on campaign performance and market trends.
4. Collaborate with team members to align marketing strategies.
5. Review and approve marketing expenditures.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant overspending compared to the allocated budget.
2. Low return on investment (ROI) from marketing campaigns.
3. Delays in campaign execution or budget approvals.
4. Lack of communication or collaboration among team members.
5. Inconsistent tracking of budget performance metrics.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current budget status (allocated vs. spent).
2. Performance metrics for ongoing marketing campaigns (e.g., ROI, engagement rates).
3. Alerts for budget overruns or underperformance.
4. Collaboration tools for team communication and updates.
5. Historical data analysis for informed decision-making.
