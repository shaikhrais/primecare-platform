List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor brand assets and ensure they are up to date.
2. Review marketing materials for consistency with brand guidelines.
3. Collaborate with design and content teams to create new assets.
4. Analyze the performance of existing brand assets.
5. Provide feedback on brand asset usage and effectiveness.
6. Ensure compliance with legal and copyright regulations regarding brand assets.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in asset updates or approvals.
2. Inconsistent branding across different marketing materials.
3. Lack of collaboration or communication with design and content teams.
4. Poor performance metrics for brand assets.
5. Frequent errors or issues reported by users regarding brand assets.
6. Non-compliance with legal requirements related to branding.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current brand assets and their status (up to date, needs review, etc.).
2. Performance metrics for each asset (engagement, reach, etc.).
3. Notifications for pending approvals or updates.
4. Collaboration tools for feedback and discussions with team members.
5. Access to brand guidelines and legal compliance information.
6. Quick links to frequently used assets or templates.
