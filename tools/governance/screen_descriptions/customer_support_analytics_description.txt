List of work Customer Support must do:
1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance.
3. Maintain a knowledge base for common issues and solutions.
4. Track and manage customer interactions and feedback.
5. Escalate unresolved issues to higher-level support or management.
6. Conduct follow-ups with customers to ensure satisfaction.
7. Analyze customer feedback for service improvement.
8. Collaborate with other departments to resolve complex issues.
9. Train new support staff on processes and tools.
10. Monitor service level agreements (SLAs) and performance metrics.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of unresolved tickets.
2. Increased response times to customer inquiries.
3. Frequent escalations to higher-level support.
4. Low customer satisfaction ratings.
5. High employee turnover in the support team.
6. Lack of updated knowledge base articles.
7. Inconsistent communication with customers.
8. Repeated issues reported by multiple customers.
9. Failure to meet SLAs.
10. Lack of training and resources for support staff.

Dashboard needs for Customer Support:
1. Ticket volume and status overview (open, closed, pending).
2. Average response and resolution times.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
4. Escalation rates and reasons for escalations.
5. Performance metrics for individual support agents.
6. Knowledge base usage statistics.
7. Common issues reported by customers.
8. SLA compliance rates.
9. Training and resource availability for staff.
10. Integration with other tools for seamless operations.
