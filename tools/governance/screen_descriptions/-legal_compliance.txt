Legal Counsel Responsibilities:
1. Conduct legal research and analysis.
2. Draft and review contracts and agreements.
3. Provide legal advice on compliance with laws and regulations.
4. Manage litigation and dispute resolution.
5. Ensure corporate governance and compliance with internal policies.
6. Monitor changes in legislation and regulatory requirements.
7. Develop and implement compliance programs.
8. Train staff on legal compliance and risk management.
9. Liaise with external legal counsel when necessary.
10. Maintain legal documentation and records.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased frequency of compliance violations.
2. Delays in contract approvals or reviews.
3. Lack of communication between departments regarding legal matters.
4. High turnover of legal staff or consultants.
5. Inconsistent application of policies and procedures.
6. Negative feedback from audits or compliance checks.
7. Unresolved legal disputes or litigation.
8. Failure to update compliance programs in response to new laws.
9. Insufficient training on legal matters for employees.
10. Lack of documentation for legal processes and decisions.

Dashboard Needs for Legal Counsel:
1. Compliance status overview.
2. Recent legal audits and their outcomes.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for legal compliance.
4. Logs of legal inquiries and resolutions.
5. Alerts for upcoming legal deadlines or renewals.
6. Summary of ongoing litigation and disputes.
7. Training completion rates for compliance programs.
8. Changes in legislation affecting the organization.
9. Metrics on contract review times and approval rates.
10. Feedback from internal stakeholders on legal services.
