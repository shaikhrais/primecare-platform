Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report changes.
3. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, feeding).
4. Provide wound care and dressing changes.
5. Document patient care and progress in medical records.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to develop care plans.
7. Educate patients and families about health management.
8. Ensure compliance with safety and infection control protocols.
9. Participate in patient assessments and evaluations.
10. Respond to patient calls and needs promptly.

Red Flags for RPN Operations:
1. Unexplained changes in patient vital signs.
2. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
3. Signs of medication errors or adverse reactions.
4. Patient complaints about care or treatment.
5. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
6. High turnover rates among staff.
7. Inadequate staffing levels leading to patient neglect.
8. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
9. Delays in patient care or treatment.
10. Failure to follow up on patient concerns or needs.

Dashboard Requirements for RPN:
1. Patient vital signs monitoring display.
2. Medication administration records.
3. Alerts for critical patient changes or red flags.
4. Compliance tracking for safety protocols.
5. Documentation status and reminders for missing entries.
6. Patient feedback and satisfaction metrics.
7. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
8. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
9. Education resources for patient and family engagement.
10. Audit logs of compliance checks and actions taken.
