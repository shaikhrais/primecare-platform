List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the status of corrective actions.
2. Review and analyze corrective action data.
3. Identify trends or recurring issues.
4. Collaborate with team members to implement corrective actions.
5. Document the outcomes of corrective actions taken.
6. Provide feedback on the effectiveness of corrective actions.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors or failures in loading data.
2. Delays in the implementation of corrective actions.
3. Lack of engagement or participation from team members.
4. Recurring issues that are not being addressed.
5. Inconsistent documentation of corrective actions.
6. Negative feedback from users regarding the effectiveness of actions taken.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current corrective actions and their statuses.
2. Visual representation of trends in issues over time.
3. Notifications for pending or overdue corrective actions.
4. Summary of team member contributions to corrective actions.
5. Access to historical data for analysis and reporting.
6. Feedback mechanism for users to report on the effectiveness of actions.
