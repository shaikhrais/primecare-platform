General Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement business strategies.
3. Manage budgets and financial performance.
4. Lead and motivate staff to achieve performance goals.
5. Monitor market trends and adjust strategies accordingly.
6. Ensure compliance with regulations and company policies.
7. Foster relationships with stakeholders and clients.
8. Analyze performance metrics and report findings.
9. Drive continuous improvement initiatives.
10. Coordinate with other departments for seamless operations.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue trends.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
5. Lack of clear communication within the team.
6. Missed deadlines or project delays.
7. Inefficient processes leading to wasted resources.
8. Inconsistent performance metrics across departments.
9. Resistance to change or innovation.
10. Poor compliance with industry regulations.

Dashboard Requirements for General Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales, revenue, and expenses.
2. Employee performance metrics and satisfaction scores.
3. Customer feedback and satisfaction ratings.
4. Budget vs. actual financial performance.
5. Operational efficiency metrics (e.g., process times, resource utilization).
6. Market trends and competitive analysis.
7. Compliance status and risk assessments.
8. Project timelines and status updates.
9. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
10. Visualizations for quick insights into overall business health.
