HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee recruitment and onboarding processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and conflict resolution.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Monitor employee performance and conduct evaluations.
6. Facilitate training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Address employee grievances and conduct investigations.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating employee dissatisfaction.
2. Increased absenteeism or tardiness among staff.
3. Lack of compliance with labor laws or regulations.
4. Poor performance metrics or low employee productivity.
5. Frequent conflicts or unresolved disputes among employees.
6. Negative employee feedback in surveys or exit interviews.
7. Ineffective onboarding processes leading to poor integration.
8. Inconsistent application of HR policies across departments.

Dashboard Needs for HR Director:
1. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, candidate sources).
2. Employee turnover and retention rates.
3. Compliance status with labor laws and regulations.
4. Employee performance metrics (evaluations, productivity).
5. Training and development participation rates.
6. Employee engagement scores and feedback.
7. Compensation and benefits analysis.
8. Incident reports and resolutions for employee grievances.
9. Diversity and inclusion statistics.
10. Overall HR operational efficiency metrics.
