Compliance Manager Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits and scans.
2. Monitor and assess compliance with regulations and standards.
3. Maintain documentation of compliance activities and findings.
4. Report compliance status to management and stakeholders.
5. Identify and mitigate compliance risks.
6. Provide training and support to staff on compliance matters.
7. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance across the organization.
8. Stay updated on changes in laws and regulations affecting the organization.
9. Develop and implement compliance policies and procedures.
10. Respond to compliance inquiries and investigations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations or incidents.
2. Delays in compliance reporting or audits.
3. Lack of staff training on compliance policies.
4. Inconsistent documentation of compliance activities.
5. High turnover in compliance-related positions.
6. Negative feedback from audits or regulatory inspections.
7. Failure to address previous compliance issues.
8. Poor communication between departments regarding compliance.
9. Lack of resources allocated for compliance activities.
10. Unclear roles and responsibilities related to compliance.

Dashboard Needs for Compliance Manager:
1. Compliance audit status and results.
2. Real-time logs of compliance activities and incidents.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to compliance.
4. Alerts for compliance violations or risks.
5. Summary of training sessions conducted and attendance.
6. Documentation repository for compliance policies and procedures.
7. Historical data on compliance trends and incidents.
8. Integration with incident management systems for tracking issues.
9. Visual representation of compliance metrics (charts/graphs).
10. Access to regulatory updates and changes affecting compliance.
