Finance Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee financial planning and analysis.
2. Manage budgeting and forecasting processes.
3. Ensure compliance with financial regulations and standards.
4. Monitor cash flow and financial performance.
5. Prepare financial reports for stakeholders.
6. Develop financial strategies to support business objectives.
7. Manage relationships with banks and financial institutions.
8. Lead financial audits and implement recommendations.
9. Analyze financial data to identify trends and opportunities.
10. Collaborate with other departments to align financial goals.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant discrepancies in financial reports.
2. Consistent cash flow issues or negative cash flow.
3. Unexplained increases in expenses.
4. Delays in financial reporting.
5. High levels of debt or poor credit ratings.
6. Frequent changes in financial policies or procedures.
7. Lack of compliance with regulatory requirements.
8. Inconsistent budgeting practices.
9. Poor communication with stakeholders regarding financial matters.
10. Low employee morale in finance team.

Dashboard Requirements for Finance Director:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial health.
2. Real-time cash flow monitoring.
3. Budget vs. actual performance analysis.
4. Compliance status indicators.
5. Financial trend analysis charts.
6. Audit findings and resolutions tracking.
7. Expense breakdown by category.
8. Revenue forecasts and projections.
9. Alerts for financial anomalies or red flags.
10. Summary of financial reports and key metrics.
