Chief Technology Officer (CTO) Responsibilities:
1. Define and execute the technology strategy aligned with business goals.
2. Oversee the development and implementation of technology solutions.
3. Manage the technology budget and resource allocation.
4. Ensure the security and compliance of technology systems.
5. Foster innovation and research new technologies.
6. Lead and mentor the technology team.
7. Collaborate with other departments to align technology with business needs.
8. Monitor technology trends and assess their impact on the organization.
9. Establish and maintain relationships with technology vendors and partners.
10. Ensure the scalability and reliability of technology infrastructure.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Frequent system outages or downtime.
2. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
3. High turnover rates within the technology team.
4. Lack of alignment between technology initiatives and business objectives.
5. Inconsistent security practices leading to vulnerabilities.
6. Poor communication within the technology team or with other departments.
7. Negative feedback from users regarding technology solutions.
8. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
9. Failure to keep up with industry trends and innovations.
10. Inability to meet compliance requirements.

Dashboard Requirements for CTO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for technology projects.
2. System uptime and performance metrics.
3. Security compliance status and incidents.
4. Budget utilization and financial metrics.
5. Team performance and productivity metrics.
6. User satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Technology trends and innovation updates.
8. Project timelines and milestones.
9. Vendor performance and relationship status.
10. Risk assessment and mitigation strategies.
