Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor operational performance metrics.
2. Ensure compliance with governance standards.
3. Conduct regular audits and scans for compliance.
4. Review and analyze telemetry logs.
5. Manage security clearances and approvals.
6. Address any operational issues or red flags.
7. Communicate with franchise team and stakeholders.
8. Implement improvements based on audit findings.
9. Maintain accurate records of operations and audits.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in operational performance metrics.
2. Unresolved compliance issues or audit failures.
3. Security clearance discrepancies.
4. Frequent network or API errors.
5. Negative trends in telemetry data.
6. Lack of timely updates or logs.
7. High number of manual interventions required.
8. Inconsistent communication from team members.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of active operations and performance metrics.
2. Real-time compliance status and audit results.
3. Telemetry charts displaying operational data trends.
4. Log of operational audit entries and actions taken.
5. Alerts for any compliance or performance issues.
6. Quick access buttons for running compliance scans.
7. Refresh functionality for real-time updates.
8. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and understanding.
