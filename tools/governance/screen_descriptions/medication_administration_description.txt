Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications as prescribed.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report any abnormalities.
3. Document medication administration and patient responses.
4. Educate patients and families about medications and potential side effects.
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members to ensure patient safety.
6. Conduct compliance audits and ensure adherence to protocols.
7. Maintain accurate and up-to-date patient records.
8. Respond to patient inquiries and provide support.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Stay updated on medication management best practices.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Delays in medication administration.
2. Inconsistent documentation of medication given.
3. High incidence of medication errors.
4. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
5. Patient complaints regarding medication side effects.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Incomplete patient education on medications.
8. Unresolved discrepancies in patient records.
9. Increased patient readmissions related to medication issues.
10. Failure to conduct regular compliance audits.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Real-time medication administration logs.
2. Alerts for overdue medications or missed doses.
3. Patient vital signs monitoring dashboard.
4. Compliance audit results and trends.
5. Patient education resources and documentation status.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Incident reporting and tracking system.
8. Performance metrics related to medication administration.
9. Access to medication guidelines and protocols.
10. Summary of patient feedback and complaints related to medications.
