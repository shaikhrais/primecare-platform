List of work for the user:
1. Coordinate staff schedules and assignments.
2. Monitor staff performance and productivity.
3. Address any operational issues or conflicts among staff.
4. Ensure compliance with operational policies and procedures.
5. Communicate effectively with team members and management.
6. Analyze operational data to identify areas for improvement.
7. Provide training and support to staff as needed.
8. Prepare reports on staff performance and operational efficiency.

Red flags of operation:
1. High turnover rates among staff.
2. Frequent complaints or conflicts reported by team members.
3. Consistent delays in project timelines or deliverables.
4. Low staff morale or engagement levels.
5. Inconsistent adherence to operational policies.
6. Lack of clear communication or misunderstandings among team members.
7. Poor performance metrics or declining productivity.

Dashboard needs for the user:
1. Real-time staff performance metrics.
2. Visual representation of staff schedules and assignments.
3. Alerts for operational issues or conflicts.
4. Summary of compliance with policies and procedures.
5. Feedback and survey results from staff regarding morale and engagement.
6. Data analytics tools for identifying trends and areas for improvement.
7. Communication tools for messaging team members and management.
8. Reporting features for tracking progress and performance over time.
