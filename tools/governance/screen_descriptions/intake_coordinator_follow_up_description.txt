Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Recruit and onboard volunteers.
2. Train and orient volunteers on their roles.
3. Schedule volunteer shifts and manage availability.
4. Communicate regularly with volunteers for updates and feedback.
5. Recognize and appreciate volunteer contributions.
6. Monitor volunteer performance and provide support.
7. Maintain volunteer records and data.
8. Organize volunteer events and activities.
9. Collaborate with other departments for volunteer needs.
10. Evaluate volunteer programs and make improvements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rates.
2. Lack of communication or engagement with volunteers.
3. Inconsistent scheduling leading to volunteer dissatisfaction.
4. Insufficient training or resources for volunteers.
5. Negative feedback from volunteers about their experience.
6. Low participation in volunteer events or activities.
7. Difficulty in tracking volunteer hours and contributions.
8. Inadequate recognition of volunteer efforts.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Volunteer recruitment metrics (number of new volunteers).
2. Volunteer retention rates and turnover statistics.
3. Shift scheduling overview (filled vs. unfilled shifts).
4. Volunteer engagement metrics (communication frequency, event participation).
5. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from volunteers.
6. Recognition and appreciation logs (volunteer of the month, awards).
7. Training completion rates for volunteers.
8. Operational metrics (number of events organized, volunteer hours logged).
9. Alerts for any red flags identified in operations.
10. Summary of ongoing and upcoming volunteer activities.
