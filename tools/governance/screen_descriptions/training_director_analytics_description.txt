List of work for Training Candidates:
1. Complete onboarding training modules.
2. Participate in hands-on workshops.
3. Engage in peer-to-peer learning sessions.
4. Attend regular feedback and review meetings.
5. Complete assessments and quizzes to evaluate understanding.
6. Collaborate on team projects to apply learned skills.
7. Utilize available resources and tools for self-paced learning.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent absenteeism or lack of participation in training sessions.
2. Poor performance in assessments or quizzes.
3. Inconsistent engagement during workshops and discussions.
4. Lack of collaboration or communication with peers.
5. Negative feedback from trainers or mentors.
6. Failure to complete assigned tasks or projects on time.
7. Resistance to feedback or unwillingness to improve.

Dashboard needs for Training Candidates:
1. Progress tracking for each training module.
2. Assessment scores and feedback summaries.
3. Attendance records for training sessions.
4. Peer collaboration metrics.
5. Upcoming training events and deadlines.
6. Personal development goals and achievements.
7. Access to resources and additional learning materials.
