System Verification Officer Responsibilities:
1. Conduct compliance audits to ensure adherence to governance policies.
2. Monitor system logs for anomalies and security breaches.
3. Manage and enforce security policies such as multi-factor authentication.
4. Review and validate operational compliance checks.
5. Maintain real-time audit telemetry and reporting.
6. Facilitate secure event reporting and ingestion of logs.
7. Document and report findings from compliance scans and audits.
8. Trigger necessary governance actions based on audit results.
9. Update and manage governance directives and policies.
10. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance across systems.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased frequency of security incidents or breaches.
2. Delays in compliance audit execution.
3. Inconsistent or missing logs in the system.
4. Non-compliance with established security policies.
5. High volume of manual interventions required.
6. Lack of real-time telemetry data.
7. Failure to enforce multi-factor authentication.
8. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
9. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding compliance processes.
10. Incomplete or inaccurate reporting of compliance status.

Dashboard Requirements for System Verification Officer:
1. Real-time compliance status overview.
2. Log of recent compliance audits with status indicators.
3. Alerts for any compliance failures or security incidents.
4. Summary of governance directives and their current status.
5. Visual representation of operational audit logs.
6. Quick access buttons for executing compliance scans and triggering actions.
7. Notifications for policy updates or changes.
8. Historical data on compliance trends and audit results.
9. Metrics on the effectiveness of security measures implemented.
10. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and data retrieval.
