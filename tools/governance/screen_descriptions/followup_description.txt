Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming patient referrals and inquiries.
2. Schedule appointments and follow-ups.
3. Collect and verify patient information and insurance details.
4. Coordinate with clinical staff for patient care.
5. Maintain accurate records of patient interactions and follow-ups.
6. Communicate with patients regarding their status and next steps.
7. Monitor compliance with regulatory requirements.
8. Provide support for patient navigation through the healthcare system.
9. Address patient concerns and resolve issues promptly.
10. Collaborate with other departments to improve intake processes.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of patient complaints or unresolved issues.
2. Delays in scheduling appointments or follow-ups.
3. Inconsistent or incomplete patient information.
4. Frequent errors in documentation or data entry.
5. Low staff morale or high turnover rates.
6. Lack of communication between departments.
7. Increased wait times for patients.
8. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
9. Poor tracking of patient referrals and outcomes.
10. Inadequate training for staff on intake processes.

Dashboard Needs for Intake Coordinator:
1. Real-time metrics on appointment scheduling and follow-up rates.
2. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
3. Compliance tracking indicators.
4. Logs of patient interactions and follow-up actions.
5. Alerts for overdue follow-ups or unresolved issues.
6. Performance metrics for staff handling intake.
7. Visual representation of patient flow and bottlenecks.
8. Integration with electronic health records for seamless data access.
9. Reports on referral sources and conversion rates.
10. Tools for monitoring and improving operational efficiency.
