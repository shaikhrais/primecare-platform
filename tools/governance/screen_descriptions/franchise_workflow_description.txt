Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Manage daily operations of the franchise.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and standards.
3. Oversee staff recruitment, training, and performance management.
4. Monitor financial performance and manage budgets.
5. Implement marketing strategies to promote the franchise.
6. Maintain inventory and supply chain management.
7. Provide excellent customer service and handle customer complaints.
8. Conduct regular audits and assessments of operations.
9. Engage with the franchisor for support and updates.
10. Analyze market trends and adjust business strategies accordingly.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue trends.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative reviews.
4. Inconsistent adherence to franchise standards.
5. Poor inventory management leading to stockouts or overstock.
6. Lack of engagement with the franchisor.
7. Unresolved operational issues or inefficiencies.
8. Failure to meet financial targets or budget overruns.
9. Low customer satisfaction scores.
10. Non-compliance with health and safety regulations.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Sales and revenue metrics.
2. Employee performance and turnover statistics.
3. Customer satisfaction and feedback scores.
4. Inventory levels and supply chain status.
5. Compliance checklists and audit results.
6. Marketing campaign performance data.
7. Financial performance indicators (profit margins, expenses).
8. Operational efficiency metrics (time to resolve issues, service times).
9. Engagement metrics with the franchisor.
10. Alerts for any red flags or operational issues.
