List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor partnership performance metrics.
2. Review and manage partner relationships.
3. Analyze partnership data for insights.
4. Communicate with partners regarding updates and issues.
5. Ensure compliance with partnership agreements.
6. Track partnership goals and objectives.

Red flags of operation:
1. Declining performance metrics from partners.
2. Increased number of partner complaints or issues.
3. Lack of communication or engagement from partners.
4. Missed deadlines or deliverables in partnership agreements.
5. Inconsistent data reporting from partners.
6. Unresolved conflicts or disputes with partners.

What is needed on the dashboard of the user:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each partner.
2. Alerts for any red flags or issues that need attention.
3. A summary of ongoing communications with partners.
4. Visualizations of partnership performance over time.
5. Quick access to partnership agreements and compliance status.
6. Tools for tracking goals and objectives related to partnerships.
