List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor shift schedules for operations staff.
2. Ensure proper staffing levels are maintained.
3. Address any staffing issues or conflicts that arise.
4. Review performance metrics related to shift operations.
5. Communicate with team members regarding shift changes or updates.
6. Analyze operational efficiency and identify areas for improvement.
7. Generate reports on shift performance and staffing levels.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent understaffing or overstaffing situations.
2. High turnover rates among shift staff.
3. Consistent delays in shift handovers.
4. Increased number of complaints from staff regarding scheduling.
5. Low morale or engagement levels among team members.
6. Missed deadlines for operational tasks.
7. Inconsistent performance metrics across shifts.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time shift schedule overview.
2. Staffing level indicators (understaffed, adequately staffed, overstaffed).
3. Alerts for any staffing issues or conflicts.
4. Performance metrics dashboard (e.g., efficiency ratings, completion rates).
5. Communication tools for team updates and announcements.
6. Historical data and trends on staffing and performance.
7. Quick access to generate reports on shift operations.
