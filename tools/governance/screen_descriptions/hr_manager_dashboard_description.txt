HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Conduct performance evaluations and manage talent development.
6. Oversee compensation and benefits programs.
7. Monitor employee engagement and satisfaction.
8. Implement training and development programs.
9. Manage HR data and reporting.
10. Collaborate with other departments to align HR strategies with business goals.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Frequent complaints or grievances from employees.
3. Low employee engagement scores.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Inconsistent performance evaluation processes.
7. Lack of training and development opportunities.
8. Poor communication between HR and other departments.
9. Inaccurate or incomplete HR data.
10. Negative feedback from exit interviews.

Dashboard Needs for HR Director:
1. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, open positions).
2. Employee turnover rates and retention statistics.
3. Employee engagement scores and feedback.
4. Compliance status with labor laws.
5. Performance evaluation completion rates.
6. Training and development participation rates.
7. Compensation and benefits analysis.
8. Employee satisfaction survey results.
9. HR operational logs and audit trails.
10. Key performance indicators aligned with business objectives.
