List of work a Chiropractor must do:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Monitor patient progress and modify treatment plans as necessary.
5. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.

Red flags for operation:
1. Patients experiencing severe pain or discomfort post-treatment.
2. Signs of neurological deficits (e.g., numbness, weakness).
3. Patients with a history of fractures or severe osteoporosis.
4. Unexplained weight loss or systemic symptoms.
5. Patients not responding to treatment as expected.
6. Any signs of infection or inflammation in the treatment area.

Dashboard needs for Chiropractor:
1. Patient management system with appointment scheduling.
2. Progress tracking for each patient.
3. Treatment plan templates and customization options.
4. Integration with billing and insurance processing.
5. Access to educational resources and latest research.
6. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
7. Alerts for red flags and patient follow-up reminders.
8. Analytics for practice performance and patient outcomes.
