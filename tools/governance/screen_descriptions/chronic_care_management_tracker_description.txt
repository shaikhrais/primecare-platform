User Work List:
1. Monitor patient health metrics.
2. Schedule and track care management appointments.
3. Document patient interactions and care plans.
4. Review and update patient medication lists.
5. Communicate with healthcare providers and patients.
6. Analyze patient data for trends and outcomes.
7. Generate reports on patient progress and care effectiveness.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Incomplete or missing patient data.
2. Delays in appointment scheduling or follow-ups.
3. High rates of missed appointments.
4. Lack of communication between care team members.
5. Inconsistent documentation practices.
6. Unresolved patient queries or concerns.
7. Poor patient engagement or adherence to care plans.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time patient health metrics overview.
2. Appointment scheduling and reminders.
3. Documentation status indicators.
4. Communication logs between patients and providers.
5. Analytics on patient outcomes and trends.
6. Alerts for red flags in patient care.
7. User activity tracking for accountability.
