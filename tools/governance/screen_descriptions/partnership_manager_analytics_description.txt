Partnership Manager Responsibilities:
1. Identify and establish partnerships with relevant stakeholders.
2. Manage relationships with existing partners to ensure satisfaction and engagement.
3. Monitor partnership performance and analyze data to assess effectiveness.
4. Develop strategies to enhance partnership value and growth.
5. Coordinate with internal teams to align partnership goals with company objectives.
6. Prepare and present partnership reports and analytics to stakeholders.
7. Negotiate partnership agreements and contracts.
8. Address any issues or conflicts that arise within partnerships.
9. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining engagement or satisfaction from partners.
2. Inconsistent communication or lack of responsiveness from partners.
3. Failure to meet partnership performance metrics.
4. Increased number of partnership disputes or conflicts.
5. Lack of alignment between partnership goals and company objectives.
6. High turnover of partnership personnel or key contacts.
7. Negative feedback from partners or stakeholders.

Dashboard Requirements for Partnership Manager:
1. Partnership performance metrics (e.g., engagement rates, satisfaction scores).
2. Overview of active partnerships and their status.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to partnership goals.
4. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
5. Historical data and trends for partnership performance over time.
6. Tools for tracking communication and interactions with partners.
7. Visualizations for partnership growth and potential opportunities.
8. Integration with other internal systems for seamless data flow.
