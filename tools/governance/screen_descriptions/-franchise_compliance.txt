Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Ensure compliance with operational standards and regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits of franchise operations.
3. Maintain accurate records of compliance activities.
4. Implement security measures, such as multi-factor authentication.
5. Monitor and report on operational performance.
6. Engage in continuous training and development for staff.
7. Address any compliance issues promptly.
8. Communicate effectively with the franchisor and other stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
2. Lack of transparency in operational reporting.
3. High turnover rates among staff.
4. Inconsistent application of policies and procedures.
5. Delays in addressing compliance issues.
6. Negative feedback from customers or stakeholders.
7. Poor communication with the franchisor.
8. Inadequate training programs for employees.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Audit logs and historical compliance data.
3. Alerts for upcoming audits or compliance deadlines.
4. Performance metrics and key performance indicators (KPIs).
5. Security policy compliance status.
6. Recent operational issues and resolutions.
7. Training and development progress tracking.
8. Communication logs with the franchisor.
