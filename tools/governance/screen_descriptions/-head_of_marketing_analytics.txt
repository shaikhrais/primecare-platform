Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies to increase brand awareness and drive sales.
2. Oversee market research and analyze consumer behavior to inform marketing decisions.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Coordinate marketing campaigns across various channels (digital, print, events).
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams to align marketing efforts with business goals.
6. Monitor and report on the performance of marketing campaigns and adjust strategies as needed.
7. Lead and mentor the marketing team, fostering a culture of creativity and innovation.
8. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities to identify opportunities and threats.
9. Build and maintain relationships with external partners and vendors.
10. Ensure brand consistency across all marketing materials and communications.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining engagement metrics or conversion rates.
2. Inconsistent messaging across different marketing channels.
3. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales teams.
4. Overspending on campaigns without measurable ROI.
5. High employee turnover within the marketing team.
6. Delays in campaign execution or project timelines.
7. Negative feedback from customers regarding marketing communications.
8. Failure to adapt to market changes or consumer preferences.
9. Inadequate tracking and reporting of marketing performance metrics.
10. Poor collaboration with other departments leading to siloed efforts.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for marketing campaigns (e.g., conversion rates, ROI).
2. Real-time analytics on website traffic and user engagement.
3. Social media performance metrics (likes, shares, comments).
4. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
5. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Market research insights and consumer behavior trends.
7. Campaign performance comparisons (A/B testing results).
8. Team performance metrics (project completion rates, employee satisfaction).
9. Competitor analysis and benchmarking data.
10. Alerts for any red flags or significant changes in performance metrics.
