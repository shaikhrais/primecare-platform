List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) must perform:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop and implement personalized treatment plans.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Stay updated on industry standards and practices.
7. Communicate effectively with clients regarding treatment progress.
8. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.
9. Participate in continuing education and training.
10. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.

Red flags of operation for RMTs:
1. Inconsistent client records or documentation.
2. Complaints from clients regarding treatment or service.
3. High cancellation or no-show rates.
4. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
5. Inability to provide evidence of continuing education.
6. Unprofessional behavior or communication.
7. Failure to obtain informed consent from clients.
8. Signs of burnout or stress in the therapist.
9. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
10. Poor feedback from peer reviews or audits.

Dashboard needs for Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs):
1. Overview of active clients and their treatment plans.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as client satisfaction scores.
4. Appointment scheduling and reminders.
5. Real-time updates on treatment outcomes.
6. Access to continuing education resources and requirements.
7. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
8. Client feedback and testimonials section.
9. Metrics on operational efficiency (e.g., appointment turnover).
10. Visual representation of treatment trends and client progress.
