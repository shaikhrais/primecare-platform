Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Monitor and assess patient health.
2. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
3. Assist in patient care planning and implementation.
4. Document patient care and progress in charts.
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
6. Educate patients and families about health conditions.
7. Maintain a safe and clean environment.
8. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
9. Conduct health screenings and assessments.
10. Respond to patient needs and emergencies.

Red Flags for RPN Operations:
1. Inconsistent patient monitoring or documentation.
2. Medication errors or discrepancies.
3. Unclear communication with healthcare team.
4. Patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
5. High rates of patient falls or incidents.
6. Delays in treatment or care delivery.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
8. Lack of timely updates in patient charts.
9. Insufficient training or knowledge of procedures.
10. Failure to follow up on patient concerns.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Patient health status overview.
2. Medication administration records.
3. Compliance audit results.
4. Operational audit logs.
5. Key performance indicators (KPIs).
6. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
7. Communication logs with healthcare team.
8. Training and certification status.
9. Patient satisfaction metrics.
10. Resource availability and inventory status.
