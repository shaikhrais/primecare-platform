Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements.
3. Manage financial records and budgets.
4. Oversee marketing and promotional activities.
5. Train and support franchise staff.
6. Maintain customer satisfaction and address complaints.
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
8. Implement operational improvements.
9. Communicate with the franchisor and other franchisees.
10. Analyze market trends and adapt strategies accordingly.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue trends.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Non-compliance with franchise standards.
5. Inconsistent marketing efforts.
6. Poor inventory management.
7. Lack of engagement with the franchisor.
8. Unresolved operational issues.
9. Failure to meet financial obligations.
10. Low customer retention rates.

Dashboard Needs for Franchise Owner:
1. Sales and revenue analytics.
2. Customer satisfaction scores.
3. Employee performance metrics.
4. Compliance checklists and alerts.
5. Marketing campaign effectiveness.
6. Inventory levels and turnover rates.
7. Financial health indicators (profit/loss).
8. Operational efficiency metrics.
9. Communication logs with the franchisor.
10. Market trend analysis and forecasts.
