Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Manage the full recruitment lifecycle from job posting to onboarding.
3. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
4. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
5. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
6. Maintain candidate databases and track recruitment metrics.
7. Ensure compliance with employment laws and regulations.
8. Build and maintain relationships with external recruitment agencies.
9. Conduct reference checks and background screenings.
10. Provide training and support to hiring managers on recruitment processes.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy time-to-fill positions suggesting inefficiencies in the recruitment process.
3. Low candidate quality leading to frequent rejections.
4. Negative feedback from candidates about the hiring process.
5. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
6. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
7. Failure to meet hiring goals or deadlines.
8. High reliance on external agencies without internal capability development.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, and candidate quality metrics.
2. Real-time tracking of open positions and their statuses.
3. Candidate pipeline visualization showing stages of recruitment.
4. Diversity metrics to assess inclusivity in hiring.
5. Source of hire analysis to identify effective recruitment channels.
6. Compliance tracking for hiring practices.
7. Feedback and satisfaction scores from candidates and hiring managers.
8. Historical data trends for recruitment metrics to identify patterns over time.
