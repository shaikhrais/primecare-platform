Customer Support Work Responsibilities:
1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Manage and resolve customer tickets efficiently.
3. Provide product information and support.
4. Escalate complex issues to appropriate teams.
5. Maintain accurate records of customer interactions.
6. Follow up with customers to ensure satisfaction.
7. Conduct compliance audits and ensure adherence to policies.
8. Document and log all customer interactions and resolutions.
9. Analyze customer feedback for service improvement.
10. Train and onboard new support staff.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased ticket resolution time.
2. High volume of escalated tickets.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Low customer satisfaction scores.
5. Inconsistent logging of customer interactions.
6. Lack of compliance with operational procedures.
7. High turnover rate among support staff.
8. Unresolved tickets exceeding standard response times.
9. Insufficient training for support staff.
10. Poor communication between support and other departments.

Dashboard Needs for Customer Support:
1. Real-time ticket status overview (open, in progress, resolved).
2. Average response and resolution times.
3. Customer satisfaction metrics (CSAT, NPS).
4. Volume of incoming tickets over time.
5. Escalation rates and reasons for escalation.
6. Compliance audit results and logs.
7. Staff performance metrics (tickets handled, resolution rates).
8. Customer feedback trends and analysis.
9. Operational audit logs for transparency.
10. Alerts for red flags in performance metrics.
