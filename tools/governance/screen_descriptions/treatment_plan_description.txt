Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient needs and develop treatment plans.
2. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
3. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
4. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation techniques.
5. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Stay updated on best practices and advancements in physiotherapy.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition or response to treatment.
2. Inconsistent patient attendance or engagement.
3. Lack of improvement in patient progress.
4. Adverse reactions to treatments or therapies.
5. High levels of patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
6. Non-compliance with treatment protocols or guidelines.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Overview of active patients and their treatment plans.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to patient outcomes.
4. Real-time telemetry data on treatment effectiveness.
5. Alerts for red flags or concerning trends in patient data.
6. Access to educational resources and latest research in physiotherapy.
7. Tools for documenting patient interactions and treatment adjustments.
