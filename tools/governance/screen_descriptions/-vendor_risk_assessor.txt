User Tasks:
1. Access the Vendor Risk Assessor screen to view vendor compliance data.
2. Refresh the vendor risk data by clicking the refresh button.
3. Review the risk levels of third-party vendors.
4. Analyze vendor details such as name, service type, and last audit date.
5. Identify vendors with high or critical risk levels for further action.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Failure to load vendor risk data, indicated by an error message.
2. Vendors with high or critical risk levels that require immediate attention.
3. Lack of recent audits for vendors, which may indicate compliance issues.
4. Inconsistent or missing vendor information, such as name or service type.

Dashboard Needs:
1. A clear display of vendor names and their compliance status.
2. Visual indicators for risk levels (e.g., color-coded risk levels).
3. Summary statistics for overall vendor risk distribution (e.g., percentage of vendors in each risk category).
4. Alerts or notifications for vendors requiring urgent review.
5. Filters to sort or search vendors based on risk level, service type, or last audit date.
