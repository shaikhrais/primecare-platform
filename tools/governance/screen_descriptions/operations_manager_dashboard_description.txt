Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
3. Conduct compliance audits and ensure adherence to regulations.
4. Manage operational risks and implement mitigation strategies.
5. Coordinate with other departments for seamless operations.
6. Analyze operational data and generate reports.
7. Implement process improvements and best practices.
8. Train and supervise staff.
9. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
10. Handle customer inquiries and resolve issues.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased productivity or efficiency.
2. Increased operational costs without justification.
3. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
4. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
5. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
6. Poor communication between teams.
7. Lack of clear operational metrics or KPIs.
8. Customer complaints or negative feedback trends.
9. Inconsistent quality of products or services.
10. Unresolved operational issues or bottlenecks.

Dashboard Requirements for Operations Manager:
1. Real-time KPIs and performance metrics.
2. Compliance audit status and results.
3. Operational logs and historical data.
4. Alerts for any red flags or issues.
5. Resource allocation and budget tracking.
6. Employee performance and satisfaction metrics.
7. Customer feedback and complaint tracking.
8. Visualizations for operational trends and forecasts.
9. Actionable insights for process improvements.
10. Quick access to communication tools for team coordination.
