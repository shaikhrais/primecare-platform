Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage and organize schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication (emails, phone calls).
3. Maintain filing systems and databases.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist with billing and invoicing processes.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Support team members with administrative tasks.
8. Monitor office supplies and place orders as needed.
9. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.
10. Provide customer service support.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Missed deadlines or appointments.
2. Inconsistent communication with team members.
3. Disorganized filing systems leading to lost documents.
4. Frequent errors in billing or invoicing.
5. Lack of responsiveness to emails or calls.
6. High turnover of administrative staff.
7. Poor feedback from team members regarding support.
8. Inability to track tasks and projects effectively.
9. Over-reliance on manual processes instead of automation.
10. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management system to track ongoing projects.
2. Calendar integration for scheduling and reminders.
3. Communication log for tracking correspondence.
4. Document management system for easy access to files.
5. Billing and invoicing overview with status updates.
6. Performance metrics to assess efficiency and productivity.
7. Inventory tracker for office supplies.
8. Feedback mechanism for team members to report issues.
9. Compliance checklist to ensure adherence to policies.
10. Quick access to frequently used resources and templates.
