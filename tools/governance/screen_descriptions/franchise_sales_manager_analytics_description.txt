Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations of the franchise.
2. Manage staff and ensure proper training.
3. Monitor sales performance and implement strategies to improve.
4. Maintain inventory and supply chain management.
5. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and company policies.
6. Handle customer service issues and feedback.
7. Conduct marketing and promotional activities.
8. Analyze financial reports and manage budgets.
9. Build relationships with the local community and stakeholders.
10. Attend franchise owner meetings and training sessions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales trends over consecutive months.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Inconsistent inventory levels leading to stockouts or overstock.
5. Non-compliance with franchise standards or policies.
6. Poor financial management or unexpected expenses.
7. Lack of engagement in marketing efforts.
8. Low customer retention rates.
9. Failure to meet operational benchmarks or KPIs.
10. Resistance to adopting new technologies or processes.

Dashboard Needs for Franchise Owner:
1. Sales performance metrics (daily, weekly, monthly).
2. Employee performance and turnover statistics.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
4. Inventory levels and turnover rates.
5. Financial summaries (profit and loss, expenses).
6. Marketing campaign performance and ROI.
7. Compliance checklists and alerts.
8. Operational KPIs and benchmarks.
9. Community engagement metrics.
10. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
