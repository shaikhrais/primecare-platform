User Work List:
1. Review quality assurance metrics and reports.
2. Analyze feedback from quality assurance reviews.
3. Identify areas for improvement in processes.
4. Collaborate with team members to address quality issues.
5. Document findings and recommendations for future reference.
6. Monitor the implementation of quality assurance improvements.
7. Participate in regular quality assurance meetings.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors or bugs reported in the system.
2. Delays in the quality assurance review process.
3. Lack of communication among team members regarding quality issues.
4. Inconsistent quality metrics across different projects.
5. Low engagement or participation in quality assurance activities.
6. High turnover rate of quality assurance personnel.
7. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding quality outcomes.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time quality assurance metrics and KPIs.
2. Visual representation of trends in quality issues over time.
3. Alerts for critical quality issues that need immediate attention.
4. Summary of recent quality assurance reviews and outcomes.
5. Feedback loop from team members on quality assurance processes.
6. Action items and follow-up tasks related to quality improvements.
7. Historical data for comparison and analysis of quality trends.
