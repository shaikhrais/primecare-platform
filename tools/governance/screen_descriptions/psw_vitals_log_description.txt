Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Monitor and record clients' vital signs and health status.
4. Assist with mobility and physical exercises.
5. Prepare and serve meals according to dietary requirements.
6. Administer medications as prescribed by healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare teams.
9. Document care provided and report any changes in clients' conditions.
10. Participate in training and professional development activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors in medication administration.
2. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
3. Lack of communication with healthcare teams.
4. Unexplained changes in clients' health status.
5. High turnover rates among staff.
6. Complaints from clients or families regarding care quality.
7. Failure to follow safety protocols and procedures.
8. Inadequate training or support for new staff.
9. Poor time management leading to missed care tasks.
10. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.

Dashboard Needs for Personal Support Worker (PSW):
1. Overview of active clients and their care plans.
2. Real-time updates on clients' vital signs and health status.
3. Alerts for medication schedules and administration.
4. Documentation logs for care provided and incidents reported.
5. Compliance status with regulatory standards.
6. Performance metrics such as client satisfaction scores.
7. Training and development resources for staff.
8. Communication tools for coordination with healthcare teams.
9. Incident reporting and tracking system.
10. Access to emergency protocols and contact information.
