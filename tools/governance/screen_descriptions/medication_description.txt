Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications as prescribed by healthcare providers.
2. Monitor patient vital signs and report any changes.
3. Assist in patient assessments and care planning.
4. Provide education to patients and families about medications and treatments.
5. Document patient care and medication administration accurately.
6. Collaborate with healthcare team members to ensure quality care.
7. Maintain a safe and clean environment for patients.
8. Participate in compliance audits and quality improvement initiatives.
9. Respond to patient inquiries and concerns regarding medications.
10. Ensure proper storage and handling of medications.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased medication errors or near misses.
2. High rates of patient complaints related to medication management.
3. Delays in medication administration.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Elevated patient vital signs indicating potential adverse reactions.
8. Failure to follow up on abnormal lab results related to medications.
9. Unresolved discrepancies in medication orders.
10. Signs of burnout or high turnover among nursing staff.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Real-time medication administration logs.
2. Alerts for overdue medications or missed doses.
3. Patient vital sign trends and alerts for abnormal readings.
4. Compliance audit results and action items.
5. Metrics on medication error rates and patient complaints.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Access to patient education resources.
8. Inventory management for medications and supplies.
9. Performance metrics for individual and team effectiveness.
10. Visual representation of patient outcomes related to medication management.
