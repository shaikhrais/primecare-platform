Talent Acquisition Manager Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement recruitment strategies.
2. Collaborate with hiring managers to understand staffing needs.
3. Source candidates through various channels (job boards, social media, networking).
4. Screen resumes and conduct interviews.
5. Manage the candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
6. Maintain relationships with potential candidates for future openings.
7. Analyze recruitment metrics to improve processes.
8. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
9. Conduct onboarding for new hires.
10. Provide training and support to hiring teams.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates indicating poor hiring decisions.
2. Lengthy hiring processes leading to lost candidates.
3. Negative candidate feedback about the recruitment experience.
4. Lack of diversity in candidate pools.
5. Inconsistent communication with candidates.
6. Failure to meet hiring targets.
7. Over-reliance on a single recruitment channel.
8. Ignoring data and metrics in decision-making.
9. Poor collaboration with hiring managers.
10. Inadequate onboarding processes leading to early attrition.

Dashboard Requirements for Talent Acquisition Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as time to fill, cost per hire, and quality of hire.
2. Candidate pipeline status showing the number of candidates at each stage.
3. Source of hire analysis to identify the most effective recruitment channels.
4. Diversity metrics to track progress towards diversity goals.
5. Candidate feedback scores to assess the recruitment experience.
6. Compliance status updates to ensure adherence to regulations.
7. Real-time updates on open positions and hiring manager requests.
8. Historical data trends to analyze recruitment performance over time.
9. Alerts for any red flags or issues in the hiring process.
10. Integration with applicant tracking systems for seamless data management.
