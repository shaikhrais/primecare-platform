Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budget Management
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Team Leadership and Development

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Unexplained fluctuations in financial metrics
2. Consistent budget overruns
3. Delays in financial reporting
4. High levels of debt or cash flow issues
5. Non-compliance with regulations
6. Lack of transparency in financial processes
7. Inconsistent or inaccurate financial data
8. Poor performance in key financial ratios
9. High employee turnover in finance department
10. Negative feedback from stakeholders

Dashboard Needs for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Overview
2. Budget vs. Actual Spending
3. Cash Flow Analysis
4. Compliance Status
5. Risk Assessment Metrics
6. Financial Forecasts
7. Operational Audit Logs
8. Revenue and Expense Trends
9. Investment Performance
10. Stakeholder Feedback Summary
