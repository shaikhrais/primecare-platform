List of caregiver tasks:
1. Schedule patient visits.
2. Monitor patient health and report changes.
3. Assist with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, etc.).
4. Administer medications as prescribed.
5. Maintain accurate patient records.
6. Communicate with healthcare professionals.
7. Ensure patient safety and comfort.
8. Provide companionship and emotional support.
9. Conduct compliance audits and report findings.
10. Participate in training and development programs.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent patient visit schedules.
2. Missed medication administration.
3. Lack of communication with healthcare teams.
4. Unreported changes in patient health.
5. Incomplete or inaccurate patient records.
6. High turnover rates among caregivers.
7. Failure to follow safety protocols.
8. Negative feedback from patients or families.
9. Delays in compliance audits.
10. Insufficient training or resources for caregivers.

Dashboard needs for caregivers:
1. Overview of scheduled visits and patient assignments.
2. Alerts for medication schedules and patient health changes.
3. Access to patient records and history.
4. Communication tools for contacting healthcare professionals.
5. Compliance audit logs and results.
6. Performance metrics (e.g., patient satisfaction, visit completion rates).
7. Training resources and updates.
8. Safety protocol reminders and checklists.
9. Log of daily activities and incidents.
10. Support resources for emotional and mental well-being.
