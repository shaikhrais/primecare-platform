Volunteer Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Recruit and onboard volunteers.
2. Train and orient volunteers on their roles and responsibilities.
3. Schedule volunteer shifts and manage availability.
4. Communicate with volunteers regarding updates and events.
5. Track volunteer hours and contributions.
6. Organize volunteer appreciation events.
7. Maintain volunteer records and databases.
8. Evaluate volunteer performance and provide feedback.
9. Address volunteer concerns and resolve conflicts.
10. Collaborate with other departments to align volunteer efforts with organizational goals.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volunteer turnover rate.
2. Lack of communication with volunteers.
3. Inconsistent scheduling leading to gaps in coverage.
4. Low volunteer engagement or participation in events.
5. Negative feedback from volunteers regarding their experience.
6. Difficulty in tracking volunteer hours or contributions.
7. Insufficient training leading to volunteer confusion.
8. Inadequate recognition or appreciation for volunteer efforts.
9. Overlapping roles or unclear responsibilities among volunteers.
10. Failure to meet organizational goals due to volunteer shortages.

Dashboard Needs for Volunteer Coordinator:
1. Volunteer recruitment metrics (number of new volunteers).
2. Volunteer retention rates.
3. Shift scheduling and coverage statistics.
4. Volunteer hours logged and contributions made.
5. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from volunteers.
6. Upcoming events and volunteer opportunities.
7. Training completion rates for volunteers.
8. Recognition and appreciation metrics (e.g., events held).
9. Communication logs with volunteers.
10. Performance evaluation summaries for volunteers.
