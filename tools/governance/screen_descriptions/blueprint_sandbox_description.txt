User Work List:
1. Monitor the lifecycle status of the screen.
2. Review the feature architecture and technical manifest.
3. Assess the development pipeline and completion percentage.
4. Evaluate platform integrity and governance actions.
5. Interact with the end drawer for additional options.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Lifecycle status remains in 'backlog', 'research', 'design', or 'legacy' for extended periods.
2. Completion percentage is significantly low or stagnant.
3. Technical manifest lacks clarity or detail.
4. Governance actions are not being addressed or are overdue.
5. Inconsistent updates or lack of communication regarding the screen's progress.

Dashboard Requirements for User:
1. Visual representation of lifecycle status with color coding.
2. Completion percentage indicator.
3. Quick access to technical manifest and development pipeline details.
4. Alerts for overdue governance actions.
5. Summary of recent activities or changes related to the screen.
