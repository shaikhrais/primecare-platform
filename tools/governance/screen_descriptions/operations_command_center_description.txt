Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Lead and mentor operational teams.
7. Collaborate with other executives to align operations with company goals.
8. Identify areas for improvement and implement changes.
9. Manage risk and ensure business continuity.
10. Communicate operational strategies to stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased productivity or efficiency metrics.
2. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
3. High employee turnover rates.
4. Frequent compliance issues or audit failures.
5. Poor customer satisfaction or service delivery.
6. Lack of clear communication within teams.
7. Inconsistent performance across departments.
8. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
9. Insufficient resource allocation leading to bottlenecks.
10. Negative trends in key performance indicators.

Dashboard Requirements for COO:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Real-time operational metrics (e.g., productivity, efficiency).
3. Compliance status and audit results.
4. Financial performance indicators (e.g., budget vs. actual).
5. Employee performance and engagement metrics.
6. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Risk management dashboard.
8. Operational logs and incident reports.
9. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
10. Alerts for any operational anomalies or red flags.
