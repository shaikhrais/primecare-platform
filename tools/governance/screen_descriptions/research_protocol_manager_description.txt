User Work List:
1. Create new research protocols.
2. Edit existing research protocols.
3. Review and approve research protocols.
4. Monitor protocol compliance and progress.
5. Generate reports on research protocols.
6. Collaborate with team members on protocol development.
7. Access historical data on past protocols.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in protocol approval process.
2. High number of revisions required for protocols.
3. Lack of collaboration or communication among team members.
4. Incomplete or missing documentation for protocols.
5. Frequent compliance issues reported.
6. Low user engagement with the platform.
7. Inconsistent data entry or reporting.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of active research protocols.
2. Status indicators for each protocol (e.g., pending, approved, in progress).
3. Notifications for pending approvals or required actions.
4. Metrics on protocol compliance and adherence.
5. Collaboration activity feed (e.g., comments, edits).
6. Quick access to create or edit protocols.
7. Visual representation of historical data and trends.
