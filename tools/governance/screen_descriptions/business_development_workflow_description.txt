Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key partners and stakeholders.
4. Conduct market research and analysis to inform strategy.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align efforts.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals and reports.
8. Manage and mentor the business development team.
9. Set and track performance metrics for business development initiatives.
10. Ensure compliance with company policies and regulations.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High turnover rates within the business development team.
3. Lack of clear communication and collaboration among teams.
4. Inconsistent or unclear business development strategies.
5. Failure to meet performance metrics or targets.
6. Negative feedback from partners or clients.
7. Limited market research or understanding of industry trends.
8. Resistance to change or innovation within the team.
9. Over-reliance on a few key clients or partners.
10. Poor tracking and reporting of business development activities.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue growth.
2. Visual representation of new business opportunities and pipeline status.
3. Metrics on team performance and individual contributions.
4. Market analysis and competitor benchmarking data.
5. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Progress tracking for ongoing business development initiatives.
7. Alerts for any red flags or issues in operations.
8. Historical data trends for informed decision-making.
9. Integration with CRM and other relevant tools.
10. Customizable views for different stakeholders.
