Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Provide personal care to residents, including bathing, grooming, and dressing.
2. Assist with mobility and transfers.
3. Monitor and document residents' health status and changes.
4. Administer medications as prescribed.
5. Support residents with daily living activities.
6. Communicate effectively with residents, families, and healthcare teams.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment.
8. Attend training and development sessions.
9. Complete required documentation and charting.
10. Report any concerns or changes in residents' conditions to supervisors.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of complaints from residents or families.
2. Low attendance or charting completion rates.
3. Missed or overdue certifications and training.
4. Excessive overtime or missed shifts.
5. High-risk flags related to resident care.
6. Poor communication logs or lack of documentation.
7. Negative feedback from supervisors or peers.

Dashboard Needs for PSW:
1. Overview of current status (active, on leave, etc.).
2. Attendance and charting completion rates.
3. List of certifications with expiry dates and statuses.
4. Schedule of shifts and assigned residents.
5. High-risk flags and alerts.
6. Complaints count and satisfaction rate.
7. Communication logs and notes.
8. Upcoming training and courses.
9. Performance notes from supervisors.
10. Logs of recent activities and updates.
