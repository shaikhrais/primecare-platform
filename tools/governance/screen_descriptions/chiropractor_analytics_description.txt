List of work Chiropractor must do:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
3. Perform spinal manipulations and adjustments.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and research.

Red flags for operation:
1. Patients experiencing severe pain or discomfort post-treatment.
2. Neurological symptoms such as numbness or weakness.
3. Unexplained weight loss or fever in patients.
4. History of cancer or other serious medical conditions.
5. Patients not responding to treatment as expected.
6. Signs of infection or inflammation in the treatment area.

Dashboard needs for Chiropractor:
1. Patient demographics and statistics.
2. Treatment effectiveness metrics.
3. Appointment scheduling and reminders.
4. Patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Integration with electronic health records (EHR).
6. Performance tracking against treatment goals.
7. Access to educational resources and latest research.
8. Alerts for red flags and patient follow-up reminders.
