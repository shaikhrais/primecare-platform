Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
3. Monitor operational performance and risk management.
4. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
5. Maintain accurate records and documentation.
6. Provide training and support on governance issues.
7. Develop and implement governance frameworks.
8. Report on governance performance and issues.
9. Manage governance-related projects and initiatives.
10. Stay updated on changes in laws and regulations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit processes or reporting.
3. Lack of communication among stakeholders.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
6. Negative feedback from audits or assessments.
7. Failure to meet regulatory deadlines.
8. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
9. Lack of training or awareness among staff.
10. Poor performance metrics in key areas.

Dashboard Requirements for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance status overview (pass/fail metrics).
2. Audit logs and history.
3. Operational performance metrics (KPIs).
4. Risk assessment indicators.
5. Communication logs with stakeholders.
6. Training completion rates.
7. Regulatory updates and deadlines.
8. Action items from previous audits.
9. Real-time alerts for compliance issues.
10. Visual representation of trends in governance performance.
