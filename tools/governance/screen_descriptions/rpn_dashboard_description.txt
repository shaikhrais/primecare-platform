Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
2. Monitor patients' vital signs and report changes to the healthcare team.
3. Assist in patient assessments and care planning.
4. Provide personal care and support to patients.
5. Educate patients and families about health management.
6. Document patient care and progress accurately.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
8. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
9. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
10. Maintain patient confidentiality and privacy.

Red Flags for RPN Operations:
1. Unexplained changes in patient vital signs.
2. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
3. High rates of medication errors.
4. Frequent patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
8. Increased incidents of falls or injuries.
9. Unresolved conflicts within the healthcare team.
10. Inadequate staffing levels impacting patient care.

Dashboard Requirements for RPN:
1. Real-time monitoring of patient vital signs.
2. Alerts for abnormal vital signs or critical lab results.
3. Compliance tracking for medication administration.
4. Documentation status indicators (e.g., completed, pending).
5. Patient satisfaction metrics and feedback.
6. Incident reporting and tracking system.
7. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
8. Access to educational resources for patient care.
9. Audit logs for compliance and operational reviews.
10. Communication tools for team collaboration.
