Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Regulatory Oversight
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Stakeholder Communication
10. Performance Measurement

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Consistent budget overruns
2. Declining cash flow
3. Increased debt levels
4. Unexplained variances in financial reports
5. Compliance issues or regulatory penalties
6. High employee turnover in finance department
7. Poor financial forecasting accuracy
8. Lack of transparency in financial processes
9. Delays in financial reporting
10. Inconsistent financial metrics

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) overview
2. Cash flow status
3. Budget vs. Actual performance
4. Profit and loss statements
5. Compliance status and alerts
6. Risk assessment metrics
7. Financial forecasts and trends
8. Operational audit logs
9. Investment performance overview
10. Stakeholder engagement metrics
