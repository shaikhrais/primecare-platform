List of work training candidates must do:
1. Complete mandatory training modules.
2. Participate in compliance audits.
3. Attend scheduled training sessions.
4. Submit secure event reports.
5. Engage in real-time system audit telemetry.
6. Update governance directives as required.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in completing training modules.
2. Non-compliance with audit requirements.
3. Lack of participation in training sessions.
4. Incomplete or missing secure event reports.
5. Failure to update governance directives.
6. High number of errors in compliance audit results.

Dashboard needs for training candidates:
1. Overview of training completion status.
2. Upcoming training session schedules.
3. Compliance audit results and logs.
4. Real-time notifications for required actions.
5. Summary of governance directives and their statuses.
6. Secure event reporting submissions and statuses.
7. Historical logs of training activities and compliance checks.
