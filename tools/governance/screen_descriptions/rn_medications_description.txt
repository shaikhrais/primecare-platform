Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and monitor patient responses.
2. Conduct patient assessments and document findings.
3. Collaborate with healthcare team members for patient care.
4. Educate patients and families about health conditions and treatments.
5. Maintain accurate patient records and logs.
6. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and protocols.
7. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
8. Respond to patient emergencies and provide appropriate interventions.
9. Manage patient care plans and coordinate with other healthcare providers.
10. Stay updated on medical practices and advancements.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
2. Delays in patient assessments or treatments.
3. High patient-to-nurse ratios leading to inadequate care.
4. Frequent complaints from patients or families.
5. Non-compliance with safety protocols or regulations.
6. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
7. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
8. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
9. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
10. Poor patient outcomes or satisfaction scores.

Dashboard Needs for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Real-time patient status updates and alerts.
2. Medication administration records and compliance tracking.
3. Patient assessment logs and documentation status.
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care.
5. Compliance audit results and action items.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Access to educational resources and training materials.
8. Incident reporting and tracking system.
9. Patient satisfaction metrics and feedback.
10. Visualizations of workload and staffing levels.
