Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements.
3. Manage franchisee relationships and support.
4. Implement marketing strategies for franchise growth.
5. Conduct regular training and development for franchisees.
6. Analyze sales data and operational efficiency.
7. Address franchisee concerns and feedback.
8. Maintain brand standards across all franchise locations.
9. Oversee financial management and budgeting.
10. Evaluate potential new franchise locations.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales trends over consecutive months.
2. High turnover rates among franchisees.
3. Frequent complaints from franchisees regarding support.
4. Non-compliance with brand standards.
5. Poor customer feedback and reviews.
6. Inconsistent marketing efforts across locations.
7. Lack of engagement from franchisees in training programs.
8. Unresolved financial discrepancies or issues.
9. Low inventory turnover rates.
10. High operational costs without corresponding revenue.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for each franchise location.
2. Sales trends and forecasts.
3. Franchisee satisfaction scores and feedback.
4. Compliance checklists and status updates.
5. Marketing campaign performance metrics.
6. Training participation rates and outcomes.
7. Financial summaries including revenue, expenses, and profit margins.
8. Customer feedback and review summaries.
9. Inventory management insights.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
