List of work the User must do:
1. Monitor ongoing marketing campaigns.
2. Analyze campaign performance metrics.
3. Adjust strategies based on data insights.
4. Collaborate with the marketing team for campaign planning.
5. Review budget allocations for campaigns.
6. Ensure alignment with overall marketing goals.
7. Report results to upper management.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant drop in engagement metrics.
2. High cost per acquisition (CPA) compared to benchmarks.
3. Low conversion rates from campaigns.
4. Delays in campaign execution.
5. Negative feedback from target audience.
6. Inconsistent messaging across channels.
7. Budget overruns without clear justification.

What is needed on the User dashboard:
1. Real-time performance metrics (CTR, CPA, conversion rates).
2. Visual representation of campaign progress (charts/graphs).
3. Alerts for any red flags or anomalies in data.
4. Budget tracking and forecasting tools.
5. Collaboration tools for team communication.
6. Access to historical campaign data for comparison.
7. A summary of key insights and recommendations for improvement.
