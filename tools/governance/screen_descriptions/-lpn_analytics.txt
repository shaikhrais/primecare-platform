Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) Responsibilities:
1. Administer medications and injections as prescribed.
2. Monitor patients' vital signs and report changes to the healthcare team.
3. Assist with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and eating.
4. Maintain patient records and documentation.
5. Provide basic wound care and assist with medical procedures.
6. Educate patients and families about health management and care plans.
7. Collaborate with registered nurses and other healthcare professionals.
8. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.
9. Participate in patient assessments and care planning.
10. Maintain a clean and safe environment for patients.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing patient records.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols and procedures.
8. Elevated rates of hospital readmissions.
9. Inadequate training or support for new staff.
10. Signs of burnout or low morale among nursing staff.

Dashboard Needs for Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN):
1. Real-time patient monitoring metrics (vital signs, medication administration).
2. Compliance tracking for medication and treatment protocols.
3. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
4. Incident reporting and tracking system for errors or adverse events.
5. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
6. Training and certification status of nursing staff.
7. Alerts for overdue tasks or assessments.
8. Performance metrics for individual LPNs and teams.
9. Access to patient care plans and documentation.
10. Communication tools for collaboration with healthcare team members.
