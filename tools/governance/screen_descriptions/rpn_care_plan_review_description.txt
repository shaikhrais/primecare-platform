Registered Practical Nurse (RPN) Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient health status and needs.
2. Develop and implement nursing care plans.
3. Administer medications and treatments as prescribed.
4. Monitor patient progress and response to treatment.
5. Collaborate with healthcare team members.
6. Educate patients and families about health conditions and care plans.
7. Document patient care and progress accurately.
8. Assist in diagnostic tests and procedures.
9. Provide emotional support to patients and families.
10. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
4. High staff turnover or low morale among team members.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols or regulations.
7. Poor communication among healthcare team members.
8. Unusual patterns in patient health outcomes.

Dashboard Needs for RPN:
1. Real-time patient health metrics and alerts.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for nursing care.
4. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
5. Medication administration records and alerts.
6. Staffing levels and workload indicators.
7. Access to patient care plans and progress notes.
8. Communication tools for team collaboration.
9. Training and compliance resources.
10. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
