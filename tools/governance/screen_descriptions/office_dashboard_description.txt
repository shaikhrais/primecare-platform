Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Organize and maintain files and records.
3. Prepare and edit correspondence, reports, and presentations.
4. Handle incoming and outgoing communications.
5. Assist with bookkeeping and invoicing.
6. Coordinate office activities and events.
7. Maintain office supplies and inventory.
8. Support team members with administrative tasks.
9. Conduct research and compile data for reports.
10. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent missed deadlines.
2. Inconsistent communication with team members.
3. Lack of organization in files and records.
4. High levels of stress or burnout among staff.
5. Poor time management leading to inefficiencies.
6. Inability to adapt to changes in priorities.
7. Low morale or engagement within the team.
8. Repeated errors in documentation or correspondence.
9. Insufficient follow-up on tasks and responsibilities.
10. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management section to track deadlines and priorities.
2. Calendar view for scheduling and appointments.
3. Communication log for tracking messages and correspondence.
4. Inventory tracker for office supplies.
5. Performance metrics for individual and team productivity.
6. Document management system for easy access to files.
7. Compliance checklist for adherence to policies.
8. Feedback section for team input and suggestions.
9. Audit log for tracking completed tasks and activities.
10. Alerts and reminders for important deadlines and events.
