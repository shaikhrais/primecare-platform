List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) must perform:
1. Conduct client assessments and intake interviews.
2. Develop and implement treatment plans based on client needs.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.
6. Communicate effectively with clients about their treatment.
7. Continuously update skills and knowledge through professional development.
8. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.
9. Handle billing and insurance claims as necessary.
10. Maintain a clean and organized treatment environment.

Red flags in operations that may indicate issues:
1. High client turnover or cancellations.
2. Inconsistent treatment outcomes or client feedback.
3. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
4. Poor communication with clients or team members.
5. Incomplete or inaccurate client records.
6. Frequent complaints or negative reviews.
7. Inability to manage time effectively during appointments.
8. Resistance to adopting new techniques or technologies.
9. Over-reliance on specific clients for income.
10. Burnout or decreased job satisfaction among staff.

Dashboard requirements for Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs):
1. Client management system with easy access to client records.
2. Appointment scheduling and reminders.
3. Treatment plan templates and tracking.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., client satisfaction scores, treatment outcomes).
5. Compliance checklists for health and safety standards.
6. Continuing education tracking and reminders.
7. Financial overview (e.g., billing, payments, revenue).
8. Communication tools for client follow-ups and reminders.
9. Feedback collection system for client reviews.
10. Integration with other healthcare systems for referrals and collaboration.
