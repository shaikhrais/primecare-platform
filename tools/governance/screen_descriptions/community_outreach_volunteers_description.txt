List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor community outreach volunteer activities.
2. Track volunteer participation and engagement.
3. Report on outreach effectiveness and volunteer feedback.
4. Manage volunteer schedules and availability.
5. Communicate with volunteers regarding upcoming events and opportunities.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low volunteer participation rates.
2. Delayed responses to volunteer inquiries.
3. Inconsistent communication regarding events.
4. Lack of feedback from volunteers on their experiences.
5. Missed deadlines for outreach initiatives.

What we need on the dashboard of the user:
1. Volunteer participation statistics (e.g., number of active volunteers, event attendance).
2. Upcoming events calendar with volunteer assignments.
3. Feedback summary from volunteers on past events.
4. Alerts for low participation or engagement levels.
5. Communication logs with volunteers for transparency.
