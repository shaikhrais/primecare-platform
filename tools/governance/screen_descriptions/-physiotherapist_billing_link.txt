Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient conditions and needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated on best practices and advancements in physiotherapy.

Red Flags for Physiotherapy Operations:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition.
2. Inconsistent patient progress.
3. Unexplained pain or discomfort reported by patients.
4. Non-compliance with treatment plans.
5. High dropout rates from therapy sessions.
6. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
7. Incomplete or inaccurate patient records.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapists:
1. Patient progress tracking metrics.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
4. Alerts for red flags or concerns in patient care.
5. Access to patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Summary of active treatment plans and goals.
7. Integration with scheduling and billing systems.
8. Real-time updates on compliance and operational audits.
