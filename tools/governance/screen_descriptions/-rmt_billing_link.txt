Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans based on client assessments.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue, sports).
4. Maintain accurate client records and treatment documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
7. Communicate effectively with clients about treatment goals and progress.
8. Adhere to ethical and professional standards in practice.
9. Manage billing and insurance claims for services rendered.
10. Participate in team meetings and collaborate with other healthcare professionals.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Frequent client complaints or negative feedback.
2. High cancellation or no-show rates.
3. Inconsistent treatment outcomes or client progress.
4. Issues with billing or insurance claims.
5. Lack of compliance with health and safety regulations.
6. Poor record-keeping practices.
7. High turnover of staff or therapists.
8. Inadequate training or professional development opportunities.
9. Failure to maintain client confidentiality.
10. Non-compliance with industry standards or regulations.

Dashboard Requirements for RMT:
1. Overview of active clients and treatment plans.
2. Metrics on client satisfaction and feedback.
3. Compliance status with health and safety regulations.
4. Billing and revenue tracking.
5. Appointment scheduling and management tools.
6. Logs of treatment sessions and client progress.
7. Alerts for upcoming renewals of licenses or certifications.
8. Educational resources and training opportunities.
9. Performance metrics for individual therapists.
10. Integration with other healthcare systems for holistic client management.
