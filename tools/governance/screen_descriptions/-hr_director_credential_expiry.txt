HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing strategies.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and conflict resolution.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Monitor employee performance and conduct evaluations.
6. Implement training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Frequent complaints or grievances from staff.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Poor performance management practices.
8. Inconsistent communication from leadership.
9. Insufficient resources for HR functions.
10. Negative feedback from exit interviews.

Dashboard Needs for HR Director:
1. Employee turnover rates and trends.
2. Employee engagement survey results.
3. Compliance status with labor laws.
4. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, open positions).
5. Training and development participation rates.
6. Performance evaluation completion rates.
7. Diversity and inclusion statistics.
8. Compensation and benefits analysis.
9. Employee satisfaction feedback.
10. Operational audit logs and compliance scan results.
