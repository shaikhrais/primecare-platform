List of tasks Legal Counsel must perform:
1. Review and draft legal documents.
2. Provide legal advice to management and staff.
3. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations.
4. Manage legal risks and disputes.
5. Conduct legal research.
6. Liaise with external legal counsel when necessary.
7. Monitor changes in legislation that may affect the organization.
8. Develop and implement legal policies and procedures.
9. Train staff on legal compliance and best practices.
10. Maintain records of legal documents and correspondence.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased frequency of legal disputes or litigation.
2. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
3. Lack of documentation for legal processes.
4. Delays in contract reviews or approvals.
5. Poor communication between legal and other departments.
6. High turnover of legal staff.
7. Inadequate training on legal matters for employees.
8. Failure to keep up with changes in laws and regulations.
9. Unclear roles and responsibilities within the legal team.
10. Insufficient resources allocated to the legal department.

Dashboard needs for Legal Counsel:
1. Overview of ongoing legal cases and their statuses.
2. Compliance tracking metrics.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for legal operations.
4. Document management system for easy access to legal documents.
5. Alerts for upcoming deadlines and important dates.
6. Reporting tools for legal expenses and budget tracking.
7. Integration with other departments for seamless communication.
8. Training resources and compliance materials for staff.
9. Analytics on legal risks and dispute trends.
10. Feedback mechanism for continuous improvement of legal processes.
