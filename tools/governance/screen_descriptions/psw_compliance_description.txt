Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Administer medications as prescribed.
5. Monitor and report changes in clients' health status.
6. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
7. Assist with meal preparation and feeding if necessary.
8. Document care provided and any changes in clients' conditions.
9. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
10. Follow care plans and protocols established by healthcare providers.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or families about care quality.
2. Inconsistent documentation of care provided.
3. Missed medications or appointments.
4. Signs of neglect or abuse.
5. High turnover rates among PSWs.
6. Lack of communication between PSWs and healthcare teams.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols and regulations.
8. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the care team.

Dashboard Needs for Personal Support Worker (PSW):
1. Overview of client assignments and schedules.
2. Compliance status indicators for care plans.
3. Log of recent activities and interventions.
4. Alerts for missed medications or appointments.
5. Performance metrics and feedback from clients.
6. Access to training resources and updates on best practices.
7. Communication tools for coordination with healthcare teams.
8. Incident reporting and tracking system for issues or concerns.
9. Summary of compliance audits and action items.
10. Personal development and training progress tracking.
