User Work List:
1. View the list of residents in the residency program.
2. Refresh the data to see the latest updates.
3. Expand each resident's card to view detailed milestones.
4. Monitor the completion percentage of each resident's tasks.
5. Identify residents who are behind on their milestones.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent loading states indicating slow API response times.
2. Errors displayed when fetching resident data, suggesting potential issues with the API or data integrity.
3. Residents with low completion percentages, indicating they may be falling behind in their program.
4. Missing or incomplete milestone data for residents, which could affect their progress tracking.

Dashboard Needs:
1. A summary of the total number of residents and their overall completion percentage.
2. Alerts for residents who are behind on milestones or have low completion percentages.
3. A filter or search option to quickly find specific residents.
4. Visual indicators (e.g., color coding) for residents based on their progress status.
5. A historical view of residents' progress over time to identify trends.
