Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Monitor and analyze performance metrics.
3. Implement operational policies and procedures.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Coordinate with different departments to ensure smooth operations.
6. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
7. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
8. Train and supervise staff.
9. Handle customer complaints and service issues.
10. Prepare reports for upper management.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased productivity or efficiency.
2. Increased number of customer complaints.
3. High employee turnover rates.
4. Frequent operational errors or issues.
5. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
6. Non-compliance with regulations.
7. Poor communication between departments.
8. Lack of clear operational goals or objectives.

Dashboard Requirements for Operations Manager:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for operational efficiency.
2. Real-time data on productivity and performance metrics.
3. Compliance status and audit logs.
4. Budget tracking and financial performance.
5. Employee performance and turnover statistics.
6. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Alerts for operational issues or red flags.
8. Historical data trends for analysis.
9. Visual representations of data (charts, graphs).
10. Actionable insights and recommendations based on data analysis.
