Customer Support Work Responsibilities:
1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance.
3. Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and transactions.
4. Follow up with customers to ensure their issues are resolved.
5. Collaborate with other departments to address customer needs.
6. Train and educate customers on product usage.
7. Gather customer feedback for product improvement.
8. Monitor and report on customer satisfaction metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased response times to customer inquiries.
2. High volume of unresolved customer issues.
3. Negative customer feedback or low satisfaction scores.
4. Frequent escalations to higher support tiers.
5. Lack of communication between support and other departments.
6. Inconsistent documentation of customer interactions.
7. High turnover rate within the support team.

Dashboard Requirements for Customer Support:
1. Real-time metrics on response and resolution times.
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
3. Volume of incoming inquiries and issues.
4. Status of open and closed tickets.
5. Performance metrics for individual support agents.
6. Alerts for unresolved issues exceeding a certain timeframe.
7. Integration with customer relationship management (CRM) tools.
8. Historical data for trend analysis and reporting.
