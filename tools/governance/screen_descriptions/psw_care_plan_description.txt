List of tasks Personal Support Workers (PSWs) must perform:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities (bathing, dressing, grooming).
2. Administer medications as prescribed.
3. Monitor and record clients' health status and changes.
4. Provide companionship and emotional support.
5. Assist with mobility and physical exercises.
6. Prepare meals and assist with nutrition.
7. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
8. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
9. Document care provided and report any concerns.
10. Participate in training and continuing education.

Red flags indicating operational issues:
1. Increased number of missed appointments or cancellations.
2. Frequent complaints from clients or families.
3. High turnover rates among PSWs.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing logs.
5. Delays in medication administration.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Low morale or job satisfaction among PSWs.
8. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
9. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.
10. Decreased client satisfaction scores.

Dashboard requirements for Personal Support Workers (PSWs):
1. Overview of active cases and client assignments.
2. Real-time updates on client health status and alerts.
3. Compliance audit results and logs.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., number of tasks completed, client feedback).
5. Communication tools for team collaboration.
6. Training and development resources.
7. Incident reporting and resolution tracking.
8. Access to client care plans and documentation.
9. Visualizations of workload and productivity trends.
10. Alerts for upcoming appointments and medication schedules.
