Personal Support Worker (PSW) Responsibilities:
1. Assist clients with daily living activities such as bathing, dressing, and grooming.
2. Provide companionship and emotional support to clients.
3. Help clients with mobility and physical exercises.
4. Administer medications as prescribed and monitor health conditions.
5. Prepare meals and assist with nutrition.
6. Maintain a clean and safe environment for clients.
7. Document and report any changes in clients' health or behavior.
8. Communicate effectively with clients, families, and healthcare professionals.
9. Follow care plans and protocols established by healthcare providers.
10. Participate in training and continuing education to enhance skills.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent complaints from clients or families about care quality.
2. High turnover rates among PSWs.
3. Inconsistent documentation of client care.
4. Missed medication administration or errors in medication management.
5. Lack of communication between PSWs and healthcare teams.
6. Signs of burnout or stress among PSWs.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols or care plans.
8. Unresolved conflicts or issues within the team.

Dashboard Needs for PSW:
1. Overview of active clients and their care plans.
2. Real-time updates on client health status and any incidents.
3. Logs of daily activities and care provided.
4. Alerts for medication schedules and compliance checks.
5. Performance metrics such as client satisfaction scores.
6. Training and certification status of PSWs.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with healthcare teams.
8. Access to resources and support for PSWs, including mental health resources.
