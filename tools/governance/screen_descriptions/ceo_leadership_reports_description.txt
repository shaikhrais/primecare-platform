List of work the user must do:
1. Review the leadership reports presented on the screen.
2. Analyze the data provided in the reports for decision-making.
3. Identify key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to the organization's goals.
4. Monitor trends and patterns in the reports for strategic planning.
5. Provide feedback or suggestions based on the reports to improve operations.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent data or discrepancies in the reports.
2. Delays in loading the reports or data.
3. Frequent errors when accessing the reports.
4. Lack of clarity in the information presented.
5. Missing key metrics that are critical for decision-making.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. A summary of key metrics and KPIs at a glance.
2. Visual representations of data (charts, graphs) for easier interpretation.
3. Alerts for any anomalies or red flags detected in the data.
4. A section for user feedback or comments on the reports.
5. Quick access to historical data for comparison purposes.
