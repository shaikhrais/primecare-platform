Clinical Nurse Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement care plans.
3. Provide education and support to patients and families.
4. Collaborate with healthcare teams to ensure quality care.
5. Monitor patient progress and outcomes.
6. Advocate for patients' needs and preferences.
7. Conduct research and apply evidence-based practices.
8. Participate in quality improvement initiatives.
9. Provide training and mentorship to nursing staff.
10. Manage complex patient cases and coordinate care.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of readmissions or complications.
3. Delays in patient care or treatment.
4. Inconsistent documentation or care plans.
5. Lack of communication among healthcare team members.
6. Non-compliance with established protocols or guidelines.
7. Increased staff turnover or burnout.
8. Failure to meet regulatory or accreditation standards.
9. Inadequate patient education leading to misunderstandings.
10. Unresolved conflicts within the healthcare team.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Nurse Specialist:
1. Real-time patient data and metrics.
2. Compliance and audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient outcomes.
4. Alerts for critical patient issues or changes.
5. Access to care plans and documentation.
6. Communication tools for team collaboration.
7. Educational resources for patient and staff training.
8. Reporting tools for tracking trends and outcomes.
9. Integration with electronic health records (EHR).
10. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and access to information.
