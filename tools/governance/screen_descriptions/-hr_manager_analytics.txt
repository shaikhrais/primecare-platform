List of tasks for HR Director:
1. Develop and implement HR strategies aligned with business goals.
2. Oversee recruitment and selection processes.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Design and implement training and development programs.
6. Monitor and improve employee performance management systems.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red flags in operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Increased number of grievances or complaints.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Poor performance management outcomes.
7. Lack of training and development opportunities.
8. Ineffective communication within the organization.
9. Negative feedback from exit interviews.
10. Discrepancies in payroll or benefits administration.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Employee turnover rate.
2. Employee engagement scores.
3. Recruitment metrics (time to fill, quality of hire).
4. Training and development participation rates.
5. Performance management statistics.
6. Compliance tracking (labor law adherence).
7. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
8. Employee satisfaction survey results.
9. Grievance and conflict resolution statistics.
10. Compensation and benefits analysis.
