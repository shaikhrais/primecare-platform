User Tasks:
1. Monitor patient care metrics.
2. Review patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
3. Track medication adherence and compliance.
4. Analyze operational efficiency and resource allocation.
5. Generate reports on patient outcomes and care quality.
6. Update patient records and care plans as needed.
7. Collaborate with healthcare team members for patient care.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in patient satisfaction scores.
2. Increased medication errors or non-compliance rates.
3. High staff turnover or absenteeism.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment plans.
5. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in patient records.
6. Low engagement from healthcare team members.
7. Unresolved patient complaints or issues.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time patient care metrics display.
2. Visual representation of patient satisfaction scores.
3. Alerts for medication adherence issues.
4. Operational efficiency indicators (e.g., average wait times).
5. Easy access to patient records and care plans.
6. Collaboration tools for team communication.
7. Reporting features for generating insights and trends.
