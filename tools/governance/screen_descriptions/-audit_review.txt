Compliance Manager Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits.
2. Monitor adherence to regulatory requirements.
3. Review and update compliance policies and procedures.
4. Train staff on compliance-related issues.
5. Investigate compliance breaches and report findings.
6. Maintain documentation of compliance activities.
7. Collaborate with other departments to ensure compliance.
8. Prepare compliance reports for management.
9. Stay updated on changes in regulations and standards.
10. Implement corrective actions for compliance failures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance breaches.
2. Delays in compliance reporting.
3. Lack of employee training on compliance matters.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. High turnover in compliance staff.
6. Negative audit findings or feedback.
7. Failure to address previous compliance issues.
8. Poor communication between departments regarding compliance.
9. Lack of updates to compliance policies.
10. Insufficient resources allocated for compliance activities.

Dashboard Requirements for Compliance Manager:
1. Overview of compliance status and metrics.
2. List of recent compliance audits and findings.
3. Alerts for compliance breaches or issues.
4. Training status of employees on compliance topics.
5. Timeline of compliance activities and deadlines.
6. Documentation repository for compliance policies.
7. Reports on regulatory changes and their impact.
8. Performance indicators for compliance effectiveness.
9. Historical data on compliance trends.
10. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and access to information.
