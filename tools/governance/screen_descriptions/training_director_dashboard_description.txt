Training Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee training program development and implementation.
2. Monitor compliance with training regulations and standards.
3. Evaluate training effectiveness and make necessary adjustments.
4. Manage training budgets and resources.
5. Coordinate with other departments to align training with organizational goals.
6. Maintain training records and documentation.
7. Provide leadership and support to training staff.
8. Develop and deliver training sessions as needed.
9. Analyze training needs and identify skill gaps.
10. Report on training outcomes and metrics to stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High turnover rates among training staff.
2. Low participation or engagement in training programs.
3. Frequent compliance issues or audits failing.
4. Negative feedback from trainees regarding training quality.
5. Inconsistent training records or documentation.
6. Budget overruns or misallocation of training resources.
7. Lack of alignment between training programs and organizational goals.
8. Delays in training program delivery or implementation.
9. Insufficient follow-up on training effectiveness.
10. Poor communication with other departments regarding training needs.

Dashboard Requirements for Training Director:
1. Overview of active training programs and their status.
2. Compliance metrics and audit results.
3. Training participation rates and engagement levels.
4. Budget utilization and financial metrics related to training.
5. Feedback and satisfaction scores from trainees.
6. Logs of training sessions conducted and attendance records.
7. Alerts for compliance issues or training gaps.
8. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to training effectiveness.
9. Visualizations of training outcomes and improvements over time.
10. Access to historical training data for trend analysis.
