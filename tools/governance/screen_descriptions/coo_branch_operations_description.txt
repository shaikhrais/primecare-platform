List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor branch operations.
2. Review operational metrics and performance indicators.
3. Identify and address any operational issues or inefficiencies.
4. Collaborate with team members to optimize branch performance.
5. Access and analyze data related to branch activities.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant delays in processing transactions.
2. High error rates in operational tasks.
3. Increased customer complaints or dissatisfaction.
4. Unusual spikes in operational costs.
5. Low employee engagement or high turnover rates.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Real-time operational metrics (e.g., transaction volume, processing times).
2. Alerts for any operational issues or anomalies.
3. Visualizations of performance trends over time.
4. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Employee performance and engagement metrics.
