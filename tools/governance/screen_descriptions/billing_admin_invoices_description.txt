List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor and manage invoices.
2. Review invoice details and statuses.
3. Handle any discrepancies or errors in invoices.
4. Generate reports on billing activities.
5. Communicate with clients regarding billing issues.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors in invoice generation.
2. Delays in invoice processing.
3. High volume of customer complaints regarding billing.
4. Inconsistent data in reports.
5. Lack of visibility into invoice statuses.

What is needed on the dashboard for the user:
1. Overview of total invoices generated.
2. Status summary (paid, unpaid, overdue).
3. Alerts for discrepancies or errors.
4. Quick access to recent invoices.
5. Performance metrics (e.g., average processing time, number of errors).
