User Tasks:
1. Review the list of risks in the Risk Register.
2. Add new risks to the register.
3. Update existing risks with new information.
4. Remove risks that are no longer relevant.
5. Monitor the status of risks and their mitigation strategies.
6. Generate reports on risk status and trends.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors when loading data, indicating potential backend issues.
2. Delays in loading times, suggesting performance bottlenecks.
3. Inconsistent data displayed, which may point to synchronization problems.
4. User complaints about the interface being confusing or difficult to navigate.
5. Lack of updates or stale data in the risk register.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of total risks categorized by severity (high, medium, low).
2. Quick access buttons for adding, updating, and removing risks.
3. Visual indicators for risks that require immediate attention.
4. Recent activity log showing changes made to the risk register.
5. Search functionality to quickly find specific risks.
6. Export options for generating reports in various formats (PDF, CSV).
