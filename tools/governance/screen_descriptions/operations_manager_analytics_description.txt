Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Manage team performance and provide guidance.
3. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
4. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics.
5. Coordinate with other departments for seamless operations.
6. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
7. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
8. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
9. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
10. Facilitate training and development for staff.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Consistent delays in project timelines.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Budget overruns and financial discrepancies.
5. Lack of clear communication within the team.
6. Inconsistent performance metrics or KPIs.
7. Resistance to change or innovation among staff.
8. Poor collaboration between departments.
9. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
10. Failure to meet compliance or regulatory standards.

Dashboard Requirements for Operations Manager:
1. Real-time performance metrics and KPIs.
2. Team productivity and engagement statistics.
3. Budget tracking and financial performance indicators.
4. Customer satisfaction and feedback scores.
5. Project timelines and status updates.
6. Compliance and regulatory adherence status.
7. Resource allocation and utilization reports.
8. Alerts for operational issues or red flags.
9. Historical data trends for performance analysis.
10. Integration with other departmental dashboards for a holistic view.
