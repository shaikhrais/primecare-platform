HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing strategies.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Monitor employee performance and conduct evaluations.
6. Design and implement training and development programs.
7. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
8. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture and employee engagement.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Increased number of employee complaints or grievances.
3. Low employee engagement scores.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws or regulations.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Poor performance management outcomes.
7. Lack of training and development opportunities.
8. Ineffective communication within the organization.
9. Absence of diversity in hiring practices.
10. Inconsistent application of HR policies.

Dashboard Needs for HR Director:
1. Employee turnover rate and retention statistics.
2. Employee engagement survey results.
3. Compliance status with labor laws.
4. Recruitment metrics (time to fill, candidate quality).
5. Performance management outcomes.
6. Training and development participation rates.
7. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
8. Employee satisfaction scores.
9. Compensation and benefits analysis.
10. Real-time logs of HR activities and incidents.
