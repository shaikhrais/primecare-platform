List of tasks for a Clinical Director:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Manage clinical staff and provide leadership and guidance.
3. Monitor patient care quality and implement improvements.
4. Conduct regular audits and compliance checks.
5. Ensure proper documentation and reporting of clinical activities.
6. Develop and implement clinical policies and procedures.
7. Coordinate with other departments to ensure seamless operations.
8. Analyze clinical data and metrics for performance improvement.
9. Address patient and staff concerns promptly.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and best practices.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased patient complaints or negative feedback.
2. High staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
4. Declining patient care quality metrics.
5. Inconsistent documentation practices.
6. Lack of communication among clinical staff.
7. Delays in patient care or service delivery.
8. Unresolved issues from previous audits or inspections.
9. Insufficient training or resources for staff.
10. Poor performance in key clinical indicators.

Dashboard needs for Clinical Director:
1. Compliance audit results and trends.
2. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
3. Staff performance metrics and turnover rates.
4. Clinical quality indicators (e.g., readmission rates, infection rates).
5. Real-time operational data (e.g., patient wait times, service delivery metrics).
6. Logs of recent compliance checks and audits.
7. Alerts for any compliance violations or issues.
8. Staff training and development progress.
9. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
10. Summary of patient care outcomes and improvements.
