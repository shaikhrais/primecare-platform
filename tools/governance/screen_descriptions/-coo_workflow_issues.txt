Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Lead and mentor department heads and staff.
7. Facilitate communication between departments.
8. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
9. Manage risk and ensure business continuity.
10. Report to the CEO and board on operational performance.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining performance metrics or KPIs.
2. Increased employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent operational errors or compliance issues.
4. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
5. Poor communication between departments.
6. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
7. Lack of innovation or responsiveness to market changes.
8. Negative customer feedback or service complaints.
9. Inconsistent adherence to policies and procedures.
10. Insufficient training or resources for staff.

Dashboard Requirements for COO:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Real-time operational metrics and analytics.
3. Compliance status and audit logs.
4. Financial performance indicators (budget vs. actual).
5. Employee engagement and turnover statistics.
6. Project timelines and status updates.
7. Risk management and incident reports.
8. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
9. Customer satisfaction and feedback scores.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
