User Tasks:
1. Monitor staffing efficiency metrics.
2. Analyze staffing data for trends and patterns.
3. Identify areas for improvement in staffing operations.
4. Generate reports on staffing efficiency.
5. Collaborate with team members to address staffing issues.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High turnover rates among staff.
2. Increased overtime hours indicating understaffing.
3. Frequent scheduling conflicts or gaps.
4. Low employee satisfaction scores.
5. Delays in filling open positions.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time staffing metrics (e.g., headcount, turnover rate).
2. Visualizations of staffing trends over time.
3. Alerts for potential staffing issues (e.g., overtime thresholds).
4. Employee satisfaction scores and feedback.
5. Quick access to reports and analytics tools.
