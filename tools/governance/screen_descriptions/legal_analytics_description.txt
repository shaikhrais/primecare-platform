Legal Counsel Responsibilities:
1. Review and draft contracts and agreements.
2. Provide legal advice on compliance and regulatory matters.
3. Manage litigation and dispute resolution.
4. Conduct legal research and analysis.
5. Oversee intellectual property matters.
6. Ensure corporate governance and compliance with laws.
7. Assist in mergers, acquisitions, and other corporate transactions.
8. Develop and implement legal policies and procedures.
9. Train staff on legal compliance and risk management.
10. Collaborate with external legal counsel as needed.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Increased frequency of legal disputes or litigation.
2. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
3. Delays in contract approvals or negotiations.
4. Lack of documentation or poor record-keeping.
5. High turnover in legal staff or lack of expertise.
6. Inconsistent application of legal policies.
7. Poor communication between legal and other departments.
8. Failure to address identified legal risks.
9. Unclear roles and responsibilities within the legal team.
10. Insufficient training on legal matters for staff.

Dashboard Requirements for Legal Counsel:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for legal operations.
2. Status of ongoing litigation and disputes.
3. Compliance tracking and reporting metrics.
4. Contract management overview, including pending approvals.
5. Risk assessment and management dashboard.
6. Training and compliance completion rates.
7. Summary of legal advice provided and outcomes.
8. External counsel engagement and costs.
9. Document management status and accessibility.
10. Alerts for upcoming legal deadlines and renewals.
