User Tasks:
1. Monitor ongoing marketing campaigns.
2. Analyze campaign performance metrics.
3. Adjust campaign strategies based on data insights.
4. Collaborate with team members for campaign planning.
5. Report on campaign outcomes to stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in campaign performance updates.
2. Inconsistent data reporting across campaigns.
3. Low engagement rates on marketing materials.
4. High bounce rates on campaign landing pages.
5. Lack of collaboration or communication among team members.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time campaign performance metrics (e.g., clicks, conversions).
2. Visual representations of data (charts, graphs).
3. Alerts for underperforming campaigns.
4. Collaboration tools for team communication.
5. Historical data comparison for trend analysis.
