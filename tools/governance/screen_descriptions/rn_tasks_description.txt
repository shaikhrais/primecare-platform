Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Patient assessment and monitoring.
2. Administering medications and treatments.
3. Collaborating with healthcare teams.
4. Educating patients and families about health conditions.
5. Documenting patient care and progress.
6. Managing medical equipment and supplies.
7. Ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations.
8. Participating in quality improvement initiatives.
9. Responding to emergencies and providing first aid.
10. Maintaining patient confidentiality and privacy.

Red Flags for RN Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High turnover rates among nursing staff.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Lack of communication among team members.
7. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
8. Elevated infection rates or complications.
9. Unusual patterns in patient vital signs.
10. Insufficient staffing levels during peak times.

Dashboard Requirements for RN:
1. Real-time patient status updates.
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient care.
4. Logs of recent activities and interventions.
5. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
6. Staffing levels and availability.
7. Medication administration records.
8. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
9. Incident reports and follow-up actions.
10. Access to educational resources and training updates.
