User Tasks:
1. Monitor and manage escalated issues.
2. Review the status of ongoing escalations.
3. Communicate with relevant stakeholders regarding escalations.
4. Analyze trends in escalated issues for future prevention.
5. Provide feedback on the escalation process for improvements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of escalated issues indicating potential systemic problems.
2. Delays in resolution times for escalated issues.
3. Lack of communication or updates on escalated issues.
4. Repeated escalations of the same issue without resolution.
5. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding the escalation process.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of current escalated issues with status indicators.
2. Metrics on resolution times and trends over time.
3. Communication logs related to escalated issues.
4. Feedback section for users to report on the escalation process.
5. Alerts for high-priority escalations or unresolved issues.
