Registered Nurse (RN) Responsibilities:
1. Patient assessment and monitoring.
2. Administering medications and treatments.
3. Collaborating with healthcare teams.
4. Educating patients and families.
5. Documenting patient care and progress.
6. Managing medical equipment.
7. Ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations.
8. Responding to emergencies.
9. Providing emotional support to patients.
10. Participating in quality improvement initiatives.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rates of medication errors.
3. Frequent staff turnover or absenteeism.
4. Inconsistent documentation practices.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Non-compliance with safety protocols.
7. Lack of communication among team members.
8. Elevated infection rates or adverse events.
9. Insufficient staffing levels.
10. Poor performance on compliance audits.

Dashboard Requirements for Registered Nurse (RN):
1. Real-time patient status updates.
2. Compliance audit results and alerts.
3. Logs of patient interactions and care provided.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., medication administration accuracy).
5. Alerts for critical patient conditions.
6. Access to educational resources for patient care.
7. Communication tools for team collaboration.
8. Historical data for trend analysis.
9. Visualizations of patient outcomes and satisfaction.
10. Tools for reporting and documenting incidents.
