Customer Support Responsibilities:
1. Respond to customer inquiries and issues promptly.
2. Provide technical support and troubleshooting assistance.
3. Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and transactions.
4. Follow up with customers to ensure their issues are resolved.
5. Gather customer feedback to improve services.
6. Collaborate with other departments to resolve complex issues.
7. Train new support staff on processes and tools.
8. Monitor support metrics and performance indicators.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High response times to customer inquiries.
2. Increased number of unresolved tickets.
3. Frequent escalations to higher support tiers.
4. Low customer satisfaction scores.
5. High turnover rate among support staff.
6. Lack of communication between support and other departments.
7. Inconsistent documentation of customer interactions.

Dashboard Requirements for Customer Support:
1. Real-time ticket status overview (open, pending, resolved).
2. Average response and resolution times.
3. Customer satisfaction ratings and feedback trends.
4. Volume of incoming tickets over time.
5. Performance metrics for individual support agents.
6. Escalation rates and reasons for escalations.
7. Integration with customer relationship management (CRM) tools.
8. Alerts for high-priority tickets or customer complaints.
