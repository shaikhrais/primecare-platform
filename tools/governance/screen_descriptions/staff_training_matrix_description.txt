User Work List:
1. Review the staff training matrix for completeness.
2. Identify any missing training modules or certifications.
3. Monitor staff progress on training completion.
4. Update training records as staff complete their training.
5. Provide feedback or report issues regarding the training process.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Incomplete training records for staff members.
2. Low completion rates for mandatory training modules.
3. Frequent errors or crashes when accessing the training matrix.
4. Lack of updates or communication regarding training requirements.
5. High turnover rates that may indicate dissatisfaction with training.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of staff training completion rates.
2. Alerts for overdue training modules.
3. Visual representation of training progress by department.
4. Access to feedback or comments from staff regarding training.
5. Quick links to update or modify training records.
