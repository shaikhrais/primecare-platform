Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders.
4. Conduct market research and analysis.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align strategies.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals and reports.
8. Manage and mentor the business development team.
9. Set and track performance metrics and KPIs.
10. Ensure compliance with regulations and company policies.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High employee turnover in the business development team.
3. Lack of communication between departments.
4. Inconsistent or unclear business development strategies.
5. Negative feedback from clients or stakeholders.
6. Failure to meet performance metrics or KPIs.
7. Increased competition without a response strategy.
8. Poor market research leading to misguided initiatives.
9. Compliance issues or regulatory breaches.
10. Lack of innovation or adaptation to market changes.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Overview of current business development strategies.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue.
3. Active business opportunities and their status.
4. Market analysis and competitive landscape.
5. Team performance metrics and employee satisfaction.
6. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Compliance status and audit logs.
8. Real-time updates on industry trends.
9. Visualizations of sales forecasts and projections.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
