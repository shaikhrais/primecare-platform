List of work for the user:
1. Review the training matrix for staff.
2. Monitor training completion status for each staff member.
3. Identify training needs based on staff roles.
4. Schedule training sessions as needed.
5. Update training records and progress.
6. Generate reports on training effectiveness.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low training completion rates among staff.
2. High number of overdue training sessions.
3. Lack of engagement in training programs.
4. Inconsistent training records or updates.
5. Feedback indicating dissatisfaction with training content or delivery.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of training completion rates.
2. Alerts for overdue training sessions.
3. List of upcoming training sessions.
4. Individual training progress for each staff member.
5. Feedback and ratings from training participants.
6. Quick access to training resources and materials.
