User Tasks:
1. Create and manage vaccination campaigns.
2. Monitor vaccination progress and statistics.
3. Schedule vaccination events and manage locations.
4. Track participant registrations and attendance.
5. Generate reports on vaccination outcomes and demographics.
6. Communicate with stakeholders and participants.
7. Update campaign details and respond to feedback.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low participation rates in vaccination events.
2. Delays in scheduling or rescheduling events.
3. Inaccurate data reporting or discrepancies in statistics.
4. High dropout rates of registered participants.
5. Negative feedback from users regarding the campaign process.
6. Technical issues with the platform affecting user experience.
7. Lack of engagement from stakeholders or community partners.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of active and upcoming vaccination campaigns.
2. Real-time statistics on vaccination progress (e.g., doses administered).
3. Participant registration and attendance tracking.
4. Alerts for low participation or other operational issues.
5. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from participants.
6. Visualizations of demographic data related to vaccination uptake.
7. Communication tools for reaching out to participants and stakeholders.
