List of tasks for the Community Outreach Lead:
1. Develop and implement community outreach strategies.
2. Build and maintain relationships with community organizations and stakeholders.
3. Organize and coordinate community events and activities.
4. Collect and analyze feedback from community members.
5. Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of outreach programs.
6. Prepare reports on outreach activities and outcomes.
7. Collaborate with internal teams to align outreach efforts with organizational goals.
8. Train and support volunteers and staff involved in outreach initiatives.
9. Manage outreach budgets and resources effectively.
10. Stay informed about community needs and trends.

Red flags of operation:
1. Lack of engagement or participation from the community.
2. Negative feedback or complaints from community members.
3. Inconsistent messaging or branding in outreach materials.
4. Failure to meet outreach goals or objectives.
5. High turnover of volunteers or staff involved in outreach.
6. Insufficient resources allocated for outreach activities.
7. Poor communication between the outreach team and other departments.
8. Lack of data or metrics to measure outreach effectiveness.
9. Limited diversity in outreach efforts or target audiences.
10. Resistance to change or adapt outreach strategies based on feedback.

Dashboard needs for the Community Outreach Lead:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for outreach activities.
2. Community engagement metrics (e.g., event attendance, feedback scores).
3. Budget tracking and resource allocation overview.
4. Progress tracking against outreach goals and objectives.
5. Reports on community needs and trends.
6. Visualizations of outreach program effectiveness.
7. Feedback collection tools from community members.
8. Volunteer and staff management metrics.
9. Communication logs with community partners.
10. Alerts for any red flags or issues in outreach operations.
