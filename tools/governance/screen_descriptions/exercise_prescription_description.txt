List of tasks a Physiotherapist must do:
1. Assess patient conditions and needs.
2. Develop personalized exercise plans.
3. Educate patients on exercises and techniques.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust plans as necessary.
5. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
6. Maintain accurate patient records.
7. Stay updated on best practices and new research.
8. Ensure patient safety during exercises.
9. Provide motivation and support to patients.
10. Conduct compliance scans and audits.

Red flags for operations:
1. Unexplained patient pain or discomfort during exercises.
2. Significant changes in patient condition.
3. Non-compliance with prescribed exercise plans.
4. Lack of progress in patient recovery.
5. Inconsistent or incomplete patient records.
6. High rates of patient dropouts or cancellations.
7. Failure to follow safety protocols.
8. Negative feedback from patients regarding care.
9. Inadequate communication with other healthcare providers.
10. Issues with equipment or facilities.

Dashboard needs for a Physiotherapist:
1. Overview of active patients and their statuses.
2. Compliance scan results and audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for patient progress.
4. Real-time telemetry data on patient engagement.
5. Alerts for red flags or issues requiring attention.
6. Access to patient history and treatment plans.
7. Metrics on exercise adherence and outcomes.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with other professionals.
9. Educational resources and updates on best practices.
10. A section for logging notes and observations.
