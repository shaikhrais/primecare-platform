List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor partnership outreach activities.
2. Track engagement metrics with potential partners.
3. Analyze the effectiveness of outreach strategies.
4. Update partnership status and notes.
5. Collaborate with team members on outreach efforts.
6. Review feedback from partners and adjust strategies accordingly.

Red flags of operation:
1. Low engagement rates with potential partners.
2. High response time to outreach efforts.
3. Inconsistent updates on partnership status.
4. Lack of collaboration or communication among team members.
5. Negative feedback from partners or stakeholders.
6. Failure to meet outreach goals or deadlines.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current partnerships and their statuses.
2. Engagement metrics (e.g., response rates, follow-up actions).
3. Notifications for pending tasks or follow-ups.
4. Visual representation of outreach effectiveness (charts/graphs).
5. Collaboration tools for team communication.
6. Feedback section for partner responses and suggestions.
