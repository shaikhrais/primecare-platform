Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Governance
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Strategic Planning
9. Performance Measurement
10. Stakeholder Communication

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining Revenue Trends
2. Increasing Expenses without Justification
3. Cash Flow Issues
4. Inconsistent Financial Reporting
5. High Employee Turnover
6. Regulatory Compliance Failures
7. Poor Audit Results
8. Lack of Financial Controls
9. Unexplained Variances in Budget vs. Actual
10. Negative Customer Feedback Impacting Revenue

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Overview
2. Revenue and Expense Trends
3. Cash Flow Analysis
4. Budget vs. Actual Performance
5. Compliance Status Indicators
6. Risk Assessment Metrics
7. Audit Logs and Findings
8. Investment Performance Overview
9. Financial Ratios and Health Metrics
10. Real-time Alerts for Red Flags
