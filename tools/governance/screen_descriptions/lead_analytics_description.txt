Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies to increase brand awareness and drive sales.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and ensure alignment with business goals.
3. Analyze market trends and customer insights to inform marketing decisions.
4. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
5. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including sales, product development, and customer service.
6. Monitor and report on the performance of marketing initiatives.
7. Lead and mentor the marketing team to achieve departmental goals.
8. Maintain relationships with external partners and vendors.
9. Ensure compliance with industry regulations and company policies.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and emerging marketing technologies.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decreased engagement metrics across marketing channels.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
4. Lack of alignment between marketing initiatives and business objectives.
5. High turnover rates within the marketing team.
6. Inconsistent brand messaging across platforms.
7. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).
8. Delays in campaign execution or project timelines.
9. Insufficient market research leading to uninformed decisions.
10. Poor collaboration with other departments.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their performance metrics.
2. Real-time analytics on website traffic, social media engagement, and conversion rates.
3. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
4. Customer feedback and sentiment analysis.
5. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to brand awareness, lead generation, and sales.
6. Market trends and competitive analysis.
7. Team performance metrics and project status updates.
8. Compliance and regulatory status reports.
9. Visualizations of customer demographics and segmentation.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues needing immediate attention.
