List of tasks a therapist must perform:
1. Conduct patient assessments.
2. Develop treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic interventions.
4. Monitor patient progress.
5. Document patient interactions and treatment outcomes.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Educate patients and families about treatment options.
8. Maintain compliance with healthcare regulations.
9. Participate in ongoing professional development.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work:
1. High number of patient complaints or negative feedback.
2. Frequent missed appointments or cancellations.
3. Inconsistent documentation or incomplete records.
4. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.
5. Low patient engagement or participation in treatment.
6. Failure to meet compliance standards or regulations.
7. High turnover rate of patients or staff.
8. Inability to track patient progress effectively.

Dashboard needs for therapists:
1. Overview of patient caseload and demographics.
2. Real-time updates on patient progress and treatment outcomes.
3. Compliance tracking metrics and alerts.
4. Logs of patient interactions and treatment history.
5. Performance metrics (e.g., patient satisfaction scores).
6. Access to educational resources and training opportunities.
7. Communication tools for collaboration with other professionals.
8. Alerts for upcoming appointments and follow-ups.
9. Visualizations of patient progress over time (charts/graphs).
