Clinical Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee clinical operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits and compliance scans.
3. Monitor and evaluate clinical performance metrics.
4. Manage clinical staff and ensure proper training and development.
5. Address any operational issues or concerns promptly.
6. Maintain communication with other departments and stakeholders.
7. Ensure patient safety and quality of care.
8. Implement and monitor clinical policies and procedures.
9. Review and analyze clinical data for decision-making.
10. Lead initiatives for continuous improvement in clinical practices.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. High error rates in clinical documentation.
2. Increased patient complaints or adverse events.
3. Non-compliance with regulatory standards.
4. Staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
5. Delays in patient care or treatment.
6. Inconsistent performance metrics across departments.
7. Lack of communication or collaboration among staff.
8. Insufficient training or resources for clinical staff.
9. Poor patient outcomes or satisfaction scores.
10. Unresolved issues from audits or compliance checks.

Dashboard Needs for Clinical Director:
1. Real-time compliance status and audit results.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for clinical operations.
3. Logs of operational activities and incidents.
4. Metrics on patient safety and quality of care.
5. Staff performance and training status.
6. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
7. Visualizations of clinical data trends over time.
8. Summary of patient feedback and satisfaction scores.
9. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
10. Access to historical audit data for trend analysis.
