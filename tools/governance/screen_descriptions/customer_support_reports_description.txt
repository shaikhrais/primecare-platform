User Work List:
1. Monitor customer support reports.
2. Analyze data for trends and issues.
3. Respond to customer inquiries based on report findings.
4. Collaborate with other departments to resolve customer issues.
5. Provide feedback for improving customer support processes.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of unresolved customer issues.
2. Increased response times to customer inquiries.
3. Frequent errors in report data.
4. Low customer satisfaction ratings.
5. Lack of communication between support and other departments.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time metrics on customer inquiries and resolutions.
2. Visual representation of trends in customer support data.
3. Alerts for red flags (e.g., high unresolved issues).
4. Feedback mechanism for users to report issues with the dashboard.
5. Integration with other tools for a holistic view of customer interactions.
