Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess operational risks.
3. Conduct audits and compliance scans.
4. Maintain accurate logs of governance activities.
5. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
6. Provide training and support on governance issues.
7. Report on governance performance and issues.
8. Implement governance frameworks and best practices.
9. Review and update governance policies regularly.
10. Manage and respond to incidents and breaches.

Red flags of operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Delays in audit processes.
3. Lack of transparency in operations.
4. Inconsistent documentation and logs.
5. High turnover in governance staff.
6. Poor communication between teams.
7. Unresolved issues from previous audits.
8. Low engagement in governance training.
9. Frequent changes in regulations affecting operations.
10. Negative feedback from stakeholders regarding governance.

Dashboard needs for a Governance Officer:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Audit logs and historical data.
3. Risk assessment metrics.
4. Operational performance metrics.
5. Alerts for compliance violations.
6. Visualizations of audit results.
7. Training completion rates for staff.
8. Stakeholder feedback summaries.
9. Incident and breach tracking.
10. Updates on regulatory changes and impacts.
