List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the intake process and ensure all reports are generated accurately.
2. Review and analyze the data presented in the reports.
3. Identify any discrepancies or issues in the intake process.
4. Communicate with relevant stakeholders regarding findings from the reports.
5. Update any necessary documentation based on report outcomes.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors or issues reported in the data loading process.
2. Delays in report generation that could impact decision-making.
3. Inconsistencies in the data presented in the reports.
4. Lack of user engagement with the reports or dashboard.
5. High volume of support requests related to report access or understanding.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of key metrics related to the intake process.
2. Alerts or notifications for any errors or discrepancies detected.
3. Quick access to frequently used reports.
4. Visual representations of data trends over time.
5. User feedback section to gather insights on report usability and effectiveness.
