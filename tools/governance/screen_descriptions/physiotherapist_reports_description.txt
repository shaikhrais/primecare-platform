Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient needs and develop treatment plans.
2. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
3. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment as necessary.
4. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
5. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Stay updated on the latest physiotherapy techniques and research.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Unexplained patient deterioration.
2. Non-compliance with treatment plans.
3. Significant changes in patient symptoms.
4. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
5. High rates of patient drop-out or dissatisfaction.
6. Equipment malfunctions or safety concerns.
7. Lack of communication with other healthcare providers.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapist:
1. Overview of active operations and patient status.
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment effectiveness.
4. Real-time telemetry data for patient monitoring.
5. Alerts for red flags and operational issues.
6. Access to patient records and treatment history.
7. Tools for logging and tracking patient progress.
