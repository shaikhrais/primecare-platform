List of work for the patient:
1. Review billing statements and ensure accuracy.
2. Verify insurance coverage and benefits.
3. Submit any necessary documentation for claims.
4. Communicate with billing department for any discrepancies.
5. Make payments on outstanding balances.
6. Schedule follow-up appointments as needed.

Red flags for operation:
1. Unexplained charges on billing statements.
2. Delays in processing insurance claims.
3. Repeated billing errors or discrepancies.
4. Lack of communication from the billing department.
5. High out-of-pocket costs not previously discussed.
6. Inability to access billing information or statements.

Effective work strategies:
1. Regular training for billing staff on compliance and regulations.
2. Implementing automated systems for billing and claims processing.
3. Establishing clear communication channels for patients.
4. Regular audits of billing practices to identify errors.
5. Utilizing patient feedback to improve billing processes.

Dashboard needs for the patient:
1. Overview of current billing status and outstanding balances.
2. Detailed breakdown of charges and payments.
3. Alerts for upcoming payments or due dates.
4. Access to insurance claim status and history.
5. Communication options with billing representatives.
6. Educational resources on billing and insurance processes.
