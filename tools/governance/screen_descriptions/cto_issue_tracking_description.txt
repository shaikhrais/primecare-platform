List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor the status of issues tracked in the system.
2. Update the status of issues as they progress.
3. Review and analyze issue reports for trends and patterns.
4. Communicate with team members regarding issue resolutions.
5. Provide feedback on the effectiveness of the tracking system.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent loading errors indicating potential backend issues.
2. Delays in updating issue statuses, suggesting bottlenecks in the workflow.
3. Lack of communication or updates from team members on issue resolutions.
4. High volume of unresolved issues over an extended period.
5. Inconsistent data being displayed on the dashboard.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current issues with their statuses (open, in progress, resolved).
2. Notifications for any updates or changes to issue statuses.
3. Analytics on issue resolution times and team performance.
4. A section for team communication or comments on specific issues.
5. Quick access buttons for updating issue statuses and adding new issues.
