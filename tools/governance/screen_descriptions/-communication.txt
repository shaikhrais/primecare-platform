Customer Support Work Responsibilities:
1. Respond to customer inquiries via various channels (phone, email, chat).
2. Resolve customer issues and complaints efficiently.
3. Provide product information and support.
4. Document customer interactions and feedback.
5. Collaborate with other departments to resolve complex issues.
6. Conduct follow-ups with customers to ensure satisfaction.
7. Maintain knowledge of company products and services.
8. Monitor and report on customer feedback and trends.
9. Train new support staff on processes and tools.
10. Participate in team meetings and training sessions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of unresolved tickets.
2. Increased response times to customer inquiries.
3. Frequent escalations to higher management.
4. Negative customer feedback or low satisfaction scores.
5. High turnover rate among support staff.
6. Lack of communication between support and other departments.
7. Inconsistent documentation of customer interactions.
8. Failure to meet service level agreements (SLAs).
9. Repeated issues from the same customers.
10. Insufficient training or resources for support staff.

Dashboard Requirements for Customer Support:
1. Real-time ticket status overview (open, pending, resolved).
2. Average response and resolution times.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
4. Volume of incoming and outgoing communications.
5. Number of escalated tickets and their status.
6. Performance metrics for individual support agents.
7. Historical data on ticket resolution rates.
8. Alerts for high-priority or overdue tickets.
9. Insights on common customer issues or inquiries.
10. Resource allocation and workload distribution among team members.
