User Tasks:
1. Monitor franchise performance metrics.
2. Review franchisee compliance and operational standards.
3. Analyze sales data and trends.
4. Identify areas for improvement in franchise operations.
5. Communicate with franchisees regarding updates and support.
6. Access reports on franchise growth and challenges.
7. Evaluate marketing strategies and their effectiveness.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales figures over consecutive months.
2. High turnover rates among franchisees.
3. Frequent complaints from customers regarding service or product quality.
4. Non-compliance with franchise operational standards.
5. Inconsistent branding or marketing efforts across franchises.
6. Lack of engagement or communication from franchisees.
7. Significant discrepancies in reported data from different franchises.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics.
2. Compliance tracking indicators.
3. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
4. Franchisee engagement statistics.
5. Visualizations of growth trends over time.
6. Alerts for any operational red flags.
7. Access to detailed reports and analytics for decision-making.
