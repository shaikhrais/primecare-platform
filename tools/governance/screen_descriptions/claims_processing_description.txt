Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist in project management and coordination.
6. Maintain office supplies and inventory.
7. Support team members with administrative tasks.
8. Facilitate meetings and take minutes.
9. Process invoices and manage budgets.
10. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent delays in task completion.
2. Increased errors in documentation.
3. Poor communication among team members.
4. Lack of organization in files and records.
5. Low morale or dissatisfaction among staff.
6. Missed deadlines or appointments.
7. Inconsistent reporting and tracking of tasks.
8. High turnover rates in administrative roles.
9. Unclear responsibilities and role confusion.
10. Lack of feedback or performance reviews.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview with deadlines.
2. Calendar integration for scheduling.
3. Communication log for correspondence tracking.
4. Document management system for file organization.
5. Performance metrics for task completion rates.
6. Budget tracking and expense reporting.
7. Meeting schedule and minutes repository.
8. Compliance checklist for company policies.
9. Inventory management for office supplies.
10. Feedback mechanism for team performance and morale.
