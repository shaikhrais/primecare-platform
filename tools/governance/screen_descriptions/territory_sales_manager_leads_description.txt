User Work List:
1. Monitor leads assigned to territory sales managers.
2. Review lead status and progress.
3. Update lead information as necessary.
4. Communicate with team members regarding lead management.
5. Analyze lead performance metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in lead updates or status changes.
2. High number of leads with no activity.
3. Frequent errors or issues loading data.
4. Lack of communication among team members.
5. Inconsistent lead performance metrics.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of total leads and their statuses.
2. Performance metrics for each territory sales manager.
3. Alerts for leads that require immediate attention.
4. Communication tools for team collaboration.
5. Visual representation of lead progress over time.
