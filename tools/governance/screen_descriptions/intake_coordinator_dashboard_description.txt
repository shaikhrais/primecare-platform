Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming patient referrals and inquiries.
2. Schedule appointments and coordinate with clinical staff.
3. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
4. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and policies.
5. Communicate effectively with patients and healthcare providers.
6. Monitor and track patient intake metrics and performance.
7. Conduct follow-ups with patients as needed.
8. Collaborate with other departments to streamline processes.
9. Provide support during audits and compliance checks.
10. Update and maintain policies related to patient intake.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of patient complaints or inquiries.
2. Increased appointment cancellations or no-shows.
3. Delays in processing patient referrals.
4. Inconsistent documentation or record-keeping.
5. Failure to meet compliance standards or regulations.
6. Lack of communication between departments.
7. Low staff morale or high turnover rates.
8. Inaccurate or incomplete patient data.
9. Frequent system errors or downtime.
10. Poor performance metrics in patient intake processes.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Active Operations Metric: Display current active operations and their status.
2. Security Clearance Level: Show the current security clearance level.
3. System Latency: Monitor system response times.
4. Data Integrity: Indicate the percentage of data integrity.
5. Telemetry Logs: Provide access to live telemetry logs for monitoring.
6. Compliance Audit Status: Show the status of compliance audits.
7. Operational Audit Logs: List recent operational logs for transparency.
8. Performance Metrics: Display key performance indicators related to patient intake.
9. Refresh Button: Allow manual refresh of dashboard data.
10. Action Buttons: Provide buttons for executing compliance scans and other actions.
