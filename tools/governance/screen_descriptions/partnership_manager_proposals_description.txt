User Work List:
1. Review partnership proposals submitted by partners.
2. Evaluate the proposals based on predefined criteria.
3. Communicate feedback to partners regarding their proposals.
4. Track the status of each proposal (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
5. Collaborate with internal teams to gather necessary information for proposal evaluation.
6. Update the dashboard with the latest proposal statuses and metrics.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in proposal review process.
2. High number of rejected proposals without clear feedback.
3. Lack of communication with partners regarding proposal status.
4. Inconsistent evaluation criteria leading to confusion.
5. Low engagement from internal teams in the evaluation process.
6. Technical issues with the dashboard affecting data visibility.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of all submitted proposals with their current status.
2. Metrics on average review time for proposals.
3. Breakdown of proposals by status (pending, approved, rejected).
4. Notifications for proposals that require urgent attention.
5. Feedback summary for rejected proposals to identify common issues.
6. Collaboration tools for internal team discussions on proposals.
