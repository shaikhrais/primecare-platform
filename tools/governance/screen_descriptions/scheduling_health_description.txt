Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Manage scheduling and resource allocation.
3. Monitor performance metrics and KPIs.
4. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
5. Address operational issues and implement solutions.
6. Coordinate with other departments for seamless operations.
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
8. Train and manage staff.
9. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
10. Prepare reports for upper management.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Frequent delays in project timelines.
3. Increased operational costs without clear justification.
4. Low employee morale or engagement.
5. Consistent customer complaints or dissatisfaction.
6. Non-compliance with regulations or standards.
7. Lack of clear communication within the team.
8. Inconsistent performance metrics.
9. Unresolved conflicts among team members.
10. Insufficient training or resources for staff.

Dashboard Needs for Operations Manager:
1. Real-time performance metrics (KPIs).
2. Compliance status indicators.
3. Resource allocation and utilization statistics.
4. Employee performance and engagement metrics.
5. Operational cost analysis.
6. Customer satisfaction ratings.
7. Audit logs and compliance reports.
8. Alerts for operational issues or red flags.
9. Historical data trends for performance analysis.
10. Visualizations for easy interpretation of data.
