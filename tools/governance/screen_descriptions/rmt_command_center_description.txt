List of tasks Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) must perform:
1. Conduct client assessments to determine treatment needs.
2. Develop and implement personalized treatment plans.
3. Perform various massage techniques (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue).
4. Maintain accurate client records and documentation.
5. Ensure a clean and safe treatment environment.
6. Stay updated on industry trends and continuing education.
7. Communicate effectively with clients regarding treatment progress.
8. Adhere to ethical and professional standards.
9. Manage appointment scheduling and client follow-ups.
10. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals as needed.

Red flags in operations for RMTs:
1. Inconsistent client records or documentation.
2. Complaints from clients regarding treatment or service.
3. High cancellation or no-show rates.
4. Lack of adherence to hygiene and safety protocols.
5. Failure to obtain informed consent from clients.
6. Inability to provide evidence of continuing education.
7. Poor communication with clients or team members.
8. Non-compliance with regulatory standards or guidelines.
9. Signs of burnout or decreased job satisfaction among staff.
10. Inadequate response to client feedback or concerns.

Dashboard requirements for Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs):
1. Overview of active client appointments and schedules.
2. Compliance status and audit logs.
3. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as client retention rates.
4. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
5. Operational metrics, including treatment outcomes and effectiveness.
6. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
7. Access to continuing education resources and requirements.
8. Summary of treatment plans and client progress.
9. Communication tools for client follow-ups and reminders.
10. Visualizations of operational data, such as appointment trends and service usage.
