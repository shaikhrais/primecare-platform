Chiropractor Work Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop treatment plans based on patient needs.
3. Perform chiropractic adjustments and manipulations.
4. Educate patients on lifestyle changes and exercises.
5. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals when needed.
8. Stay updated on chiropractic techniques and practices.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. High rate of treatment failures or lack of improvement.
3. Significant changes in patient demographics or volume.
4. Non-compliance with regulatory standards or guidelines.
5. Frequent errors in patient documentation or records.
6. Unusual patterns in billing or insurance claims.
7. Staff turnover or dissatisfaction.
8. Negative feedback from other healthcare providers.

Dashboard Requirements for Chiropractor:
1. Active Operations Metric: Display current active patient treatments.
2. Security Clearance Status: Show compliance level and any exceptions.
3. Telemetry Chart: Visual representation of patient treatment metrics over time.
4. Operational Audit Logs: List of recent activities and compliance checks.
5. Compliance Scan Button: Option to run compliance audits.
6. Loading Indicator: Show progress during compliance scans.
7. Manual Refresh Button: Allow refreshing of dashboard data.
8. Notifications for any red flags or alerts related to operations.
