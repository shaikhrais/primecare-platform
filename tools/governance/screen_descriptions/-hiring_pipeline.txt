HR Director Responsibilities:
1. Oversee recruitment and hiring processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
4. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
5. Monitor employee performance and development.
6. Conduct training and development programs.
7. Analyze HR metrics and report on workforce trends.
8. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
9. Foster a positive workplace culture.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Frequent compliance issues or audits.
4. Delays in hiring processes.
5. Increased employee grievances or complaints.
6. Lack of training and development opportunities.
7. Poor performance management outcomes.
8. Inconsistent application of HR policies.

HR Director Dashboard Needs:
1. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, candidate sources).
2. Employee engagement and satisfaction scores.
3. Compliance audit results and action items.
4. Turnover rates and reasons for leaving.
5. Training and development participation rates.
6. Performance management statistics.
7. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
8. Compensation and benefits analysis.
9. Employee grievances and resolutions tracking.
10. Overall HR operational efficiency indicators.
