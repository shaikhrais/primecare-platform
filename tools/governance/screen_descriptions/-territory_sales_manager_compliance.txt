Territory Sales Manager Responsibilities:
1. Manage sales territories effectively.
2. Develop and implement sales strategies.
3. Monitor sales performance and adjust strategies accordingly.
4. Build and maintain relationships with clients.
5. Conduct market research to identify new opportunities.
6. Collaborate with marketing and product teams.
7. Provide training and support to sales representatives.
8. Analyze sales data and prepare reports.
9. Ensure compliance with company policies and regulations.
10. Attend industry events and conferences.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales figures over consecutive periods.
2. High turnover rates among sales staff.
3. Inconsistent communication with clients.
4. Lack of follow-up on leads and opportunities.
5. Poor customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
6. Failure to meet sales targets and quotas.
7. Inadequate training and support for team members.
8. Ignoring compliance and regulatory requirements.
9. Lack of collaboration with other departments.
10. Insufficient market research and understanding of competition.

Dashboard Needs for Territory Sales Manager:
1. Sales performance metrics (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2. Client relationship management tools.
3. Lead tracking and follow-up status.
4. Market analysis and opportunity identification.
5. Compliance status and audit logs.
6. Team performance and training progress.
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction ratings.
8. Sales strategy effectiveness indicators.
9. Real-time updates on market trends.
10. Alerts for potential operational issues or red flags.
