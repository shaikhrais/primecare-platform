User Work List:
1. Monitor regional business development metrics.
2. Analyze territory growth data.
3. Identify areas for improvement in sales performance.
4. Collaborate with local teams to strategize growth initiatives.
5. Review and adjust territory assignments based on performance.
6. Report findings and recommendations to management.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant decline in sales in specific territories.
2. High turnover rates among sales personnel.
3. Inconsistent data reporting from regional teams.
4. Lack of engagement from local teams in growth initiatives.
5. Delays in implementing recommended strategies.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time sales performance metrics by territory.
2. Visual representation of growth trends over time.
3. Alerts for any significant drops in performance.
4. Collaboration tools for communication with local teams.
5. Reporting features to summarize findings and recommendations.
