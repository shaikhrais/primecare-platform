Intake Coordinator Responsibilities:
1. Manage incoming patient referrals and inquiries.
2. Schedule appointments and coordinate with clinical staff.
3. Gather and verify patient information and insurance details.
4. Maintain accurate records of patient interactions and outcomes.
5. Communicate with patients regarding their intake process.
6. Collaborate with other departments to ensure smooth operations.
7. Monitor and follow up on pending referrals and appointments.
8. Provide support and guidance to patients throughout the intake process.
9. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and policies.
10. Analyze intake data to identify trends and areas for improvement.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High volume of missed appointments or no-shows.
2. Delays in processing referrals or scheduling appointments.
3. Incomplete or inaccurate patient information.
4. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
5. Lack of communication between departments.
6. Frequent errors in documentation or data entry.
7. Inconsistent follow-up on pending cases.
8. Overwhelmed staff or high turnover rates.
9. Failure to meet compliance standards.
10. Inefficient use of technology or tools.

Dashboard Requirements for Intake Coordinator:
1. Overview of incoming referrals and their statuses.
2. Appointment scheduling calendar.
3. Patient information summary (demographics, insurance, etc.).
4. Metrics on appointment no-shows and cancellations.
5. Follow-up reminders for pending cases.
6. Communication logs with patients and staff.
7. Compliance checklists and alerts.
8. Performance metrics (e.g., average processing time).
9. Integration with electronic health records (EHR).
10. Data visualization for trends in patient intake.
