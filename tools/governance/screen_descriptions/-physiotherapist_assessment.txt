Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop personalized treatment plans.
3. Implement therapeutic exercises and modalities.
4. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatments as necessary.
5. Educate patients on injury prevention and rehabilitation.
6. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
7. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
8. Stay updated with the latest physiotherapy practices and research.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition or response to treatment.
2. Inconsistent patient progress or lack of improvement.
3. Patient complaints about treatment pain or discomfort.
4. Non-compliance with prescribed treatment plans.
5. Unusual patterns in patient attendance or engagement.
6. Documentation errors or missing records.
7. Equipment malfunctions or safety concerns.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapists:
1. Overview of active patient cases and their statuses.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for treatment outcomes.
3. Compliance audit results and logs.
4. Real-time telemetry data on patient progress.
5. Alerts for red flags or critical patient updates.
6. Access to educational resources and latest research.
7. Tools for scheduling and managing patient appointments.
8. Communication tools for collaboration with other healthcare providers.
