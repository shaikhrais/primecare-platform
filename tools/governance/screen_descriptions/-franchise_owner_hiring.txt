List of work for the User:
1. Review the hiring process for franchise owners.
2. Monitor the status of job postings and applications.
3. Evaluate candidate profiles and qualifications.
4. Communicate with potential hires.
5. Track hiring metrics and performance indicators.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of unfilled positions over an extended period.
2. Frequent candidate dropouts during the application process.
3. Negative feedback from candidates regarding the hiring process.
4. Lack of engagement from potential hires.
5. Discrepancies in candidate qualifications versus job requirements.

Dashboard needs for the User:
1. Overview of current job postings and their statuses.
2. Metrics on application rates and candidate engagement.
3. Visual representation of hiring timelines and bottlenecks.
4. Alerts for unresponsive candidates or overdue hiring tasks.
5. Performance analytics comparing hiring success rates over time.
