Home Support Worker Responsibilities:
1. Log Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) including hygiene assistance and meals.
2. Save drafts of ADL logs for later completion.
3. Submit completed ADL logs for review.
4. Track meals assistance and intake.
5. Complete hygiene support checklists.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Incomplete or missing ADL logs.
2. Delays in submitting completed logs.
3. Frequent errors in logged data.
4. Lack of updates or drafts saved.
5. Inconsistent tracking of meals assistance.

Dashboard Needs for Home Support Worker:
1. Overview of logged ADLs and their statuses.
2. Alerts for incomplete or overdue logs.
3. Summary of meals assistance provided.
4. Checklist completion rates for hygiene support.
5. Quick access to save and submit buttons for efficiency.
