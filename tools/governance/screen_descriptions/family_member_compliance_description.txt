List of work Family Member must do:
1. Run compliance scans regularly to ensure adherence to governance policies.
2. Maintain logs of all actions and events for auditing purposes.
3. Update governance directives as necessary based on operational needs.
4. Submit secure event reports for any operational incidents.
5. Monitor and review recent compliance verification audits.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors or failures during compliance scans.
2. Inconsistent or missing logs which may indicate operational issues.
3. Non-compliance statuses in recent audits.
4. Delays in submitting secure event reports.
5. Changes in governance directives that are not communicated effectively.

What is needed on the dashboard of Family Member:
1. Real-time status of compliance scans and their results.
2. A summary of recent logs and actions taken.
3. Alerts for any non-compliance issues or operational red flags.
4. A section for governance directives with toggle options for updates.
5. Access to recent compliance verification audits and their statuses.
