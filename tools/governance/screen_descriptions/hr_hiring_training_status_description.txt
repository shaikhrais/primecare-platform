User Work List:
1. Monitor the hiring training status of HR personnel.
2. Review training completion rates and identify any gaps.
3. Ensure all HR staff are enrolled in necessary training programs.
4. Update training materials and resources as needed.
5. Provide feedback on the training process and suggest improvements.
6. Track and report on the effectiveness of training initiatives.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Low training completion rates among HR staff.
2. High error rates in training assessments.
3. Delays in training program updates or material availability.
4. Lack of engagement or participation in training sessions.
5. Inconsistent feedback from users regarding training effectiveness.
6. Technical issues with the training platform or dashboard.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time training completion statistics.
2. Visual representation of training progress (e.g., charts or graphs).
3. Alerts for overdue training or upcoming deadlines.
4. User feedback section for continuous improvement.
5. Access to training resources and materials.
6. Summary of user engagement and participation metrics.
