List of tasks HR Director must do:
1. Oversee recruitment and staffing processes.
2. Develop and implement HR policies and procedures.
3. Manage employee relations and resolve conflicts.
4. Ensure compliance with labor laws and regulations.
5. Conduct performance management and evaluations.
6. Design and implement training and development programs.
7. Monitor employee engagement and satisfaction.
8. Manage compensation and benefits programs.
9. Analyze HR metrics and prepare reports for management.
10. Lead diversity and inclusion initiatives.

Red flags of operation:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Low employee engagement scores.
3. Increased number of grievances or complaints.
4. Non-compliance with labor laws.
5. Delays in recruitment processes.
6. Poor performance management outcomes.
7. Lack of training and development opportunities.
8. Ineffective communication within the organization.
9. High absenteeism rates.
10. Negative feedback from exit interviews.

Dashboard needs for HR Director:
1. Recruitment metrics (time to hire, open positions).
2. Employee engagement scores.
3. Turnover rates and reasons for leaving.
4. Compliance status with labor laws.
5. Performance management statistics.
6. Training and development participation rates.
7. Employee satisfaction survey results.
8. Diversity and inclusion metrics.
9. Compensation and benefits analysis.
10. HR budget and expenditure overview.
