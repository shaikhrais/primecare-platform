Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Monitor compliance with regulations and standards.
3. Manage team performance and productivity.
4. Analyze operational data and metrics.
5. Implement process improvements.
6. Coordinate with other departments for seamless operations.
7. Handle operational issues and troubleshoot problems.
8. Maintain communication with stakeholders.
9. Prepare reports on operational performance.
10. Conduct audits and assessments.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decreased productivity or performance metrics.
2. Increased error rates or compliance violations.
3. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
4. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
5. Poor communication among team members.
6. Lack of adherence to safety protocols.
7. Inconsistent quality of products or services.
8. Negative feedback from customers or clients.
9. Unresolved operational issues or complaints.
10. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.

Dashboard Requirements for Operations Manager:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for productivity and efficiency.
2. Compliance status and audit results.
3. Real-time operational metrics and trends.
4. Team performance statistics.
5. Incident and issue tracking logs.
6. Financial performance overview.
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Resource allocation and utilization data.
9. Alerts for any operational red flags.
10. Historical data for trend analysis and forecasting.
