List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor and track deals in the regional market.
2. Update deal statuses and relevant information.
3. Analyze deal performance and trends.
4. Collaborate with team members on deal strategies.
5. Report on deal outcomes and forecasts.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delays in deal updates or status changes.
2. Inconsistent data entry or discrepancies in deal information.
3. Lack of communication among team members regarding deal progress.
4. High number of deals falling through or not closing.
5. Unclear ownership of deals or responsibilities.

What we need on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of all active deals with status indicators.
2. Performance metrics (e.g., conversion rates, average deal size).
3. Alerts for overdue deals or required actions.
4. Collaboration tools for team communication.
5. Visualizations of deal trends over time (graphs/charts).
