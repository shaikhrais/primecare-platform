List of work for the User:
1. Monitor quality metrics related to clinical operations.
2. Analyze data trends and identify areas for improvement.
3. Review performance indicators and compliance with standards.
4. Collaborate with clinical staff to implement quality improvement initiatives.
5. Generate reports on quality metrics for stakeholders.
6. Attend meetings to discuss quality metrics and strategies for enhancement.

Red flags of operation:
1. Significant deviations from established quality benchmarks.
2. Increased error rates in clinical processes.
3. Low compliance with quality standards.
4. Negative feedback from staff or patients regarding quality of care.
5. Delays in reporting or addressing quality issues.
6. Lack of engagement from clinical staff in quality improvement efforts.

Dashboard needs for the User:
1. Real-time display of key quality metrics.
2. Visualizations of trends over time (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for any metrics that fall below acceptable thresholds.
4. Access to detailed reports and historical data.
5. User-friendly interface for easy navigation and data interpretation.
6. Options to filter data by department, time period, or specific metrics.
