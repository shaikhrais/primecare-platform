QA Specialist Responsibilities:
1. Review and analyze requirements and specifications.
2. Develop and execute test plans, test cases, and test scripts.
3. Perform manual and automated testing.
4. Identify, document, and track defects.
5. Collaborate with development teams to resolve issues.
6. Conduct regression testing after fixes and enhancements.
7. Ensure compliance with quality standards and best practices.
8. Participate in code reviews and design discussions.
9. Maintain test documentation and reports.
10. Provide feedback on product usability and functionality.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High number of critical defects found late in the development cycle.
2. Frequent changes in requirements leading to scope creep.
3. Inconsistent testing processes and lack of documentation.
4. Poor communication between QA and development teams.
5. Lack of automated tests leading to increased manual testing workload.
6. Unclear definitions of done for features and stories.
7. Inadequate test coverage for critical functionalities.
8. High turnover rates in the QA team.
9. Delays in defect resolution impacting release schedules.
10. Negative feedback from users regarding product quality.

Dashboard Needs for QA Specialist:
1. Overview of current testing status (e.g., tests passed, failed, in progress).
2. Metrics on defect density and severity.
3. Historical data on defect trends over time.
4. Test coverage reports indicating areas tested vs. untested.
5. Automated test results and execution times.
6. Compliance scan results and audit logs.
7. Performance metrics for testing processes (e.g., time to resolve defects).
8. User feedback and satisfaction scores.
9. Alerts for critical defects or testing bottlenecks.
10. Integration with project management tools for tracking progress.
