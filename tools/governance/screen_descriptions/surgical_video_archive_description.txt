User Tasks:
1. View surgical videos in the archive.
2. Refresh the video list.
3. Upload new surgical case videos.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Slow loading times for video data.
2. Frequent errors when fetching video data.
3. Inability to upload videos due to errors or lack of permissions.
4. Poor user interface responsiveness or crashes during video playback.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Total number of videos in the archive.
2. Number of views for each video.
3. Recent uploads and their details (title, surgeon, date).
4. Error logs or alerts for failed video fetches or uploads.
5. User activity metrics (e.g., number of uploads, views per user).
