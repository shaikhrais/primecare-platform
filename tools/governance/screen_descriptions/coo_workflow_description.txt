Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
3. Monitor and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs).
4. Collaborate with other executives to align operations with company goals.
5. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
6. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
7. Lead and mentor operational teams.
8. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
9. Foster a culture of continuous improvement.
10. Communicate operational strategies to stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining performance metrics or KPIs.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent operational bottlenecks or delays.
4. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
5. Lack of alignment between departments.
6. Poor customer feedback or service issues.
7. Compliance violations or regulatory issues.
8. Ineffective communication within teams.
9. Resistance to change or innovation.
10. Inconsistent reporting and data accuracy.

Dashboard Needs for COO:
1. Real-time performance metrics (KPIs).
2. Financial overview (budget vs. actual).
3. Employee engagement and turnover rates.
4. Operational efficiency indicators.
5. Customer satisfaction scores.
6. Compliance status and alerts.
7. Project status updates and timelines.
8. Resource allocation and utilization.
9. Risk management indicators.
10. Actionable insights and recommendations.
