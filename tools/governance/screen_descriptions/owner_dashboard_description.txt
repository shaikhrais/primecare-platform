Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor operational performance metrics.
2. Ensure compliance with governance and security standards.
3. Conduct regular audits and scans for compliance.
4. Synchronize operational posture and policies.
5. Maintain and review audit logs.
6. Respond to alerts and issues promptly.
7. Update security policies as needed.
8. Export logs for external review or compliance purposes.
9. Engage in manual synchronization sweeps when necessary.
10. Provide feedback and suggestions for operational improvements.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in operational performance metrics.
2. Non-compliance alerts or issues.
3. Unresolved security exceptions.
4. Inconsistent audit logs or missing entries.
5. Delays in compliance scans or audits.
6. Frequent manual interventions required.
7. Negative trends in telemetry data.
8. Lack of synchronization with security posture.
9. High number of operational incidents reported.
10. Feedback from staff indicating operational inefficiencies.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Real-time telemetry data and trends.
3. Compliance status and alerts.
4. Audit logs with timestamps and actions taken.
5. Operational performance metrics (e.g., active operations).
6. Security clearance levels and exceptions.
7. Manual refresh and synchronization options.
8. Action buttons for executing compliance scans and updates.
9. Historical data for trend analysis.
10. Notifications for critical issues or alerts.
