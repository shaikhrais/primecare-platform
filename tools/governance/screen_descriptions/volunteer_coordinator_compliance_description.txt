List of work volunteers must do:
1. Participate in compliance audits.
2. Report any security incidents or anomalies.
3. Maintain accurate logs of activities.
4. Ensure adherence to governance policies.
5. Engage in training sessions for compliance updates.
6. Provide feedback on operational processes.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent log entries or missing logs.
2. Delays in reporting incidents.
3. Non-compliance with governance policies.
4. Lack of participation in audits or training.
5. Frequent errors in secure event reporting.

Dashboard needs for volunteers:
1. Real-time compliance status updates.
2. Access to recent audit logs and findings.
3. Notifications for upcoming training sessions.
4. A summary of governance policies and directives.
5. A log entry feature for reporting incidents.
6. A section for feedback on operational processes.
