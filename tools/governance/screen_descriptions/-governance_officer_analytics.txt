Responsibilities of a Governance Officer:
1. Ensure compliance with regulations and policies.
2. Monitor and assess governance frameworks.
3. Facilitate communication between stakeholders.
4. Conduct risk assessments and manage risks.
5. Develop and implement governance strategies.
6. Provide training and support on governance issues.
7. Prepare reports and documentation for governance activities.
8. Oversee audits and compliance checks.
9. Maintain records of governance activities.
10. Engage with external regulators and stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Increased number of compliance violations.
2. Lack of communication among stakeholders.
3. Delays in reporting or documentation.
4. High turnover rates in governance-related roles.
5. Inconsistent application of governance policies.
6. Poor audit results or findings.
7. Unresolved risks or issues.
8. Negative feedback from stakeholders.
9. Lack of training or awareness on governance matters.
10. Failure to meet regulatory deadlines.

Dashboard Needs for Governance Officer:
1. Compliance metrics and violation trends.
2. Risk assessment results and mitigation status.
3. Stakeholder engagement statistics.
4. Audit findings and follow-up actions.
5. Training completion rates and feedback.
6. Governance strategy implementation progress.
7. Document management and version control.
8. Real-time alerts for compliance issues.
9. Performance indicators for governance activities.
10. Summary reports of governance activities and outcomes.
