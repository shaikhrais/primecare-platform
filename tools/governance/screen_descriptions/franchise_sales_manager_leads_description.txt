User Tasks:
1. Monitor and manage franchise sales leads.
2. Review lead data and performance metrics.
3. Communicate with potential franchisees.
4. Update lead statuses and follow-up actions.
5. Analyze lead conversion rates and trends.
6. Generate reports on lead performance.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High lead drop-off rates.
2. Delays in follow-up communications.
3. Low conversion rates from leads to franchise agreements.
4. Inconsistent data entry or updates.
5. Frequent errors in lead information.
6. Lack of engagement from potential franchisees.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time lead status overview.
2. Conversion rate metrics and trends.
3. Follow-up reminders and alerts.
4. Performance comparison against targets.
5. Visual representation of lead sources and effectiveness.
6. Error logs for data entry issues.
