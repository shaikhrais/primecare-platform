List of tasks Personal Support Workers (PSWs) must perform:
1. Clock in and out of shifts.
2. Maintain an active checklist of Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) for clients.
3. Log vital signs (blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, oxygen levels).
4. Track travel mileage to and from client locations.
5. Communicate with clients and healthcare teams regarding client needs and status.
6. Document any changes in client condition or care requirements.
7. Ensure compliance with care plans and protocols.
8. Report any incidents or concerns to supervisors.

Red flags of operation that indicate ineffective work:
1. Frequent clocking in and out without valid reasons.
2. Incomplete or inconsistent logging of ADLs.
3. Delays in submitting vital signs.
4. High travel mileage without justification.
5. Lack of communication with clients or healthcare teams.
6. Repeated incidents of missed care tasks.
7. Failure to report changes in client condition.
8. Non-compliance with care plans.

Dashboard needs for Personal Support Workers (PSWs):
1. Real-time clock in/out status.
2. Active client information.
3. Summary of logged ADLs with completion status.
4. Vital signs submission status and history.
5. Travel mileage tracker.
6. Alerts for overdue tasks or submissions.
7. Communication log with clients and healthcare teams.
8. Incident reporting feature for quick documentation.
