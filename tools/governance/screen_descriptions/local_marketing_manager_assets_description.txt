List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor local marketing assets.
2. Analyze performance metrics of local marketing campaigns.
3. Update and manage marketing materials.
4. Collaborate with local teams for asset utilization.
5. Report on marketing effectiveness and ROI.

Red flags of operation:
1. Delayed loading times for data.
2. Frequent errors when fetching features.
3. Lack of updates or stale content in marketing assets.
4. Low engagement metrics from local campaigns.
5. Inconsistent branding across marketing materials.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of current marketing assets.
2. Performance metrics and analytics dashboard.
3. Notifications for updates or required actions.
4. Quick access to frequently used tools and resources.
5. Error reporting and troubleshooting section.
