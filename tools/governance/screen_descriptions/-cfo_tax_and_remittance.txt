User Work List:
1. Review tax and remittance data.
2. Ensure compliance with tax regulations.
3. Generate tax reports.
4. Submit remittance payments.
5. Monitor deadlines for tax submissions.
6. Communicate with tax authorities if needed.
7. Update financial records related to tax and remittance.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Delays in data loading or processing.
2. Frequent errors when fetching tax data.
3. Inconsistent or missing data in reports.
4. Notifications of upcoming deadlines not being received.
5. Issues with payment processing for remittances.
6. Lack of communication from tax authorities.
7. User interface not responding or crashing.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Overview of tax and remittance status.
2. Alerts for upcoming deadlines.
3. Quick access to generate reports.
4. Summary of recent transactions and submissions.
5. Notifications for any errors or issues.
6. Visual indicators for compliance status.
7. Access to historical data for analysis.
