List of work for the Patient:
1. Review appointment schedules.
2. Attend scheduled appointments.
3. Provide necessary medical history and documentation.
4. Follow up on any post-appointment instructions.
5. Engage in compliance scans as required.

Red flags for operation:
1. High number of missed appointments.
2. Unresolved compliance issues from scans.
3. Significant delays in appointment confirmations.
4. Repeated errors in patient data entry.
5. Low security clearance levels.

Dashboard needs:
1. Overview of upcoming appointments.
2. Compliance scan results and status.
3. Logs of operational activities and issues.
4. Metrics on appointment attendance and cancellations.
5. Alerts for any red flags or issues needing attention.
