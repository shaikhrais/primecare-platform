Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies to drive growth.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities and partnerships.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders and clients.
4. Conduct market research and analysis to inform strategic decisions.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align efforts.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals and reports to senior management.
8. Set and track performance metrics for business development initiatives.
9. Lead negotiations and close deals with potential partners.
10. Manage and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. High turnover rates within the business development team.
3. Lack of clear communication and alignment between teams.
4. Inconsistent tracking of performance metrics.
5. Negative feedback from clients or partners.
6. Failure to meet strategic goals or deadlines.
7. Poor market research leading to misguided strategies.
8. Increased competition without a response strategy.
9. Lack of innovation in business development approaches.
10. Insufficient resources allocated to business development efforts.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue growth.
2. Metrics on new business opportunities and partnerships.
3. Client relationship management data.
4. Market research insights and competitor analysis.
5. Team performance metrics and individual contributions.
6. Progress tracking against strategic goals and initiatives.
7. Feedback and satisfaction ratings from clients and partners.
8. Visualizations of industry trends and market dynamics.
9. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
10. Summary of ongoing negotiations and deal statuses.
