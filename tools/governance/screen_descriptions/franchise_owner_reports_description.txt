Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Monitor operational performance metrics.
2. Conduct compliance audits and scans.
3. Review and analyze operational audit logs.
4. Ensure security clearance and compliance with regulations.
5. Manage and respond to any operational issues or red flags.
6. Maintain communication with the franchise support team.
7. Implement improvements based on audit findings and performance metrics.
8. Train staff on compliance and operational procedures.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Decrease in operational performance metrics.
2. Increase in compliance issues or audit failures.
3. Unresolved security clearance exceptions.
4. Negative trends in telemetry data.
5. High frequency of operational errors or incidents.
6. Delays in responding to compliance audit findings.
7. Lack of communication or support from the franchise team.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Real-time operational performance metrics.
2. Compliance audit status and results.
3. Security clearance levels and exceptions.
4. Telemetry data visualizations (charts/graphs).
5. Recent operational audit logs.
6. Alerts for any red flags or issues.
7. Action buttons for executing compliance scans and audits.
8. Manual refresh option for data synchronization.
