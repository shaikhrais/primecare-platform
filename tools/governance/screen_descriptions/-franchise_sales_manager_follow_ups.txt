List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor follow-ups with franchise leads.
2. Track the status of each franchise lead.
3. Update the status of follow-ups as they progress.
4. Analyze follow-up effectiveness and conversion rates.
5. Communicate with team members regarding franchise leads.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of leads with no follow-up activity.
2. Leads that have been in the same status for an extended period.
3. Low conversion rates from follow-ups to franchise agreements.
4. Inconsistent follow-up patterns among team members.
5. Frequent errors or issues reported during the follow-up process.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of all franchise leads and their current statuses.
2. Metrics on follow-up activities (e.g., number of follow-ups, response rates).
3. Alerts for leads requiring immediate attention.
4. Historical data on follow-up effectiveness and conversion rates.
5. Team performance metrics to identify areas for improvement.
