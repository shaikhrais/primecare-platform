Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations of the franchise.
2. Ensure compliance with franchise agreements and company policies.
3. Manage staff recruitment, training, and performance.
4. Monitor financial performance and manage budgets.
5. Implement marketing strategies to promote the franchise.
6. Maintain customer service standards and address customer complaints.
7. Conduct regular audits and assessments of franchise operations.
8. Communicate with the franchisor and attend franchise meetings.
9. Manage inventory and supply chain logistics.
10. Analyze market trends and adjust business strategies accordingly.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Consistent decline in sales or revenue.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative reviews.
4. Non-compliance with franchise standards or policies.
5. Poor inventory management leading to stockouts or overstock.
6. Lack of effective marketing or promotional activities.
7. Inconsistent financial reporting or discrepancies in accounts.
8. Failure to meet operational benchmarks or KPIs.
9. Resistance to change or adaptation to new processes.
10. Poor communication with the franchisor or within the team.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Sales performance metrics (daily, weekly, monthly).
2. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback.
3. Employee performance and turnover rates.
4. Financial overview (expenses, profits, losses).
5. Inventory levels and supply chain status.
6. Marketing campaign performance and ROI.
7. Compliance checklist and audit results.
8. Operational KPIs and benchmarks.
9. Alerts for red flags or areas needing attention.
10. Communication logs with the franchisor and team members.
