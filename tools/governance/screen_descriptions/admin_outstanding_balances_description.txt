User Tasks:
1. Monitor outstanding balances for users.
2. Review and manage any discrepancies in balances.
3. Generate reports on outstanding balances.
4. Communicate with users regarding their balances.
5. Update any necessary information related to outstanding balances.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors in loading data indicating potential backend issues.
2. Long loading times suggesting performance bottlenecks.
3. Inconsistent data displayed on the screen.
4. User complaints regarding balance inaccuracies.
5. Lack of updates or notifications for outstanding balances.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of total outstanding balances.
2. Breakdown of outstanding balances by user.
3. Alerts for balances that exceed a certain threshold.
4. Recent activity log related to outstanding balances.
5. Quick access to generate reports or send notifications to users.
