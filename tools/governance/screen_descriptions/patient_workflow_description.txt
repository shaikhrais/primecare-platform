List of work for the patient:
1. Complete pre-operative assessments.
2. Review and sign consent forms.
3. Attend pre-operative education sessions.
4. Schedule and attend follow-up appointments.
5. Monitor and report any changes in health status.
6. Follow post-operative care instructions.

Red flags for operation:
1. Uncontrolled pain or discomfort.
2. Signs of infection (fever, redness, swelling).
3. Unexpected bleeding or discharge.
4. Severe allergic reactions.
5. Difficulty breathing or chest pain.
6. Non-compliance with pre-operative instructions.

Dashboard needs for the patient:
1. Overview of upcoming appointments and tasks.
2. Notifications for pre-operative and post-operative instructions.
3. Health status tracking and reporting feature.
4. Access to educational resources and consent forms.
5. Communication channel with healthcare providers.
6. Summary of completed tasks and next steps.
