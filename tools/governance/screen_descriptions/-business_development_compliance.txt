Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders.
4. Conduct market research and analysis.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals.
8. Negotiate contracts and agreements.
9. Track and report on business development metrics.
10. Lead and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. Increased customer churn rates.
3. Lack of alignment between business development and other departments.
4. Poor communication within the team.
5. Inconsistent tracking of business development metrics.
6. High turnover rates in the business development team.
7. Negative feedback from clients or partners.
8. Failure to meet business development targets.
9. Lack of innovation in strategies or offerings.
10. Insufficient market research leading to missed opportunities.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Business Development:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for sales and revenue.
2. Customer acquisition and retention metrics.
3. Status of ongoing business development initiatives.
4. Market analysis and competitive landscape overview.
5. Team performance metrics and individual contributions.
6. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Financial forecasts and projections.
8. Progress on strategic partnerships and collaborations.
9. Compliance and regulatory status updates.
10. Real-time updates on market trends and opportunities.
