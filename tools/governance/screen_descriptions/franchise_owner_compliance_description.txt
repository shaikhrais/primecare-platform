Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits.
2. Monitor operational performance metrics.
3. Ensure security clearance levels are maintained.
4. Review and address any compliance issues.
5. Maintain logs of operational activities.
6. Execute operational audit scans as needed.
7. Refresh and synchronize dashboard data regularly.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of compliance issues reported.
2. Decrease in operational performance metrics.
3. Security clearance levels dropping below acceptable thresholds.
4. Inconsistent or missing logs of operational activities.
5. Frequent network or API errors during compliance scans.
6. Delays in executing operational audit scans.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Title and overview of compliance status.
2. Active operations metric with productivity trends.
3. Security clearance status with exception logs.
4. Real-time telemetry chart displaying operational data.
5. Operational audit logs with timestamps and details.
6. Buttons for executing compliance scans and refreshing data.
7. Loading indicators for ongoing processes.
