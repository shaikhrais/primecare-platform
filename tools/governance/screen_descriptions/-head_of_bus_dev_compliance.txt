Head of Business Development Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement business development strategies.
2. Identify and pursue new business opportunities.
3. Build and maintain relationships with key clients and stakeholders.
4. Conduct market research and analysis to inform strategy.
5. Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to align efforts.
6. Monitor industry trends and competitor activities.
7. Prepare and present business proposals and presentations.
8. Negotiate contracts and agreements with clients.
9. Track and report on business development metrics and KPIs.
10. Lead and mentor the business development team.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining sales or revenue growth.
2. Increased customer churn or dissatisfaction.
3. Lack of alignment between business development and other departments.
4. Inconsistent communication with clients and stakeholders.
5. Failure to meet business development targets or deadlines.
6. High employee turnover in the business development team.
7. Poor market research leading to misguided strategies.
8. Inability to adapt to changing market conditions.
9. Lack of innovation in service or product offerings.
10. Negative feedback from clients or partners.

Dashboard Needs for Head of Business Development:
1. Sales performance metrics (monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2. Customer acquisition and retention rates.
3. Pipeline status and forecast of potential deals.
4. Market analysis and competitive landscape overview.
5. Key performance indicators (KPIs) for business development activities.
6. Client feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Team performance metrics and individual contributions.
8. Financial projections and budget tracking.
9. Upcoming business development initiatives and timelines.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
