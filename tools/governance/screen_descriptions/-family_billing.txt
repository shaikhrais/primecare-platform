List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor family billing information.
2. Review billing history and transactions.
3. Update family member billing details.
4. Handle billing disputes or inquiries.
5. Access reports on family billing trends.

Red flags of operation:
1. Frequent errors when loading billing data.
2. Delays in updating billing information.
3. Inconsistent billing amounts or discrepancies.
4. Lack of user feedback during loading states.
5. Unclear error messages that do not guide users on how to resolve issues.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Summary of current billing status.
2. Notifications for upcoming payments or overdue bills.
3. Quick access to billing history and transaction details.
4. Visual representation of billing trends (graphs/charts).
5. User-friendly error messages and troubleshooting tips.
