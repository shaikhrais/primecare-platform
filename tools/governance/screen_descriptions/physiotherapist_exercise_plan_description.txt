Physiotherapist Responsibilities:
1. Assess patient physical conditions and needs.
2. Develop personalized exercise plans.
3. Monitor patient progress and adjust plans as necessary.
4. Educate patients on exercises and proper techniques.
5. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
6. Maintain accurate patient records and documentation.
7. Stay updated on best practices and new techniques in physiotherapy.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Sudden changes in patient condition.
2. Non-compliance with prescribed exercise plans.
3. Increased pain or discomfort during exercises.
4. Signs of infection or unusual swelling.
5. Lack of progress in rehabilitation goals.
6. Patient reports of dizziness or fainting during sessions.

Dashboard Requirements for Physiotherapists:
1. Overview of active patients and their status.
2. Compliance metrics for exercise plans.
3. Patient progress tracking and historical data.
4. Alerts for red flags or concerns.
5. Access to educational resources and best practices.
6. Log of recent activities and compliance audits.
7. Metrics on overall departmental performance.
