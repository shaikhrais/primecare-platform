List of tasks a Social Worker must do:
1. Conduct compliance audits.
2. Maintain accurate logs of activities and audits.
3. Ensure adherence to governance policies.
4. Report any incidents or events through secure forms.
5. Monitor and update security measures, such as multi-factor authentication.
6. Participate in training and development for compliance and governance.
7. Collaborate with other departments to ensure comprehensive compliance.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent or missing logs.
2. Delays in compliance audits.
3. Failure to report incidents or events.
4. Non-adherence to security policies.
5. Lack of communication with other departments.
6. High number of compliance violations or issues.
7. Negative feedback from audits or reviews.

Dashboard needs for a Social Worker:
1. Overview of compliance status and recent audits.
2. Log of operational activities and incidents.
3. Alerts for any compliance issues or red flags.
4. Quick access to governance policies and directives.
5. Tools for secure event reporting.
6. Metrics on compliance performance over time.
7. Notifications for required actions or updates.
