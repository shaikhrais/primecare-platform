User Tasks:
1. Monitor revenue summary data.
2. Analyze trends and patterns in revenue.
3. Identify key performance indicators (KPIs).
4. Generate reports based on revenue data.
5. Review and adjust strategies based on insights.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant drops in revenue compared to previous periods.
2. Inconsistent data loading times or frequent errors.
3. Lack of updates or stale data in the dashboard.
4. Unclear or misleading visualizations of revenue data.
5. High error rates when generating reports.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time revenue data display.
2. Visualizations for revenue trends (graphs/charts).
3. Alerts for significant changes or anomalies in revenue.
4. Easy access to historical data for comparison.
5. User-friendly interface for generating reports and insights.
