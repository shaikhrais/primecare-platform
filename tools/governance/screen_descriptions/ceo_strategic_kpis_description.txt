User Tasks:
1. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to strategic goals.
2. Analyze data trends and insights for informed decision-making.
3. Review operational efficiency and identify areas for improvement.
4. Collaborate with team members to align on strategic objectives.
5. Generate reports based on KPI performance for stakeholders.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Significant deviations from expected KPI targets.
2. Consistent delays in data retrieval or reporting.
3. High error rates in data processing or analysis.
4. Lack of alignment among team members on strategic goals.
5. Inconsistent feedback from stakeholders regarding performance.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Real-time display of key performance indicators.
2. Visualizations for trend analysis (charts, graphs).
3. Alerts for KPI deviations or operational issues.
4. Summary reports for quick insights.
5. Collaboration tools for team communication and alignment.
