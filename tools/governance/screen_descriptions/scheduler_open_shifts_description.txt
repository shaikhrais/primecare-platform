List of responsibilities for a Shift Supervisor:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure adherence to company policies.
2. Manage staff schedules and ensure adequate coverage.
3. Conduct regular compliance audits and safety checks.
4. Address employee concerns and resolve conflicts.
5. Monitor performance metrics and provide feedback to staff.
6. Maintain communication with upper management regarding operational issues.
7. Ensure quality control and customer satisfaction.
8. Train and onboard new employees.
9. Document operational logs and incidents.
10. Implement process improvements for efficiency.

Red flags in operations:
1. High employee turnover or frequent absences.
2. Consistent failure to meet performance metrics.
3. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Safety incidents or compliance violations.
5. Lack of communication among team members.
6. Unresolved conflicts or low morale within the team.
7. Inconsistent adherence to schedules or procedures.

Dashboard needs for a Shift Supervisor:
1. Real-time performance metrics (e.g., productivity rates, customer satisfaction scores).
2. Compliance audit results and logs.
3. Staff attendance and scheduling overview.
4. Incident reports and resolution status.
5. Communication tools for team updates and alerts.
6. Visual representation of operational trends (charts/graphs).
7. Access to training resources and onboarding materials.
8. Alerts for any red flags or operational issues that need immediate attention.
