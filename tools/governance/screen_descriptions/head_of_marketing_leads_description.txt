User Tasks:
1. Monitor marketing leads and their status.
2. Analyze lead conversion rates.
3. Review marketing campaign performance.
4. Collaborate with the sales team for lead follow-up.
5. Update lead information as necessary.
6. Generate reports on lead performance.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High lead drop-off rates.
2. Low conversion rates from leads to customers.
3. Delays in lead follow-up by the sales team.
4. Inconsistent lead data updates.
5. Negative feedback from leads regarding marketing efforts.

Dashboard Needs:
1. Real-time lead status overview.
2. Conversion rate metrics.
3. Performance analytics for marketing campaigns.
4. Notifications for overdue lead follow-ups.
5. Visual representation of lead trends over time.
6. Access to detailed reports for in-depth analysis.
