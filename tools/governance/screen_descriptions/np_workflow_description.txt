Nurse Practitioner (NP) Responsibilities:
1. Conduct patient assessments and evaluations.
2. Develop and implement patient care plans.
3. Prescribe medications and treatments.
4. Perform diagnostic tests and interpret results.
5. Educate patients and families about health conditions and preventive care.
6. Collaborate with healthcare teams to provide comprehensive care.
7. Monitor patient progress and adjust treatment plans as necessary.
8. Document patient interactions and care provided.
9. Stay updated on medical guidelines and best practices.
10. Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations and standards.

Red Flags for Operations:
1. Increased patient complaints or dissatisfaction.
2. Frequent medication errors or adverse drug reactions.
3. High rates of patient readmissions.
4. Delays in patient care or treatment.
5. Inconsistent documentation or missing records.
6. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
7. Staff burnout or high turnover rates.
8. Inefficient workflow processes leading to delays.
9. Lack of communication within the healthcare team.
10. Unresolved patient safety incidents.

Dashboard Needs for Nurse Practitioners:
1. Patient load and caseload statistics.
2. Compliance metrics and performance indicators.
3. Real-time access to patient records and logs.
4. Alerts for critical patient updates or changes.
5. Workflow efficiency metrics.
6. Patient satisfaction scores and feedback.
7. Medication management and error tracking.
8. Telemetry data for monitoring patient health trends.
9. Communication tools for team collaboration.
10. Training and resource links for continuous education.
