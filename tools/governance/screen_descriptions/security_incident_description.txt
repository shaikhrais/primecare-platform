User Work List:
1. Monitor and review security incidents reported in the system.
2. Submit new security incidents with detailed descriptions and relevant categories.
3. Update the status of ongoing incidents as they progress through investigation and resolution.
4. Analyze incident metrics such as active alerts, incidents under investigation, and resolved incidents.
5. Collaborate with the cybersecurity team to address critical incidents and ensure compliance with safety protocols.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. High number of active incidents indicating potential security vulnerabilities.
2. Increased severity of incidents reported, especially critical and high severity cases.
3. Delays in resolving incidents, particularly those marked as investigating.
4. Recurring types of incidents suggesting systemic issues that need addressing.
5. Lack of timely updates on incident statuses, leading to potential oversight.

Dashboard Requirements for User:
1. Overview of active alerts, incidents under investigation, and resolved incidents with metrics.
2. Visual representation of incident trends over time (e.g., charts or graphs).
3. Quick access to submit new incidents with a user-friendly form.
4. Detailed incident log table displaying all reported incidents with filters for status and severity.
5. Notifications or alerts for critical incidents that require immediate attention.
