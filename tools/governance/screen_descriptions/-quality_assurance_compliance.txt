List of tasks a QA Specialist must perform:
1. Conduct compliance audits to ensure adherence to standards.
2. Monitor and log system performance and compliance metrics.
3. Review and analyze audit logs for discrepancies.
4. Collaborate with development teams to address identified issues.
5. Maintain documentation of compliance processes and findings.
6. Implement and monitor governance directives and policies.
7. Provide training and support to staff on compliance requirements.
8. Facilitate secure event reporting and ingestion of logs.
9. Update and maintain the QA dashboard with relevant metrics.
10. Communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders.

Red flags in operations that indicate ineffective work:
1. Increased number of compliance violations or audit failures.
2. Delays in compliance audit execution or reporting.
3. Lack of clear documentation or updates on compliance processes.
4. Inconsistent logging of operational activities.
5. High volume of unresolved issues or discrepancies.
6. Poor communication between QA and development teams.
7. Frequent changes in governance policies without proper training.
8. Low engagement from staff in compliance training sessions.
9. Inadequate response to identified security vulnerabilities.
10. Lack of updates or maintenance on the QA dashboard.

Essential elements for a QA Specialist dashboard:
1. Compliance audit status and results.
2. Real-time system performance metrics.
3. Log of recent compliance verification audits.
4. Summary of governance directives and their statuses.
5. Operational audit logs with timestamps and details.
6. Alerts for compliance violations or issues needing attention.
7. Metrics on staff engagement in compliance training.
8. Historical data on compliance trends over time.
9. Visual representation of compliance metrics (charts/graphs).
10. Quick access to tools for conducting audits and logging findings.
