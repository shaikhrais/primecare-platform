List of tasks a therapist must perform:
1. Conduct patient assessments.
2. Develop treatment plans.
3. Document patient progress.
4. Collaborate with other healthcare professionals.
5. Monitor compliance with treatment protocols.
6. Execute quality verification sweeps.
7. Maintain operational logs and telemetry.
8. Respond to alerts and notifications.

Red flags indicating operational issues:
1. High error rates in compliance checks.
2. Delays in patient documentation.
3. Unresolved patient complaints or feedback.
4. Inconsistent treatment plan adherence.
5. Lack of communication with other healthcare team members.
6. Frequent network or API errors during task execution.
7. Low telemetry metrics indicating poor performance.

Dashboard needs for therapists:
1. Real-time compliance status.
2. Active logs of operations and alerts.
3. Metrics on patient progress and treatment effectiveness.
4. Visual representation of telemetry data.
5. Quick access to execute quality verification sweeps.
6. Notifications for any flagged issues or required actions.
7. Summary of collaboration with other healthcare professionals.
