Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage scheduling and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence and communication.
3. Maintain filing systems and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist in budgeting and financial tracking.
6. Coordinate meetings and events.
7. Manage office supplies and inventory.
8. Support team members with administrative tasks.
9. Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.
10. Handle customer inquiries and support.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent missed deadlines.
2. Inconsistent communication with team members.
3. Lack of organization in filing and documentation.
4. High error rates in reports and data entry.
5. Unresolved customer complaints or inquiries.
6. Low morale or engagement among team members.
7. Inability to adapt to new tools or processes.
8. Overreliance on manual processes instead of automation.
9. Lack of clarity in roles and responsibilities.
10. Poor time management leading to backlog of tasks.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task management overview with deadlines.
2. Calendar integration for scheduling.
3. Communication log for tracking correspondence.
4. Document management system for easy access to files.
5. Performance metrics for task completion and efficiency.
6. Compliance checklist for adherence to policies.
7. Inventory tracker for office supplies.
8. Customer feedback and inquiry tracking.
9. Meeting and event coordination tools.
10. Reporting tools for budget and financial tracking.
