User Tasks:
1. Review and analyze corrective actions related to quality assurance.
2. Document findings and recommendations based on the analysis.
3. Collaborate with team members to implement corrective actions.
4. Monitor the effectiveness of implemented actions.
5. Report any ongoing issues or new findings to the management.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent errors or defects in products/services.
2. Delays in implementing corrective actions.
3. Lack of communication among team members regarding quality issues.
4. Inconsistent documentation of quality assurance processes.
5. Low engagement or feedback from team members on quality initiatives.

Dashboard Requirements:
1. Overview of current corrective actions and their statuses.
2. Metrics on the effectiveness of past corrective actions.
3. Alerts for overdue or pending actions.
4. User engagement statistics related to quality assurance activities.
5. Visual representation of trends in quality issues over time.
