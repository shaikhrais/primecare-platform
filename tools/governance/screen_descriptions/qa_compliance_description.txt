List of tasks a QA Specialist must do:
1. Conduct regular compliance audits.
2. Monitor and log system performance and compliance metrics.
3. Review and validate governance policies and procedures.
4. Ensure adherence to security protocols.
5. Collaborate with development teams to identify and resolve issues.
6. Provide feedback on system functionality and user experience.
7. Maintain documentation of audit findings and compliance status.
8. Facilitate training sessions on compliance standards.
9. Analyze data from compliance scans and audits.
10. Report on compliance status to stakeholders.

Red flags in operations that indicate ineffective work:
1. Frequent compliance failures or audit discrepancies.
2. Delays in compliance reporting or audit execution.
3. Lack of clear documentation or tracking of compliance activities.
4. Inconsistent application of governance policies.
5. High volume of unresolved issues reported by users.
6. Poor communication between QA and development teams.
7. Insufficient training or awareness of compliance standards among staff.
8. Increased number of security incidents or breaches.
9. Low engagement or feedback from stakeholders on compliance matters.
10. Inadequate response to identified compliance risks.

Dashboard needs for a QA Specialist:
1. Real-time compliance status indicators.
2. Logs of recent compliance audits and findings.
3. Metrics on compliance scan results and trends.
4. Alerts for compliance failures or red flags.
5. Summary of governance policy adherence.
6. User feedback and issue tracking.
7. Historical data on compliance performance.
8. Visualizations of audit results and compliance trends.
9. Access to training resources and documentation.
10. Integration with incident reporting tools for security breaches.
