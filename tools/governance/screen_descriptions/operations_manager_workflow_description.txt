Operations Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Manage team performance and provide guidance.
3. Develop and implement operational policies and procedures.
4. Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics.
5. Coordinate with other departments to ensure smooth operations.
6. Identify areas for improvement and implement solutions.
7. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
8. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
9. Conduct regular audits and assessments.
10. Facilitate training and development for staff.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Decrease in productivity or efficiency.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Frequent operational errors or issues.
4. Inconsistent performance metrics.
5. Poor communication between teams.
6. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
7. Budget overruns or financial discrepancies.
8. Lack of adherence to policies and procedures.
9. Negative customer feedback or complaints.
10. Difficulty in meeting compliance requirements.

Dashboard Requirements for Operations Manager:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Real-time operational metrics and analytics.
3. Team performance tracking and reports.
4. Budget and financial overview.
5. Compliance status and alerts.
6. Project timelines and status updates.
7. Employee satisfaction and feedback metrics.
8. Incident reports and resolution tracking.
9. Resource allocation and utilization metrics.
10. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring attention.
