General Manager Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure compliance with regulations.
2. Manage and lead the team to achieve operational goals.
3. Monitor performance metrics and implement improvements.
4. Conduct regular compliance audits and risk assessments.
5. Ensure effective communication across departments.
6. Develop and implement strategic plans for growth.
7. Maintain relationships with stakeholders and partners.
8. Address and resolve operational issues promptly.
9. Ensure adherence to company policies and procedures.
10. Prepare reports for upper management and stakeholders.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Inconsistent performance metrics or declining trends.
4. Delayed project timelines or missed deadlines.
5. Poor communication or collaboration among teams.
6. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
7. Lack of adherence to safety and security protocols.
8. Unresolved operational issues or bottlenecks.
9. Insufficient training or resources for staff.
10. Inadequate response to market changes or competition.

Dashboard Requirements for General Manager:
1. Real-time performance metrics (KPIs).
2. Compliance audit results and status.
3. Employee satisfaction and turnover rates.
4. Operational issue tracking and resolution status.
5. Financial performance indicators (revenue, expenses).
6. Customer feedback and complaint trends.
7. Project timelines and milestones.
8. Risk assessment and mitigation strategies.
9. Team performance and productivity metrics.
10. Strategic plan progress and updates.
