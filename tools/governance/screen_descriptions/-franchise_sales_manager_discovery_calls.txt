List of work the user must do:
1. Conduct discovery calls with potential franchisees.
2. Gather and document information from calls.
3. Analyze the data collected to identify potential franchise opportunities.
4. Follow up with leads after initial calls.
5. Update the status of leads in the system.
6. Collaborate with the sales team to strategize on lead conversion.
7. Monitor performance metrics related to discovery calls.

Red flags of operation:
1. High number of unreturned calls or emails from leads.
2. Low conversion rate from discovery calls to franchise agreements.
3. Inconsistent documentation of call outcomes.
4. Delays in follow-up communications with leads.
5. Frequent technical issues during calls (e.g., connectivity problems).
6. Lack of engagement or interest from potential franchisees during calls.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of scheduled and completed discovery calls.
2. Metrics on call outcomes (e.g., successful calls, follow-ups needed).
3. Conversion rates from discovery calls to franchise agreements.
4. Alerts for leads that require immediate follow-up.
5. Performance trends over time (e.g., weekly or monthly).
6. Access to resources or scripts for conducting effective calls.
7. Integration with calendar for scheduling calls.
