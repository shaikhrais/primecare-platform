Chief Operating Officer (COO) Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure efficiency.
2. Develop and implement operational policies and strategies.
3. Monitor performance metrics and operational KPIs.
4. Manage budgets and resource allocation.
5. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.
6. Lead and mentor department heads and staff.
7. Foster a culture of continuous improvement.
8. Coordinate cross-departmental collaboration.
9. Analyze operational data to identify trends and areas for improvement.
10. Report to the CEO and board on operational performance.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Declining performance metrics or KPIs.
2. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue.
3. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
4. Frequent compliance issues or regulatory violations.
5. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
6. Poor communication between departments.
7. Lack of innovation or resistance to change.
8. Inconsistent customer feedback or complaints.
9. Inefficient resource utilization.
10. Failure to meet strategic goals or objectives.

Dashboard Requirements for COO:
1. Overview of key performance indicators (KPIs).
2. Real-time operational metrics (e.g., productivity, efficiency).
3. Financial performance indicators (e.g., revenue, expenses).
4. Compliance status and audit results.
5. Employee performance and satisfaction metrics.
6. Project timelines and status updates.
7. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
8. Resource allocation and utilization reports.
9. Risk management and incident reports.
10. Alerts for any operational red flags or issues.
