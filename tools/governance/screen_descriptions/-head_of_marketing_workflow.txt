Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and initiatives.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Analyze market trends and customer insights.
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams.
6. Monitor and report on marketing performance metrics.
7. Lead and mentor the marketing team.
8. Ensure brand consistency across all channels.
9. Manage relationships with external partners and vendors.
10. Stay updated on industry developments and competitor activities.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining engagement or conversion rates.
2. Inconsistent messaging across marketing channels.
3. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
4. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales teams.
5. Poor performance of marketing campaigns.
6. High turnover rates within the marketing team.
7. Negative feedback from customers or stakeholders.
8. Failure to meet key performance indicators (KPIs).
9. Delays in project timelines or deliverables.
10. Insufficient market research or understanding of target audience.

Dashboard Requirements for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their performance.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) tracking (e.g., ROI, conversion rates).
3. Budget utilization and financial performance metrics.
4. Customer engagement metrics (e.g., website traffic, social media interactions).
5. Market trends and competitive analysis insights.
6. Team performance and productivity metrics.
7. Feedback and sentiment analysis from customers.
8. Upcoming project timelines and milestones.
9. Integration with sales data for alignment tracking.
10. Alerts for any red flags or issues requiring immediate attention.
