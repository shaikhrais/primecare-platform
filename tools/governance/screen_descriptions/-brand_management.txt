Head of Marketing Responsibilities:
1. Develop and implement marketing strategies.
2. Oversee marketing campaigns and initiatives.
3. Manage the marketing budget and allocate resources effectively.
4. Analyze market trends and customer insights.
5. Collaborate with sales and product teams.
6. Monitor and report on marketing performance metrics.
7. Lead and mentor the marketing team.
8. Ensure brand consistency across all channels.
9. Manage relationships with external partners and agencies.
10. Stay updated on industry trends and competitor activities.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Declining engagement or conversion rates.
2. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
3. Budget overruns or misallocation of resources.
4. Lack of alignment between marketing and sales teams.
5. Inconsistent brand messaging or identity.
6. High employee turnover within the marketing team.
7. Failure to meet campaign deadlines or objectives.
8. Poor performance in key marketing metrics.
9. Ineffective use of marketing technology or tools.
10. Lack of innovation or adaptation to market changes.

Dashboard Needs for Head of Marketing:
1. Overview of current marketing campaigns and their performance.
2. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as ROI, conversion rates, and customer acquisition costs.
3. Real-time analytics on website traffic and engagement.
4. Social media performance metrics.
5. Budget tracking and expenditure reports.
6. Customer feedback and satisfaction scores.
7. Competitive analysis and market trends.
8. Team performance and productivity metrics.
9. Compliance and regulatory updates.
10. Upcoming marketing initiatives and timelines.
