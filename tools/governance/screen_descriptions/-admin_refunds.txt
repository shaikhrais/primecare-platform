List of work the user must do:
1. Monitor refund requests submitted by users.
2. Approve or reject refund requests based on predefined criteria.
3. Communicate with users regarding the status of their refund requests.
4. Analyze refund trends and patterns for operational insights.
5. Ensure compliance with refund policies and regulations.

Red flags of operation:
1. High volume of refund requests indicating potential issues with products or services.
2. Frequent rejections of refund requests suggesting unclear policies or user misunderstandings.
3. Delays in processing refunds leading to user dissatisfaction.
4. Lack of communication with users regarding their refund status.
5. Inconsistent approval rates among different staff members.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Overview of pending refund requests with status indicators.
2. Metrics on refund request volume and trends over time.
3. Notifications for urgent or high-priority refund requests.
4. Communication tools for interacting with users about their requests.
5. Access to refund policy documentation for quick reference.
