Administrative Assistant Responsibilities:
1. Manage schedules and appointments.
2. Handle correspondence (emails, phone calls, etc.).
3. Organize and maintain files and records.
4. Prepare reports and presentations.
5. Assist in project management and coordination.
6. Manage office supplies and inventory.
7. Support team members with administrative tasks.
8. Facilitate communication between departments.
9. Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information.
10. Provide customer service and support.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. Frequent missed deadlines or appointments.
2. High volume of errors in documentation.
3. Lack of communication among team members.
4. Inconsistent filing and record-keeping practices.
5. Low morale or high turnover among staff.
6. Inefficient use of office resources.
7. Poor customer feedback or complaints.
8. Difficulty in accessing important documents.
9. Unclear roles and responsibilities within the team.
10. Resistance to adopting new technologies or processes.

Dashboard Needs for Administrative Assistant:
1. Task and appointment calendar.
2. Email and communication log.
3. Document management system.
4. Performance metrics (e.g., tasks completed, deadlines met).
5. Inventory management for office supplies.
6. Customer feedback and service metrics.
7. Team collaboration tools.
8. Compliance and policy adherence tracker.
9. Training and development resources.
10. Alerts for upcoming deadlines and important events.
