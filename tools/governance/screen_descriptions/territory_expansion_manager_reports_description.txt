List of work the user must do:
1. Review the territory expansion reports.
2. Analyze data trends and patterns in the reports.
3. Identify potential areas for expansion based on the reports.
4. Collaborate with the sales and marketing teams to strategize expansion efforts.
5. Monitor the performance of territories post-expansion.

Red flags of operation:
1. Inconsistent data reporting or discrepancies in the reports.
2. Lack of actionable insights from the data.
3. Delays in report generation or loading times.
4. High error rates when accessing or interpreting the reports.
5. Negative feedback from users regarding the usability of the reports.

What is needed on the user dashboard:
1. Key performance indicators (KPIs) related to territory performance.
2. Visualizations of data trends (charts, graphs).
3. Alerts for any anomalies or red flags in the data.
4. Quick access to recent reports and historical data.
5. Collaboration tools for communication with other teams.
