Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Responsibilities:
1. Financial Planning and Analysis
2. Budgeting and Forecasting
3. Financial Reporting
4. Risk Management
5. Compliance and Regulatory Oversight
6. Cash Flow Management
7. Investment Management
8. Tax Strategy and Compliance
9. Financial Strategy Development
10. Stakeholder Communication

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Unexplained fluctuations in cash flow
2. High levels of outstanding receivables
3. Discrepancies in financial reports
4. Frequent budget overruns
5. Non-compliance with regulations
6. Inconsistent financial metrics
7. High employee turnover in finance department
8. Delays in financial reporting
9. Unresolved discrepancies in ledger transactions
10. Increased audit findings

Dashboard Requirements for CFO:
1. Current Cash Balance
2. Projected Growth Rate
3. Expense Buffer
4. Tax Liability Overview
5. Ledger Transaction Summary
6. Compliance Scan Results
7. Financial Performance Metrics (e.g., revenue, expenses)
8. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
9. Recent Activity Logs
10. Alerts for Red Flags and Discrepancies
