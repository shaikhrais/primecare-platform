Franchise Owner Responsibilities:
1. Ensure compliance with operational standards and regulations.
2. Conduct regular audits and assessments of franchise operations.
3. Maintain accurate records of compliance activities.
4. Implement security measures, such as multi-factor authentication.
5. Monitor and report on operational performance metrics.
6. Engage in continuous training and development for staff.
7. Address customer feedback and operational issues promptly.
8. Collaborate with corporate to align with franchise policies.
9. Manage financial performance and budgeting.
10. Participate in franchisee meetings and training sessions.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent compliance violations or audit failures.
2. High employee turnover or dissatisfaction.
3. Inconsistent operational performance metrics.
4. Lack of timely reporting or record-keeping.
5. Negative customer feedback or complaints.
6. Failure to implement security protocols.
7. Poor communication with corporate or other franchisees.
8. Unresolved operational issues or delays in addressing them.
9. Financial discrepancies or budget overruns.
10. Resistance to training or development initiatives.

Dashboard Requirements for Franchise Owner:
1. Compliance status overview with real-time updates.
2. Audit logs and recent compliance verification results.
3. Operational performance metrics (sales, customer satisfaction, etc.).
4. Security compliance status (e.g., MFA implementation).
5. Alerts for any compliance violations or operational issues.
6. Employee performance and training progress tracking.
7. Financial performance indicators (revenue, expenses, etc.).
8. Customer feedback and complaint tracking.
9. Action items and follow-up tasks for ongoing issues.
10. Historical data for trend analysis and decision-making.
