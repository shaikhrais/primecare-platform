Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Responsibilities:
1. Define and communicate the company's vision and mission.
2. Develop and implement strategic plans to achieve business goals.
3. Oversee company operations and ensure efficiency.
4. Build and maintain relationships with stakeholders, including investors, partners, and customers.
5. Monitor industry trends and adapt strategies accordingly.
6. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations.
7. Lead and motivate the executive team and employees.
8. Manage the company's financial performance and budget.
9. Represent the company in public and at industry events.
10. Foster a positive corporate culture and work environment.

Red Flags of Operations:
1. Declining revenue or profit margins.
2. High employee turnover rates.
3. Frequent customer complaints or negative feedback.
4. Delays in project timelines or product launches.
5. Increased operational costs without corresponding revenue growth.
6. Lack of innovation or stagnation in product development.
7. Poor communication within teams or departments.
8. Non-compliance with regulatory requirements.
9. Negative media coverage or public perception issues.
10. Inconsistent performance metrics across departments.

Dashboard Needs for CEO:
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for financial performance (revenue, profit margins, expenses).
2. Employee engagement and turnover metrics.
3. Customer satisfaction scores and feedback trends.
4. Operational efficiency metrics (project timelines, resource allocation).
5. Compliance status and audit results.
6. Market trends and competitive analysis.
7. Real-time data on sales and marketing performance.
8. Risk management indicators (potential threats and mitigation strategies).
9. Updates on strategic initiatives and progress towards goals.
10. Visualizations of data for quick insights (charts, graphs, and tables).
