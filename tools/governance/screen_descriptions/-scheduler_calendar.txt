Work Shift Supervisor Responsibilities:
1. Oversee daily operations and ensure compliance with company policies.
2. Manage staff schedules and ensure adequate coverage for all shifts.
3. Monitor employee performance and provide feedback.
4. Address any operational issues or employee concerns promptly.
5. Conduct regular safety and compliance audits.
6. Maintain accurate records of operations and employee performance.
7. Facilitate communication between management and staff.
8. Implement training programs for new employees.
9. Ensure a safe and productive work environment.
10. Analyze operational data to identify areas for improvement.

Red Flags in Operations:
1. High employee turnover rates.
2. Frequent safety incidents or near misses.
3. Consistent failure to meet production targets.
4. Increased customer complaints or negative feedback.
5. Lack of communication or unresolved conflicts among staff.
6. Inconsistent adherence to safety protocols.
7. Low employee morale or engagement.
8. Delays in operational processes or workflow disruptions.
9. Unresolved compliance issues from audits.
10. Poor performance metrics or KPIs.

Dashboard Needs for Shift Supervisor:
1. Real-time employee attendance and scheduling overview.
2. Performance metrics (KPIs) for productivity and efficiency.
3. Safety incident tracking and reporting.
4. Compliance audit results and action items.
5. Employee feedback and engagement scores.
6. Operational logs and incident reports.
7. Alerts for any red flags or issues that require immediate attention.
8. Training and development progress for staff.
9. Customer satisfaction metrics and feedback.
10. Visualizations of operational data trends over time.
