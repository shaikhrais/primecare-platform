Home Support Worker Responsibilities:
1. Manage active shifts and schedules.
2. Track client locations and visits.
3. Log travel mileage for reimbursement.
4. Request schedule swaps when necessary.
5. Utilize client map locator for efficient travel.
6. Generate travel mileage reports for documentation.

Red Flags of Operation:
1. Frequent schedule changes or swaps indicating instability.
2. High mileage logs suggesting inefficient routing.
3. Missed visits or appointments.
4. Lack of communication regarding client needs or changes.
5. Inconsistent logging of travel mileage.
6. Complaints from clients about missed or late visits.

Dashboard Needs for Home Support Worker:
1. Overview of active shifts and upcoming visits.
2. Quick access to request schedule swaps.
3. Mileage tracking and reporting tools.
4. Client map locator for route optimization.
5. Notifications for missed visits or schedule changes.
6. Summary of client feedback and satisfaction metrics.
